(4) 分块矩阵的转置

$$
A = \begin{bmatrix} A_{11} & A_{12} & \cdots & A_{1t} \\ A_{21} & A_{22} & \cdots & A_{2t} \\ \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots \\ A_{s1} & A_{s2} & \cdots & A_{st} \end{bmatrix}
$$

则

$$
A^{\mathrm{T}} = \begin{bmatrix} A_{11}^{\mathrm{T}} & A_{21}^{\mathrm{T}} & \cdots & A_{s1}^{\mathrm{T}} \\ A_{12}^{\mathrm{T}} & A_{22}^{\mathrm{T}} & \cdots & A_{s2}^{\mathrm{T}} \\ \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots \\ A_{1t}^{\mathrm{T}} & A_{2t}^{\mathrm{T}} & \cdots & A_{st}^{\mathrm{T}} \end{bmatrix}
$$

注：大家可以理解为“大转+小转”.
