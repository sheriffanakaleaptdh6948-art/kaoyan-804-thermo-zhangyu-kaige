**6.3.3 将标准形化为规范形**

若 $n$ 元二次型 $\boldsymbol{x}^{\mathrm{T}} \boldsymbol{A} \boldsymbol{x}$ 经过坐标变换 $\boldsymbol{x}=\boldsymbol{C} \boldsymbol{y}$ 化为标准形
$$ \boldsymbol{x}^{\mathrm{T}} \boldsymbol{A} \boldsymbol{x} \xrightarrow{\boldsymbol{x}=\boldsymbol{C} \boldsymbol{y}} d_{1} y_{1}^{2}+\cdots+d_{p} y_{p}^{2}-d_{p+1} y_{p+1}^{2}-\cdots-d_{p+q} y_{p+q}^{2}, $$
其中 $d_{i}>0(i=1,2, \cdots, p+q)$. 再用坐标变换
$$ \left\{\begin{array}{l} y_{1}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{d_{1}}} z_{1} \\ y_{2}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{d_{2}}} z_{2} \\ \cdots \cdots \\ y_{p+q}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{d_{p+q}}} z_{p+q} \\ y_{p+q+1}=z_{p+q+1} \\ \cdots \cdots \\ y_{n}=z_{n} \end{array}\right. $$
则二次型化为 $z_{1}^{2}+\cdots+z_{p}^{2}-z_{p+1}^{2}-\cdots-z_{p+q}^{2}$，称为规范形. 对应坐标变换为
$$ \boldsymbol{y}=\left(\begin{array}{cccccc} \frac{1}{\sqrt{d_{1}}} & & & & & \\ & \ddots & & & & \\ & & \frac{1}{\sqrt{d_{p+q}}} & & & \\ & & & 1 & & \\ & & & & \ddots & \\ & & & & & 1 \end{array}\right) \boldsymbol{z} $$
经过上述变换，即可将二次型转为对应的规范形.

【例 4】请把二次型 $f\left(x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3}\right)=x_{1} x_{2}+x_{1} x_{3}+2 x_{2} x_{3}$ 化为规范形，并求所做的坐标变换.
