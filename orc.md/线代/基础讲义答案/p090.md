### 6.3 二次型的标准形和规范形

**【例1】** 设二次型 $f(x_1, x_2, x_3) = x_1^2 + x_2^2 + x_3^2 + 4x_1x_2 + 4x_1x_3 + 4x_2x_3$，试用正交变换 $\boldsymbol{x} = \boldsymbol{Qy}$ 将其化成标准形.

**【解】** $f(x_1, x_2, x_3)$ 对应的矩阵为 $\boldsymbol{A} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 2 \\ 2 & 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 2 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$

第一步，计算得 $\boldsymbol{A}$ 的特征多项式 $|\lambda \boldsymbol{E} - \boldsymbol{A}| = \begin{vmatrix} \lambda - 1 & -2 & -2 \\ -2 & \lambda - 1 & -2 \\ -2 & -2 & \lambda - 1 \end{vmatrix} = (\lambda - 5)(\lambda + 1)^2$

令 $|\lambda \boldsymbol{E} - \boldsymbol{A}| = 0$，求出 $\boldsymbol{A}$ 的特征值 $\lambda_1 = \lambda_2 = -1, \quad \lambda_3 = 5$.

第二步，解特征向量。对于特征值 $\lambda_1 = \lambda_2 = -1$，解 $(-\boldsymbol{E} - \boldsymbol{A})\boldsymbol{x} = \boldsymbol{0}$，对系数矩阵作初等行变换

$$-\boldsymbol{E} - \boldsymbol{A} = \begin{pmatrix} -2 & -2 & -2 \\ -2 & -2 & -2 \\ -2 & -2 & -2 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$$

解得基础解系为： $\boldsymbol{\xi}_1 = (-1, 1, 0)^{\mathrm{T}}, \quad \boldsymbol{\xi}_2 = (-1, -1, 2)^{\mathrm{T}}$.

对于特征值 $\lambda_3 = 5$，解 $(5\boldsymbol{E} - \boldsymbol{A})\boldsymbol{x} = \boldsymbol{0}$，对系数矩阵作初等行变换 $5\boldsymbol{E} - \boldsymbol{A} = \begin{pmatrix} 4 & -2 & -2 \\ -2 & 4 & -2 \\ -2 & -2 & 4 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & -1 \\ 0 & 1 & -1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$

解得基础解系为： $\boldsymbol{\xi}_3 = (1, 1, 1)^{\mathrm{T}}$.

$\boldsymbol{\xi}_1, \boldsymbol{\xi}_2, \boldsymbol{\xi}_3$ 已正交，无需施密特正交化。进一步，单位化得

$$\boldsymbol{e}_1 = \frac{1}{\|\boldsymbol{\beta}_1\|}\boldsymbol{\beta}_1 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(-1, 1, 0)^{\mathrm{T}}, \quad \boldsymbol{e}_2 = \frac{1}{\|\boldsymbol{\beta}_2\|}\boldsymbol{\beta}_2 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{6}}(-1, -1, 2)^{\mathrm{T}}, \quad \boldsymbol{e}_3 = \frac{1}{\|\boldsymbol{\xi}_3\|}\boldsymbol{\xi}_3 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}(1, 1, 1)^{\mathrm{T}}$$

令 $\boldsymbol{Q} = (\boldsymbol{e}_1, \boldsymbol{e}_2, \boldsymbol{e}_3)$，即 $\boldsymbol{Q} = \begin{pmatrix} -\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} & -\frac{1}{\sqrt{6}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} \\ \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} & -\frac{1}{\sqrt{6}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} \\ 0 & \frac{2}{\sqrt{6}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} \end{pmatrix}$，使得 $\boldsymbol{Q}^{\mathrm{T}}\boldsymbol{A}\boldsymbol{Q} = \boldsymbol{Q}^{-1}\boldsymbol{A}\boldsymbol{Q} = \begin{pmatrix} -1 & & \\ & -1 & \\ & & 5 \end{pmatrix}$.

于是，作 $\boldsymbol{x} = \boldsymbol{Qy}$，则 $f(x_1, x_2, x_3) = \boldsymbol{x}^{\mathrm{T}}\boldsymbol{A}\boldsymbol{x} \xrightarrow{\boldsymbol{x}=\boldsymbol{Qy}} \boldsymbol{y}^{\mathrm{T}}\boldsymbol{\Lambda}\boldsymbol{y} = -y_1^2 - y_2^2 + 5y_3^2$.
