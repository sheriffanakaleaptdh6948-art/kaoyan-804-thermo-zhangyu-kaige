**【例8】** 计算 $D_n = \begin{vmatrix} a_1+b_1 & a_2 & \cdots & a_n \\ a_1 & a_2+b_2 & \cdots & a_n \\ \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots \\ a_1 & a_2 & \cdots & a_n+b_n \end{vmatrix} (b_i \neq 0)$.

$$
\text{原式} = \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 & \cdots & 0 \\ 1 & a_1+b_1 & a_2 & \cdots & a_n \\ 1 & a_1 & a_2+b_2 & \cdots & a_n \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots \\ 1 & a_1 & a_2 & \cdots & a_n+b_n \end{vmatrix} = \begin{vmatrix} 1 & -a_1 & -a_2 & \cdots & -a_n \\ 1 & b_1 & 0 & \cdots & 0 \\ 1 & 0 & b_2 & \cdots & 0 \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots \\ 1 & 0 & 0 & \cdots & b_n \end{vmatrix} = \cdots = \left(1 + \sum_{i=1}^n \frac{a_i}{b_i}\right) \prod_{j=1}^n b_j
$$

**【例9】** 计算 $D_n = \begin{vmatrix} a_1 & -1 & 0 & \cdots & 0 & 0 \\ a_2 & x & -1 & \cdots & 0 & 0 \\ a_3 & 0 & x & \cdots & 0 & 0 \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots & \vdots \\ a_{n-1} & 0 & 0 & \cdots & x & -1 \\ a_n & 0 & 0 & \cdots & 0 & x \end{vmatrix}$.

当 $n=1$ 时，$D_1 = a_1$；
当 $n \ge 2$ 时，按第 $n$ 行展开，有
$$
D_n = x \begin{vmatrix} a_1 & -1 & 0 & \cdots & 0 \\ a_2 & x & -1 & \cdots & 0 \\ a_3 & 0 & x & \cdots & 0 \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots \\ a_{n-1} & 0 & 0 & \cdots & x \end{vmatrix} + a_n (-1)^{1+n} \begin{vmatrix} -1 & 0 & 0 & \cdots & 0 \\ x & -1 & 0 & \cdots & 0 \\ 0 & x & -1 & \cdots & 0 \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & \cdots & -1 \end{vmatrix}
$$
故 $D_n = x D_{n-1} + a_n (-1)^{n+1} \cdot (-1)^{n-1} = x D_{n-1} + a_n$.

由递推关系可得，
$$
\begin{aligned}
D_n &= x D_{n-1} + a_n & D_n &= x D_{n-1} + a_n \\
D_{n-1} &= x D_{n-2} + a_{n-1} & x D_{n-1} &= x^2 D_{n-2} + x a_{n-1} \\
D_{n-2} &= x D_{n-3} + a_{n-2} & x^2 D_{n-2} &= x^3 D_{n-3} + x^2 a_{n-2} \\
&\vdots & &\vdots \\
D_2 &= a_1 x + a_2 & x^{n-2} D_2 &= a_1 x^{n-1} + x^{n-2} a_2
\end{aligned}
$$
相加可得，$D_n = a_1 x^{n-1} + a_2 x^{n-2} + \cdots + a_{n-1} x + a_n$.

**【例10】** 证明：$D_n = \begin{vmatrix} 2a & 1 & & & \\ a^2 & 2a & 1 & & \\ & \ddots & \ddots & \ddots & \\ & & \ddots & \ddots & 1 \\ & & & a^2 & 2a \end{vmatrix} = (n+1)a^n$. （三对角行列式，对角线均为 $2a$）

当 $n=1$ 时，$D_1 = 2a$，显然成立。
当 $n \ge 2$ 时，按第1列展开，得 $D_n = 2a \cdot D_{n-1} - a^2 \cdot D_{n-2}$.
故 $D_n - a D_{n-1} = a D_{n-1} - a^2 \cdot D_{n-2} = a(D_{n-1} - a D_{n-2})$
$$
= a^2(D_{n-2} - a D_{n-3}) = \cdots = a^{n-2}(D_2 - a D_1) = a^n
$$
也就是说，当 $n \ge 2$ 时，有
$$
\left.
\begin{aligned}
D_n &= a^n + a D_{n-1} & D_n &= a^n + a D_{n-1} \\
D_{n-1} &= a^{n-1} + a D_{n-2} & \Rightarrow a D_{n-1} &= a^n + a^2 D_{n-2} \\
&\vdots & &\vdots \\
D_2 &= a^2 + a \cdot D_1 & a^{n-2} D_2 &= a^n + a^{n-1} D_1
\end{aligned}
\right\} \text{相加可得，} D_n = (n-1)a^n + a^{n-1} D_1 = (n+1)a^n
$$
