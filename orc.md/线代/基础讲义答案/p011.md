**【例5】**
$$
\begin{vmatrix}
1 & -1 & 1 & a-1 \\
1 & -1 & a+1 & -1 \\
1 & a-1 & 1 & -1 \\
a+1 & -1 & 1 & -1
\end{vmatrix}
= a \begin{vmatrix}
1 & -1 & 1 & a-1 \\
1 & -1 & a+1 & -1 \\
1 & a-1 & 1 & -1 \\
1 & -1 & 1 & -1
\end{vmatrix}
= a \begin{vmatrix}
1 & 0 & 0 & a \\
1 & 0 & a & 0 \\
1 & a & 0 & 0 \\
1 & 0 & 0 & 0
\end{vmatrix}
= a(-1)^{\frac{4 \times 3}{2}} a^3 = a^4
$$

法2：画成“爪形”
$$
\begin{vmatrix}
1 & -1 & 1 & a-1 \\
0 & 0 & a & -a \\
0 & a & 0 & -a \\
a & 0 & 0 & -a
\end{vmatrix}
= \begin{vmatrix}
1 & -1 & 1 & a \\
0 & 0 & a & 0 \\
0 & a & 0 & 0 \\
a & 0 & 0 & 0
\end{vmatrix}
= (-1)^{\frac{4 \times 3}{2}} \cdot a^4 = a^4
$$
（注：图中红字标注“前3列都加到最后一列”，“解爪消平爪”）

**【例6】**
$$
\begin{vmatrix}
x & a & a & \cdots & a \\
a & x & a & \cdots & a \\
a & a & x & \cdots & a \\
\vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\
a & a & a & \cdots & x
\end{vmatrix}_{n\text{阶}}
= \frac{1}{a} \begin{vmatrix}
a & a & a & \cdots & a \\
0 & x & a & \cdots & a \\
0 & a & x & \cdots & a \\
\vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\
0 & a & a & \cdots & x
\end{vmatrix}
= \frac{1}{a} \begin{vmatrix}
a & a & a & \cdots & a \\
-a & x-a & 0 & \cdots & 0 \\
-a & 0 & x-a & \cdots & 0 \\
\vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\
-a & 0 & 0 & \cdots & x-a
\end{vmatrix}
$$
$$
= \begin{vmatrix}
1 + \frac{a}{x-a}n & 1 & 1 & \cdots & 1 \\
0 & x-a & 0 & \cdots & 0 \\
0 & 0 & x-a & \cdots & 0 \\
\vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\
0 & 0 & 0 & \cdots & x-a
\end{vmatrix}
= \left[ 1 + \frac{a}{x-a}n \right](x-a)^n = [x-a+an](x-a)^{n-1}
$$

**【例7】**
$$
\begin{vmatrix}
1 & -1 & 1 & a-1 \\
1 & -1 & a+1 & -1 \\
1 & a-1 & 1 & -1 \\
a+1 & -1 & 1 & -1
\end{vmatrix}
= \begin{vmatrix}
1 & 1 & -1 & 1 & -1 \\
0 & 1 & -1 & 1 & a-1 \\
0 & 1 & -1 & a+1 & -1 \\
0 & 1 & a-1 & 1 & -1 \\
0 & a+1 & -1 & 1 & -1
\end{vmatrix}
= \begin{vmatrix}
1 & 1 & -1 & 1 & -1 \\
-1 & 0 & 0 & 0 & a \\
-1 & 0 & 0 & a & 0 \\
-1 & 0 & a & 0 & 0 \\
-1 & a & 0 & 0 & 0
\end{vmatrix}
$$
$$
= \begin{vmatrix}
1+\frac{1}{a}-\frac{1}{a}+\frac{1}{a}-\frac{1}{a} & 1 & -1 & 1 & -1 \\
0 & 0 & 0 & 0 & a \\
0 & 0 & 0 & a & 0 \\
0 & 0 & a & 0 & 0 \\
0 & a & 0 & 0 & 0
\end{vmatrix}
= \begin{vmatrix}
& & & a \\
& & a & \\
& a & & \\
a & & &
\end{vmatrix}
= (-1)^{\frac{4 \times 3}{2}} \cdot a^4 = a^4
$$
