15. 设矩阵 $A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$，则当 $n \geqslant 2$ 时，$A^n =$ \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_.

【答案】 $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & n & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 2^n \end{pmatrix}$

【解析】由题知 $B = E_{12}(1) E_3(2)$，则 $B^2 = B E_{12}(1) E_3(2) = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 2 \end{pmatrix} E_{12}(1) E_3(2) = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 2^2 \end{pmatrix}$，

$B^3 = B^2 E_{12}(1) E_3(2) = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 2^2 \end{pmatrix} E_{12}(1) E_3(2) = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 3 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 2^3 \end{pmatrix}$.
