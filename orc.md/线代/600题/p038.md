4. $\begin{vmatrix} 1+a & 1 & 1 & \cdots & 1 \\ 2 & 2+a & 2 & \cdots & 2 \\ 3 & 3 & 3+a & \cdots & 3 \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots \\ n & n & n & \cdots & n+a \end{vmatrix} = \underline{\hspace{3cm}}$.

【答案】 $\left[ \frac{n(n+1)}{2} + a \right] a^{n-1}$

【解析】（法一）考虑列和相等，将第 $2, 3, \cdots, n$ 行分别加到第一行，进而计算即可.

$$ \text{原式} = \begin{vmatrix} \frac{n(n+1)}{2}+a & \frac{n(n+1)}{2}+a & \frac{n(n+1)}{2}+a & \cdots & \frac{n(n+1)}{2}+a \\ 2 & 2+a & 2 & \cdots & 2 \\ 3 & 3 & 3+a & \cdots & 3 \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots \\ n & n & n & \cdots & n+a \end{vmatrix} $$

$$ = \left[ \frac{n(n+1)}{2} + a \right] \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 1 & 1 & \cdots & 1 \\ 2 & 2+a & 2 & \cdots & 2 \\ 3 & 3 & 3+a & \cdots & 3 \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots \\ n & n & n & \cdots & n+a \end{vmatrix} \xlongequal[\cdots]{-c_1+c_2 \atop -c_1+c_3 \atop -c_1+c_n} \left[ \frac{n(n+1)}{2} + a \right] \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 & \cdots & 0 \\ 2 & a & 0 & \cdots & 0 \\ 3 & 0 & a & \cdots & 0 \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots \\ n & 0 & 0 & \cdots & a \end{vmatrix} $$

$$ = \left[ \frac{n(n+1)}{2} + a \right] a^{n-1}. $$
