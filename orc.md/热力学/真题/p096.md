$$ \Delta s_{\text{iso}} = \Delta s_H + \Delta s_L + \Delta s_{\text{工质}} = \frac{-q_1}{T_H} + \frac{q_2}{T_L} = \frac{-940.3\text{kJ/kg}}{1573.15\text{K}} + \frac{462.3\text{kJ/kg}}{310.15\text{K}} $$
$$ = 0.893\text{kJ/(kg} \cdot \text{K)} $$
$1\text{kg}$ 气体的烟损失 $i = T_0 \Delta s_{\text{iso}} = 310.15\text{K} \times 0.893\text{kJ/(kg} \cdot \text{K)} = 276.964\text{kJ/kg}$

丙烷的燃烧化学反应式
$$ C_3H_8 + 5O_2 \rightarrow 3CO_2 + 4H_2O(g) $$
在标准状态 $298.15\text{K}$，$101325\text{Pa}$ 条件下，标准定压热效应
$$ Q_p^0 = H_{Pr}^0 - H_{Re}^0 = (\sum n_k \Delta H_{f,k}^0)_{Pr} - (\sum n_j H_{f,j}^0)_{Re} $$
故 $Q_p^0 = 3 \Delta H_{f,CO_2}^0 + 4 \Delta H_{f,H_2O(g)}^0 - (\Delta H_{f,C_3H_8}^0 + 5 \Delta H_{f,O_2}^0)$
$$ Q_p^0 = 3 \times (-393522\text{J/mol}) + 4 \times (-241826\text{J/mol}) - (-103900\text{J/mol} + 5 \times 0) $$
$$ = -2043970\text{J/mol} $$

在 $500\text{K}, 101325\text{Pa}$ 条件下，对于反应
$$ C_3H_8 + 5O_2 \rightarrow 3CO_2 + 4H_2O(g) $$
应用基尔霍夫定律，可得定压热效应
$$ Q_p = \Delta H^0 + [\sum n_k(H_{m,k} - H_{m,k}^0)]_{Pr} - [\sum n_j(H_{m,j} - H_{m,j}^0)]_{Re} $$
其中， $\Delta H^0 = H_{Pr}^0 - H_{Re}^0 = Q_p^0 = -2043970\text{J/mol}$。
$$ [\sum n_k(H_{m,k} - H_{m,k}^0)]_{Pr} = 3 \Delta H_{m,CO_2} + 4 \Delta H_{m,H_2O(g)} = 3 \times 8304.9\text{J/mol} + 4 \times 6926.2\text{J/mol} $$
$$ = 52619.5\text{J/mol} $$

丙烷的平均摩尔定压热容
$$ C_{P,m} = 44 \times 10^{-3}\text{kg/mol} \times 2.1\text{kJ/(kg} \cdot \text{K)} = 92.1\text{J/(mol} \cdot \text{K)} $$
$$ [\sum n_j(H_{m,j} - H_{m,j}^0)]_{Re} = (H_{m,500\text{K}} - H_m^0)_{C_3H_8} + 5 \Delta H_{m,O_2} $$
$$ [\sum n_j(H_{m,j} - H_{m,j}^0)]_{Rc} = 92.1\text{J/(mol} \cdot \text{K)}(500\text{K} - 298.15\text{K}) + 5 \times (6084.3\text{J/mol}) $$
$$ = 49011.885\text{J/mol} $$

定压热效应
$$ Q_p = \Delta H^0 + [\sum n_k(H_{m,k} - H_{m,k}^0)]_{Pr} - [\sum n_j(H_{m,j} - H_{m,j}^0)]_{Re} $$
$$ Q_p = -2043970\text{J/mol} + 52619.5\text{J/mol} - 40911.885\text{J/mol} = -2040362.385\text{J/mol} $$
