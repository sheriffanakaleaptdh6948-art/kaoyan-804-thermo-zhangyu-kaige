第2题：已知：氮气、氧气初态 $p_1=2MPa, T_1=300\text{K}$，总容积 $V=1000\text{cm}^3$，各 $500\text{cm}^3$。活塞绝热无摩擦，缸壁绝热，仅氧气端面换热。氧气加热后 $p_2=4MPa$。

氮气侧（可逆绝热压缩）：$k=1.4, R_{g,N_2}=0.2969\frac{\text{kJ}}{\text{kg}\cdot\text{K}}$

$$
V_{2,N_2} = V_{1,N_2}\left(\frac{p_1}{p_2}\right)^{\frac{1}{k}} = 500 \times \left(\frac{2}{4}\right)^{\frac{1}{1.4}} = 500 \times 0.6095 = 304.75\text{cm}^3
$$

$$
T_{2,N_2} = T_1\left(\frac{p_2}{p_1}\right)^{\frac{k-1}{k}} = 300 \times 2^{0.2857} = 365.7\text{K}
$$

$$
m_{N_2} = \frac{p_1 V_1}{R_g T_1} = \frac{2 \times 10^6 \times 500 \times 10^{-6}}{296.9 \times 300} = 0.01123\text{kg}
$$

氧气侧：$R_{g,O_2}=0.2598\frac{\text{kJ}}{\text{kg}\cdot\text{K}}$

$$
V_{2,O_2} = 1000 - 304.75 = 695.25\text{cm}^3
$$

$$
m_{O_2} = \frac{2 \times 10^6 \times 500 \times 10^{-6}}{259.8 \times 300} = 0.01283\text{kg}
$$

$$
T_{2,O_2} = \frac{p_2 V_{2,O_2}}{m_{O_2} R_{g,O_2}} = \frac{4 \times 10^6 \times 695.25 \times 10^{-6}}{0.01283 \times 259.8} = 834.4\text{K} = 561.4^{\circ}\text{C}
$$

所需热量（取整体系统，对外不作功，$Q=\Delta U$）：

$$
\Delta U_{N_2} = m_{N_2} c_{v,N_2} (T_{2,N_2} - T_1) = 0.01123 \times 0.7423 \times (365.7 - 300) = 0.548\text{kJ}
$$

$$
\Delta U_{O_2} = m_{O_2} c_{v,O_2} (T_{2,O_2} - T_1) = 0.01283 \times 0.6495 \times (834.4 - 300) = 4.455\text{kJ}
$$

$$
Q = \Delta U_{N_2} + \Delta U_{O_2} = 0.548 + 4.455 = 5.00\text{kJ}
$$

第3题：已知：空气绝热压缩，$p_1=0.098\text{MPa}, T_1=298\text{K}; p_2=0.588\text{MPa}, T_2=513\text{K} c_p=1.005, R_g=0.287\frac{\text{kJ}}{\text{kg}\cdot\text{K}}, k=1.4$。

(1) 判断是否可逆：

$$
\Delta s = c_p \ln\frac{T_2}{T_1} - R_g \ln\frac{p_2}{p_1} = 1.005 \ln\frac{513}{298} - 0.287 \ln\frac{0.588}{0.098} = 1.005 \times 0.5432 - 0.287 \times 1.7918
$$

$$
= 0.5459 - 0.5142 = 0.0317 \frac{\text{kJ}}{\text{kg}\cdot\text{K}} > 0
$$

绝热过程熵变大于零，过程不可逆（有摩擦损失）。

验证：可逆绝热终温 $T_{2s} = 298 \times 6^{0.2857} = 497.2\text{K} = 224^{\circ}\text{C}$，实际终温 $240^{\circ}\text{C}$ 更高，也说明不可逆。

(2) 实际绝热压缩消耗的轴功：

$$
w_s = h_1 - h_2 = c_p(T_1 - T_2) = 1.005 \times (298 - 513) = -216.1\text{kJ/kg}
$$

消耗轴功 $216.1\text{kJ/kg}$（负号表示外界对工质做功）。

(3) 可逆等温压缩消耗的轴功：

$$
w_t = -R_g T_1 \ln\frac{p_2}{p_1} = -0.287 \times 298 \times \ln 6 = -0.287 \times 298 \times 1.7918 = -153.3\text{kJ/kg}
$$

消耗轴功 $153.3\text{kJ/kg}$
