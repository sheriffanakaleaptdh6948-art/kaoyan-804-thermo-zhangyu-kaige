(2) 循环热效率：

吸热量：

$$
\begin{aligned}
Q_1 &= Q_{23} + Q_{34} \\
Q_{23} &= c_v(T_3 - T_2) \\
Q_{34} &= c_p(T_4 - T_3)
\end{aligned}
$$

放热量：

$$
Q_2 = Q_{51} = c_v(T_5 - T_1)
$$

热效率：

$$
\eta = 1 - \frac{Q_2}{Q_1}
$$

$$
\eta = 1 - \frac{c_v(T_5 - T_1)}{[c_v(T_3 - T_2) + c_p(T_4 - T_3)]}
$$

二、计算题
(1)

$$
\because du = c_v dT + \left[ T \left( \frac{\partial p}{\partial T} \right)_v - p \right] dv
$$

$$
p(v - b) = R_g T
$$

$$
\therefore p = \frac{R_g T}{(v - b)}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\therefore T \left( \frac{\partial p}{\partial T} \right)_v - p &= \frac{R_g T}{(v - b)} - p \\
&= \frac{p(v - b)}{v - b} - p \\
&= 0
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\therefore du = c_v dT
$$

$$
\because c_p - c_v = -T \left( \frac{\partial v}{\partial T} \right)_p^2 \left( \frac{\partial p}{\partial v} \right)_T
$$

$$
v = \frac{R_g T}{p} + b, p = \frac{R_g T}{v - b}
$$

(2)

$$
\left( \frac{\partial v}{\partial T} \right)_p^2 = \left( \frac{R_g}{p} \right)^2 ; \left( \frac{\partial p}{\partial v} \right)_T = - \frac{R_g T}{(v - b)^2}
$$
