瓶内空气绝热放气，经历可逆绝热膨胀：
$$T = T_1 \left(\frac{p}{p_1}\right)^{\frac{(k-1)}{k}}$$
涡轮机入口参数为瓶内瞬时参数(p,T)，可逆绝热膨胀到背压 pb：
$$T_{\text{out}} = T \left(\frac{p_b}{p}\right)^{\frac{(k-1)}{k}}$$
单位质量空气通过涡轮机做功：
$$w = c_p(T - T_{\text{out}}) = c_p T \left[1 - \left(\frac{p_b}{p}\right)^{\frac{(k-1)}{k}}\right]$$
瓶内空气质量 $m = \frac{pV}{R_g T}$，对 p 求导得放气量微分关系，积分总功：
$$W = \left(\frac{c_p V}{k R_g}\right) \left[(p_1 - p_2) - k p_b^{\frac{(k-1)}{k}} \left(p_1^{\frac{1}{k}} - p_2^{\frac{1}{k}}\right)\right]$$
代入数值：
$$\frac{c_p}{k R_g} = \frac{1004}{1.4287} = 2.499$$
$$p_b^{\frac{(k-1)}{k}} = (10^5)^{0.2857} = 26.83$$
$$p_1^{\frac{1}{k}} = (3 \times 10^6)^{0.7143} = 42307, p_2^{\frac{1}{k}} = (3 \times 10^5)^{0.7143} = 8172$$
$$W = 3.542 \times 10^6 \times V$$
由 W = 150 kJ = 150000 J：
$$V = \frac{150000}{3.542 \times 10^6} = 0.0423 m^3 = 42.3 L$$

已知：$m = 1 \text{ kg}$，$p = 0.1013 \text{ MPa}$，$T_1=7^\circ\text{C}=280 \text{ K}$，$T_2=317^\circ\text{C}=590 \text{ K}$，$T_H=283^\circ\text{C}=556 \text{ K}$
空气：$c_v=0.718 \text{ kJ/(kg}\cdot\text{K)}$，$R_g=0.287 \text{ kJ/(kg}\cdot\text{K)}$
(1) 系统熵变与热源供热量 QH 的关系：
$$\Delta S_{\text{sys}} = c_v \ln\left(\frac{T_2}{T_1}\right) = 0.718 \times \ln\left(\frac{590}{280}\right) = 0.536 \text{ kJ/K}$$
$$Q = Q_H + W_s = \Delta U = 222.58 \text{ kJ} \quad (Q_H\text{为热源供热，}W_s\text{为搅拌功})$$
$$Sg = \Delta S_{\text{sys}} - \frac{Q_H}{T_H} = 0.536 - \frac{Q_H}{556}$$
由于题目未给出 QH 的具体分配，(1)的答案应为上式：Sg 随 $Q_H$ 在 0~222.58 kJ 之间变化，即
$$0.136 \le Sg \le 0.536 \text{ \frac{kJ}{K}}$$
