循环的 T-s 图如图所示。$T_2 = T_1 \left(\frac{p_2}{p_1}\right)^{\frac{k-1}{k}} = T_1 \pi^{\frac{k-1}{k}}, T_4 = T_3 \left(\frac{p_4}{p_3}\right)^{\frac{k-1}{k}} = T_3 \left(\frac{p_1}{p_2}\right)^{\frac{k-1}{k}} = T_3 \left(\frac{1}{\pi}\right)^{\frac{k-1}{k}}$

绝热效率 $\eta_{C,s} = \frac{w_{Cs}}{w_C} = \frac{h_2 - h_1}{h_{2'} - h_1} = \frac{T_2 - T_1}{T_{2'} - T_1}$，那么 $T_{2'} = T_1 + \frac{T_2 - T_1}{\eta_{C,s}}$

相对内效率 $\eta_T = \frac{w'_T}{w_T} = \frac{h_3 - h_{4'}}{h_3 - h_4} = \frac{T_3 - T_{4'}}{T_3 - T_4}$，那么 $T_{4'} = T_3 - \eta_T (T_3 - T_4)$

循环吸热量 $q_1 = c_p (T_3 - T_{2'})$，循环的放热量 $q_2 = c_p (T_{4'} - T_1)$

循环热效率 $\eta_t = 1 - \frac{q_2}{q_1} = 1 - \frac{T_{4'} - T_1}{T_3 - T_1 - \frac{T_2 - T_1}{\eta_{C,s}}} = 1 - \frac{T_3 - T_1 - \eta_T \left[T_3 - T_3 \left(\frac{1}{\pi}\right)^{\frac{k-1}{k}}\right]}{T_3 - T_1 - \frac{T_2 - T_1}{\eta_{C,s}}} = 1 - \frac{T_3 - T_1 - \eta_T \left[T_3 - T_3 \left(\frac{1}{\pi}\right)^{\frac{k-1}{k}}\right]}{T_3 - T_1 - \frac{T_1 \pi^{\frac{k-1}{k}} - T_1}{\eta_{C,s}}}$

对于反应 $CO + \frac{1}{2} O_2 \rightarrow CO_2$ 生成物的摩尔焓 $H_{Pr} = \Delta H^0_{f, CO_2} + \left(H_{m, 2000\text{K}} - H^0_m\right)_{CO_2}$

即 $H_{Pr} = -393522 \text{ J/mol} + (100811.2 \text{ J/mol} - 9624 \text{ J/mol}) = -302334.8 \text{ J/mol}$

反应物的摩尔 $H_{Re} = \Delta H^0_{f, CO} + \left(H_{m, 2000\text{K}} - H^0_m\right)_{CO} + \frac{1}{2} \left[\Delta H^0_{f, O_2} + \left(H_{m, 2000\text{K}} - H^0_m\right)_{O_2}\right]$

$$
H_{Re} = -110527 \text{ J/mol} + (65413.9 \text{ J/mol} - 8671.0 \text{ J/mol})_{CO}
$$
$$
+ \frac{1}{2} [0 + (67857.5 \text{ J/mol} - 8683.0 \text{ J/mol})_{O_2}]
$$

那么 $H_{Re} = -24196.85 \text{ J/mol}$

定压热效应 $Q_p = H_{Pr} - H_{Re} = -302334.8 \text{ J/mol} - (-24196.85 \text{ J/mol}) = -278137.95 \text{ J/mol}$
