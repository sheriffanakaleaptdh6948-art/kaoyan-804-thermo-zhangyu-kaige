依题意：$\Delta U_{\text{ad}} = 45\text{kJ} - 5\text{kJ} = 40\text{kJ}$

$$ \frac{T_2}{T_1} = \left(\frac{p_2}{p_1}\right)^{\frac{n-1}{n}} $$
$$ \text{故 } \frac{n-1}{n} = \frac{\ln \frac{T_2}{T_1}}{\ln \frac{p_2}{p_1}} = \frac{\ln \frac{600\text{K}}{300\text{K}}}{\ln \frac{0.5\text{MPa}}{0.1\text{MPa}}} = \frac{0.693}{1.609} = 0.431 $$
