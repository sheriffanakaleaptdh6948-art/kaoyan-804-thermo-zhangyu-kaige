$s_{g1-2} = s_{1-2} = -0.1 \text{ kJ/(kg}\cdot\text{K)} < 0$ 所以流动的截面是从 2-2 截面到 1-1 截面。
$s_{g2-1} = s_{2-1} = 0.1 \text{ kJ/(kg}\cdot\text{K)} > 0$

由稳定能量流动方程可得：$\frac{1}{2}\Delta c_f^2 = h_1 - h_2$

又由质量守恒可知：$\frac{c_{f1}}{c_{f2}} = \frac{A_1}{A_2} \cdot \frac{v_1}{v_2} = \frac{v_1}{v_2}$

两式联立可得：
$$
\begin{aligned}
c_{f1} &= 257.744 \text{ m/s} \\
c_{f2} &= 310.535 \text{ m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$

(1) $\Delta U_{1-2} = 0, \ W_{1-2} = Q_{1-2} - \Delta U_{1-2} = 100 \text{ kJ}$
$$ \Delta U_{3-4} = 0, \ Q_{3-4} = \Delta U_{3-4} + W_{3-4} = -48 \text{ kJ} $$
$$ Q_{4-1} = 0, \ \Delta S_{4-1} = 0 $$
$$ \because \Delta S_{1-2} + \Delta S_{2-3} + \Delta S_{3-4} + \Delta S_{4-1} = 0 $$
$$ \therefore \Delta S_{2-3} = 0.06 \text{ kJ/K} $$
$$ \because Q_{1-2} = 100 \text{ kJ}, \ \Delta S_{1-2} = 0.1 \text{ kJ/K} $$
$$ \therefore T_1 = \frac{Q_{1-2}}{\Delta S_{1-2}} = 1000 \text{ K} $$
$$ \because Q_{3-4} = -48 \text{ kJ}, \ \Delta S_{3-4} = -0.16 \text{ kJ/K} $$
$$ \therefore T_2 = \frac{Q_{3-4}}{\Delta S_{3-4}} = 300 \text{ K} $$
$$ \therefore \Delta U_{2-3} = c_v(T_2 - T_1) = -502.6 \text{ kJ} $$
$$ \therefore \Delta U_{4-1} = 502.6 \text{ kJ} $$

(2) $W_{2-3} = Q_{2-3} - \Delta U_{2-3} = 10 \text{ kJ} + 502.6 \text{ kJ} = 512.6 \text{ kJ}$
$$ W_{4-1} = Q_{4-1} - \Delta U_{4-1} = 0 - 502.6 \text{ kJ} = -502.6 \text{ kJ} $$

(3) $\eta_t = 1 - \frac{|Q_{3-4}|}{Q_{1-2} + Q_{2-3}} = 1 - \frac{48 \text{ kJ}}{100 \text{ kJ} + 10 \text{ kJ}} = 56.36\%$

(4) $\because W = \frac{k-1}{n-1} c_\tau (T_2 - T_3) = -\Delta U_{2-3} \times \frac{k-1}{n-1} = 512.6 \text{ kJ}$
$$ \therefore n = 1.392 $$
