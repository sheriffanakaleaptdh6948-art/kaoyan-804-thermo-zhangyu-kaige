(1) 容积效率：
$$
\begin{aligned}
\eta &= 1 - c\left[\left(\frac{p_2}{p_1}\right)^{\frac{1}{n}} - 1\right] \\
&= 0.9216
\end{aligned}
$$

(2) 吸气状态比体积：
$$
V_1 = \frac{R_{\text{g}}T_1}{p_1} = 0.287 \times \frac{290}{98} = \frac{83.23}{98} = \frac{0.8493 \text{m}^3}{\text{kg}}
$$

(3) 每循环吸气容积：
$$
V_{\text{in}} = m \times V_1 = 0.5 \times 0.8493 = 0.4246 \text{m}^3
$$

(4) 气缸排量（活塞排量）：
$$
V_h = \frac{V_{\text{in}}}{\eta} = \frac{0.4246}{0.9216} = 0.4608 \text{m}^3
$$

(5) 余隙容积：
$$
V_c = c \times V_h = 0.05 \times 0.4608 = 0.02304 \text{m}^3
$$

(6) 压缩终温：
$$
T_2 = T_1 \left(\frac{p_2}{p_1}\right)^{\frac{n-1}{n}} = 403.4 \text{K}
$$

(7) 余隙容积中残留的高压空气质量：
$$
m_{\text{res}} = \frac{p_2 V_c}{R_{\text{g}} T_2} = 350 \times \frac{0.02304}{0.287 \times 403.4} = \frac{8.064}{115.8} = 0.0696 \text{kg}
$$

(8) 压缩开始时气缸内总空气质量（新吸入空气+余隙残留空气）：
$$
m_{\text{total}} = m_{\text{out}} + m_{\text{res}} = 0.5 + 0.0696 = 0.570 \text{kg}
$$

答：压缩过程中气缸内空气质量约为 0.570 kg。
