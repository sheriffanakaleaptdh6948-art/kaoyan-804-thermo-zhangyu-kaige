$$T_2 = T_1 \cdot \varepsilon^{k-1} = T_1 \cdot \left(\frac{T_3}{T_1}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}} = \sqrt{(T_1 T_3)}$$

$$T_4 = \frac{T_3}{\varepsilon^{k-1}} = \frac{T_3}{\frac{T_3^{\frac{1}{2}}}{T_1^{\frac{1}{2}}}} = \sqrt{(T_1 T_3)}$$

所以：

$$T_2 = T_4 = \sqrt{(T_1 T_3)}$$

热效率：

$$\eta = 1 - \frac{1}{\varepsilon^{k-1}} = 1 - \frac{1}{\left(\frac{T_3}{T_1}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}} = 1 - \sqrt{\left(\frac{T_1}{T_3}\right)}$$
