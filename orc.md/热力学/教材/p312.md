点 5:

$\begin{aligned}v_{4}=\frac{R_{g} T_{4}}{p_{4}}=\frac{287 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times 1987.4 \mathrm{~K}}{10.3 \times 10^{6} \mathrm{~Pa}}=0.0554 \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{kg} \\\rho=\frac{v_{4}}{v_{3}}=\frac{0.0554 \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{kg}}{0.03879 \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{kg}}=1.428 \\v_{5}=v_{1}=0.5624 \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{kg}\end{aligned}$

$\begin{aligned}p_{5}=p_{4}\left(\frac{v_{4}}{v_{5}}\right)^{\kappa}=10.3 \mathrm{MPa} \times\left(\frac{0.0554 \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{kg}}{0.5624 \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{kg}}\right)^{1.4}=0.4015 \mathrm{MPa} \\T_{5}=\frac{p_{5} v_{5}}{R_{g}}=\frac{0.4015 \times 10^{6} \mathrm{~Pa} \times 0.5624 \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{kg}}{287 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})}=786.8 \mathrm{~K} \\q_{2}=c_{v}\left(T_{5}-T_{1}\right)=0.718 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times(786.8 \mathrm{~K}-333.15 \mathrm{~K})=325.7 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \\w_{\text {net }}=q_{1}-q_{2}=900 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}-325.7 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}=574.3 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}\end{aligned}$

$\begin{aligned}\eta_{\mathrm{i}}=1-\frac{\lambda \rho^{\kappa}-1}{\varepsilon^{\kappa-1}}\left[(\lambda-1)+\kappa \lambda(\rho-1)\right] \\=1-\frac{1.43 \times 1.42^{1.4}-1}{14.5^{1.4-1} \times(1.43-1)+1.4 \times 1.43 \times(1.42-1)}=0.639\end{aligned}$

$\eta_{\mathrm{i}}=\frac{w_{\text {net }}}{q_{1}}=\frac{574.3 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}}{900 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}}=0.638$

在吸热过程中空气熵增为

$\begin{aligned}\Delta s_{2-4} & =c_{p} \ln \frac{T_{4}}{T_{2}}-R_{\mathrm{g}} \ln \frac{p_{4}}{p_{2}} \\& =1.004 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times \ln \frac{1987.4 \mathrm{~K}}{971.0 \mathrm{~K}}-0.287 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times \\& \ln \frac{10.3 \mathrm{MPa}}{7.187 \mathrm{MPa}}=0.6158 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})\end{aligned}$

所以平均吸热温度为

$T_{1 \mathrm{~m}}=\frac{q_{1}}{\Delta s_{2-4}}=\frac{900 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}}{0.6158 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})}=1461.5 \mathrm{~K}$

循环吸热量 $q_{1}$ 中的可用能

$e_{x, q}=\left(1-\frac{T_{0}}{T_{1 \mathrm{~m}}}\right) q_{1}=\left(1-\frac{293.15 \mathrm{~K}}{1461.5 \mathrm{~K}}\right) \times 900 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}=719.5 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$

循环㶲效率

$\eta_{e_{x}}=\frac{w_{\text {net }}}{e_{x, q}}=\frac{574.3 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}}{719.5 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}}=0.798$
