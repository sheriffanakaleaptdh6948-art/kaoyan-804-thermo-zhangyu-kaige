$T_{2^{\prime}}=T_{1}+\frac{T_{2^{\prime}}-T_{1}}{\eta_{c, s}}$

例 8-3 叶轮式压缩机, 氮气进口 $p_{1}=0.0972 \mathrm{MPa}, t_{1}=20{ }^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$, 出口压力 $p_{2}=$ $311.11 \mathrm{kPa}$, 进口处氮气流量 $q_{V}=113.3 \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{min}$, 压气机绝热效率 $\eta_{\mathrm{c}, \mathrm{s}}=0.80$, 略去进出口动能差和位能差, 求: (1) 压气机定熵压缩的耗功率; (2) 实际耗功率; (3) 由于不可逆而多耗功; (4) 若 $t_{0}=20{ }^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$, 求作功能力损失 $\dot{i}$ 。氮气的比热容取常数, $c_{p}=1.038 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) 、 R_{\mathrm{g}}=0.297 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})$ 。

解 (1) 定熵压缩功率

$\begin{aligned} & T_{2}=T_{1}\left(\frac{p_{2}}{p_{1}}\right)^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}}=(20+273.15) \mathrm{K} \times\left(\frac{311.11 \mathrm{kPa}}{97.2 \mathrm{kPa}}\right)^{\frac{1.4-1}{1.4}}=408.5 \mathrm{~K} \\ & v_{1}=\frac{R_{\mathrm{g}} T_{1}}{p_{1}}=\frac{297 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times 293 \mathrm{~K}}{97.2 \mathrm{kPa}}=0.895 \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{kg} \\ & q_{m}=\frac{q_{V}}{v_{1}}=\frac{113.3 \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{min}}{0.895 \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{kg}}=126.6 \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{min} \end{aligned}$

$\begin{aligned} P_{s} & =q_{m} c_{p}\left(T_{1}-T_{2}\right) \\ & =126.6 \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{min} \times 1.038 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times(293-408.5) \mathrm{K} \\ & =-15178 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{min}=-252.97 \mathrm{kW} \end{aligned}$

(2) 实际功率

$P_{s}^{\prime}=\frac{P_{s}}{\eta_{c, s}}=\frac{-15178 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{min}}{0.8}=-18972.5 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{min}=-316.21 \mathrm{kW}$

(3) 不可逆而多耗功率

$\begin{aligned} \Delta P & =P_{s}^{\prime}-P_{s}=-18972.5 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{min}+15178 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{min} \\ & =-3794.5 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{min}=-63.24 \mathrm{kW} \end{aligned}$

(4) 作功能力损失

$\begin{aligned} T_{2^{\prime}} & =T_{1}+\frac{T_{2}-T_{1}}{\eta_{\mathrm{C}, s}}=293 \mathrm{~K}+\frac{408.5 \mathrm{~K}-293 \mathrm{~K}}{0.8}=437.4 \mathrm{~K} \\ \Delta \dot{S}_{\mathrm{N}_{2}} & =q_{m}\left(c_{p} \ln \frac{T_{2^{\prime}}}{T_{2}}-R_{\mathrm{g}} \ln \frac{p_{2^{\prime}}}{p_{2}}\right)=q_{m} c_{p} \ln \frac{T_{2^{\prime}}}{T_{2}} \\ & =126.6 \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{min} \times 1.038 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times \ln \frac{437.4 \mathrm{~K}}{408.5 \mathrm{~K}} \\ & =8.983 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{K} \cdot \mathrm{min}) \\ \dot{\mathrm{i}} & =T_{0} \Delta S_{\text {iso }}=T_{0}\left(\Delta \dot{\mathrm{S}}_{\mathrm{N}_{2}}+\Delta \dot{\mathrm{S}}_{0}\right)=T_{0} \Delta \dot{\mathrm{S}}_{\mathrm{N}_{2}} \end{aligned}$
