整个大系统列出熵平衡方程，确定各处熵产 $S_g$，然后求出焱损失。本题采用熵分析法。

将整个过程划分为三个子过程：热源 $T_H$ 和中间热源 $T_1$ 间单纯地传热；两个中间热源 $T_1$ 和 $T_2$ 与实际热机完成热量 $q_1 - q_2$ 转变为功 $w_{\text{net}}$ 的循环；中间热源 $T_2$ 和环境 $T_0$ 间单纯地传热。三个子过程完成的效果与整个过程相同，并且各自可作为孤立系统（或闭口绝热系）。下面对三个子过程分别计算。

(1) 由 $T_H \to T_1$，热源放热，熵变 $\Delta s_{T_1} = \frac{q_1}{T_H}$；中间热源吸热，熵变 $\Delta s_{T_1} = \frac{q_1}{T_1}$，孤立系统的熵增 $\Delta s_{\text{iso}, 1}$ 即不可逆传热的熵产 $s_{\text{g}, 1}$，等于热源熵变与中间热源熵变之和

$\Delta s_{\text{iso}, 1} = s_{\text{g}, 1} = \Delta s_{T_1} + \Delta s_{T_1} = \frac{q_1}{T_H} + \frac{q_1}{T_1}$

不可逆传热的焱损失为 $I_1 = T_0 \Delta s_{\text{iso}, 1} = T_0 s_{\text{g}, 1}$，得

$I_1 = T_0 \left( \frac{1}{T_1} - \frac{1}{T_H} \right) q_1 = 288 \, \text{K} \times \left( \frac{1}{600 \, \text{K}} - \frac{1}{1300 \, \text{K}} \right) \times 100 \, \text{kJ/kg} = 25.8 \, \text{kJ/kg}$

(2) 工作在 $T_1$ 和 $T_2$ 间的卡诺机

$\begin{gathered} \eta_c = 1 - \frac{T_2}{T_1} = 1 - \frac{300 \, \text{K}}{600 \, \text{K}} = 0.5 \\ w_c = \eta_c q_1 = 0.5 \times 100 \, \text{kJ/kg} = 50 \, \text{kJ/kg} \end{gathered}$

由题意，实际循环的热效率

$\eta_t = 0.8 \eta_c = 0.8 \times 0.5 = 0.4$

实际循环净功

$w_{\text{net}} = \eta_t q_1 = 0.4 \times 100 \, \text{kJ/kg} = 40.0 \, \text{kJ/kg}$

实际放热量

$q_2 = q_1 - w_{\text{net}} = 100 \, \text{kJ/kg} - 40 \, \text{kJ/kg} = 60 \, \text{kJ/kg}$

$\begin{aligned} \Delta s_{\text{iso}, 2} & = s_{\text{g}, 2} = \Delta s_{T_1} + \oint ds + \Delta s_{T_2} = \frac{q_1}{T_1} + 0 + \frac{q_2}{T_2} \\ & = \frac{-100 \, \text{kJ/kg}}{600 \, \text{K}} + \frac{60 \, \text{kJ/kg}}{300 \, \text{K}} = 0.0333 \, \text{kJ/(kg·K)} \\ I_2 & = T_0 s_{\text{g}, 2} = 288 \, \text{K} \times 0.0333 \, \text{kJ/(kg·K)} = 9.6 \, \text{kJ/kg} \end{aligned}$

(3) 由 $T_2 \to T_0$，不可逆传热引起的孤立系熵增

$\Delta s_{\text{iso}, 3} = s_{\text{g}, 3} = \Delta s_{T_2} + \Delta s_{T_0} = \frac{q_2}{T_2} + \frac{q_2}{T_0}$

焱损失

$I_3 = T_0 \Delta s_{\text{iso}, 3} = T_0 \left( -\frac{1}{T_2} + \frac{1}{T_0} \right) q_2$
