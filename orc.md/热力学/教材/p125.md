例 4-1 1 kg 空气, 已知其初始状态时 $p_1 = 0.1 \text{ MPa}$, $t_1 = 100^\circ \text{C}$, 按定压过程 $1-2_p$ 加热到温度 $t_2 = 400^\circ \text{C}$。求终态压力 $p_2_p$ 和比体积 $v_2_p$, 以及过程的 $\Delta u$ 、 $\Delta h$ 、$\Delta s$ 、$q$ 、$w$ 和 $w_1$ 。(1) 按定值比热容计算, 且 $c_V = 0.717 \text{ kJ/(kg} \cdot \text{K)}$, $c_p = 1.004 \text{ kJ/(kg} \cdot \text{K)}$; (2) 利用平均热容表计算; (3) 利用气体热力性质表计算。

空气的气体常数

$\begin{aligned} R_g & = c_p - c_V = 1.004 \text{ kJ/(kg} \cdot \text{K)} - 0.717 \text{ kJ/(kg} \cdot \text{K}) \\ & = 0.287 \text{ kJ/(kg} \cdot \text{K}) = 287 \text{ J/(kg} \cdot \text{K}) \end{aligned}$

初态 1 的比体积

$\begin{aligned} v_1 & = \frac{R_g T_1}{p_1} \\ & = \frac{287 \text{ J/(kg} \cdot \text{K)} \times (100 + 273) \text{ K}}{0.1 \times 10^6 \text{ Pa}} = 1.0705 \text{ m}^3/\text{kg} \end{aligned}$

状态 $2_p: p_2_p = p_1 = 0.1 \times 10^6 \text{ Pa}$

$v_{2p} = \frac{R_g T_2}{p_2p} = \frac{287 \text{ J/(kg} \cdot \text{K)} \times (400 + 273) \text{ K}}{0.1 \times 10^6 \text{ Pa}} = 1.9315 \text{ m}^3/\text{kg}$

或

$v_{2p} = \frac{T_2}{T_1} v_1 = \frac{(400 + 273) \text{ K} \times 1.0705 \text{ m}^3/\text{kg}}{(100 + 273) \text{ K}} = 1.9315 \text{ m}^3/\text{kg}$

(1) 按定值比热容计算

由于理想气体的 $u$ 、$h$ 是温度的单值函数, 故

$\Delta u_{1-2} = c_V (t_2 - t_1) = 0.717 \text{ kJ/(kg} \cdot \text{K)} \times (400^\circ \text{C} - 100^\circ \text{C}) = 215.1 \text{ kJ/kg}$

$\Delta h_{1-2} = c_p (t_2 - t_1) = 1.004 \text{ kJ/(kg} \cdot \text{K}) \times (400^\circ \text{C} - 100^\circ \text{C}) = 301.2 \text{ kJ/kg}$

$\Delta s_{1-2} = c_p \ln \frac{T_2}{T_1} = 1.004 \text{ kJ/(kg} \cdot \text{K)} \times \ln \frac{673 \text{ K}}{373 \text{ K}} = 0.5925 \text{ kJ/(kg} \cdot \text{K)}$

$q = \Delta h_{1-2} = c_p (t_2 - t_1) = 301.2 \text{ kJ/kg}$

$w = p (v_1 - v_2) = R_g (T_2 - T_1) = 0.287 \text{ kJ/(kg} \cdot \text{K)} \times (673 - 373) \text{ K} = 86.1 \text{ kJ/kg}$

或

$w = q - \Delta u = 301.2 \text{ kJ/kg} - 215.1 \text{ kJ/kg} = 86.1 \text{ kJ/kg}$

$w_1 = 0$
