忽略水泵功, 实际耗汽率

$d_{i}=\frac{1}{h_{1}-h_{2 \text {, act }}}=\frac{1}{1316.3 \times 10^{3} \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{kg}}=7.597 \times 10^{-7} \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{J}$

(3) 过程作功能力损失

过程作功能力损失可据熵产计算。查水和水蒸气图表, 得: $h_{2^{\prime}}=$ $137.72 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg} 、 s_{2^{\prime}}=0.4761 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}), t_{2^{\prime}}=32.88^{\circ} \mathrm{C}, s_{4}=s_{2^{\prime}}, s_{2 \text {, act }}=6.920 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})$ 、 $v_{2 \text {, act }}=23 \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{kg}$ 。在蒸汽轮机中, 工质绝热膨胀, 工质熵增即为熵产, 所以

$\begin{aligned} I_{\mathrm{T}} & =T_{0} s_{\mathrm{g}}=T_{0}\left(\Delta s-s_{\mathrm{i}}\right)=T_{0}\left(s_{2 \text {, act }}-s_{1}\right) \\ & =298 \mathrm{~K} \times[6.920 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})-6.442 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})]=142.44 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \end{aligned}$

在冷凝器中, 工质维持 $32.88^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$, 向 $298 \mathrm{~K}$ 的环境介质放热, 所以

$q_{2}=h_{2 \text {, act }}-h_{2^{\prime}}=2109.8 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}-137.72 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}=1972.08 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$

$\begin{aligned} I_{\mathrm{C}} & =T_{0} s_{\mathrm{g}}=T_{0} s_{\mathrm{iso}}=T_{0}\left(s_{2^{\prime}}-s_{2 \text {, act }}-s_{\mathrm{i}}\right)=T_{0}\left(s_{2^{\prime}}-s_{2 \text {, act }}-\frac{q_{2}}{T_{0}}\right) \\ & =298 \mathrm{~K} \times\left[0.4761 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})-6.920 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})+\frac{1972.08 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}}{298 \mathrm{~K}}\right] \\ & =51.80 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \end{aligned}$

由于水泵内进行的过程是可逆绝热过程 $\left(s_{4}=s_{2^{\prime}}\right)$, 所以作功能力的不可逆损失为零。在锅炉中, 若取热源平均温度为 $831.45 \mathrm{~K}$, 工质吸热量 $q_{1}=$ $3271.22 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$, 则

$\begin{aligned} I_{\mathrm{B}} & =T_{0} s_{\mathrm{g}}=T_{0}\left(s_{1}-s_{4}-s_{\mathrm{i}}\right)=T_{0}\left(s_{1}-s_{4}-\frac{q_{1}}{T_{\mathrm{H}}}\right) \\ & =298 \mathrm{~K} \times\left[6.442 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})-0.4761 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \frac{3271.22 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}}{831.45 \mathrm{~K}}\right] \\ & =605.40 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \end{aligned}$

所以循环的不可逆损失

$\begin{aligned} I & =\sum I_{\mathrm{i}}=I_{\mathrm{T}}+I_{\mathrm{C}}+I_{\mathrm{B}}=142.44 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}+51.80 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}+605.40 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \\ & =799.64 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \end{aligned}$

或者, 考虑到循环后, 工质熵变为零, 所以

$\begin{aligned} I & =T_{0} \Delta s_{\mathrm{iso}}=T_{0}\left(\Delta s_{0}+\Delta s_{\mathrm{H}}\right)=T_{0}\left(\frac{q_{2}}{T_{0}}-\frac{q_{1}}{T_{\mathrm{H}}}\right) \\ & =298 \mathrm{~K} \times\left(\frac{1972.08 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}}{298 \mathrm{~K}}-\frac{3271.22 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}}{831.45 \mathrm{~K}}\right)=799.64 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \end{aligned}$
