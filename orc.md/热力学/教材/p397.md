已知 $T_{\mathrm{A} 1}=288 \mathrm{~K} 、 T_{\mathrm{B} 1}=328 \mathrm{~K}, \mathrm{O}_{2} 、 \mathrm{~N}_{2}$ 都是双原子气体, 摩尔定容热容为 $\frac{5}{2} R$, 即 $C_{V, \mathrm{~m}, \mathrm{~A}}=C_{V, \mathrm{~m}, \mathrm{~B}}=\frac{5}{2} \times 8.3145 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{mol} \cdot \mathrm{K})$, 将已知数值代入式 (a) 后, 解出 $T_{2}=315.6 \mathrm{~K}$ 。混合气体的压力

$\begin{aligned} p_{2} & =\frac{n R T_{2}}{V}=\frac{n R T_{2}}{V_{\mathrm{A} 1}+V_{\mathrm{B} 1}} \\ & =\frac{161.1 \mathrm{~mol} \times 8.3145 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{mol} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times 315.6 \mathrm{~K}}{(0.3+0.6) \mathrm{m}^{3}} \\ & =0.4697 \times 10^{6} \mathrm{~Pa} \end{aligned}$

(2) $\mathrm{O}_{2}$ 和 $\mathrm{N}_{2}$ 的分压力

$\begin{aligned} p_{\mathrm{A} 2} & =x_{\mathrm{A}} p_{2}=\frac{n_{\mathrm{A}}}{n} p_{2}=\frac{50.1 \mathrm{~mol}}{161.1 \mathrm{~mol}} \times 0.4697 \mathrm{MPa}=0.1461 \mathrm{MPa} \\ p_{\mathrm{B} 2} & =x_{\mathrm{B}} p_{2}=\frac{n_{\mathrm{B}}}{n} p_{2}=\frac{111.0 \mathrm{~mol}}{161.1 \mathrm{~mol}} \times 0.4697 \mathrm{MPa}=0.3236 \mathrm{MPa} \end{aligned}$

(3) 热力系的熵变

$\begin{aligned} \Delta S & =\Delta S_{\mathrm{A}}+\Delta S_{\mathrm{B}}=n_{\mathrm{A}} \Delta S_{\mathrm{m}, \mathrm{A}}+n_{\mathrm{B}} \Delta S_{\mathrm{m}, \mathrm{B}} \\ & =n_{\mathrm{A}}\left(C_{p, \mathrm{~m}, \mathrm{~A}} \ln \frac{T_{2}}{T_{\mathrm{A} 1}}-R \ln \frac{p_{\mathrm{A} 2}}{p_{\mathrm{A} 1}}\right)+n_{\mathrm{B}}\left(C_{p, \mathrm{~m}, \mathrm{~B}} \ln \frac{T_{2}}{T_{\mathrm{B} 1}}-R \ln \frac{p_{\mathrm{B} 2}}{p_{\mathrm{B} 1}}\right) \end{aligned}$

因摩尔定压热容 $C_{p, \mathrm{~m}, \mathrm{~A}}=C_{p, \mathrm{~m}, \mathrm{~B}}=\frac{7}{2} \times 8.3145 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{mol} \cdot \mathrm{K})=29.10 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{mol} \cdot \mathrm{K})$, 故

$\begin{aligned} \Delta S & =50.1 \mathrm{~mol} \times\left(29.10 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{mol} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times \ln \frac{315.6 \mathrm{~K}}{288 \mathrm{~K}}-\right. \\ & \left.8.3145 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{mol} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times \ln \frac{0.1461 \mathrm{MPa}}{0.4 \mathrm{MPa}}\right)+ \\ & 111.0 \mathrm{~mol} \times\left(29.10 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{mol} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times \ln \frac{315.6 \mathrm{~K}}{328 \mathrm{~K}}-\right. \\ & \left.8.3145 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{mol} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times \ln \frac{0.3236 \mathrm{MPa}}{0.504 \mathrm{MPa}}\right) \\ & =836.6 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{K} \end{aligned}$

因过程绝热, 熵流 $S_{\mathrm{f}}=0$, 故 $S_{\mathrm{g}}=\Delta S=836.6 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{K}$ 。
