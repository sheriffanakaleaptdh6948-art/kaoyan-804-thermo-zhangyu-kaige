$p_{0}=p_{1}\left(\frac{T_{0}}{T_{1}}\right)^{\frac{\kappa}{\kappa-1}}=0.6 \mathrm{MPa} \times\left(\frac{1077.3 \mathrm{~K}}{1073 \mathrm{~K}}\right)^{\frac{1.379}{1.379-1}}=0.609 \mathrm{MPa}$

$p_{\mathrm{cr}}=\nu_{\mathrm{cr}} p_{0}=\left(\frac{2}{\kappa+1}\right)^{\frac{\kappa}{\kappa-1}} p_{0}=\left(\frac{2}{1+1.379}\right)^{\frac{1.379}{1.379-1}} \times 0.609 \mathrm{MPa}=0.324 \mathrm{MPa}>p_{\mathrm{b}}$

所以喷管出口截面的压力 $p_{2}=p_{\mathrm{cr}}=0.324 \mathrm{MPa}_{\circ}$

若可逆膨胀，则：

$\begin{aligned} & T_{2_{\mathrm{s}}}=T_{0}\left(\frac{p_{2}}{p_{0}}\right)^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}}=1077.3 \mathrm{~K} \times\left(\frac{0.324 \mathrm{MPa}}{0.609 \mathrm{MPa}}\right)^{\frac{1.379-1}{1.379}}=905.77 \mathrm{~K} \\ & v_{2_{\mathrm{s}}}=\frac{R_{\mathrm{g}} T_{2_{\mathrm{s}}}}{p_{2}}=\frac{318.3 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times 905.77 \mathrm{~K}}{0.324 \times 10^{6} \mathrm{~Pa}}=0.8898 \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{kg} \\ & c_{\mathrm{12}}=\sqrt{2\left(h_{0}-h_{2_{\mathrm{s}}}\right)}=\sqrt{2 c_{p}\left(T_{0}-T_{2_{\mathrm{s}}}\right)} \\ & =\sqrt{2 \times 1159 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times(1077.3-905.77) \mathrm{K}}=630.93 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s} \\ & q_{m}=\frac{A_{2} c_{\mathrm{12}}}{v_{2_{\mathrm{s}}}}=\frac{2400 \times 10^{-6} \mathrm{~m}^{2} \times 630.93 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}}{0.8898 \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{kg}}=1.702 \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{s} \end{aligned}$

由于过程不可逆，所以

$c_{\mathrm{12}}=\varphi c_{\mathrm{12}}=0.92 \times 630.93 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}=580.46 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$

因摩擦而损耗的动能被气流吸收，故需对温度修正。据能量方程 $h_{2}=h_{0}-\frac{c_{2}^{2}}{2}$，有

$\begin{aligned} & T_{2}=T_{0}-\frac{c_{\mathrm{12}}^{2}}{2 c_{p}}=1077.3 \mathrm{~K}-\frac{(580.46 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s})^{2}}{2 \times 1159 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})}=931.94 \mathrm{~K} \\ & v_{2}=\frac{R_{\mathrm{g}} T_{2}}{p_{2}}=\frac{318.3 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times 931.94 \mathrm{~K}}{0.324 \times 10^{6} \mathrm{~Pa}}=0.9155 \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{kg} \\ & q_{m}^{\prime}=\frac{A_{2} c_{\mathrm{12}}}{v_{2}}=\frac{2400 \times 10^{-6} \mathrm{~m}^{2} \times 580.46 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}}{0.9155 \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{kg}}=1.522 \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{s} \end{aligned}$

由于流动过程不可逆绝热，所以过程的熵增即是熵产

$s_{\mathrm{g}}=\Delta s_{1-2}=\Delta s_{2_{\mathrm{s}}-2}=c_{p} \ln \frac{T_{2}}{T_{2_{\mathrm{s}}}}=1.159 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times \ln \frac{931.94 \mathrm{~K}}{905.77 \mathrm{~K}}=0.033 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})$

焮损失

$\dot{I}=q_{m}^{\prime} T_{0} s_{\mathrm{g}}=1.522 \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{s} \times 300 \mathrm{~K} \times 0.033 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})=15.07 \mathrm{kW}$
