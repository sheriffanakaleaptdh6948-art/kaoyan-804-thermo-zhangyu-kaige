例 6-7 1 kg 水由 \(t_1 = 50\,^\circ\mathrm{C}\)、\(p_1 = 0.1\,\) MPa 经定熵增压过程到 \(p_2 = 15\,\) MPa。已知 \(50\,^\circ\mathrm{C}\) 时水的 \(v = 0.001012\, \mathrm{m}^3/\mathrm{kg}\)，\(\alpha_V = 465 \times 10^{-6}\, \mathrm{K}^{-1}\)，\(c_p =\) 4.186 kJ/(kg·K)，并可以将它们视为定值。试确定水的终温及焓的变化量。

解 由第二 ds 方程

$\mathrm{d}s = \frac{c_p}{T} \mathrm{dT} - \left( \frac{\partial v}{\partial T} \right)_p \mathrm{dp} = \frac{c_p}{T} \mathrm{dT} - v \alpha_V \mathrm{dp}$

根据状态参数特性，选择先沿 \(T_1 = (50 + 273.15)\, \mathrm{K} = 323.15\, \mathrm{K}\) 等温由 \(p_1\) 到 \(p_2\)，再在 \(p_2\) 下定压地由 \(T_1\) 到 \(T_2\) 进行积分，即

$\begin{aligned}\Delta s_{12} & = \left( -\int_{p_1}^{p_2} v \alpha_V \mathrm{dp} \right)_{T_1} + \left( \int_{T_1}^{T_2} \frac{c_p}{T} \mathrm{dT} \right)_{p_2} \\& = \left( c_p \ln \frac{T_2}{T_1} \right)_{p_2} - \left[ v \alpha_V (p_2 - p_1) \right]_{T_1}\end{aligned}$

因等熵增压，所以 \(\Delta s_{12} = 0\)，于是

$\left( c_p \ln \frac{T_2}{T_1} \right)_{p_2} = \left[ v \alpha_V (p_2 - p_1) \right]_{T_1}$

即

$\begin{aligned}\ln \frac{T_2}{T_1} & = \frac{v \alpha_V (p_2 - p_1)}{c_p} \\& = \frac{0.001012\, \mathrm{m}^3/\mathrm{kg} \times 465 \times 10^{-6}\, \mathrm{K}^{-1} \times (15 \times 10^6\, \mathrm{Pa} - 0.1 \times 10^6\, \mathrm{Pa})}{4.186 \times 10^3\, \mathrm{J}/(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})} \\& = 0.001675\end{aligned}$

解得 \(T_2 = 323.69\, \mathrm{K}\)，\(t_2 = 50.54\,^\circ\mathrm{C}\)。

由焓的一般关系式 (6-35)：

$\begin{aligned}\mathrm{dh} & = c_p \mathrm{dT} + \left[ v - T\left( \frac{\partial v}{\partial T} \right) \right]_p \mathrm{dp} \\& = c_p \mathrm{dT} + (1 - T \alpha_V) v \mathrm{dp}\end{aligned}$

仍沿上述两途径积分得

$\begin{aligned}\Delta h_{12} & = \left[ \int_{p_1}^{p_2} (1 - T \alpha_V) v \mathrm{dp} \right]_{T_1} + \left[ \int_{T_1}^{T_2} c_p \mathrm{dT} \right]_{p_2} \\& = \left[ (1 - T \alpha_V) v (p_2 - p_1) \right]_{T_1} + \left[ c_p (T_2 - T_1) \right]_{p_2}\end{aligned}$

所以

$\begin{aligned}\Delta h_{12} & = (1 - 323.15\, \mathrm{K} \times 465 \times 10^{-6}\, \mathrm{K}^{-1}) \times 0.001012\, \mathrm{m}^3/\mathrm{kg} \times \\& (15 - 0.1) \times 10^6\, \mathrm{Pa} + 4.186 \times 10^3\, \mathrm{J}/(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times \\& (323.69\, \mathrm{K} - 323.15\, \mathrm{K}) = 15.07 \times 10^3\, \mathrm{J}/\mathrm{kg}\end{aligned}$
