$$T_{2}=T_{1}-\left(T_{1}-T_{2}\right) \eta_{\mathrm{T}}=299.95 \mathrm{~K}-\left(299.95-189.38\right) \mathrm{K} \times 0.8=211.5 \mathrm{~K}$$
