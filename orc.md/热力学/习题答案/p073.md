A 方案: 稳定流动系空气的熵方程为 $s_{2}-s_{1}=s_{t}+s_{g}$

因为该控制系为绝热, 所以 $s_{t}=0$

$s_{\mathrm{g}}=s_{2}-s_{1}=s_{2}^{\circ}-s_{1}^{\circ}-R_{\mathrm{g}} \ln \frac{p_{2}}{p_{1}}=s_{2}^{\circ}-s_{1}^{\circ}$

根据 $T_{1}=17+273=290 \mathrm{~K} 、 T_{2}=57+273=330 \mathrm{~K}$ 查附表得

$s_{1}^{\circ}=6.6732 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) s_{2}^{\circ}=6.8029 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})$

$s_{\mathrm{g}}=s_{2}-s_{1}=s_{2}^{\circ}-s_{1}^{\circ}$

$=6.8029-6.6732=0.1297 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})$

B 方案: 空气和水蒸汽均为稳定流动, 根据稳定流动热力系的熵方程

$q_{\mathrm{ml}}\left(s_{2}-s_{1}\right)+q_{\mathrm{m} 3}\left(s_{4}-s_{3}\right)=\dot{s}_{\mathrm{t}}+\dot{s}_{\mathrm{g}}$

由于绝热, $s_{\mathrm{t}}=0$

$s_{\mathrm{g}}=\frac{\dot{s}_{\mathrm{g}}}{q_{\mathrm{ml}}}=s_{2}-s_{1}+\frac{q_{\mathrm{m} 3}}{q_{\mathrm{ml}}}\left(s_{4}-s_{3}\right), q_{\mathrm{m} 3}\left(h_{3}-h_{4}\right)=q_{\mathrm{ml}}\left(h_{2}-h_{1}\right)$

查附表得 $T_{1} 、 T_{2}$ 时, $h_{1}=292.25 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}, h_{2}=332.42 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$

查饱和水和饱和蒸汽表得 $h_{3}=2675.14 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}, s_{3}=7.3589 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})$, $h_{4}=417.52 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}, s_{4}=1.3028 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})$

所以 $\frac{q_{\mathrm{m} 3}}{q_{\mathrm{ml}}}=\frac{h_{2}-h_{1}}{h_{3}-h_{4}}=\frac{332.42-292.25}{2675.14-417.52}=0.0178$

代入(1)式得

$\begin{aligned} s_{\mathrm{g}} & =6.8029-6.6732+0.0178 \times(1.3028-7.3589) \\ & =0.022 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \end{aligned}$

$\because$ A 方案熵产大于 B 方案, 即做功能力损失大

$\therefore$ B 方案合理

由质量守恒得

$\begin{aligned} & \frac{p_{1} V_{1}}{R_{\mathrm{g}} T_{1}}=\frac{p_{2} V_{2}}{R_{\mathrm{g}} T_{2}} \\ & V_{2}=V_{1} \cdot \frac{p_{1}}{p_{2}} \cdot \frac{T_{2}}{T_{1}}=1 \times \frac{250}{100} \times \frac{235}{273+27}=1.96 \mathrm{~cm}^{3} \end{aligned}$

因为过程绝热, 有

$W=U_{1}-U_{2}=\frac{m R_{\mathrm{g}}}{k-1}\left(T_{1}-T_{2}\right)=\frac{1}{k-1}\left(p_{1} V_{1}-p_{2} V_{2}\right)$
