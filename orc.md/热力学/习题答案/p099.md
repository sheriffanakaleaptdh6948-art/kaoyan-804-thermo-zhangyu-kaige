$\begin{aligned}h^{\prime \prime}-h^{\prime} & =\left.T_{\mathrm{s}}\left(v^{\prime \prime}-v^{\prime}\right) \frac{\mathrm{d} p}{\mathrm{~d} T}\right|_{\mathrm{s}} \approx T_{\mathrm{s}}\left(v^{\prime \prime}-v^{\prime}\right) \frac{\Delta p}{\Delta T} \\& =(273+200) \times(0.1273-0.0012) \times \frac{1722.9-1397.6}{205-195} \\& =1940.3 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}\end{aligned}$

所求得 $200{ }^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ 时水的汽化潜热约为 $1940.3 \mathrm{~kJ}$.

6-22 解题过程 由题意得

$\begin{aligned}& \left.\frac{\mathrm{d} p}{\mathrm{~d} T}\right|_{\mathrm{s}}=\frac{h^{\prime \prime}-h^{\prime}}{T_{\mathrm{s}}\left(v^{\prime \prime}-v^{\prime}\right)} \\& \mathrm{d} p=\frac{h^{\prime \prime}-h^{\prime}}{T_{\mathrm{s}}\left(v^{\prime \prime}-v^{\prime}\right)} \mathrm{dT}\end{aligned}$

考虑到 $v^{\prime \prime} \gg v^{\prime}$, 且 R134a 蒸汽近似服从理想气体规律, 所以上式简化为

$\begin{aligned}& \mathrm{d} p=\frac{p\left(h^{\prime \prime}-h^{\prime}\right)}{T_{20 \mathrm{C}} R_{\mathrm{g}} T_{0 \mathrm{C}}} \mathrm{dT} \\& \frac{\mathrm{d} p}{p}=\frac{h^{\prime \prime}-h^{\prime}}{T_{20 \mathrm{C}} R_{\mathrm{g}} T_{0 \mathrm{C}}} \mathrm{dT}\end{aligned}$

积分并整理得

$\begin{aligned}\ln \frac{p_{2}}{p_{1}} & =\frac{\left(h^{\prime \prime}-h^{\prime}\right)\left(T_{20 \mathrm{C}}-T_{0 \mathrm{C}}\right)}{T_{20 \mathrm{C}} R_{\mathrm{g}} T_{0 \mathrm{C}}} \\& =\frac{182.4 \times 10^{3} \times 102.03 \times 10^{-3} \times(0-20)}{8.3145 \times 293 \times 273}=-0.56 \\p_{2} & =p_{1} \mathrm{e}^{-0.56}=571.6 \times \mathrm{e}^{-0.56}=326.5 \mathrm{kPa}\end{aligned}$

6-23 解题过程 由克拉贝隆方程

$p_{2}-p_{1}=-\frac{\Delta H_{\mathrm{m}}}{\Delta V} \ln \frac{T_{2}}{T_{1}}$

其中 $p_{2}=1 \mathrm{MPa}, p_{1}=1.013 \times 10^{5} \mathrm{~Pa}$

$\begin{aligned}\Delta H_{\mathrm{m}} & =335000 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{kg} \quad \Delta V=0.091 \times 10^{-3} \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{kg} \\T_{1} & =273.15 \mathrm{~K}\end{aligned}$

即 $1 \times 10^{6}-1.013 \times 10^{5}=-\frac{335000}{0.091 \times 10^{-3}} \ln \frac{T_{2}}{273.15}$

解得 $T_{2}=273.09 \mathrm{~K} \quad t_{s}=-0.06{ }^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$

6-24 解题过程 由于低压下液相比体积远小于气相由 $(\mathrm{d} p / \mathrm{d} t)_{\mathrm{s}}=\frac{\gamma}{T_{\mathrm{s}}} \frac{p_{\mathrm{s}}}{R_{\mathrm{g}} T_{\mathrm{s}}}$积分得 $\ln \frac{p_{2}}{p_{1}}=-\frac{\gamma}{R_{\mathrm{g}}}\left(\frac{1}{T_{2}}-\frac{1}{T_{1}}\right)$

$\begin{aligned}& R_{\mathrm{g}}=\frac{R}{M}=\frac{8.3145}{4}=2.0786 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{mol} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \\& p_{2}=1.01325 \times 10^{5} \mathrm{~Pa} \quad p_{1}=133.3 \mathrm{~Pa} \\& T_{2}=4.2 \mathrm{~K} \quad T_{1}=1.2 \mathrm{~K}\end{aligned}$

代入解得 $\gamma=23147 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{kg}$
