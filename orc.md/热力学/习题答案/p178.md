氧气由 A 渗透到 B，至最终 A 和 B 中氧气均匀分布，渗透后氧气的压力为

$p_{\mathrm{O}_{2}}=\frac{n_{\mathrm{O}_{2}} R T}{V_{\mathrm{A}}+V_{\mathrm{B}}}=\frac{32.87 \times 8.3145 \times 300}{0.15+1}=71.3 \mathrm{kPa}$

由 $p_{2}^{A}=p_{\mathrm{O}_{2}}^{A 2}=\frac{n_{\mathrm{O}_{2}}^{A 2} R T}{V_{\mathrm{A}}}=71.3 \mathrm{kPa}$ 可知

$n_{\mathrm{O}_{2}}^{A 2}=\frac{p_{2}^{A} V_{\mathrm{A}}}{R T}=\frac{71.3 \times 10^{3} \times 0.15}{8.3145 \times 300}=4.287 \mathrm{~mol}$

(2) 由 (1) 得 $n_{\mathrm{O}_{2}}^{A 2}=4.287 \mathrm{~mol}$ 可得 B 侧 $\mathrm{O}_{2}$ 的量为

$\begin{aligned} n_{\mathrm{O}_{2}}^{\mathrm{B}} & =n_{\mathrm{O}_{2}}-n_{\mathrm{O}_{2}}^{A 2}=32.87-4.287=28.583 \mathrm{~mol} \\ p_{2}^{B} & =\frac{n_{\mathrm{O}_{2}}^{B} R T}{V_{\mathrm{B}}}=\frac{\left(n_{\mathrm{O}_{2}}^{B 2}+n_{\mathrm{N}_{2}}^{B 1}\right) R T}{V_{\mathrm{B}}} \\ & =\frac{(28.583+31.27) \times 8.3145 \times 300}{1} \\ & =149293.4 \mathrm{~Pa} \end{aligned}$

$x_{\mathrm{N}_{2}}^{\mathrm{B}}=\frac{n_{\mathrm{N}_{2}}^{\mathrm{B}}}{n_{2}^{B}}=\frac{31.27}{28.583+31.27}=0.5224$

$x_{\mathrm{O}_{2}}^{\mathrm{B}}=\frac{n_{\mathrm{O}_{2}}^{\mathrm{B}}}{n_{2}^{B}}=\frac{28.583}{28.583+31.27}=0.4776$

$p_{\mathrm{O}_{2}}^{\mathrm{B}}=x_{\mathrm{O}_{2}}^{\mathrm{B}} p_{2}^{\mathrm{B}}=0.4776 \times 149293.4=71.3 \mathrm{kPa}$

$p_{\mathrm{N}_{2}}^{\mathrm{B}}=x_{\mathrm{N}_{2}}^{\mathrm{B}} p_{2}^{\mathrm{B}}=0.5224 \times 149293.4=78 \mathrm{kPa}$

(3) 又因为 $T=T_{\mathrm{A}}=T_{\mathrm{B}}=300 \mathrm{~K}$，氧气熵变温度项为零，且氮气温度和分压力均不变，故

$\begin{aligned} \Delta S_{1-2} & =-R\left[n_{\mathrm{O}_{2}}^{A 2} \ln \frac{p_{2}^{A}}{p_{1}^{A}}+\left(n_{\mathrm{O}_{2}}^{A 1}-n_{\mathrm{O}_{2}}^{A 1}\right) \ln \frac{p_{\mathrm{O}_{2}}^{B 2}}{p_{1}^{A}}+n_{\mathrm{O}_{2}}^{B 1} \ln \frac{p_{\mathrm{O}_{2}}^{B 2}}{p_{\mathrm{O}_{2}}^{A}}\right] \\ \Delta S_{1-2} & =-8.3145 \times\left(4.287 \times \ln \frac{71.3}{400}+19.763 \times \ln \frac{71.3}{400}+8.82 \times \ln \frac{71.3}{22}\right) \\ & =258.6 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{K} \end{aligned}$
