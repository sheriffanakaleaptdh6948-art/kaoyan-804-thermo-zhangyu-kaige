(2) 已知 $Q_{1}=1000 \mathrm{~kJ}$

$Q_{2}=\frac{T_{2}}{T_{1}} Q_{1}=\frac{350}{950} \times 1000=368 \mathrm{~kJ}$

高温热源放出热量 $1000 \mathrm{~kJ}$, 与工质二者组成孤立系, 其熵增

$\Delta S_{\text {iso }, 1}=\Delta S_{H}+\Delta S_{\text {sb }}=\frac{-Q_{1}}{T_{H}}+\frac{Q_{1}}{T_{1}}=\frac{-1000}{1000}+\frac{1000}{950}=0.0526 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{K}$

由于不等温传热引起的熵损失

$I_{1}=T_{0} \Delta S_{\text {iso }, 1}=300 \times 0.0526=15.78 \mathrm{~kJ}$

350 K 的工质放热 $368 \mathrm{~kJ}$, 被 $300 \mathrm{~K}$ 的冷源吸收, 二者组成孤立系, 其熵增

$\Delta S_{\text {iso }, 2}=\Delta S_{\text {od }}+\Delta S_{L}=\frac{-Q_{2}}{T_{L}}+\frac{Q_{2}}{T_{2}}=\frac{-368}{350}+\frac{368}{300}=0.1752 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{K}$

不等温传热引起的熵损失

$I_{2}=T_{0} \Delta S_{\text {iso }, 2}=300 \times 0.1752=52.56 \mathrm{~kJ}$

总的熵损失

$I=I_{1}+I_{2}=15.78+52.56=68.34 \mathrm{~kJ}$

由题意可知, 闭口系, $W=0, Q=0$, 故 $\Delta U=0$

设混合后水温为 $t$, 有

$\begin{aligned} & m_{1} c_{\mathrm{w}}\left(t-t_{1}\right)=m_{2} c_{\mathrm{w}}\left(t_{2}-t\right) \\ & t=\frac{m_{2} t_{2}+t_{1} m_{1}}{m_{2}+m_{1}}=\frac{100 \times 20+200 \times 80}{100+200}=60^{\circ} \mathrm{C} \end{aligned}$

即 $T_{1}=20+273=293 \mathrm{~K}, T_{2}=80+273=353 \mathrm{~K}, T=60+273=333 \mathrm{~K}$

$\begin{aligned} \Delta S_{1-2} & =\Delta S_{1}+\Delta S_{2}=m_{1} c_{\mathrm{w}} \ln \frac{T}{T_{1}}+m_{2} c_{\mathrm{w}} \ln \frac{T}{T_{2}} \\ & =4.187 \times\left(100 \times \ln \frac{333}{293}+200 \times \ln \frac{333}{353}\right) \\ & =4.7392 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{K} \end{aligned}$
