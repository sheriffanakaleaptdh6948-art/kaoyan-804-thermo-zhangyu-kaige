5-10 解题过程 由题意可知，燃气的气体常数

$R_{g}=c_{p}-c_{v}=1.01-0.732=0.278 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})$

(1) $\Delta s=c_{p} \ln \frac{T_{2}}{T_{1}}-R_{g} \ln \frac{p_{2}}{p_{1}}$

$\begin{aligned} & =1.01 \times \ln \frac{130+273}{420+273}-0.278 \times \ln \frac{0.1}{0.8} \\ & =0.03057 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})>0 \end{aligned}$

所以该过程为不可逆绝热过程。

(2) 根据稳定流动系统能量方程得

$\begin{aligned} w_{1} & =w_{1}=h_{1}-h_{2}=c_{p}\left(T_{1}-T_{2}\right) \\ & =1.01 \times(693-403)=292.9 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \end{aligned}$

5-11 解题过程 由题意可得 $T_{1}=120+273=393 \mathrm{~K}, T_{2}=25+273=298 \mathrm{~K}$

根据闭口系统能量方程得

$\begin{aligned} Q & =\Delta U+W=m c_{\mathrm{v}}\left(T_{2}-T_{1}\right)+W \\ & =0.25 \times 0.747 \times(298-393)+8 \\ & =-9.74 \mathrm{~kJ}<0 \end{aligned}$

环境吸收的热量 $Q_{\text {sur }}=-Q=9.74 \mathrm{~kJ}$

环境的熵变 $\Delta S_{\text {sur }}=\frac{Q_{\text {sur }}}{T_{\text {sur }}}=\frac{9.74}{25+273}=0.03268 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{K}$

系统的熵变 $\Delta S_{1-2}=m\left(c_{p} \ln \frac{T_{2}}{T_{1}}-R_{g} \ln \frac{p_{2}}{p_{1}}\right)=m\left[\left(c_{v}+R_{g}\right) \ln \frac{T_{2}}{T_{1}}-R_{g} \ln \frac{p_{2}}{p_{1}}\right]$

$\begin{aligned} & =0.25 \times\left[(0.747+0.297) \times \ln \frac{298}{393}-0.297 \times \ln \frac{0.125}{0.25}\right] \\ & =-0.02076 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{K} \end{aligned}$

该孤立系的熵变

$\Delta S_{\text {iso }}^{\prime}=\Delta S_{1-2}+\Delta S_{\text {sur }}=-0.02076+0.03268=0.011923 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{K}>0$

所以该过程为不可逆膨胀过程
