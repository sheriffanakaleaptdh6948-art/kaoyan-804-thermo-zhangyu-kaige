4-27 解 题过程

查未饱和水和过热蒸汽表得:

当 $p=3.5 \mathrm{MPa}, t_{1}=60^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ 时, $h_{1}=254.08 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}, s_{1}=0.8294 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})$

当 $p=3.5 \mathrm{MPa}, t_{2}=350^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ 时, $h_{2}=3102.95 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}, s_{2}=6.661 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})$

水的加热过程可看作定压过程, 则技术功 $\omega_{1}=-\int_{1}^{2} v d p=0$

所以有

$\begin{aligned}& q_{p}=h_{2}-h_{1}=3102.95-254.08=2848.9 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \\& \Delta s_{1-2}=s_{2}-s_{1}=6.661-0.8294=5.8316 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \\& T=\frac{q_{p}}{\Delta s_{1-2}}=\frac{2848.9}{5.8316}=488.53 \mathrm{~K}\end{aligned}$
