4-3 解题过程 查附表得氧气的摩尔质量

$M = 32 \times 10^{-3} \text{ kg/mol}$

$R_{\mathrm{g}} = \frac{R}{M} = \frac{8.3145}{32 \times 10^{-3}} = 0.26 \text{ kJ/(kg·K)}$

(1) 定温压缩

$\begin{aligned}w_{\mathrm{t}} &= -\int_{p_{1}}^{p_{2}} v \mathrm{~d} p = -\int_{p_{1}}^{p_{2}} \frac{R_{\mathrm{g}} T_{1}}{p} \mathrm{~d} p = R_{\mathrm{g}} T_{1} \ln \frac{p_{1}}{p_{2}} \\&= 0.26 \times (273 + 40) \times \ln \frac{0.1}{4} = -112.82 \text{ kJ/kg}\end{aligned}$

(2) 按绝热压缩计算且为定值比热容

由 $q = \Delta h + w_{\mathrm{t}}$ 可知, $w_{\mathrm{t}} = q - \Delta h = q - (h_{2} - h_{1})$

因为过程绝热, 所以 $q = 0$

$\begin{aligned}w_{\mathrm{t}} &= h_{1} - h_{2} = c_{\mathrm{p}}(T_{1} - T_{2}) = \frac{7}{2} R_{\mathrm{g}}\left[T_{1} - \left(\frac{p_{2}}{p_{1}}\right)^{\frac{k-1}{k}} T_{1}\right] \\&= \frac{7}{2} \times 0.26 \times (40 + 273) \times \left[1 - \left(\frac{4}{0.1}\right)^{\frac{1.4-1}{1.4}}\right] = -138.34 \text{ kJ/kg}\end{aligned}$
