(1) 当 $p_{1}=3 \mathrm{MPa}, t_{1}=450^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ 时, 查得 $h_{1}=3344 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$, 当 $p_{01}=0.3 \mathrm{MPa}, p_{02}=$ $0.12 \mathrm{MPa}, p_{2}=0.005 \mathrm{MPa}$ 且 $t_{1}=450^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ 时, 查 $h-s$ 图得 $h_{01}=2765 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}, h_{02}=2603$ $\mathrm{kJ} / \mathrm{kg}, h_{2}=2159 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$, 由饱和水和饱和蒸汽表查得, $h_{01}^{\prime}=561.58 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}, h_{02}=$ $439.37 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}, h_{2}^{\prime}=137.72 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}, s_{01}^{\prime}=1.6721 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}), s_{2}^{\prime}=0.4761 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})$由过热蒸汽表查得 $s_{1}=7.0817 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})$

由热平衡方程 $\alpha_{1}\left(h_{01}-h_{01}^{\prime}\right)=\left(1-\alpha_{1}\right)\left(h_{01}^{\prime}-h_{02}^{\prime}\right)$ 得

$\alpha_{1}=\frac{h_{01}^{\prime}-h_{02}^{\prime}}{h_{01}-h_{02}^{\prime}}=\frac{561.58-439.37}{2765-439.37}=0.0525$

而 $\quad \alpha_{2}\left(h_{02}-h_{02}^{\prime}\right)=\left(1-\alpha_{1}-\alpha_{2}\right)\left(h_{02}^{\prime}-h_{2}^{\prime}\right)$

因此得 $\quad \alpha_{2}=\frac{\left(1-\alpha_{1}\right)\left(h_{02}^{\prime}-h_{2}^{\prime}\right)}{h_{02}-h_{2}^{\prime}}=\frac{(1-0.0525) \times(439.37-137.72)}{2603-137.72}$

$=0.1159$

(2) $q_{1}=h_{1}-h_{01}^{\prime}=3344-561.58=2782.4 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$

$q_{2}=\left(1-\alpha_{1}-\alpha_{2}\right)\left(h_{2}-h_{2}^{\prime}\right)$

$=\left(1-0.0525-0.1159\right) \times(2159-137.72)=1680.9 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$

$\eta=1-\frac{q_{2}}{q_{1}}=1-\frac{1680.9}{2782.4}=39.6 \%$

(3) $d=\frac{1}{w_{\text {net }}}=\frac{1}{\left(h_{1}-h_{01}\right)+\left(1-\alpha_{1}\right)\left(h_{01}-h_{02}\right)+\left(1-\alpha_{1}-\alpha_{2}\right)\left(h_{02}-h_{2}\right)}$

$=\frac{1}{(3344-2765)+(1-0.0525) \times(2765-2603)+(1-0.0525-0.1159) \times(2603-2159)}$

$=\frac{1}{1101.7}=9.08 \times 10^{-7} \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{J}$

(4) $\Delta s=s_{1}-s_{01}^{\prime}=7.0817-1.6721=5.4096 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})$

$\bar{T}_{1}=\frac{q_{1}}{\Delta s}=\frac{2782.4}{5.4096}=514.3 \mathrm{~K}$

(5) 若采用朗肯循环, 则循环热效率

$\dot{\eta}_{1}=\frac{h_{1}-h_{2}}{h_{1}-h_{2}^{\prime}}=\frac{3344-2159}{3344-137.72}=37 \%$

耗汽率 $d^{\prime}=\frac{1}{h_{1}-h_{2}}=\frac{1}{(3344-2159) \times 10^{3}}=8.44 \times 10^{-7} \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{J}$
