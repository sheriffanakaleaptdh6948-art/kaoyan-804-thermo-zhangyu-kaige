9-23 解题过程 据题意 $T_{1}=290 \mathrm{~K} 、 T_{3}=1500 \mathrm{~K} 、 p_{1}=95 \mathrm{kPa} 、 p_{3}=950 \mathrm{kPa}$

$\begin{aligned}& T_{2}=T_{1}\left(\frac{p_{2}}{p_{1}}\right)^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}}=T_{1}\left(\frac{p_{3}}{p_{1}}\right)^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}}=290 \times\left(\frac{950}{95}\right)^{\frac{1.4-1}{1.4}}=559.88 \mathrm{~K} \\& T_{2^{\prime}}=T_{1}+\frac{T_{2}-T_{1}}{\eta_{c, s}}=290+\frac{559.88-290}{0.85}=607.51 \mathrm{~K} \\& T_{4}=T_{3}\left(\frac{p_{4}}{p_{3}}\right)^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}}=T_{3}\left(\frac{p_{1}}{p_{3}}\right)^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}}=1500 \times\left(\frac{95}{950}\right)^{\frac{1.4-1}{1.4}}=776.95 \mathrm{~K} \\& T_{4^{\prime}}=T_{3}-\eta_{\mathrm{T}}\left(T_{3}-T_{4}\right)=1500-0.87 \times(1500-776.95)=870.95 \mathrm{~K}\end{aligned}$

由 $\sigma=\frac{h_{7}-h_{2^{\prime}}}{h_{4}^{\prime}-h_{2^{\prime}}}=\frac{T_{7}-T_{2^{\prime}}}{T_{4^{\prime}}-T_{2^{\prime}}}$, 可得

$T_{7}=T_{2^{\prime}}+\sigma\left(T_{4^{\prime}}-T_{2^{\prime}}\right)=607.51+0.75 \times(870.94-607.51)=805.09 \mathrm{~K}$

同理得

$T_{8}=T_{4^{\prime}}-\sigma\left(T_{4^{\prime}}-T_{2^{\prime}}\right)=870.95-0.75 \times(870.94-607.51)=673.37 \mathrm{~K}$

(1) $q_{1}=h_{3}-h_{7}=c_{\mathrm{p}}\left(T_{3}-T_{7}\right)=1.005 \times(1500-805.09)=698.4 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$

$q_{2}=h_{8}-h_{1}=c_{p}\left(T_{8}-T_{1}\right)=1.005 \times\left(673.37-290\right)=385.3 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$

$w_{\text {net }}=q_{1}-q_{2}=698.4-385.3=313.1 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$

由 $P=q_{\mathrm{m}} w_{\text {net }}$ 可知, $q_{\mathrm{m}}=\frac{P}{w_{\text {net }}}=\frac{20 \times 10^{6}}{313.1 \times 10^{3}}=63.88 \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{s}$

气轮机输出总功率为

$P_{\mathrm{T}}=q_{\mathrm{m}} c_{\mathrm{p}}\left(T_{3}-T_{4^{\prime}}\right)=63.88 \times 1.005 \times(1500-870.95)=40.4 \times 10^{3} \mathrm{kW}$

压气机消耗的功率为

$P_{\mathrm{c}}=q_{\mathrm{m}} c_{\mathrm{p}}\left(T_{2^{\prime}}-T_{1}\right)=63.88 \times 1.005 \times(607.51-290)=20.4 \times 10^{3} \mathrm{kW}$

(2) $\eta=\frac{w_{\text {net }}}{q_{1}}=\frac{313.1}{698.4}=44.8 \%$
