10-15 解题过程 由 \(p_1 = 3 \, \text{MPa}, t_1 = 400^\circ \text{C}, p_2 = 0.12 \, \text{MPa}\)，查 h-s 图得 \(h_1 = 3232 \, \text{kJ/kg}, h_2 = 2540 \, \text{kJ/kg}\)，查水蒸气表得 \(h_2' = 439.37 \, \text{kJ/kg}\)

每 kg 蒸汽凝结放热量为

\(\begin{aligned} q_2 & = h_2 - h_2' = 2540 - 439.37 \\ & = 2100.63 \, \text{kJ/kg} \end{aligned}\)

\(q_m = \frac{q_{\text{ev}}}{q_2} = \frac{1.06 \times 10^7}{2100.63} = 5046.1 \, \text{kg/h}\)

汽轮机总功

\(\begin{aligned} W_{\text{net}} & = q_m w_{\text{net}} = q_m (h_1 - h_2) \\ & = 5046.1 \times (3232 - 2540) \\ & = 3.492 \times 10^6 \, \text{kJ/h} \end{aligned}\)

所以汽轮机输出功率为

\(P = \frac{W_{\text{net}}}{3600} = \frac{3.492 \times 10^6}{3600} = 970 \, \text{kW}\)
