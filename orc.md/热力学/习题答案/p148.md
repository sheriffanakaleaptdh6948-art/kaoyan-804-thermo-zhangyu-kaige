(1) 当 \( p_1 = 3 \) MPa, \( t_1 = 300 \) °C, \( p_2 = 6 \) kPa 时, 查 h-s 图得:

$$ h_1 = 2996 \text{ kJ/kg} \quad h_2 = 2005 \text{ kJ/kg} \quad x_2 = 0.761, \quad t_2' = 36 \text{ °C} $$

$$ h_2' = c_w t_2' = 4.187 \times 36 = 150.7 \text{ kJ/kg} $$

若不计水泵耗功, 则热效率

$$ \eta = \frac{h_1 - h_2}{h_1 - h_2'} = \frac{2996 - 2005}{2996 - 150.7} = 34.83 \% $$

$$ d = \frac{1}{h_1 - h_2} = \frac{1}{(2996 - 2005) \times 10^3} = 1.009 \times 10^{-6} \text{ kg/J} $$

(2) 当 \( p_1 = 3 \) MPa, \( t_2 = 500 \) °C, \( p_2 = 6 \) kPa 时, 查 h-s 图得: \( h_1 = 3453 \text{ kJ/kg} \)、 \( h_2 = 2226 \text{ kJ/kg} \)、\( x_2 = 0.859 \)、\( t_2' = 36 \) °C

$$ h_2' = c_w t_2' = 4.187 \times 36 = 150.7 \text{ kJ/kg} $$

$$ d = \frac{1}{h_1 - h_2} = \frac{1}{(3453 - 2226) \times 10^3} = 8.15 \times 10^{-7} \text{ kg/J} $$

若不计水泵耗功, 则热效率

$$ \eta = \frac{h_1 - h_2}{h_1 - h_2'} = \frac{3453 - 2226}{3453 - 150.7} = 37.16 \% $$

说明在 \( p_1 p_2 \) 相同时, 提高初温则 \( \eta \) 上升, \( d \) 下降, \( x \) 上升
