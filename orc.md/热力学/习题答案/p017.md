2-15 解 题过程 根据稳定流动能量方程有

$q+\left(h_{1}+\frac{c_{11}^{2}}{2}+g z_{1}\right)-\left(h_{2}+\frac{c_{12}^{2}}{2}+g z_{2}\right)-w_{1}=0$

据题意: $\Delta u=0, \Delta e_{k}=0, h_{1}=u_{1}+p_{1} v_{1}, h_{2}=u_{2}+p_{2} v_{2}, q=0$, 代入上式得

$\begin{aligned} w_{1} & =-\left[\left(p_{2} v_{2}-p_{1} v_{1}\right)+g \Delta z\right] \\ & =-\left(200 \times 0.001-600 \times 0.001\right)-9.81 \times(150+5) \times 10^{-3} \\ & =-1.12 \mathrm{kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \end{aligned}$

水泵消耗的功率 $P=q_{m} w_{1}=10 \times 1.12=11.2 \mathrm{kW}$

2-16 解 题过程 由题意可知

$\begin{aligned} Q & =q_{m}\left[\Delta h+g\left(z_{2}-z_{1}\right)\right]+P_{\text {水泵 }} \\ & =q_{m}\left[c_{p}\left(t_{2}-t_{1}\right)+g\left(z_{2}-z_{1}\right)\right]+P_{\text {水泵 }} \\ & =\frac{25 \times 1000}{3600} \times[4.187 \times(4-3.5)+9.81 \times(30+6) \times 10^{-3}]-12 \\ & =4.99 \mathrm{kW}=1.8 \times 10^{4} \mathrm{kJ} / \mathrm{h}>0 \end{aligned}$

所以有必要加入加热器, 且加热量最小为 $1.8 \times 10^{4} \mathrm{kJ} / \mathrm{h}$.

2-17 解 题过程 由题意可得质量流量

$q_{m}=\frac{q_{v}}{v}=\frac{c_{1} A}{4}=\frac{1000 \times \pi \times(0.002)^{2}}{4 \times 0.001002}=3.135 \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{s}$

由能量守恒得

$q+\left(h_{1}+\frac{c_{11}^{2}}{2}+g z_{1}\right)-\left(h_{2}+\frac{c_{12}^{2}}{2}+g z_{2}\right)-w_{1}=0$

据题意 $q=0, t_{1}=t_{2}, v_{2}=v_{1}, u_{1}=u_{2}, z_{2}=z_{1}$, 所以有

$\begin{aligned} w_{1} & =-\left[\frac{c_{12}^{2}}{2}+\left(p_{2} v_{2}-p_{1} v_{1}\right)\right]=-\left[\frac{c_{12}^{2}}{2}+\left(p_{2}-p_{1}\right) v_{1}\right] \\ & =-\left[\frac{1000^{2}}{2 \times 1000}+(100-200) \times 0.001002\right]=-500 \mathrm{kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \\ & =-1567.2 \mathrm{kW} \end{aligned}$

所以水泵的功率为 $1567.2 \mathrm{kW}$.
