由题意可知 $V_{2}=V_{1}$

$p_{2} V_{2}=m R_{\mathrm{g}} T_{2}, p_{1} V_{1}=m R_{\mathrm{g}} T_{1}$

所以 $p_{2}=\frac{T_{2}}{T_{1}} p_{1}=\frac{600}{477} \times 0.32=0.4025 \mathrm{MPa}$

由附表得

$M_{\mathrm{CO}}=28.01 \times 10^{-3} \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{mol}$

所以 $R_{\mathrm{g}}=\frac{R}{M}=\frac{8.3145}{28.01 \times 10^{-3}}=296.8 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})$

(1) 比热容为定值, $\mathrm{CO}$ 为双原子分子, 所以

$c_{\mathrm{v}}=\frac{5}{2} R_{\mathrm{g}}=\frac{5}{2} \times 296.8=742.1 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})$

$c_{\mathrm{p}}=\frac{7}{2} R_{\mathrm{g}}=\frac{7}{2} \times 296.8=1038.94 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})$

$\Delta U=m c_{\mathrm{v}}\left(T_{2}-T_{1}\right)=2.3 \times 742.1 \times(600-477)=209.94 \mathrm{~kJ}$

$\Delta H=m c_{\mathrm{p}}\left(T_{2}-T_{1}\right)=2.3 \times 1038.94 \times(600-477)=293.92 \mathrm{~kJ}$

$\Delta S=m c_{\mathrm{v}} \ln \frac{T_{2}}{T_{1}}=2.3 \times 742.1 \times \ln \frac{600}{477}=0.3916 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{K}$

因过程定容, 所以 $W=0$

$Q=\Delta U+W=\Delta U=209.94 \mathrm{~kJ}$

(2) 比热容为变值时

由附表查得

$T_{1}=477 \mathrm{~K} \text { 时, } H_{\mathrm{m}, 1}=13921.704 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{mol}, S_{\mathrm{m}, 1}^{0}=211.312 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{mol} \cdot \mathrm{K})$

$T_{2}=600 \mathrm{~K} \text { 时, } H_{\mathrm{m}, 2}=17612.7 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{mol}, S_{\mathrm{m}, 2}^{0}=218.217 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{mol} \cdot \mathrm{K})$

$U_{\mathrm{m}, 1}=H_{\mathrm{m}, 1}-R T_{1}=13921.704-8.3145 \times 477=9955.69 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{mol}$

$U_{\mathrm{m}, 2}=H_{\mathrm{m}, 2}-R T_{2}=17612.7-8.3145 \times 600=12624 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{mol}$

所以 $\Delta U=\frac{m}{M} \Delta U_{\mathrm{m}}=\frac{2.3}{28.01 \times 10^{-3}} \times(12624-9955.69)=219.1 \mathrm{~kJ}$
