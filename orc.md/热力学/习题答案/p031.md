3-11 解題过程

(1) 由焓的定义式 $H=U+p V$ 可得出

$$\begin{aligned} \Delta U & =\Delta H-\Delta(p V)=\Delta H-\left(p_{2} V_{2}-p_{1} V_{1}\right) \\ & =-67.95 \mathrm{~kJ}-\left(170 \mathrm{kPa} \times 0.2744 \mathrm{~m}^{3}-520 \mathrm{kPa} \times 0.1419 \mathrm{~m}^{3}\right)=-40.81 \mathrm{~kJ} \end{aligned}$$

(2) 定值热容时 $\Delta U=m c_{\mathrm{V}} \Delta T, \Delta H=m c_{\mathrm{p}} \Delta T$, 所以

$$\begin{aligned} & c_{\mathrm{V}}=\frac{c_{\mathrm{p}}}{\frac{\Delta H}{\Delta U}}=\frac{5.20 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})}{\frac{-67.95 \mathrm{~kJ}}{-40.81 \mathrm{~kJ}}}=3.123 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \\ & R_{\mathrm{g}}=c_{\mathrm{p}}-c_{\mathrm{V}}=5.20 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})-3.123 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})=2.077 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \end{aligned}$$

(1) 终态温度和热力学能的变化量

$$\begin{aligned} \Delta U & =Q_{V}-W=367.6 \mathrm{~kJ}-(-468.3 \mathrm{~kJ})=835.9 \mathrm{~kJ} \\ t_{2} & =t_{1}+\frac{\Delta U}{m c_{\mathrm{V}}}=280^{\circ} \mathrm{C}+\frac{835.9 \mathrm{~kJ}}{2 \mathrm{~kg} \times 0.934 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})}=727.48^{\circ} \mathrm{C} \end{aligned}$$
