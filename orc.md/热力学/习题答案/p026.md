已知压气机吸入空气体积流量 \( q_{\text{vin}} = 0.2 \, \text{m}^3/\text{min} \)，故质量流量

$q_{\text{m,in}} = \frac{p_{\text{in}} q_{\text{v,in}}}{R_{\text{g}} T_{\text{in}}} = \frac{p_{\text{b}} q_{\text{v,in}}}{R_{\text{g}} T_{\text{in}}} = \frac{750 \times 133.3 \times 0.2}{R_{\text{g}} (17 + 273)} = \frac{68.95}{R_{\text{g}}}$

若充气时间为 \( \tau \) 分钟，由质量守恒 \( q_{\text{m,in}} \tau = m_2 - m_1 \)，得

$\tau = \frac{m_2 - m_1}{q_{\text{m,in}}} = \frac{2167.18 / R_{\text{g}} - 517.16 / R_{\text{g}}}{68.95 / R_{\text{g}}} = 23.93 \, \text{min}$

3-6 解题过程

(1) 标准状态下 \( p_0 = 0.101325 \, \text{MPa} \)，\( T_0 = 273 \, \text{K} \)，\( V_{\text{m}} = 22.4 \times 10^{-3} \, \text{m}^3/\text{mol} \)。送入锅炉的空气的量

$q_{\text{n}} = \frac{q_{\text{v}_0}{}}{q_{\text{V}}_{\text{m,0}}} = \frac{5000 \, \text{m}^3/\text{h}}{22.4 \times 10^{-3} \, \text{m}^3/\text{mol}}$

$= 223.21 \, \text{kmol/h} = 0.062 \, \text{kmol/s}$

实际送风的体积流率

$q_{\text{v,in}} = \frac{q_{\text{n}} RT}{p}$

$= \frac{223.21 \, \text{kmol/h} \times 8.3145 \, \text{J/(mol·K)} \times (250 + 273) \, \text{K}}{(150 + 765) \times 133.3 \, \text{Pa}}$

$= 7962.7 \, \text{m}^3/\text{h}$

或 \( \frac{p_0 q_{\text{v}_0}{}}{RT_0} = \frac{p q_{\text{v}}}{RT} \)

$q_{\text{v,in}} = \frac{p_0 q_{\text{v}_0}{}}{p T_0} = \frac{101325 \, \text{Pa} \times 5000 \, \text{m}^3/\text{h} \times 523 \, \text{K}}{(150 + 765) \times 133.3 \, \text{Pa} \times 273 \, \text{K}} = 7962.7 \, \text{m}^3/\text{h}$

(2) 烟囱出口处烟气的体积流量

$q_{\text{v,out}} = \frac{q_{\text{n}} RT_{2}}{p_{2}} = \frac{0.062 \, \text{mol/s} \times 8.3145 \, \text{J/(mol·K)} \times 480 \, \text{K}}{0.1 \times 10^{6} \, \text{Pa}}$

$= 2.4745 \, \text{m}^3/\text{s}$

设烟囱出口截面直径为 \( D \)

$q_{\text{v,out}} = c_{\text{f}} \frac{\pi D^{2}}{4}$
