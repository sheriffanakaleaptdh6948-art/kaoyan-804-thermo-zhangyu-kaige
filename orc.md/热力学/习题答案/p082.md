(2) 充气过程的熵产

熵方程简化为

$$\mathrm{d} S_{\mathrm{cv}}=\frac{\partial Q}{T_{\mathrm{r}}}+s_{0} \mathrm{~dm}+\delta S_{\mathrm{g}}$$

积分得 $S_{2}-S_{1}=\frac{Q}{T_{0}}+s_{0}\left(m_{2}-m_{1}\right)+S_{\mathrm{g}}$

式中 $S_{2}=m_{2} s_{2}=m_{2} s_{0}, S_{1}=0, m_{1}=0$

故 $S_{\mathrm{g}}=-\frac{Q}{T_{0}}$

由能量方程的简化式, $\mathrm{d} Q=\mathrm{d} U_{\mathrm{cv}}-h_{0} \mathrm{~dm}$, 积分得, $Q=U_{2}-U_{1}-h_{0}\left(m_{2}-m_{1}\right)$.

因 $m_{1}=0, U_{1}=0, u_{2}=u_{0}, h_{0}-u_{0}=p_{0} u_{0}$, 代入后有

$$\begin{aligned} & Q=m_{2} u_{0}-m_{2} h_{0}=\left(u_{0}-h_{0}\right) m_{2}=-p_{0} u_{0} m_{2}=-p_{0} V \\ & S_{\mathrm{g}}=\frac{p_{0} V}{T_{0}}=\frac{10^{5} \mathrm{~Pa} \times 10^{-3} \times 0.1 \mathrm{~m}^{3}}{303 \mathrm{~K}}=0.0330 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{K} \end{aligned}$$
