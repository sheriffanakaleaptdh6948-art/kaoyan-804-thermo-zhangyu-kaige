2-4 解题过程 取气体为系统，由 $Q = \Delta U + W$ 可知

$W = Q - \Delta U = 50 - 84 = -34 \, \mathrm{J} < 0 \, \mathrm{J}$

所以该过程为压缩过程，外界对气体做功 34 J。

2-5 解题过程 要使车间温度保持不变，必须使车间内每小时产生的热量等于散失的热量，即

$\begin{aligned}&Q_{\text{散}} = Q_{\text{产}} = Q_{\text{机床}} + Q_{\text{灯}} + Q_{\text{另}} \\&Q_{\text{机床}} = 375 \times 3600 = 1.35 \times 10^{6} \, \mathrm{kJ} \\&Q_{\text{灯}} = 50 \times 100 \times 3600 \times 10^{-3} = 1.8 \times 10^{4} \, \mathrm{kJ}\end{aligned}$

由(1)(2)(3)式可得

$\begin{aligned}Q_{\text{另}} &= Q_{\text{散}} - Q_{\text{机床}} - Q_{\text{灯}} = 3 \times 10^{6} - 1.35 \times 10^{6} - 0.018 \times 10^{6} \\&= 1.632 \times 10^{6} \, \mathrm{kJ}\end{aligned}$

2-6 解题过程 取气体为系统，由 $Q = \Delta U + W$ 可知

$\Delta U = Q - W = m\Delta u = 0.72 m\Delta T$

由 $pV = mR_{\mathrm{g}}T$ 可知

$m = \frac{pV}{R_{\mathrm{g}}T} = \frac{0.1 \times 10^{6} \times 15 \times 3}{287 \times (273.15 + 28)} = 52.06 \, \mathrm{kg}$

代入上式得

$\begin{aligned}\Delta T &= \frac{\Delta U}{0.72 m} = \frac{0.1 \times 3600 + 0.06 \times 3600 + 418.7 \times 3 - 1800}{0.72 \times 52.06} \\&= 0.86 \, ^{\circ} \mathrm{C} = 0.86 \, \mathrm{K}\end{aligned}$
