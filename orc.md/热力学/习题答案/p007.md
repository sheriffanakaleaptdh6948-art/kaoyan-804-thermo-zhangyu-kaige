$\begin{aligned}W & =\int_{V_{1}}^{V_{2}} p \mathrm{~d} V=\int_{V_{1}}^{V_{2}}(0.24-0.4 \mathrm{~V}) \mathrm{d} V=0.24\left(V_{2}-V_{1}\right)-\frac{0.4}{2} \times\left(V_{2}^{2}-V_{1}^{2}\right) \\& =0.24 \times(0.25-0.1) \times 10^{6}-0.2 \times\left(0.25^{2}-0.1^{2}\right) \times 10^{6} \\& =0.0255 \times 10^{6} \mathrm{~J}\end{aligned}$

$W_{1}=p_{\mathrm{b}}\left(V_{2}-V_{1}\right)=0.1 \times(0.25-0.1) \times 10^{6}=0.015 \times 10^{6} \mathrm{~J}$

摩擦力消耗的功 $W_{2}$

$W_{2}=F L=F \frac{V_{2}-V_{1}}{A}=1200 \times \frac{0.25-0.1}{0.1}=1800 \mathrm{~J}$

(3) 若活塞与气缸无摩擦，则有用功 $W_{\mathrm{u}, \mathrm{re}}$

$W_{\mathrm{u}, \mathrm{re}}=W-W_{1}=0.0255 \times 10^{6}-0.015 \times 10^{6}=10500 \mathrm{~J}$
