2-13 解题过程

(1) 由题意可知

$\begin{aligned} & p_{1}=p_{e, 1}+p_{b}=9+0.101325=9.1 \mathrm{MPa} \\ & p_{2}=p_{b}-p_{v, 2}=0.101325-0.0974=3.925 \times 10^{-3} \mathrm{MPa}=3925 \mathrm{Pa} \end{aligned}$

(2) 不计进出口动能和位能差时汽轮机的功率

由 $q=\Delta h t w_{t}$ 得

$\begin{aligned} P & =\Phi-q_{\mathrm{m}} \cdot \Delta h=\frac{-6.81 \times 10^{5}}{3600}-40 \times \frac{1000}{3600} \times(2248-3441) \\ & =13066.7 \mathrm{kW} \end{aligned}$

即所得汽轮机的功率为 $13066.7 \mathrm{kW}$.

(3) 若计进出口的动能差, 则

$\begin{aligned} P_{1}^{\prime} & =\left(\Phi-q_{\mathrm{m}} \cdot \Delta h\right)-\frac{q_{\mathrm{m}}}{2}\left(c_{\mathrm{t} 2}^{2}-c_{\mathrm{fl}}^{2}\right) \\ & =P-\frac{q_{\mathrm{m}}}{2}\left(c_{\mathrm{t} 2}^{2}-c_{\mathrm{fl}}^{2}\right) \\ & =13066.7-\frac{40}{2} \times \frac{1000}{3600} \times\left(140^{2}-70^{2}\right) \times 10^{-3}=12985 \mathrm{kW} \end{aligned}$

即若计进出口的动能差, 则此时汽轮机功率将减小 $81.7 \mathrm{kW}$.

(4) 若计位能差, 则

$\begin{aligned} P_{1}^{\prime \prime} & =P-q_{\mathrm{m}} g \Delta z=13066.7-\frac{40 \times 1000}{3600} \times 9.81 \times(-1.6) \\ & =13066.9 \mathrm{kW} \end{aligned}$

所以此时汽轮机功率将增加 $0.2 \mathrm{kW}$.
