$W=\int_{1}^{2} p d v$ 求解过程功, 但气缸内气体所做的功等于克服外力的功, 故

$W=p_{2} A \Delta H=0.196 \times 10^{6} \mathrm{~Pa} \times 0.05 \mathrm{~m} \times 100 \times 10^{-4} \mathrm{~m}^{2}=98 \mathrm{~J}$

理想气体 $T_{2}=T_{1}$ 时, 即 $U_{2}=U_{1}$, 所以

$Q=\Delta U+W=W=98 \mathrm{~J}$

3-9 解题过程

(1) $t_{1}=T_{1}-273=480-273=207^{\circ} \mathrm{C}, t_{2}=T_{2}-273=1100-273=827^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$

由附表查得空气的气体常数 $R_{\mathrm{g}}=0.287 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})$

$\begin{aligned} & c_{\mathrm{p}}\left.\left.\right|_{0^{\circ} \mathrm{C}}^{207^{\circ} \mathrm{C}}=1.0125 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}), c_{\mathrm{p}}\left.\right|_{0^{\circ} \mathrm{C}}^{82.7^{\circ} \mathrm{C}}=1.0737 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})\right. \\ & c_{\mathrm{v}}\left.\left.\right|_{0^{\circ} \mathrm{C}}^{207^{\circ} \mathrm{C}}=c_{\mathrm{p}}\left.\right|_{0^{\circ} \mathrm{C}}^{207^{\circ} \mathrm{C}}-R_{\mathrm{g}}\right. \\ & =1.0125 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})-0.287 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \\ & =0.7255 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \\ & c_{\mathrm{v}}\left.\left.\right|_{0^{\circ} \mathrm{C}}^{827^{\circ} \mathrm{C}}=c_{\mathrm{p}}\left.\right|_{0^{\circ} \mathrm{C}}^{827^{\circ} \mathrm{C}}-R_{\mathrm{g}}\right. \\ & =1.0737 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})-0.287 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \\ & =0.7867 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \end{aligned}$

$\begin{aligned} & u_{1}=c_{\mathrm{v}}\left.\left.\right|_{0^{\circ} \mathrm{C}}^{207^{\circ} \mathrm{C}} t_{1}=0.7255 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times 207^{\circ} \mathrm{C}=150.2 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}\right. \\ & u_{2}=c_{\mathrm{v}}\left.\left.\right|_{0^{\circ} \mathrm{C}}^{827^{\circ} \mathrm{C}} t_{2}=0.7867 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times 827^{\circ} \mathrm{C}=650.6 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}\right. \\ & \Delta u=u_{2}-u_{1}=650.6 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}-150.2 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}=500.4 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \end{aligned}$

$\begin{aligned} & h_{1}=c_{\mathrm{p}}\left.\left.\right|_{0^{\circ} \mathrm{C}}^{207^{\circ} \mathrm{C}} t_{1}=1.0125 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times 207^{\circ} \mathrm{C}=209.6 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}\right. \\ & h_{2}=c_{\mathrm{p}}\left.\left.\right|_{0^{\circ} \mathrm{C}}^{827^{\circ} \mathrm{C}} t_{2}=1.0737 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times 827^{\circ} \mathrm{C}=887.9 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}\right. \\ & \Delta h=h_{2}-h_{1}=887.9 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}-209.6 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}=678.3 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}\end{aligned}$

(2) 利用空气的热力性质表

根据 $T_{1}=480 \mathrm{~K}, T_{2}=1100 \mathrm{~K}$, 查得 $h_{1}=484.49 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}, h_{2}=1162.95 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$, 由定义

$u_{1}=h_{1}-R_{\mathrm{g}} T_{1}=484.49 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}-0.287 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times 480 \mathrm{~K}=346.73 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$

$u_{2}=h_{2}-R_{\mathrm{g}} T_{2}=1162.95 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}-0.287 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times 1100 \mathrm{~K}=847.25 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$
