4-13 解题过程 由图可知 $\overline{14}=s_{4}-s_{1}=c_{\mathrm{p}} \ln \frac{T_{4}}{T_{1}}-R_{\mathrm{g}} \ln \frac{p_{2}}{p_{1}}$

$$\overline{23}=s_{3}-s_{2}=c_{\mathrm{p}} \ln \frac{T_{3}}{T_{2}}-R_{\mathrm{g}} \ln \frac{p_{2}}{p_{1}}$$

因为 $T_{4}=T_{1}, T_{3}=T_{2}$, 所以(1)(2)式简化得

$$\begin{aligned} & \overline{14}=-R_{\mathrm{g}} \ln \frac{p_{2}}{p_{1}} \\ & \overline{23}=-R_{\mathrm{g}} \ln \frac{p_{2}}{p_{1}} \end{aligned}$$

所以有 $\overline{14}=\overline{23}$

4-14 解题过程 由题意可知 12 为定压过程, 23 为定容过程

(1) 由 $Q=\Delta U+W$ 可知

$$\begin{aligned} & Q_{12}=U_{2}-U_{1}+p_{1}\left(v_{2}-v_{1}\right) \\ & Q_{23}=U_{3}-U_{2}+W_{23}=U_{3}-U_{2} \end{aligned}$$

$$Q_{13}=U_{3}-U_{1}+W_{13}$$

$$=U_{3}-U_{1}+p_{1}\left(v_{2}-v_{1}\right)+\frac{1}{2}\left(p_{3}-p_{1}\right)\left(v_{2}-v_{1}\right)$$

$$=U_{3}-U_{1}+\frac{3}{2} p_{1}\left(v_{2}-v_{1}\right)$$

$$Q_{12}+Q_{23}=U_{2}-U_{1}+p_{1}\left(v_{2}-v_{1}\right)+U_{3}-U_{2}$$

$$=U_{3}-U_{2}+p_{1}\left(v_{2}-v_{1}\right) \neq Q_{13}$$

(2)

$$\Delta S_{12}=c_{\mathrm{p}} \ln \frac{T_{2}}{T_{1}}-R_{\mathrm{g}} \ln \frac{p_{2}}{p_{1}}=c_{\mathrm{p}} \ln \frac{T_{2}}{T_{1}}=c_{\mathrm{p}} \ln \frac{v_{2}}{v_{1}}=c_{\mathrm{p}} \ln \frac{v_{3}}{v_{1}}$$

$$\Delta S_{23}=c_{\mathrm{v}} \ln \frac{T_{3}}{T_{2}}-R_{\mathrm{g}} \ln \frac{v_{2}}{v_{1}}=c_{\mathrm{v}} \ln \frac{T_{3}}{T_{2}}=c_{\mathrm{v}} \ln \frac{p_{3}}{p_{2}}=c_{\mathrm{v}} \ln \frac{p_{3}}{p_{1}}$$

$$\Delta S_{13}=c_{\mathrm{v}} \ln \frac{p_{3}}{p_{1}}+c_{\mathrm{p}} \ln \frac{v_{3}}{v_{1}}=\Delta S_{12}+\Delta S_{23}$$
