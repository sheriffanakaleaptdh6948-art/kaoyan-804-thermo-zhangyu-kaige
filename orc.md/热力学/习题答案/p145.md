(3) 压缩过程的不可逆损失

$\begin{aligned}\dot{I}_{1} & = q_{m} T_{0} s_{g l} = q_{m} T_{0} \Delta s_{1-2^{\prime}} = q_{m} T_{0} c_{p} \ln \frac{T_{2^{\prime}}}{T_{2}} \\& = 63.88 \times 290 \times 1.005 \times \ln \frac{607.51}{559.88} = 1520 \text{ kJ/s}\end{aligned}$

吸热过程的不可逆损失

$\begin{aligned}\dot{I}_{2} & = q_{m} T_{0} s_{g^{2}} = q_{m} T_{0} \left( \Delta s_{7-3} - s_{12} \right) = q_{m} T_{0} \left( c_{p} \ln \frac{T_{3}}{T_{7}} - \frac{q_{1}}{T_{\mathrm{H}}} \right) \\& = 63.88 \times 290 \times \left( 1.005 \times \ln \frac{1500}{805.09} - \frac{698.4}{1800} \right) = 4397.5 \text{ kJ/s}\end{aligned}$

膨胀过程的不可逆损失

$\begin{aligned}\dot{I}_{3} & = q_{m} T_{0} s_{g^{3}} = q_{m} T_{0} \Delta s_{3-4^{\prime}} = q_{m} T_{0} c_{p} \ln \frac{T_{4^{\prime}}}{T_{4}} \\& = 63.88 \times 290 \times 1.005 \times \ln \frac{870.94}{776.95} = 2126.1 \text{ kJ/s}\end{aligned}$

放热过程的不可逆损失

$\begin{aligned}\dot{I}_{4} & = q_{m} T_{0} s_{g 4} = q_{m} T_{0} \left( \Delta s_{8-1} - s_{14} \right) = q_{m} T_{0} \left( c_{p} \ln \frac{T_{1}}{T_{8}} - \frac{q_{2}}{T_{\mathrm{L}}} \right) \\& = 63.88 \times 290 \times \left( 1.005 \times \ln \frac{290}{673.37} - \frac{-385.3}{290} \right) = 8929 \text{ kJ/s}\end{aligned}$

换热器内的不可逆损失

$\begin{aligned}\dot{I}_{5} & = q_{m} T_{0} \left( \Delta s_{2^{\prime}-7} + \Delta s_{4^{\prime}-8} \right) = q_{m} T_{0} c_{p} \left( \ln \frac{T_{7}}{T_{2^{\prime}}} + \ln \frac{T_{8}}{T_{4^{\prime}}} \right) \\& = 63.88 \times 290 \times 1.005 \times \left( \ln \frac{805.09}{607.51} + \ln \frac{673.37}{870.95} \right) \\& = 452.6 \text{ kJ/s}\end{aligned}$
