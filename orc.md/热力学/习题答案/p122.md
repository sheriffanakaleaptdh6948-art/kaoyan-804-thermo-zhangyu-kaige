$=186.7+0.99 \times(392.7-186.7)=390.6 \mathrm{kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$

$s_{1}=s^{\prime}+x\left(s^{\prime \prime}-s^{\prime}\right)$

$=0.95+0.99 \times(1.733-0.95)=1.725 \mathrm{kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})$

$\eta_{\mathrm{c}, \mathrm{s}}=\frac{h_{2 \mathrm{~s}}-h_{1}}{h_{2}-h_{1}}=\frac{423.5-390.6}{441.5-390.6}=0.646$

$\omega_{\mathrm{c}}=h_{2}-h_{1}=441.5-390.6=50.9 \mathrm{kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$

8-16 解题过程 状态 1: $T_{1}=300 \mathrm{~K}, p_{1}=100 \mathrm{kPa}$

状态 2: $p_{2}=\pi p_{1}=5 \times 100=500 \mathrm{kPa}$

$T_{2 \mathrm{~s}}=T_{1}\left(\frac{p_{2}}{p_{1}}\right)^{\frac{k-1}{k}}=T_{1} \pi^{\frac{k-1}{k}}=300 \times 5^{\frac{1.4-1}{1.4}}=475.13 \mathrm{~K}$

$T_{2}=T_{1}+\frac{T_{2 \mathrm{~s}}-T_{1}}{\eta_{\mathrm{c}, \mathrm{s}}}=\frac{300+475.13-300}{0.82}=513.57 \mathrm{~K}$

状态 3: $p_{3}=p_{2}=500 \mathrm{kPa} \quad T_{3}=330 \mathrm{~K} ;$

状态 4: $p_{4}=\pi p_{3}=5 \times 500=2500 \mathrm{kPa}$

$T_{4 \mathrm{~s}}=T_{3}\left(\frac{p_{2}}{p_{1}}\right)^{\frac{k-1}{k}}=T_{3} \pi^{\frac{k-1}{k}}=330 \times 5^{\frac{1.4-1}{1.4}}=522.65 \mathrm{~K}$

$T_{4}=T_{3}+\frac{T_{4 \mathrm{~s}}-T_{3}}{\eta_{\mathrm{c}, \mathrm{s}}}=330+\frac{522.65-330}{0.82}=564.94 \mathrm{~K}$
