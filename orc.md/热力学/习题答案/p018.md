2-18 解 题 过 程 取刚性容器为控制体, 则有

$\delta Q=d E_{c V}+\left(h_{t 2}+\frac{1}{2} c_{t 2}^{2}+g z_{2}\right) \partial m_{2}-\left(h_{\mathrm{t} 1}+\frac{1}{2} c_{\mathrm{t} 1}^{2}+g z_{1}\right) \partial m_{1}+\delta W_{i}$

据题意快速充气 $\delta Q=0, \delta W_{i}=0, \partial m_{2}=0$, 动能差位能差可忽略不计

所以 $\quad \mathrm{d} E_{\mathrm{c} \mathrm{V}}=h_{\mathrm{t} 1} \partial m_{1}=h_{\mathrm{in}} \mathrm{dm}_{\mathrm{in}}$

积分得 $\Delta E_{c V}=h_{\mathrm{in}} m_{\mathrm{in}}$

即

$\Delta U=h_{\mathrm{in}} m_{\mathrm{in}}$

$m_{2} u_{2}-m_{1} u_{1}=h_{\mathrm{in}}\left(m_{2}-m_{1}\right)$

$T_{2}=\frac{h_{\mathrm{in}}\left(m_{2}-m_{1}\right)+m_{1} u_{1}}{m_{2} c_{\mathrm{V}}}=\frac{c_{\mathrm{P}} T_{\mathrm{in}}\left(m_{2}-m_{1}\right)+m_{1} 0.72 T_{1}}{m_{2} c_{\mathrm{V}}}$

$m_{2}=\frac{p_{2} V_{2}}{R_{g} T_{2}}=\frac{0.2 \times 10^{6} \times 0.028}{287 \times T_{2}}=\frac{19.5}{T_{2}}$

$m_{1}=\frac{p_{1} V_{1}}{R_{g} T_{1}}=\frac{0.2 \times 10^{6} \times 0.02}{287 \times 294.15}=0.0332 \mathrm{~kg}$

由(1)(2)(3)式解得:

$T_{2}=342.69 \mathrm{~K}, m_{2}=0.057 \mathrm{~kg}$

2-19 解 题 过 程 由能量守恒得

$\delta Q=\mathrm{d} E_{c V}+\left(h_{2}+\frac{c_{\mathrm{P}}^{2}}{2}+g z_{2}\right) \partial m_{\text {out }}-\left(h_{1}+\frac{c_{\mathrm{t} 1}^{2}}{2}+g z_{1}\right) \partial m_{\mathrm{in}}+\delta W_{i}$

据题意, $\delta Q=0, \partial m_{\text {out }}=0, \mathrm{~d} E_{\mathrm{c} \mathrm{V}}=\mathrm{d} U$, 忽略动能差和位能差, 则有

$m_{1}=0, v_{1}=0$

$\mathrm{d} U=h_{\mathrm{in}} \partial m_{\mathrm{in}}+\delta W_{\mathrm{i}}=0$

$\Delta U-h_{\mathrm{in}} m_{\mathrm{in}}+\Delta W_{\mathrm{i}}=0$

积分后得 $m_{2} u_{2}-h_{\mathrm{in}} m_{2}+p_{0}\left(V_{2}-V_{1}\right)=0$

$\begin{aligned} & m_{2} u_{2}-h_{\mathrm{in}} m_{2}+p_{0} V_{2}=0 \\ & p_{2} V_{2}=m_{2} R_{g} T_{2} \end{aligned}$

代入已知数据得

$T_{2}=313.2 \mathrm{~K}, m_{2}=0.0147 \mathrm{~kg}$
