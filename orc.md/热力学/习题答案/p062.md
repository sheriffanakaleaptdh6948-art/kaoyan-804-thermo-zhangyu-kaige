$\begin{aligned} & v^{\prime}=0.0008 \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{kg}, h^{\prime}=227.4 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \\ & v^{\prime \prime}=0.036 \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{kg}, h^{\prime \prime}=409.8 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \\ & v_{1}=v^{\prime}+x_{1}\left(v^{\prime \prime}-v^{\prime}\right) \\ & =0.0008+0.15 \times(0.036-0.0008) \\ & =0.00608 \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{kg} \\ & m=\frac{V_{\mathrm{A}}}{v_{1}}=\frac{1}{0.00608}=164.5 \mathrm{~kg} \\ & h_{1}=h^{\prime}+x_{1}\left(h^{\prime \prime}-h^{\prime}\right) \\ & =227.4+0.15 \times\left(409.8-227.4\right)=254.8 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \\ & u_{1}=h_{1}-p_{s} v_{1}=254.8-571.88 \times 0.00608=251.3 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \end{aligned}$

终态: $v_{2}=\frac{V_{\mathrm{A}}+V_{\mathrm{B}}}{m}=\frac{1+1}{164.5}=0.0122 \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{kg}$

由于 $v^{\prime}<v_{2}<v^{\prime \prime}$, 所以终态仍是湿蒸汽状态

$\begin{aligned} & x_{2}=\frac{v_{2}-v^{\prime}}{v^{\prime \prime}-v^{\prime}}=\frac{0.0122-0.0008}{0.036-0.0008}=0.323 \\ & h_{2}=h^{\prime}+x_{2}\left(h^{\prime \prime}-h^{\prime}\right) \\ & =227.4+0.323 \times\left(409.8-227.4\right)=286.3 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \\ & u_{2}=h_{2}-p_{s} v_{2} \\ & =286.3-571.88 \times 0.0122=279.3 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \\ & Q=\Delta U+W=m\left(u_{2}-u_{1}\right) \\ & =164.5 \times(279.3-251.3)=4603.7 \mathrm{~kJ} \end{aligned}$
