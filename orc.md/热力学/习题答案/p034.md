3-18 解题过程 取喷管为控制体积,列能量方程

$$h_{1}+\frac{c_{11}^{2}}{2}+g z_{1}=h_{2}+\frac{c_{12}^{2}}{2}+g z_{2}$$

3-19 解题过程 据题意

$$p_{1}=k V_{1}, p_{2}=k V_{2}$$

$$V_{1}=\frac{m R_{g} T_{1}}{p_{1}}=\frac{3 \mathrm{~kg} \times 0.260 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times(27+273) \mathrm{K}}{1000 \mathrm{~kPa}}=0.234 \mathrm{~m}^{3}$$

$$V_{2}=\frac{m R_{g} T_{2}}{p_{2}}=\frac{3 \mathrm{~kg} \times 0.260 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times(327+273) \mathrm{K}}{1500 \mathrm{~kPa}}=0.312 \mathrm{~m}^{3}$$

$$W=\int_{1}^{2} p \mathrm{~d} V=\int_{1}^{2} k V \mathrm{~d} V=\frac{k\left(V_{2}^{2}-V_{1}^{2}\right)}{2}=\frac{p_{2}+p_{1}}{2}\left(V_{2}-V_{1}\right)$$

$$=\frac{1000 \mathrm{~kPa}+1500 \mathrm{~kPa}}{2} \times\left(0.312 \mathrm{~m}^{3}-0.234 \mathrm{~m}^{3}\right)=97.5 \mathrm{~kJ}$$

$$Q=\Delta U+W=m c_{\mathrm{v}}\left(T_{2}-T_{1}\right)+W$$

$$=3 \mathrm{~kg} \times 0.658 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times(600 \mathrm{~K}-300 \mathrm{~K})+97.5 \mathrm{~kJ}=689.7 \mathrm{~kJ}$$
