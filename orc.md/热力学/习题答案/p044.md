(2) 由(1)可知, $R_{\mathrm{g}}=\frac{8.3145}{28.97 \times 10^{-3}}=287 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})$

$\begin{aligned} W & =m\left(u_{1}-u_{2}\right)=m\left(h_{1}-h_{2}\right)-m R_{\mathrm{g}}\left(T_{1}-T_{2}\right) \\ & =3 \times\left[934.91-489.3-287 \times(900-489.30) \times 10^{-3}\right] \\ & =983.22 \mathrm{~kJ} \\ W_{\mathrm{t}} & =m\left(h_{1}-h_{2}\right)=3 \times(934.91-489.3)=1336.82 \mathrm{~kJ} \end{aligned}$

$\begin{aligned} & \Delta U=-W=-983.22 \mathrm{~kJ} \\ & \Delta H=-W_{\mathrm{t}}=-1336.82 \mathrm{~kJ} \end{aligned}$
