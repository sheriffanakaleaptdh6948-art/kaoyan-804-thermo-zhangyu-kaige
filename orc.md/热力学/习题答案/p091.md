$\alpha_{V}=\frac{1}{v}\left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial T}\right)_{p} \text {. 即 }$

$\alpha_{V}=\frac{1}{v} \frac{R_{\mathrm{g}}}{p}=\frac{R_{\mathrm{g}}}{R_{\mathrm{g}} T}=\frac{1}{T}$
