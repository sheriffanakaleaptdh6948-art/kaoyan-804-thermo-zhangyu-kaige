$\left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial T}\right)_{v}=\frac{R_{g}}{v-b}, T\left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial T}\right)_{v}-p=\frac{R_{g} T}{v-b}-\left(\frac{R_{g} T}{v-b}-\frac{a}{v^{2}}\right)=\frac{a}{v^{2}}$
