由题意可知: 混凝土板的放热量

$Q=m c \Delta T=\rho V c \Delta T=2300 \times 5 \times 8 \times 0.3 \times 0.65 \times(23-18)=89700 \mathrm{~kJ}$

环境介质的熵变 $\Delta S_{\text {sur }}=\frac{Q_{\text {sur }}}{T_{0}}=\frac{Q}{T_{0}}=\frac{89700}{273+18}=308.25 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{K}$

混凝土的熵变

$\begin{aligned} \Delta S & =m \int_{1}^{2} \frac{\partial q}{T}=m c \int_{1}^{2} \frac{\mathrm{dT}}{T}=m \operatorname{cln} \frac{T_{2}}{T_{1}} \\ & =\rho V \operatorname{cln} \frac{T_{2}}{T_{1}}=2300 \times 5 \times 8 \times 0.3 \times 0.65 \times \ln \frac{273+18}{273+23} \\ & =-305.63 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{K} \end{aligned}$

孤立系统的熵增即为熵产, 所以有

$S_{\mathrm{g}}=\Delta S_{\mathrm{iso}}=\Delta S+\Delta S_{\mathrm{sur}}=-305.63+308.25=2.62 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{K}$

5-13 解题过程 由题意可知 $\Delta U=Q-W=0-0=0 \mathrm{~kJ}$

即 $\Delta U_{\mathrm{w}}+\Delta U_{\mathrm{m}}=0$

$\begin{aligned} & m_{\mathrm{w}} c_{\mathrm{w}}\left(T_{\mathrm{f}}-T_{\mathrm{w}}\right)+m_{\mathrm{m}} c_{\mathrm{m}}\left(T_{\mathrm{f}}-T_{\mathrm{m}}\right)=0 \\ & T_{\mathrm{f}}=\frac{m_{\mathrm{w}} c_{\mathrm{w}} T_{\mathrm{w}}+m_{\mathrm{m}} c_{\mathrm{m}} T_{\mathrm{m}}}{m_{\mathrm{w}} c_{\mathrm{w}}+m_{\mathrm{m}} c_{\mathrm{m}}} \\ & =\frac{9 \times 4187 \times 295+0.36 \times 420 \times 1060}{9 \times 4187+0.36 \times 420}=298.1 \mathrm{~K} \end{aligned}$

该孤立系统的熵变

$\begin{aligned} \Delta S_{\mathrm{iso}} & =\Delta S_{\mathrm{w}}+\Delta S_{\mathrm{m}}=m_{\mathrm{w}} c_{\mathrm{w}} \ln \frac{T_{\mathrm{f}}}{T_{\mathrm{w}}}+m_{\mathrm{m}} c_{\mathrm{m}} \ln \frac{T_{\mathrm{f}}}{T_{\mathrm{m}}} \\ & =9 \times 4.187 \times \ln \frac{298.1}{295}+0.36 \times 0.42 \times \ln \frac{298.1}{1060} \\ & =0.3939-0.1918=0.2021 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{K} \end{aligned}$
