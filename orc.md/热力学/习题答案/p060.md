4-32 解题过程 由题意可知, 取全部 R134a 为热力系统. 终态时容器 A 和气缸 B 内 R134a 状态相同.

初态: $t_{1}=30^{\circ} \mathrm{C}, x_{1}=1$, 查 R134a 热力性质表得

$v_{1}=0.0266 \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{kg}$

$p_{1}=770.6 \mathrm{kPa}, h_{1}=414.8 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$

终态: $t_{2}=30^{\circ} \mathrm{C}, p_{2}=200 \mathrm{kPa}$, 查表得 $h_{2}=427 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}, v_{2}=0.01187 \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{kg}$

$m=\frac{V_{1}}{v_{1}}=\frac{0.1}{0.0266}=3.759 \mathrm{~kg}$

$V_{2}=m v_{2}=3.759 \times 0.01187=0.4462 \mathrm{~m}^{3}$

$u_{1}=h_{1}-p_{1} v_{1}=414.8-770.6 \times 0.0266=394.3 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$

$u_{2}=h_{2}-p_{2} v_{2}=427-200 \times 0.1187=403.3 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$

$\Delta V_{\mathrm{B}}=V_{2}-V_{\mathrm{A}}=0.4462-0.1=0.3462 \mathrm{~m}^{3}$

$Q=\Delta U+W=m\left(u_{2}-u_{1}\right)+p_{2} \Delta V_{\mathrm{B}}$

$=3.759 \times(403.3-394.3)+200 \times 0.3462=103.1 \mathrm{~kJ}$
