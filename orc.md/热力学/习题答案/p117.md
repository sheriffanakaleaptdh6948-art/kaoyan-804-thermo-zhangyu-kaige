$$T_{2}=T_{1} \pi^{\frac{n-1}{n}}=293.15 \times 7^{\frac{1.3-1}{1.3}}=459.32 \mathrm{~K}$$

$$q_{\mathrm{n}}=\frac{n-k}{n-1} c_{\mathrm{v}} \Delta T$$

$$=\frac{1.3-1.4}{1.3-1} \times 0.718 \times(459.32-293.15)=-39.77 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$$

$$P_{Q}=q_{\mathrm{m}} q_{\mathrm{n}}=\frac{381.36}{3600} \times(-39.77)=-4.21 \mathrm{kW}$$

由题意可知 $\pi=\frac{p_{2}}{p_{1}}=8, n=1.25, \sigma=\frac{V_{\mathrm{c}}}{V_{\mathrm{h}}}=0.05$

$$\eta_{\mathrm{N}}=1-\sigma\left(\pi^{\frac{1}{n}}-1\right)=1-0.05 \times\left(8^{\frac{1}{1.25}}-1\right)=0.786$$

$$q_{\mathrm{m}}=n \frac{p_{1} V}{R_{\mathrm{g}} T_{1}}=n \frac{p_{1} \eta_{\mathrm{V}} V_{\mathrm{h}}}{R_{\mathrm{g}} T_{1}}$$

$$=\frac{400}{60} \times \frac{0.1 \times 10^{6} \times 0.786 \times \frac{\pi}{4} \times 0.3^{2} \times 0.2}{287 \times 293.15}=0.088 \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{s}$$

可逆定温压缩功率为

$$\begin{aligned} P_{\mathrm{C}, \mathrm{T}} & =-p_{1} V_{1} \ln \frac{p_{2}}{p_{1}} \cdot n \\ & =-\frac{400}{60} \times 0.1 \times 10^{6} \times 0.786 \times \frac{\pi}{4} \times 0.3^{2} \times 0.2 \times \ln \frac{8}{1}=15.4 \mathrm{kW} \end{aligned}$$

由 $\eta_{\mathrm{C}, \mathrm{T}}=\frac{P_{\mathrm{C}, \mathrm{T}}}{P_{\mathrm{C}}}$ 可知

$$P_{\mathrm{C}}=\frac{P_{\mathrm{C}, \mathrm{T}}}{\eta_{\mathrm{C}, \mathrm{T}}}=\frac{15.4}{0.77}=20 \mathrm{kW}$$
