3-17 解题过程 初态时 A 室和 B 室空气质量

$\begin{aligned}& m_{\mathrm{A}} = \frac{p_{\mathrm{A}} V_{\mathrm{A}}}{R_{\mathrm{g}} T_{\mathrm{A}}} = \frac{250 \times 10^{3} \mathrm{~Pa} \times 0.5 \mathrm{~m}^{3}}{287 \mathrm{~J} / (\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times 300 \mathrm{~K}} = 1.452 \mathrm{~kg} \\& m_{\mathrm{B}} = \frac{p_{\mathrm{B}} V_{\mathrm{B}}}{R_{\mathrm{g}} T_{\mathrm{B}}} = \frac{150 \times 10^{3} \mathrm{~Pa} \times 1 \mathrm{~m}^{3}}{287 \mathrm{~J} / (\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times 1000 \mathrm{~K}} = 0.523 \mathrm{~kg} \\& m = m_{\mathrm{A}} + m_{\mathrm{B}} = 1.452 \mathrm{~kg} + 0.523 \mathrm{~kg} = 1.975 \mathrm{~kg}\end{aligned}$
