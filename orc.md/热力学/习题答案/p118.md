(3) $T_{2}=T_{1} \pi^{\frac{n-1}{n}}=425 \mathrm{~K}$

$T_{3}=T_{2} \pi^{\frac{n-1}{n}}=425 \times 5^{\frac{1.3-1}{1.3}}=616.25 \mathrm{~K}$

$T_{4}=T_{3} \pi^{\frac{n-1}{n}}=616.25 \times 5^{\frac{1.3-1}{1.3}}=893.56 \mathrm{~K}$

$w_{c}=\frac{n}{n-1} R_{g} T_{1}\left(\pi^{\frac{n-1}{n}}-1\right)+\frac{n}{n-1} R_{g} T_{2}\left(\pi^{\frac{n-1}{n}}-1\right)+\frac{n}{n-1} R_{g} T_{3}\left(\pi^{\frac{n-1}{n}}-1\right)$

$=\frac{n}{n-1} R_{g}\left(\pi^{\frac{n-1}{n}}-1\right)\left(T_{1}+T_{2}+T_{3}\right)$

$=\frac{1.3}{1.3-1} \times 287 \times\left(5^{\frac{1.3-1}{1.3}}-1\right) \times(293.15+425+616.25)$
