2-9 解题过程

(1) 由气球内的压力变化与体积成正比可设为

$$\Delta p = p - p_{\mathrm{b}} = k V + b$$

当 $V_{1} = 0.3 \mathrm{~m}^{3}$ 时, $\Delta p = 0 ; V_{2} = 0.6 \mathrm{~m}^{3}$ 时, $\Delta p = 0.05 \mathrm{MPa}$

代入上式得 $b = -0.05, k = 0.1667$

所以 $\Delta p = 0.1667 V - 0.05$

气体所做的功

$$\begin{aligned} W & = \int_{V_{1}}^{V_{2}} p \mathrm{~d} V = \int_{V_{1}}^{V_{2}} (p_{\mathrm{b}} + 0.1667 V - 0.05) \mathrm{d} V \\ & = \int_{V_{1}}^{V_{2}} (0.1667 V - 0.05 + 0.1) \times 10^{6} \mathrm{~d} V = 37.5 \mathrm{~kJ} \end{aligned}$$

(2) 气球膨胀克服大气所做的功

$$W_{0} = p_{\mathrm{b}} (V_{2} - V_{1}) = 0.1 \times 10^{6} \times (0.6 - 0.3) = 30 \mathrm{~kJ}$$

克服橡皮气球弹力所做的功

$$W_{\mathrm{e}} = W - W_{0} = 37.5 - 30 = 7.5 \mathrm{~kJ}$$

(3) 气体质量计算: 由 $p_{1} V_{1} = m_{\mathrm{a}} R_{\mathrm{g}} T_{1}$ 得

$$m_{\mathrm{a}} = \frac{p_{1} V_{1}}{R_{\mathrm{g}} T_{1}} = \frac{0.1 \times 10^{6} \times 0.3}{287 \times (273.15 + 17)} = 0.36 \mathrm{~kg}$$

气体吸热量

$$T_{2} = \frac{p_{2} V_{2}}{m R_{\mathrm{g}}} = \frac{0.15 \times 10^{6} \times 0.6}{0.36 \times 287} = 871.08 \mathrm{~K}$$

$$Q = \Delta U + W = 0.72 m \Delta T + W$$

$$= 0.72 \times 0.36 \times (871.08 - 290.15) + 37.5 = 188.1 \mathrm{~kJ}$$
