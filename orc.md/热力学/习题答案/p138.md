(1) 由题意可知，过程 1-2 和 3-4 为可逆绝热过程

$$T_{2}=T_{1}\left(\frac{p_{2}}{p_{1}}\right)^{\frac{k}{k}}=T_{1} \pi^{\frac{k}{k}}=300 \times 6^{\frac{1.4-1}{1.4}}=500.6 \mathrm{~K}$$

$q_{1}=q_{2-3}=c_{p}\left(T_{3}-T_{2}\right)=1.03 \times(923-500.6)=432.1 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$

$T_{4}=T_{3}\left(\frac{p_{4}}{p_{3}}\right)^{\frac{k}{k}}=T_{3}\left(\frac{1}{\pi}\right)^{\frac{k}{k}}=300 \times\left(\frac{1}{6}\right)^{\frac{1.4-1}{1.4}}=553.2 \mathrm{~K}$

$q_{2}=q_{4-1}=c_{p}\left(T_{4}-T_{1}\right)=1.03 \times(553.2-300)=260.8 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$

$w_{\text {net }}=q_{1}-q_{2}=432.1-260.8=174.3 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$

$\eta=\frac{w_{\text {net }}}{q_{1}}=\frac{174.3}{432.1}=40.1 \%$
