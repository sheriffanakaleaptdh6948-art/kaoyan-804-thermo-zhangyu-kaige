取压缩空气为系统，由于空气与外界无传热现象，所以 $Q=0$

由 $Q=\Delta U+W$ 可知

$\begin{aligned}\Delta U & =Q-W=-W=-\left[p_{\mathrm{b}}\left(V_{2}-V_{1}\right)+\frac{m}{2} c_{\mathrm{b}}^{2}\right] \\& =-\left[0.1 \times 10^{6} \times(0.99-0.28)-\frac{2722}{2} \times 61^{2}\right] \\& =-5.135 \times 10^{6} \mathrm{~J}\end{aligned}$

设活塞质量为 $m_{p}$, 由题意可知, 空气的质量

$\begin{aligned}m_{\mathrm{a}} & =\frac{p_{1} V_{1}}{R_{\mathrm{g}} T_{1}}=\frac{0.1013 \times 10^{6} \times 0.008}{287 \times(273.15+17)} \\& =9.73 \times 10^{-3} \mathrm{~kg}\end{aligned}$

初始时弹簧处于自由状态

即 $m_{\mathrm{p}} g+p_{\mathrm{b}} A=p_{1} A$

$m_{\mathrm{p}}=\frac{\left(p_{1}-p_{\mathrm{b}}\right) A}{g}=\frac{(0.1013-0.1) \times 10^{6} \times 0.08}{9.81}=10.61 \mathrm{~kg}$

当压力达到 $0.15 \mathrm{MPa}$ 时, 即 $p_{2}=0.15 \mathrm{MPa}$, 设此时弹簧的变形量为 $x_{2}$

则有 $p_{2} A=m_{\mathrm{p}} g+p_{\mathrm{b}} A+k x_{2}$

$x_{2}=\frac{\left(p_{2}-p_{\mathrm{b}}\right) A-m_{\mathrm{p}} g}{k}$

$\begin{aligned}& =\frac{(0.15-0.1) \times 10^{6} \times 0.08-10.61 \times 9.81}{40000} \\& =0.0974 \mathrm{~m} \\& V_{2}=V_{1}+A x_{2}=0.008+0.08 \times 0.0974=0.0158 \mathrm{~m}^{3}\end{aligned}$

由 $p_{2} V_{2}=m_{\mathrm{a}} R_{\mathrm{g}} T_{2}$ 可得 $T_{2}=\frac{p_{2} V_{2}}{m_{\mathrm{a}} R_{\mathrm{g}}}=\frac{0.15 \times 10^{6} \times 0.0158}{9.73 \times 10^{-3} \times 287}=848.26 \mathrm{~K}$

$\begin{aligned}\Delta U & =m_{\mathrm{a}} \Delta u \\& =9.73 \times 10^{-3} \times 0.718 \times(848.26-290.15)=3.9 \mathrm{~kJ} \\\mathrm{~W} & =\int_{1}^{2} p \mathrm{~d} V=\int_{x_{1}}^{x_{2}}\left(p_{\mathrm{b}}+\frac{m_{\mathrm{p}} g+k x}{A}\right) \mathrm{d}(\mathrm{Ax}) \\& =\int_{x_{1}}^{x_{2}}\left(p_{\mathrm{b}} A+m_{\mathrm{p}} g+k x\right) \mathrm{d} x \\& =\left(p_{\mathrm{b}} A+m_{\mathrm{p}} g\right)\left(x_{2}-x_{1}\right)+\frac{k}{2}\left(x_{2}^{2}-x_{1}^{2}\right)\end{aligned}$
