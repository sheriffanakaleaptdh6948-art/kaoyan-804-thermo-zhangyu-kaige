3-23 解题过程 据水蒸汽表 $p = 0.5 \, \text{MPa}$ 时，$v' = 0.0010925 \, \text{m}^3/\text{kg}, v'' = 0.37486 \, \text{m}^3/\text{kg}$，因 $v' < v < v''$，所以该水蒸汽不是过热蒸汽，而是饱和湿蒸汽。

据同一表，$t_s = 151.867 \, ^\circ\text{C}, h' = 640.35 \, \text{kJ/kg}, h'' = 2748.59 \, \text{kJ/kg}, s' = 1.8610$

$\text{kJ/(kg·K)}, s'' = 6.8214 \, \text{kJ/(kg·K)}$

$x = \frac{v - v'}{v'' - v'} = \frac{0.35 \, \text{m}^3/\text{kg} - 0.0010925 \, \text{m}^3/\text{kg}}{0.37486 \, \text{m}^3/\text{kg} - 0.0010925 \, \text{m}^3/\text{kg}} = 0.9335$

$h = h' + x(h'' - h')$

$= 640.35 \, \text{kJ/kg} + 0.9335 \times (2748.59 - 640.35) \, \text{kJ/kg} = 2608.4 \, \text{kJ/kg}$

$s = s' + x(s'' - s')$

$= 1.8610 \, \text{kJ/(kg·K)} + 0.9335 \times (6.8214 - 1.8610) \, \text{kJ/(kg·K)}$

$= 6.4915 \, \text{kJ/(kg·K)}$
