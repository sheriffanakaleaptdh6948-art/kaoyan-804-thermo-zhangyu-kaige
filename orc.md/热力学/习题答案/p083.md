$$E_{x, U}=m T_{0} R_{g} \ln \frac{p}{p_{0}}+p_{0} V-p_{0} V_{0}$$

$$=p_{0} V\left(1-\frac{V_{0}}{V}+\frac{p}{p_{0}} \ln \frac{p}{p_{0}}\right)$$

$$=p_{0} V\left(1-\frac{p}{p_{0}}+\frac{p}{p_{0}} \ln \frac{p}{p_{0}}\right)$$

证毕.

(1) $c_{V}=c_{p}-R_{g}=1.04-0.296=0.744 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})$

$m=\frac{p_{1} V_{1}}{R_{g} T_{1}}=\frac{0.7 \times 10^{6} \times 2.45 \times 10^{-3}}{296 \times(867+273)}=0.00508 \mathrm{~kg}$

$V_{0}=\frac{m R_{g} T_{0}}{p_{0}}=\frac{0.00508 \times 296 \times 300}{0.1013 \times 10^{6}}=4.455 \times 10^{-3} \mathrm{~m}^{3}$

$E_{x, U_{1}}=U_{1}-U_{0}-T_{0}\left(S_{1}-S_{0}\right)+p_{0}\left(V_{1}-V_{0}\right)$

$$=m c_{\mathrm{V}}\left(T_{1}-T_{0}\right)-m T_{0}\left(c_{p} \ln \frac{T_{1}}{T_{0}}-R_{g} \ln \frac{p_{1}}{p_{0}}\right)+p_{0}\left(V_{1}-V_{0}\right)$$

$$=1727.7 \mathrm{~J}$$

(2) $W_{\mathrm{u}, \max }=E_{x, \mathrm{U}_{1}}-E_{x, \mathrm{U}_{2}}=U_{1}-U_{2}-T_{0}\left(S_{1}-S_{2}\right)+p_{0}\left(V_{1}-V_{2}\right)$

$V_{2}=\frac{m R_{g} T_{2}}{p_{2}}=\frac{0.00508 \times 296 \times(273+637)}{0.3 \times 10^{6}}=4.5633 \times 10^{-3} \mathrm{~m}^{3}$

$W_{\mathrm{u}, \max }=m c_{\mathrm{v}}\left(T_{1}-T_{2}\right)-m T_{0}\left(c_{p} \ln \frac{T_{1}}{T_{2}}-R_{g} \ln \frac{p_{1}}{p_{2}}\right)+p_{0}\left(V_{1}-V_{2}\right)$

$$=680.3 \mathrm{~J}$$

对于理想气体

$$\mathrm{e}_{\mathrm{x}, \mathrm{H}}=h-h_{0}-T_{0}\left(s-s_{0}\right)=c_{p}\left(T-T_{0}\right)-T_{0}\left(c_{p} \ln \frac{T}{T_{0}}-\frac{k-1}{k} c_{p} \ln \frac{p}{p_{0}}\right)$$

$$=\mathrm{c}_{\mathrm{p}} T_{0}\left[\frac{T}{T_{0}}-1-\ln \frac{T}{T_{0}}+\ln \left(\frac{p}{p_{0}}\right)^{\frac{k-1}{k}}\right]$$

$$\frac{\mathrm{e}_{\mathrm{x}, \mathrm{H}}}{\mathrm{c}_{\mathrm{p}} T_{0}}=\frac{T}{T_{0}}-1-\ln \frac{T}{T_{0}}+\ln \left(\frac{p}{p_{0}}\right)^{\frac{k-1}{k}}$$
