$d_{1}=0.622 \frac{\varphi_{1} p_{\mathrm{s} 1}}{p-\varphi_{1} p_{\mathrm{s} 1}}=0.622 \times \frac{0.4 \times 2.337}{100-0.4 \times 2.337}$

$d_{2}=0.622 \frac{\varphi_{2} p_{\mathrm{s} 2}}{p-\varphi_{2} p_{\mathrm{s} 2}}=0.622 \times \frac{0.8 \times 0.259}{100-0.8 \times 0.259}$

$=0.001291 \mathrm{~kg}$ 水蒸气 $/ \mathrm{kg}$ (干空气)

$h_{1}=1.005 t_{1}+d_{1}\left(2501+1.86 t_{1}\right)$

$h_{2}=1.005 t_{2}+d_{2}\left(2501+1.86 t_{2}\right)$

$=1.005 \times(-10)+0.00129 \times\left[2501+1.86 \times(-10)\right]=6.844 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$
