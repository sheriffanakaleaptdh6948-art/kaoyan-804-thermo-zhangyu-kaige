(2) 打开旁通阀, 关闭压缩机后, 取 A、B 和旁通阀为热力系。因 $T_{A 3} = T_{A 1}$, 压力为

$$p_{3} = \frac{2 m_{A 1} R_{\mathrm{g}} T_{A 3}}{V_{A} + V_{\mathrm{B}}} = \frac{2 \times 2.7875 \times 287 \times 300}{3.375} = 0.142 \times 10^{6} \mathrm{~Pa}$$

$$m_{A 3} = \frac{p_{3} V_{A}}{R_{\mathrm{g}} T_{A 3}} = \frac{0.1422 \times 10^{6} \times 3}{287 \times 300} = 4.954 \mathrm{~kg}$$

$$m_{\mathrm{B} 3} = 2 m_{A 1} - m_{A 3} = 2 \times 2.7875 - 4.954 = 0.621 \mathrm{~kg}$$

$$I = E_{x, U_{2}} - E_{x, U_{3}} = U_{2} - U_{3} - T_{0} \left( s_{2} - s_{3} \right) + p_{0} \left( V_{2} - V_{3} \right)$$
