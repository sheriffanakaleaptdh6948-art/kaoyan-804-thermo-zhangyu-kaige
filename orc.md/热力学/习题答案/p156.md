假定膨胀过程为等熵过程，由 $s_{01} = s_1 = 5.83 \, \text{kJ/(kg·K)}$ 及抽气压力 $p_{01} = 0.782 \, \text{MPa}$，查表得 $h_{01} = 2395.9 \, \text{kJ/kg}$、$h'_{01} = 717 \, \text{kJ/kg}$，由 $s_2 = s_1$、$p_2 = 0.007 \, \text{MPa}$，查表得 $x_2 = 0.68$，$h_2 = 1808.7 \, \text{kJ/kg}$，$h_3 = h'_2 = 163.4 \, \text{kJ/kg}$

(1) 根据热平衡方程 $\alpha(h_{01} - h'_{01}) = (1 - \alpha)(h'_{01} - h'_2)$ 可得

$\alpha = \frac{h'_{01} - h'_2}{h_{01} - h'_2} = \frac{717 - 163.4}{2395.9 - 163.4} = 0.248$

(2) $q_1 = h_1 - h'_{01} = 2772.5 - 717 = 2055.5 \, \text{kJ/kg}$

$\begin{aligned}w_{\text{net}} & = (h_1 - h_{01}) + (1 - \alpha)(h_{01} - h_2) \\& = 2772.5 - 2395.9 + (1 - 0.248) \times (2395.5 - 1808.7) \\& = 817.9 \, \text{kJ/kg}\end{aligned}$

$\eta = \frac{w_{\text{net}}}{q_1} = \frac{817.9}{2055.5} = 39.8\%$

(3) $d = \frac{1}{\omega_{\text{net}}} = \frac{1 \times 10^{-3}}{817.9} = 1.22 \times 10^{-6} \, \text{kg/J}$

(4) 若为朗肯循环，则

$\begin{aligned}\eta' & = \frac{h_1 - h_2}{h_1 - h'_2} = \frac{2772.5 - 1808.7}{2772.5 - 163.4} = 36.9\% \\d' & = \frac{1}{h_1 - h_2} = \frac{1 \times 10^{-3}}{2772.5 - 1808.7} = 1.04 \times 10^{-6} \, \text{kg/J}\end{aligned}$
