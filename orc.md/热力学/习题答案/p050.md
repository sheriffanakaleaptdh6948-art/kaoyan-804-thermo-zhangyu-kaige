$$c_{V, O_{2}}=\frac{5}{2} R_{\mathrm{g}, O_{2}}=\frac{5}{2} \times 259.83=649.6 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})$$

$$m_{\mathrm{A}}=\frac{p_{\mathrm{A} 1} V_{\mathrm{A} 1}}{R_{\mathrm{g}, \mathrm{N}_{2}} T_{\mathrm{A} 1}}=\frac{0.1 \times 10^{6} \times 0.5}{298.84 \times 300}=0.5615 \mathrm{~kg}$$

$$m_{\mathrm{B}}=\frac{p_{\mathrm{Bl}} V_{\mathrm{Bl}}}{R_{\mathrm{g}, O_{2}} T_{\mathrm{Bl}}}=\frac{0.1 \times 10^{6} \times 0.5}{259.83 \times 300}=0.6414 \mathrm{~kg}$$

(1) 由活塞可自由移动可知, $p_{\mathrm{B} 2}=p_{\mathrm{A} 2}=0.22 \mathrm{MPa}$

据题意可知, $B$ 内进行的是可逆绝热过程

$$\begin{aligned} & T_{\mathrm{B} 2}=\left(\frac{p_{\mathrm{B} 2}}{p_{\mathrm{Bl}}}\right)^{\frac{k-1}{k}} T_{\mathrm{Bl}}=\left(\frac{0.22}{0.1}\right)^{\frac{1.4-1}{1.4}} \times 300=375.8 \mathrm{~K} \\ & V_{\mathrm{B} 2}=\frac{m_{\mathrm{B}} R_{\mathrm{g}, O_{2}} T_{\mathrm{B} 2}}{p_{\mathrm{B} 2}}=\frac{0.6414 \times 259.83 \times 375.8}{0.22 \times 10^{6}}=0.2847 \mathrm{~m}^{3} \end{aligned}$$

(2) 由 $V_{\mathrm{A} 1}+V_{\mathrm{Bl}}=V_{\mathrm{A} 2}+V_{\mathrm{B} 2}$ 可知

$$\begin{aligned} & V_{\mathrm{A} 2}=V_{\mathrm{A} 1}+V_{\mathrm{Bl}}-V_{\mathrm{B} 2}=0.5+0.5-0.2847=0.7153 \mathrm{~m}^{3} \\ & T_{\mathrm{A} 2}=\frac{p_{\mathrm{A} 2} V_{\mathrm{A} 2}}{m_{\mathrm{A}} R_{\mathrm{g}, \mathrm{N}_{2}}}=\frac{0.22 \times 10^{6} \times 0.7153}{0.5615 \times 296.84}=944.15 \mathrm{~K} \end{aligned}$$

(3) 取 $A$ 与 $B$ 作为系统, 系统不对外做功, 故 $W=0$

$$\begin{aligned} & Q=Q_{\mathrm{A}}+Q_{\mathrm{B}}=\Delta U_{\mathrm{A}}+\Delta U_{\mathrm{B}}+W=\Delta U_{\mathrm{A}}+\Delta U_{\mathrm{B}} \\ & =m_{\mathrm{A}} c_{\mathrm{V}, \mathrm{N}_{2}}\left(T_{\mathrm{A} 2}-T_{\mathrm{A} 1}\right)+m_{\mathrm{B}} c_{\mathrm{V}, O_{2}}\left(T_{\mathrm{B} 2}-T_{\mathrm{Bl}}\right) \\ & =0.5615 \times 742.1 \times(944.15-300)+0.6414 \times 649.6 \times(375.8-300) \\ & =299.99 \mathrm{~kJ} \end{aligned}$$

$$W_{\mathrm{A}}=-W_{\mathrm{B}}=\Delta U_{\mathrm{B}}=m_{\mathrm{B}} c_{\mathrm{V}, O_{2}}\left(T_{\mathrm{B} 2}-T_{\mathrm{Bl}}\right)$$

$$=0.6414 \times 649.6 \times(375.8-300)=31.58 \mathrm{~kJ}$$

(4) $B$ 内进行的可逆绝热过程, 即等熵过程

所以 $\Delta s_{\mathrm{O}_{2}}=0 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{K}$

$$\begin{aligned} \Delta s_{\mathrm{N}_{2}} & =m_{\mathrm{A}} c_{\mathrm{p}, \mathrm{N}_{2}} \ln \frac{T_{\mathrm{A} 2}}{T_{\mathrm{A} 1}}-m_{\mathrm{A}} R_{\mathrm{g}, \mathrm{N}_{2}} \ln \frac{p_{\mathrm{A} 2}}{p_{\mathrm{A} 1}} \\ & =m_{\mathrm{A}}\left(c_{\mathrm{V}, \mathrm{N}_{2}}+R_{\mathrm{g}, \mathrm{N}_{2}}\right) \ln \frac{T_{\mathrm{A} 2}}{T_{\mathrm{A} 1}}-m_{\mathrm{A}} R_{\mathrm{g}, \mathrm{N}_{2}} \ln \frac{p_{\mathrm{A} 2}}{p_{\mathrm{A} 1}} \\ & =0.5615 \times\left[(0.7421+0.29684) \times \ln \frac{944.5}{300}-0.29684 \times \ln \frac{0.22}{0.1}\right] \\ & =0.5374 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{K} \end{aligned}$$
