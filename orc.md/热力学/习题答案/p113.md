$H_{\mathrm{m} 1} \approx H_{\mathrm{m} 2}$, 所以取 $T_{2}=155 \mathrm{~K}$.

由节流微分效应的公式 $\nu_{1}=\frac{T\left(\frac{\partial \nu}{\partial T}\right)_{p}-\nu}{c_{p}}=\frac{T^{2}\left[\frac{\partial}{\partial T}\left(\frac{\nu}{T}\right)\right]_{p}}{c_{p}}$

$\therefore\left[\frac{\partial}{\partial T}\left(\frac{\nu}{T}\right)\right]_{p}=\frac{\nu_{1} c_{p}}{T^{2}}$ 在定压下对 $T$ 积分

$\frac{\nu}{T}=\int \frac{\nu_{1}(p, T) c_{p}(p, T)}{T^{2}} \mathrm{~d} T+\varphi(p)$

在 $p \rightarrow 0$ 时，所有实际气体趋近于理想气体，对于理想气体，$\nu_{1}=0$

$\therefore \varphi(p)=\frac{R_{k}}{p}$，代入上式，证毕

其中

$T\left(\frac{\partial \nu}{\partial T}\right)_{p}-\nu=T^{2}\left[\frac{\left(\frac{\partial \nu}{\partial T}\right)_{p}-\frac{\nu}{T}}{T}\right]=T^{2}\left[\frac{\nu}{T}+T \frac{\partial}{\partial T}\left(\frac{\nu}{T}\right)-\frac{\nu}{T}\right]$

$=\mathrm{T}^{2}\left[\frac{\partial}{\partial \mathrm{T}}\left(\frac{\nu}{\mathrm{T}}\right)\right]_{p}$

令 $\frac{\nu}{T}=x, \nu=T x \quad \frac{\partial \nu}{\partial T}=x+T \frac{\partial x}{\partial T}=\frac{\nu}{T}+T \frac{\partial}{\partial T}\left(\frac{\nu}{T}\right)$
