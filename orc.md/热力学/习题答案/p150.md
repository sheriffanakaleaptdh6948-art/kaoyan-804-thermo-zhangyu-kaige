$\begin{aligned} & x_{2}=\frac{s_{2}-s_{1}^{\prime}}{s^{\prime}-s_{1}^{\prime}}=\frac{1.718-1.048}{1.722-1.048}=0.994 \\ & h_{2}=h^{\prime}+x_{2}\left(h^{\prime \prime}-h^{\prime}\right)=213.5+0.994 \times(404.3-213.5)=403.2 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \end{aligned}$

$h_{4}=h_{3}+w_{\mathrm{p}}=213.5+0.126=213.6 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$

$\quad w_{\text {net }}=w_{\mathrm{T}}-w_{\mathrm{p}}=6.6-0.126=6.474 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$
