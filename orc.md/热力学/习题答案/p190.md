13-4 解题过程 对于反应 $\mathrm{CO}+\frac{1}{2} \mathrm{O}_{2}=\mathrm{CO}_{2}$ 应用基尔霍夫定律有

$\begin{aligned}& Q_{\mathrm{T}}=\Delta H^{\circ}+\left[\sum n_{\mathrm{k}}\left(H_{\mathrm{m}, \mathrm{k}}-H_{\mathrm{m}, \mathrm{k}}^{0}\right)\right]_{\mathrm{pr}}-\left[\sum n_{\mathrm{j}}\left(H_{\mathrm{m}, \mathrm{j}}-H_{\mathrm{m}, \mathrm{j}}^{0}\right)\right]_{\mathrm{Re}} \\& \Delta H^{\circ}=\Delta H_{\mathrm{i}, \mathrm{CO}_{2}}^{\circ}-\Delta H_{\mathrm{i}, \mathrm{CO}}^{\circ}-\frac{1}{2} \Delta H_{\mathrm{i}, \mathrm{O}_{2}}^{\circ}\end{aligned}$

查附表有

$\begin{aligned}& \Delta H_{\mathrm{i}, \mathrm{CO}_{2}}^{\circ}=-393552 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{mol}, \Delta H_{\mathrm{i}, \mathrm{CO}}^{\circ}=-110527 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{mol} \\& \Delta H^{\circ}=1 \times[-393552-(-110527)=-0 \times \frac{1}{2}]=--283025 \mathrm{~J}\end{aligned}$

查气体热力性质表

$\begin{aligned}& H_{\mathrm{m}, \mathrm{CO}_{2}}-H_{\mathrm{m}, \mathrm{CO}_{2}}^{\circ}=100811.2-9624=91447.2 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{mol} \\& H_{\mathrm{m}, \mathrm{O}_{2}}-H_{\mathrm{m}, \mathrm{O}_{2}}^{\circ}=67857.5-8683=59174.5 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{mol} \\& H_{\mathrm{m}, \mathrm{CO}}-H_{\mathrm{m}, \mathrm{CO}}^{\circ}=65413.9-8671=56742.9 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{mol}\end{aligned}$

所以 $Q_{\mathrm{T}}=-283025+1 \times 91447.2-1 \times 56742.9-\frac{1}{2} \times 59174.5$

$=-277908 \mathrm{~J}$
