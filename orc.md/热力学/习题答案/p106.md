7-11 解题过程 查氨的热力性质表得, 当 $p=800 \mathrm{kPa} 、 T=293.15 \mathrm{~K}$ 时, 氨的参数为

$h_{1}=1485 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}, v_{2}=0.1614 \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{kg}$

由 $h_{1}+\frac{c_{\mathrm{t} 1}^{2}}{2}=h_{2}+\frac{c_{\mathrm{t} 2}^{2}}{2}, c_{\mathrm{t} 1}=0$ 可得

$h_{2}=h_{1}-\frac{c_{\mathrm{t} 2}^{2}}{2}=1485-\frac{450^{2}}{2} \times 10^{-3}=1387.75 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$

查氨的热力性质表得, 当 $p=300 \mathrm{kPa}$ 时, 氨的参数为:

$\begin{aligned} & h^{\prime}=157.5 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}, v^{\prime}=0.0015 \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{kg} \\ & h^{\prime \prime}=1451.6 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}, v^{\prime \prime}=0.4061 \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{kg} \end{aligned}$

因为 $h^{\prime}<h_{2}<h^{\prime \prime}$, 所以出口截面上氨为湿饱和蒸汽

$\begin{aligned} & x_{2}=\frac{h_{2}-h^{\prime}}{h^{\prime \prime}-h^{\prime}}=\frac{1387.75-157.5}{1451.6-157.5}=0.95 \\ & v_{2}=v^{\prime}+x_{2}\left(v^{\prime \prime}-v^{\prime}\right) \\ & \quad=0.0015+0.95 \times(0.4061-0.0015)=0.3861 \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{kg} \end{aligned}$
