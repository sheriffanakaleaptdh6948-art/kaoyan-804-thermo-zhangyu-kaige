(1) 由热平衡方程 $m_{\mathrm{g}} c_{\mathrm{p}}\left(t_{\mathrm{g}, 1}-t_{\mathrm{g}, 2}\right)=m\left(h_{2}-h_{1}\right)$，得

$$m_{\mathrm{g}}=\frac{m\left(h_{2}-h_{1}\right)}{c_{\mathrm{p}}\left(t_{\mathrm{g}, 1}-t_{\mathrm{g}, 2}\right)}=\frac{1 \times(3256-133.86)}{1.079 \times(1500-250)}=2.315 \mathrm{~kg}$$

(2) $\Delta S_{\mathrm{g}}=m_{\mathrm{g}} c_{\mathrm{p}} \ln \frac{T_{\mathrm{g}, 2}}{T_{\mathrm{g}, 1}}=2.315 \times 1.079 \times \ln \frac{523.15}{1773.15}=-3.0488 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{K}$
