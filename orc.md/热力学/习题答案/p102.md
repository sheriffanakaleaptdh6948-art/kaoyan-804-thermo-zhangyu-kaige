7-1 解题过程 由题意可知

$\begin{aligned}& T_{0}=T_{1}+\frac{c_{1}^{2}}{2 c_{p}} \\& T_{1}=T_{0}-\frac{c_{1}^{2}}{2 c_{p}}=\left(70+273.15\right)-\frac{180^{2}}{2 \times 1005}=327.03 \mathrm{~K}\end{aligned}$

所以 $t_{1}=53.88{ }^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$

7-2 解题过程 由题意可知

滞止温度 $T_{0}=T_{1}+\frac{c_{1}^{2}}{2 c_{p}}=800+\frac{600^{2}}{2 \times 1005}=979.1 \mathrm{~K}$

滞止压力 $p_{0}=p_{1}\left(\frac{T_{0}}{T_{1}}\right)^{\frac{k}{k-1}}=0.5 \times\left(\frac{979.1}{800}\right)^{\frac{1.4-1}{1.4-1}}=1.014 \mathrm{MPa}$

7-3 解题过程 由题意可知, 扩压段出口的温度

$\begin{aligned}& T_{2}=T_{1}+\frac{c_{1}^{2}-c_{2}^{2}}{2 c_{p}}=(273.15-5)+\frac{\left(\frac{900000}{3600}\right)^{2}-80^{2}}{2 \times 1005} \\& \text { 由连续性方程得, } q_{m}=\frac{A_{1} c_{11}}{v_{1}}=\frac{A_{2} c_{12}}{v_{2}}=\frac{0.8 A_{1} c_{12}}{v_{2}} \\& v_{2}=0.8 v_{1} \frac{c_{12}}{c_{11}}\end{aligned}$

即

$\frac{R_{s} T_{2}}{p_{2}}=0.8 \frac{R_{s} T_{1}}{p_{1}} \cdot \frac{c_{12}}{c_{11}}$

$p_{2}=\frac{1}{0.8} \cdot \frac{T_{2}}{T_{1}} \cdot \frac{c_{11}}{c_{12}} \cdot p_{1}=\frac{296.06 \times 250 \times 50}{0.8 \times 268.15 \times 80}=215.7 \mathrm{kPa}$

7-4 解题过程 由题意可知, $p_{\mathrm{cr}}=\nu_{\mathrm{cr}} p_{1}=0.528 \times 2=1.056 \mathrm{MPa}<p_{\mathrm{b}}=1.5 \mathrm{MPa}$

所以 $p_{2}=p_{\mathrm{b}}=1.5 \mathrm{MPa}$

$\begin{aligned}& T_{2}=T_{1}\left(\frac{p_{2}}{p_{1}}\right)^{\frac{k-1}{k}}=\left(27+273.15\right) \times\left(\frac{1.5}{2}\right)^{\frac{1.4-1}{1.4-1}}=276.47 \mathrm{~K} \\& t_{2}=3.32{ }^{\circ} \mathrm{C}\end{aligned}$
