6-9 解题过程

$\begin{aligned}dh &= T ds + v dp \\&\left( \frac{\partial h}{\partial s} \right)_T = T + v \left( \frac{\partial p}{\partial s} \right)_T = T + v \frac{1}{\left( \partial s / \partial p \right)_T} \\\text{据麦克斯韦关系} \left( \frac{\partial p}{\partial s} \right)_T &= - \left( \frac{\partial v}{\partial T} \right)_p \\\text{所以} \left( \frac{\partial h}{\partial s} \right)_T &= T - v \frac{1}{\left( \partial v / \partial t \right)_p} = T - v \frac{1}{v \alpha_v} = T - \frac{1}{\alpha_v} \\\text{证毕.}\end{aligned}$

6-10 解题过程

(1) 因为固液体的体积膨胀系数的数量级为 $10^{-5} \sim 10^{-4} K^{-1}$, 而固液体的等温压缩率 $\kappa_T$ 为 $10^{-11} Pa^{-1}$. 相差 6 到 7 个数量级, 故改变温度但维持固体或液体系统体积不变是很困难的.

(2) $v = v(p, T)$, 故 $dv = \left( \frac{\alpha v}{\partial T} \right)_p dT + \left( \frac{\alpha v}{\partial p} \right)_T dp$

由 $\alpha_v = \frac{1}{v} \left( \frac{\alpha v}{\partial T} \right)_p$, $\kappa_T = \frac{1}{v} \left( \frac{\alpha v}{\partial p} \right)_T$ 可知

$\begin{aligned}dv &= v \alpha_v dT - v \kappa_T dp \\\frac{dv}{v} &= \alpha_v dT - \kappa_T dp\end{aligned}$

积分得 $\ln \frac{v_2}{v_1} = \int_1^2 \alpha v \cdot dT - \int_1^2 \kappa_T dp = 0$

$\alpha_v (T_2 - T_1) = \kappa_T (p_2 - p_1)$

$p_2 = \frac{\alpha_v (T_2 - T_1)}{\kappa_T} + p_1 = \frac{80.8 \times 10^{-5} \times (120 - 99.634)}{4.93 \times 10^{-4}} + 0.1 = 33.4 MPa.$

6-11 解题过程

(1) 据热力学能的一般关系式

$d u = c_v dT + \left[ T \left( \frac{\partial p}{\partial T} \right)_v - p \right] dv$

对 $p(v-b) = R_g T$ 求导, 得 $\left( \frac{\partial p}{\partial T} \right)_v = \frac{R_g}{v-b}$

$\begin{gathered}T \left( \frac{\partial p}{\partial T} \right)_v - p = \frac{R_g T}{v-b} - p = p - p = 0 \\\text{即 } du = c_v dT\end{gathered}$
