$\begin{aligned} T_{4, \mathrm{a}} & =T_{3}\left(\frac{p_{4}}{p_{3}}\right)^{\frac{1-1}{k}}=T_{0}\left(\frac{p_{1}}{p_{2}}\right)^{\frac{1-1}{k}} \\ & =T_{0}\left(\frac{1}{\pi_{a}}\right)^{\frac{1-1}{k}}=300 \times\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{\frac{1.4-1}{1.4}} \\ & =219.18 \mathrm{~K} \end{aligned}$

$q_{\mathrm{c}, \mathrm{a}}=c_{\mathrm{p}}\left(T_{1}-T_{4, \mathrm{a}}\right)=1.005 \times(290-219.18)=71.2 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$

循环增压比为 6 时 $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{b}}=\frac{1}{\pi_{\mathrm{b}}^{\mathrm{k}}-1}=\frac{1}{6^{\frac{1-4-1}{1.4-1}}}=1.496$

$T_{4, \mathrm{~b}}=T_{0}\left(\frac{1}{\pi_{\mathrm{b}}}\right)^{\frac{1-1}{k}}=300 \times\left(\frac{1}{6}\right)^{\frac{1.4-1}{1.4}}=179.81 \mathrm{~K}$

$q_{\mathrm{c}, \mathrm{b}}=c_{\mathrm{p}}\left(T_{1}-T_{4, \mathrm{~b}}\right)=1.005 \times(290-179.81)=110.7 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$

(1) $\eta_{\mathrm{T}}=\frac{T_{3}-T_{4}^{\prime}}{T_{3}-T_{4}} \Rightarrow T_{4}^{\prime}=T_{3}-\eta_{\mathrm{T}}\left(T_{3}-T_{4}\right)$

即 $T_{4, \mathrm{a}}^{\prime}=300-0.85 \times(300-219.18)=231.303 \mathrm{~K}$

$T_{4, \mathrm{~b}}^{\prime}=300-0.85 \times(300-179.81)=197.8385 \mathrm{~K}$

$\eta_{\mathrm{c}, \mathrm{s}}=\frac{T_{2}-T_{1}}{T_{2}-T_{1}}$

即 $T_{2}^{\prime}=\frac{T_{2}-T_{1}}{\eta_{\mathrm{c}, \mathrm{s}}-1}+T_{1}$

又 $T_{2, \mathrm{a}}=T_{1} \cdot \pi_{\mathrm{a}}^{\frac{\mathrm{k}-1}{k}}=290 \times 3^{\frac{1.4-1}{1.4}}=396.93 \mathrm{~K}$

$T_{2, \mathrm{~b}}=T_{1} \cdot \pi_{\mathrm{b}}^{\frac{\mathrm{k}-1}{k}}=290 \times 6^{\frac{1.4-1}{1.4}}=483.96 \mathrm{~K}$

所以 $T_{2, \mathrm{a}}^{\prime}=\frac{T_{2, \mathrm{a}}-T_{1}}{\eta_{\mathrm{c}, \mathrm{s}}}+T_{1}=\frac{396.93-290}{0.82}+290=420.4 \mathrm{~K}$

$T_{2, \mathrm{~b}}^{\prime}=\frac{T_{2, \mathrm{~b}}-T_{1}}{\eta_{\mathrm{c}, \mathrm{s}}}+T_{1}=\frac{483.86-290}{0.82}+290=526.41 \mathrm{~K}$
