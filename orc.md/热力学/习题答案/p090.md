(2) 查表, 氮气的范德瓦尔常数

$a=0.1361 \mathrm{~m}^{6} \cdot \mathrm{Pa} \cdot \mathrm{mol}^{-2}, b=3.85 \times 10^{-5} \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{mol}$

$\left(p+\frac{a}{V_{\mathrm{m}}^{2}}\right)\left(V_{\mathrm{m}}-b\right)=R T$

$\left(16.21 \times 10^{6}+\frac{0.1361}{V_{\mathrm{m}}^{2}}\right)\left(V_{\mathrm{m}}-3.85 \times 10^{-5}\right)=8.3145 \times 189$

解得 $V_{\mathrm{m}}=0.081 \times 10^{-3} \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{mol}$

$m=\frac{V}{V_{\mathrm{m}}} M=\frac{0.425}{0.081} \times 28.01 \times 10^{-3}=147 \mathrm{~kg}$

(3) 查附表得氮气的临界参数为 $T_{\mathrm{cr}}=126.2 \mathrm{~K}, p_{\mathrm{cr}}=3.39 \mathrm{MPa}$

$T_{\mathrm{r}}=\frac{189}{126.2}=1.5, p_{\mathrm{r}}=\frac{16.21}{3.39}=4.78$

查通用压缩因子图得 $Z=0.84$

$m=\frac{p V}{Z R T} M=\frac{16.21 \times 10^{6} \times 0.425}{0.84 \times 8.3145 \times 189} \times 28.01 \times 10^{-3}=146.2 \mathrm{~kg}$

(4) 利用 $R-K$ 方程

$a=\frac{0.427480 R^{2} T_{\mathrm{cr}}^{2.5}}{p_{\mathrm{cr}}}=\frac{0.42748 \times 8.3145^{2} \times 126.2^{2.5}}{3.39 \times 10^{6}}$

$=0.13864 \mathrm{~Pa} \cdot \mathrm{m}^{6} \cdot \mathrm{K}^{1 / 2} \cdot \mathrm{mol}^{-2}$

$b=\frac{0.08664 R T_{\mathrm{cr}}}{p_{\mathrm{cr}}}=\frac{0.08664 \times 8.3145 \times 126.2}{3.39 \times 10^{6}}$

$=0.0268 \times 10^{-3} \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{mol}$

将 $a, b$ 值代入 $R-K$ 方程

$p=\frac{R T}{V_{\mathrm{m}}-b}-\frac{a}{T^{0.5} V_{\mathrm{m}}\left(V_{\mathrm{m}}+b\right)}$

$=\frac{8.3145 \times 189}{V_{\mathrm{m}}-0.0268 \times 10^{-3}}-\frac{0.13864}{189^{0.5} V_{\mathrm{m}}\left(V_{\mathrm{m}}+0.0268 \times 10^{-3}\right)}$

迭代后解得 $V_{\mathrm{m}}=0.080238 \times 10^{-3} \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{mol}$

$m=\frac{V}{V_{\mathrm{m}}} M=\frac{0.425}{0.080238 \times 10^{-3}} \times 28.01 \times 10^{-3}=148.84 \mathrm{~kg}$
