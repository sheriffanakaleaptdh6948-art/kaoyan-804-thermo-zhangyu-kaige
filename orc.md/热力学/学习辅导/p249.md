解 由题意, 如图 9-25 所示, 状态 1: \(p_{1}=1500 \mathrm{kPa} 、 T_{1}=1250 \mathrm{~K}\), 查空气热力性质表, \(h_{1}=\) \(1388.39 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg} 、 p_{\mathrm{rl}}=285.98\) 。

状态 2:

$\begin{aligned} & \frac{p_{r, 2}}{p_{r, 1}}=\frac{p_{2}}{p_{1}} \\ & p_{r, 2}=p_{r, 1} \frac{p_{2}}{p_{1}}=285.98 \times \frac{250 \mathrm{kPa}}{1500 \mathrm{kPa}}=47.663 \end{aligned}$

由 \(p_{r, 2}\) 查表, \(T_{2}\) 介于 790 K 和 800 K 之间, 所以

$\begin{aligned} T_{2} & =790 \mathrm{~K}+\frac{47.663-46.288}{48.568-46.288} \times(800 \mathrm{~K}-790 \mathrm{~K}) \\ & =796.03 \mathrm{~K} \\ h_{2} & =812.97 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}+\frac{823.94 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}-812.97 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}}{800 \mathrm{~K}-790 \mathrm{~K}} \times(796.03 \mathrm{~K}-790 \mathrm{~K}) \\ & =820.10 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \\ h_{2} & =h_{1}-\eta_{\mathrm{T}}\left(h_{1}-h_{2}\right) \\ & =1388.39 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}-0.85 \times(1388.39 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}-820.10 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg})=905.34 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \end{aligned}$

由 \(h_{2}\) 查表, \(T_{2}\) 介于 870 K 和 880 K 之间, 则

$\begin{aligned} T_{2} & =870 \mathrm{~K}+\frac{905.34 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}-901.39 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}}{912.54 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}-901.39 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}} \times(880 \mathrm{~K}-870 \mathrm{~K})=873.54 \mathrm{~K} \\ p_{r, 2} & =67.107+\frac{905.34 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}-901.39 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}}{912.54 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}-901.39 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}} \times(70.155-67.107)=68.187 \end{aligned}$

状态 3:

$\begin{gathered} \frac{p_{r, 3}}{p_{r, 2}}=\frac{p_{3}}{P_{2}} \\ p_{r, 3}=p_{r, 2} \frac{p_{3}}{p_{2}}=68.187 \times \frac{100 \mathrm{kPa}}{250 \mathrm{kPa}}=27.275 \end{gathered}$

所以

$\begin{aligned} T_{3} & =680 \mathrm{~K}+\frac{27.275-26.291}{27.763-26.291} \times(690 \mathrm{~K}-680 \mathrm{~K})=686.68 \mathrm{~K} \\ h_{3} & =693.84 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}+\frac{27.275-26.291}{27.763-26.291} \times(704.55-693.84) \mathrm{kJ} / \mathrm{kg}=701.00 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \end{aligned}$

$c_{\mathrm{f}, 3}=\sqrt{2\left(h_{2}-h_{3}\right)}=\sqrt{2 \times(905.34-701.00) \mathrm{kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \times 1000}=639.28 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$

$c_{\mathrm{f}, 3}=c_{\mathrm{f}, 3} \varphi=639.28 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s} \times 0.95=607.32 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$

$h_{3}=h_{3},+\frac{1}{2}\left(c_{\mathrm{f}, 2}^{2}-c_{\mathrm{f}, 2}^{2}\right)$
