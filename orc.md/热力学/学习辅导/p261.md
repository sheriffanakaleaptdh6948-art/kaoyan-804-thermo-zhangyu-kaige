由 $10 \mathrm{kPa}$, 查饱和水蒸气表, 得

$h^{\prime \prime}=2583.7 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}, h^{\prime}=191.7 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}, s^{\prime \prime}=8.149 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}), s^{\prime}=0.649 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})$

据 $s_{1}=s_{2}, s^{\prime}<s_{2}<s^{\prime \prime}$, 所以状态 2 为饱和湿蒸汽状态

$x_{2}=\frac{s_{2}-s_{2}^{\prime}}{s^{\prime \prime}-s_{2}^{\prime}}=\frac{5.794 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})-0.649 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})}{8.149 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})-0.649 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})}=0.686$

$h_{2}=h^{\prime}+x_{2}\left(h^{\prime \prime}-h^{\prime}\right)$

$=191.7 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}+0.686 \times(2583.7 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}-191.7 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg})=1832.6 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$

状态 3:

$h_{3}=191.7 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}, v_{3}=0.0010 \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{kg}$

状态 4:

$\begin{aligned} & s_{3}=s_{4} \\ & h_{4} \approx h_{3}+v_{3}\left(p_{4}-p_{3}\right) \\ & =191.7 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}+0.0010 \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{kg} \times(30000 \mathrm{kPa}-10 \mathrm{kPa})=221.7 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \end{aligned}$

汽轮机输出功

$w_{1}=h_{1}-h_{2}=3083.5 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}-1832.6 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}=1250.9 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$

水泵耗功

$w_{\mathrm{p}}=h_{4}-h_{3}=221.7 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}-191.7 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}=30.0 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$

从锅炉吸热量

$q_{1}=h_{1}-h_{4}=3083.5 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}-221.7 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}=2861.8 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$

冷凝器中放热量

$q_{2}=h_{2}-h_{3}=1832.6 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}-191.7 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}=1640.9 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$

循环热效率

$\eta_{1}=\frac{w_{\text {net }}}{q_{1}}=\frac{w_{1}-w_{\mathrm{P}}}{q_{1}}=\frac{1250.9 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}-30.0 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}}{2861.8 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}}=0.427$

或

$\eta_{\mathrm{t}}=1-\frac{q_{2}}{q_{1}}=1-\frac{1640.9 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}}{2861.8 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}}=0.427$

点评 超临界朗肯循环虽然没有回热等措施, 但由于使蒸汽发生器内加热面两侧流体(烟气和水蒸气)的温差最小化, 减小了锅炉内不等温传热的不可逆损失, 使燃料的能量得到合理应用, 热效率也有较大提高。
