$\begin{aligned}& T_{2}=T_{0}-\frac{c_{12}^{2}}{2 c_{p}}=1077.3 \mathrm{~K}=\frac{(580.46 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s})^{2}}{2 \times 1159 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})}=931.94 \mathrm{~K} \\& v_{2}=\frac{R_{\mathrm{g}} T_{2}}{p_{2}}=\frac{318.3 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times 931.94 \mathrm{~K}}{0.324 \times 10^{6} \mathrm{~Pa}}=0.9155 \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{kg} \\& q_{m}^{\prime}=\frac{A_{2} c_{12}}{v_{2}}=\frac{2400 \times 10^{-6} \mathrm{~m}^{2} \times 580.46 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}}{0.9155 \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{kg}}=1.522 \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{s}\end{aligned}$

由于流动过程不可逆绝热，所以过程的熵增即是熵产

$\begin{aligned}& s_{\mathrm{g}}=\Delta s_{1-2}=\Delta s_{2 \mathrm{i}-2}=c_{p} \ln \frac{T_{2}}{T_{2 \mathrm{i}}} \\& =1.159 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times \ln \frac{931.94 \mathrm{~K}}{905.77 \mathrm{~K}}=0.033 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})\end{aligned}$
