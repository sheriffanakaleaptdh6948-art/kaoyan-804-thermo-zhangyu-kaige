$s^{\prime \prime}=8.149 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}), s^{\prime}=0.649 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})$

$s^{\prime}<s_{2}=s_{1}<s^{\prime \prime}$, 所以状态 2 为饱和湿蒸汽状态

$\begin{aligned} & x_{2}=\frac{s_{2}-s^{\prime}}{s^{\prime \prime}-s^{\prime}}=\frac{6.921 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})-0.649 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})}{8.149 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})-0.649 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})}=0.836 \\ & h_{2}=h^{\prime}+x_{2}\left(h^{\prime \prime}-h^{\prime}\right) \\ & =191.7 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}+0.836 \times(2583.7-191.7) \mathrm{kJ} / \mathrm{kg}=2191.4 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \end{aligned}$

状态 3:

$h_{3}=191.7 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}, v_{3}=0.0010 \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{kg}$

抽汽量

$\alpha=\frac{h_{01}-h_{3}}{h_{01}-h_{3}}=\frac{721.1 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}-191.7 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}}{2890.1 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}-191.7 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}}=0.1962$

汽轮机输出功

$\begin{aligned} w_{1} & =h_{1}-h_{01}+(1-\alpha)\left(h_{01}-h_{2}\right) \\ & =3230.7 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}-2890.1 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}+(1-0.1962) \times(2890.1-2191.4) \mathrm{kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \\ & =902.2 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \end{aligned}$

从锅炉吸热量

$q_{1}=h_{1}-h_{01}=3230.7 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}-721.1 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}=2509.6 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$

冷凝器中放热量

$q_{2}=(1-\alpha)\left(h_{2}-h_{3}\right)=(1-0.1962) \times(2191.4-191.7) \mathrm{kJ} / \mathrm{kg}=1607.4 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$

循环热效率

或

$\eta_{1}=\frac{w_{\text {net }}}{q_{1}}=\frac{w_{1}}{q_{1}}=\frac{902.2 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}}{2509.6 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}}=0.360$

$\eta_{1}=1-\frac{q_{2}}{q_{1}}=1-\frac{1607.4 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}}{2509.6 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}}=0.36$

点

采用抽汽回热后, 放热温度不变, 仍是 $p_{2}$ 的饱和温度, 但是平均吸热温度由于加热过程的起点从 3 移到 $0_{1}^{\prime}$ 而提高, 所以循环热效率提高。请考虑下面的分析错在哪里: 从图 10-10, 循环吸热量可用面积 $0_{1}^{\prime} 1 \mathrm{mn} 0_{1}^{\prime}$ 表示, 比原循环的吸热量 (面积 $31 \mathrm{ml} 3$ ) 减小, 但放热量 (面积 $2 \mathrm{ml} 32$ )不变, 所以, 据 $\eta_{1}=1-q_{2} / q_{1}$, 热效率应减小。提示: 抽汽后, $q_{2}$ 是否仍可用面积 $2 \mathrm{ml} 32$ 表示?
