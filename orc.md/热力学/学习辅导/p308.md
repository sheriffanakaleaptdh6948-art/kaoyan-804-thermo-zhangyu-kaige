解 取容器内全部气体为系统。

因 $V_{\mathrm{A}}=V_{\mathrm{B}}=T_{\mathrm{A}}=T_{\mathrm{B}}$, 且均为 $1 \mathrm{~mol}$, 所以

混合前

$p_{\mathrm{A} 1}=\frac{R T_{\mathrm{A}}}{V_{\mathrm{A}}} p_{\mathrm{B} 1}=\frac{R T_{\mathrm{B}}}{V_{\mathrm{B}}}$

混合后

$\begin{aligned} & n=n_{\mathrm{O}_{2}}+n_{\mathrm{N}_{2}}=1 \mathrm{~mol}+1 \mathrm{~mol}=2 \mathrm{~mol} \\ & x_{\mathrm{O}_{2}}=\frac{1 \mathrm{~mol}}{2 \mathrm{~mol}}=\frac{1}{2}, x_{\mathrm{N}_{2}}=\frac{1 \mathrm{~mol}}{2 \mathrm{~mol}}=\frac{1}{2} \\ & p_{2}=\frac{n R T_{2}}{V_{2}}=\frac{2 R T_{2}}{2 V_{\mathrm{A}}}=\frac{R T_{\mathrm{A}}}{V_{\mathrm{A}}}=p_{\mathrm{A} 1}=p_{\mathrm{B} 1} \\ & p_{\mathrm{O}_{2}, 2}=x_{\mathrm{O}_{2}} p_{2}=\frac{1}{2} p_{\mathrm{A} 1}, p_{\mathrm{N}_{2}, 2}=x_{\mathrm{N}_{2}} p_{2}=\frac{1}{2} p_{\mathrm{B} 1} \end{aligned}$

取混合前气体状态 $\left(p_{\mathrm{A} 1}, T_{\mathrm{A}}\right)$ 为参考状态, 则 $\mathrm{O}_{2}$ 及 $\mathrm{N}_{2}$ 终态的熵值即为从参考状态到终态的熵变, 所以, 据

$\mathrm{dS}_{\mathrm{m}}=\sum x_{i} C_{p, \mathrm{~m}, i} \frac{\mathrm{dT}}{T}-\sum x_{i} R \frac{\mathrm{dp}_{i}}{p_{i}}=-R \sum x_{i} \frac{\mathrm{dp}_{i}}{p_{i}}$

系统的熵变

$\begin{aligned} \Delta S & =n \Delta S_{\mathrm{m}}=2\left(-R \sum x_{i} \ln \frac{p_{i 2}}{p_{i 1}}\right) \\ & =-2 R\left[\frac{1}{2} \ln \frac{p_{\mathrm{O}_{2}, 2}}{p_{\mathrm{A} 1}}+\frac{1}{2} \ln \frac{p_{\mathrm{N}_{2}, 2}}{p_{\mathrm{B} 1}}\right] = -2 R \ln \frac{1}{2} = 2 R \ln 2 > 0 \end{aligned}$
