$\Delta T=\left(\frac{\partial T}{\partial p}\right)_{s} \Delta p$

由循环关系 $\left(\frac{\partial T}{\partial p}\right)_{s}\left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial s}\right)_{T}\left(\frac{\partial s}{\partial T}\right)_{p}=-1$, 并注意到 $c_{p}=\left(\frac{\partial q}{\mathrm{~d} T}\right)_{p}=T\left(\frac{\partial s}{\partial T}\right)_{p}$, 则有

$\begin{gathered} \left(\frac{\partial T}{\partial p}\right)_{s}=-\frac{\left(\frac{\partial s}{\partial p}\right)_{T}}{\left(\frac{\partial s}{\partial T}\right)_{p}}=-\frac{T}{c_{p}}\left(\frac{\partial s}{\partial p}\right)_{T} \end{gathered}$

据麦克斯韦关系 $\left(\frac{\partial T}{\partial v}\right)_{s}=-\left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial s}\right)_{v}$, 故

$\begin{gathered} \left(\frac{\partial T}{\partial p}\right)_{s}=-\frac{T}{c_{p}}\left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial T}\right)_{p}=-\frac{T \alpha_{v} v}{c_{p}} \end{gathered}$

所以

$\begin{aligned} \Delta T & =-\frac{T \alpha_{v} v}{c_{p}} \Delta p=-\frac{T p g \alpha_{v} v}{c_{p}} \Delta H=-\frac{T g \alpha_{v}}{c_{p}} \Delta H \\ & =\frac{300 \mathrm{~K} \times 9.81 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}^{2} \times 1.3 \times 10^{-4} \mathrm{~K}^{-1} \times 1000 \mathrm{~m}}{4.187 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})}=0.091 \mathrm{~K} \end{aligned}$

6-11 在某温度时可逆恒温地将作用在质量为 $m$ 的水面上的压力由 $p_{1}$ 提高到 $p_{2}$, 试求该过程的传热量、功, 假定该温度下水的体积膨胀系数 $\alpha_{v}$ 和等温压缩系数 $\kappa_{T}$ 为常数。

解 据定义

$\kappa_{T}=-\frac{1}{v}\left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial p}\right)_{T}$

所以恒温时

积分得

$\frac{\mathrm{d} v}{v}=-\kappa_{T} \mathrm{~d} p$

$v=\mathrm{e}^{-\kappa_{T}\left(p-p_{1}\right)}$

过程可逆, 故 $\delta Q=T \mathrm{~d} S=m T \mathrm{~d} s$, 而 $\mathrm{d} s=\left(\frac{\partial s}{\partial T}\right)_{p} \mathrm{~dT}+\left(\frac{\partial s}{\partial p}\right)_{T} \mathrm{~dp}$

因

$\left(\frac{\partial s}{\partial T}\right)_{p}=\frac{c_{p}}{T},\left(\frac{\partial s}{\partial p}\right)_{T}=\left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial T}\right)_{p}=-\alpha_{v} v$

所以

$\mathrm{d} s=\frac{c_{p} \mathrm{~dT}}{T}-\alpha_{v} v \mathrm{~d} p$
