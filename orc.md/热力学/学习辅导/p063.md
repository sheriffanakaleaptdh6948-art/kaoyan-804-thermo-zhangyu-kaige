解 (1) 使用真实热容经验式

由数据表查得 CO2 的摩尔定压热容为

$C_{p, \mathrm{~m}} / R=2.401+8.735 \times 10^{-3} T-6.607 \times 10^{-6} T^{2}+2.002 \times 10^{-9} T^{3}$

$\Delta h=\frac{R}{M} \int_{T_{1}}^{T_{2}} \frac{C_{p, \mathrm{~m}}}{R} \mathrm{~d} T$

$\begin{aligned} & =\frac{R}{M}\left[2.401 \times\left(T_{2}-T_{1}\right)+\frac{8.735 \times 10^{-3}}{2} \times\left(T_{2}^{2}-T_{1}^{2}\right)-\frac{6.607 \times 10^{-6}}{3} \times\right. \\ & \left.\quad\left(T_{2}^{3}-T_{1}^{3}\right)+\frac{2.002 \times 10^{-9}}{4} \times\left(T_{2}^{4}-T_{1}^{4}\right)\right] \\ & =\frac{8.3145 \times 10^{-3} \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{mol} \cdot \mathrm{K})}{44.01 \times 10^{-3} \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{mol}} \times\{2.401 \times(366 \mathrm{~K}-600 \mathrm{~K})+\end{aligned}$

$\begin{aligned} & \frac{8.735 \times 10^{-3}}{2} \times\left[(366 \mathrm{~K})^{2}-(600 \mathrm{~K})^{2}\right]-\frac{6.607 \times 10^{-6}}{3} \times\left[(366 \mathrm{~K})^{3}-\right. \\ & \left.(600 \mathrm{~K})^{3}\right]+\frac{2.002 \times 10^{-9}}{4} \times(366 \mathrm{~K})^{4}-(600 \mathrm{~K})^{4}]=-233.74 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \end{aligned}$

$\Delta u=\Delta h-R_{\mathrm{g}} \Delta T$

$=-233.74 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})-0.1889 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times(366 \mathrm{~K}-600 \mathrm{~K})$

$=-189.54 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$

$\Delta s=\frac{R}{M} \int_{T_{1}}^{T_{2}} \frac{C_{p, \mathrm{~m}}}{R T} \mathrm{~d} T-R_{\mathrm{g}} \ln \frac{p_{2}}{p_{1}}=\frac{R}{M} \int_{T_{1}}^{T_{2}} \frac{C_{p, \mathrm{~m}}}{R} \frac{\mathrm{d} T}{T}$

$\begin{aligned} = & \frac{8.3145 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{mol} \cdot \mathrm{K})}{44.01 \times 10^{-3} \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{mol}} \times\left\{2.401 \times \ln \frac{366 \mathrm{~K}}{600 \mathrm{~K}}+8.735 \times 10^{-3} \times\right. \\ & \left.(366 \mathrm{~K}-600 \mathrm{~K})-\frac{6.607 \times 10^{-6}}{2} \times\left[(366 \mathrm{~K})^{2}-(600 \mathrm{~K})^{2}\right]+\right. \\ & \left.\frac{2.002 \times 10^{-9}}{3} \times\left[(366 \mathrm{~K})^{3}-(600 \mathrm{~K})^{3}\right]\right\} \end{aligned}$

$=-0.4903 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$

(2) 使用比热容算术平均值

由数据表查得 $c_{p 1}=1.075 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}), c_{v 1}=0.886 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})$

$c_{p 2}=0.90908 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}), c_{v 2}=0.72018 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})$

$T_{1}$ 到 $T_{2}$ 之间的比热容算术平均值为

$c_{p, \mathrm{av}}=\frac{c_{p 1}+c_{p 2}}{2}=\frac{1.075 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})+0.90908 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})}{2}=0.99204 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})$

$c_{v, \mathrm{av}}=\frac{c_{v 1}+c_{v 2}}{2}=\frac{0.886 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})+0.72018 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})}{2}=0.80309 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})$
