9-2 某理想回热的燃气轮机定压加热理想循环 (图 9-23) 的最高温度为 $1100\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}$, 压气机进气温度为 $20\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}$, 进气压力 $0.1\,\mathrm{MPa}$, 压气机中的压比 $\pi=12$, 若循环最高温度不变, 空气比热容可取定值, 求: (1) 每千克工质在压气机内消耗的功、在燃气轮机产生的功、循环的热效率; (2) 压力比为多少时回热不能进行?

解 (1) 状态 2:

$\begin{aligned}T_{2} & =T_{1}\left(\frac{p_{2}}{p_{1}}\right)^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}}=T_{1} \pi^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}} \\& =(20+273.15) \mathrm{K} \times 12^{\frac{1.4-1}{1.4}} \\& =596.23 \mathrm{~K}=303.1\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}\end{aligned}$

状态 4:

$\begin{aligned}T_{4} & =T_{3}\left(\frac{p_{4}}{p_{3}}\right)^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}}=T_{3}\left(\frac{p_{1}}{p_{2}}\right)^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}}=T_{3}\left(\frac{1}{\pi}\right)^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}} \\& =(1100+273.15) \mathrm{K} \times\left(\frac{1}{12}\right)^{\frac{1.4-1}{1.4}} \\& =675.14 \mathrm{~K}=401.2\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}\end{aligned}$

压气机耗功

$\begin{aligned}w_{\mathrm{C}} & =h_{2}-h_{1}=c_{p}\left(T_{2}-T_{1}\right) \\& =1.005 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times(596.23 \mathrm{~K}-293.15 \mathrm{~K})=304.60 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}\end{aligned}$

气轮机输出功

$\begin{aligned}w_{\mathrm{T}} & =h_{3}-h_{4}=c_{p}\left(T_{3}-T_{4}\right) \\& =1.005 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times(1373.15 \mathrm{~K}-675.14 \mathrm{~K})=701.5 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}\end{aligned}$

循环吸热

$\begin{aligned}q_{1} & =q_{m}\left(h_{3}-h_{2}\right)=c_{p}\left(T_{3}-T_{5}\right)=c_{p}\left(T_{3}-T_{4}\right) \\& =1.005 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times(1375.15 \mathrm{~K}-675.14 \mathrm{~K})=703.5 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}\end{aligned}$

循环效率

$\eta_{1}=\frac{w_{\mathrm{T}}-w_{\mathrm{C}}}{q_{1}}=\frac{701.5 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}-304.6 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}}{703.5 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}}=0.564$

(2) 提高压比后循环 $12^{\prime} 3^{\prime} 4^{\prime} 1^{\prime}$, 若 $T_{2^{\prime}}=T_{4^{\prime}}$, 则回热器将失效, 所以循环压力比不超过

$T_{2^{\prime}}=T_{1} \pi^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}}=T_{4^{\prime}}=T_{3}\left(\frac{1}{\pi}\right)^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}}$
