解 由题意, 如图 8-11 所示,

状态 $1: p_{1}=100 \mathrm{kPa}, T_{1}=290 \mathrm{~K}$

状态 $2: p_{2}=500 \mathrm{kPa}, \pi_{1}=\frac{p_{2}}{p_{1}}=\frac{500 \mathrm{kPa}}{100 \mathrm{kPa}}=5$

$T_{2}=T_{1}\left(\frac{p_{2}}{p_{1}}\right)^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}}=T_{1} \pi_{1}^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}}=290 \mathrm{~K} \times 5^{\frac{1.4-1}{1.4}}=459.30 \mathrm{~K}$

状态 $3: p_{3}=500 \mathrm{kPa}, T_{3}=300 \mathrm{~K}$

状态 $4: p_{4}=1000 \mathrm{kPa}, \pi_{\mathrm{h}}=\frac{p_{4}}{p_{3}}=\frac{1000 \mathrm{kPa}}{500 \mathrm{kPa}}=2$

$T_{4}=T_{3}\left(\frac{p_{4}}{p_{3}}\right)^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}}=T_{1} \pi_{\mathrm{h}}^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}}=300 \mathrm{~K} \times 2^{\frac{1.4-1}{1.4}}=365.70 \mathrm{~K}$

状态 $5: p_{5}=1000 \mathrm{kPa}$

$T_{5}=T_{1}\left(\frac{p_{5}}{p_{1}}\right)^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}}=290 \mathrm{~K} \times\left(\frac{1000 \mathrm{kPa}}{100 \mathrm{kPa}}\right)^{\frac{1.4-1}{1.4}}=559.88 \mathrm{~K}$

两级压缩耗功

$w_{\mathrm{C}}=\left(h_{2}-h_{1}\right)+\left(h_{4}-h_{3}\right)=c_{p}\left[\left(T_{2}-T_{1}\right)+\left(T_{4}-T_{3}\right)\right]$

$=1.005 \mathrm{kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times[(459.30 \mathrm{~K}-290 \mathrm{~K})+(365.70 \mathrm{~K}-300 \mathrm{~K})]$

$=236.2 \mathrm{kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$

若一级压缩耗功:

$w_{\mathrm{C}}^{\prime}=h_{5}-h_{1}=c_{p}\left(T_{5}-T_{1}\right)=1.005 \mathrm{kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times(559.88 \mathrm{~K}-290 \mathrm{~K})=271.2 \mathrm{kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$

本例中两级压缩中间冷却耗功比一级压缩少耗功

$\Delta w_{\mathrm{C}}=w_{\mathrm{C}}^{\prime}-w_{\mathrm{C}}=271.2 \mathrm{kJ} / \mathrm{kg}-236.2 \mathrm{kJ} / \mathrm{kg}=35 \mathrm{kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$
