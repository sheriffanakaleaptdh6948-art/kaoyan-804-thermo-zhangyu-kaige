解 据题意, $T_{1}=T_{C}=253.15 \mathrm{~K}, T_{3}=T_{0}=293.15 \mathrm{~K}$

$\pi=\frac{p_{2}}{p_{1}}=\frac{0.5 \mathrm{MPa}}{0.1 \mathrm{MPa}}=5, \frac{T_{2}}{T_{1}}=\left(\frac{p_{2}}{p_{1}}\right)^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}}=\frac{T_{3}}{T_{4}}$

故

$\begin{aligned} & T_{2}=T_{1} \pi^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}}=253.15 \mathrm{~K} \times 5^{\frac{1.4-1}{1.4}}=401.13 \mathrm{~K} \\ & T_{4}=T_{3}\left(\frac{1}{\pi}\right)^{-\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}}=293.15 \mathrm{~K} \times\left(\frac{1}{5}\right)^{-\frac{1.4-1}{1.4}}=185.01 \mathrm{~K} \end{aligned}$

压缩机耗功为

$\begin{aligned} w_{\mathrm{C}} & =h_{2}-h_{1}=c_{p}\left(T_{2}-T_{1}\right) \\ & =1.005 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times(401.13 \mathrm{~K}-253.15 \mathrm{~K})=148.72 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \end{aligned}$

膨胀机作出的功为

$\begin{aligned} w_{\mathrm{T}} & =h_{3}-h_{4}=c_{p}\left(T_{3}-T_{4}\right) \\ & =1.005 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times(293.15 \mathrm{~K}-185.01 \mathrm{~K})=108.68 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \end{aligned}$

空气在冷却器中放热量为

$\begin{aligned} q_{0} & =h_{2}-h_{3}=c_{p}\left(T_{2}-T_{3}\right) \\ & =1.005 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times(401.13 \mathrm{~K}-293.15 \mathrm{~K})=108.52 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \end{aligned}$

1 kg 空气在冷库中的吸热量即为每 kg 空气的制冷量为

$q_{\mathrm{C}}=h_{1}-h_{4}=c_{p}\left(T_{1}-T_{4}\right)$

$=1.005 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times(253.15 \mathrm{~K}-185.01 \mathrm{~K})=68.48 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$

循环输入的净功为

$w_{\text {net }}=w_{\mathrm{C}}-w_{\mathrm{T}}=148.72 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}-108.68 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}=40.04 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$

循环的净热量为

$q_{\text {net }}=q_{0}-q_{\mathrm{C}}=108.52 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}-68.48 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}=40.04 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$

循环的制冷系数为

$\varepsilon=\frac{q_{\mathrm{C}}}{w_{\text {net }}}=\frac{68.48 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}}{40.04 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}}=1.71$

点评 从计算结果可以看到, 压缩空气制冷循环 $1 \mathrm{~kg}$ 空气的制冷量较小,循环的制冷系数也不大。
