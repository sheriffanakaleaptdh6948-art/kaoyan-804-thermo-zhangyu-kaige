$\kappa=\frac{c_{p}}{c_{V}}=\frac{1.03 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})}{0.743 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})}=1.386$
