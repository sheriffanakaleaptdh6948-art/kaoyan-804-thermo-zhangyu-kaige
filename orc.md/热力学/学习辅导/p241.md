【例 9-6】某活塞式汽油机的进气压力 95 kPa，进气温度 300 K，循环的压缩比为 8，燃烧过程中燃料向 1 kg 工质放热 1073 kJ，若循环可视为定容加热理想循环，工质为空气，由于循环中温度变化较大，比热容不宜取常数，求：(1) 循环的最高温度和最高压力；(2) 循环的热效率。

解 如图 9-16 所示，状态 1：由 \( p_1 = 95 \) kPa、\( T_1 = 300 \) K 查空气热力性质表，得

$$ h_1 = 302.29 \, \text{kJ/kg}_\text{s}, p_{r1} = 1.410 \, 8, v_{r1} = 212.65 $$

$$ u_1 = h_1 - p_1 v_1 = h_1 - R_s T_1 $$

$$ = 302.29 \, \text{kJ/kg} - 0.287 \, \text{kJ/(kg·K)} \times 300 \, \text{K} = 216.19 \, \text{kJ/kg} $$

据理想气体状态方程式

$$ v_1 = \frac{R_g T_1}{p_1} = \frac{287 \, \text{J/(kg·K)} \times 300 \, \text{K}}{95 \times 1000 \, \text{Pa}} = 0.906 \, 3 \, \text{m}^3/\text{kg} $$

状态 2：

$$ v_2 = \frac{v_1}{\varepsilon} = \frac{0.906 \, 3 \, \text{m}^3/\text{kg}}{\varepsilon} = 0.113 \, 3 \, \text{m}^3/\text{kg} $$

$$ v_{r2} = v_{r1} \frac{v_2}{v_1} = v_{r1} \frac{1}{\varepsilon} = 212.65 \times \frac{1}{8} = 26.581 $$

查空气热力性质表

$$ T_2 = 670 \, \text{K} + \frac{26.581 - 26.929}{25.864 - 26.929} \times (680 \, \text{K} - 670 \, \text{K}) = 673.27 \, \text{K} $$

$$ p_2 = \frac{R_g T_2}{v_2} = \frac{287 \, \text{J/(kg·K)} \times 673.27 \, \text{K}}{0.113 \, 3 \, \text{m}^3/\text{kg}} = 1705.5 \, \text{kPa} $$

$$ h_2 = 683.15 \, \text{kJ/kg} + \frac{26.581 - 26.929}{25.864 - 26.929} \times (683.15 - 693.84) \, \text{kJ/kg} $$

$$ = 679.66 \, \text{kJ/kg} $$

$$ u_2 = h_2 - p_2 v_2 $$

$$ = 679.66 \, \text{kJ/kg} - 1705.5 \, \text{kPa} \times 0.113 \, 3 \, \text{m}^3/\text{kg} = 486.43 \, \text{kJ/kg} $$

$$ u_3 = u_2 + q_v = 486.43 \, \text{kJ/kg} + 1073 \, \text{kJ/kg} = 1559.43 \, \text{kJ/kg} $$

由 \( h_3 = u + p_3 v_3 = u_3 + R_g T_3 \)，据空气的热力性质表，经试算得 \( T_3 = 1875.0 \, \text{K} \)，查得 \( v_{r3} = 1183.0 \)

$$ p_3 = \frac{R_g T_3}{v_3} = \frac{287 \, \text{J/(kg·K)} \times 1875.0 \, \text{K}}{0.113 \, 3 \, \text{m}^3/\text{kg}} = 4749.6 \, \text{kPa} $$
