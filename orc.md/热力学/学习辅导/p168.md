$\left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial T}\right)_{p}\left(\frac{\partial T}{\partial p}\right)_{v}\left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial v}\right)_{T}=-1,\left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial T}\right)_{p}=-\frac{\left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial T}\right)_{v}}{\left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial v}\right)_{T}}$

代入 $c_{p}-c_{v}$ 的一般表达式

$c_{p}-c_{v}=T\left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial T}\right)_{p}\left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial T}\right)_{v}$

得

$\begin{aligned} & c_{p}-c_{v}=T\left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial T}\right)_{p}\left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial T}\right)_{v}=-T \frac{\left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial T}\right)_{v}^{2}}{\left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial v}\right)_{T}} \\ & \text { 范德瓦尔方程可写成 } \end{aligned}$

$p=\frac{R_{g} T}{v-b}-\frac{a}{v^{2}}$

$\begin{aligned} & \left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial T}\right)_{v}=\frac{R_{g}}{v+b} \\ & \left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial v}\right)_{T}=-\frac{R_{g} T}{(v-b)^{2}}+\frac{2 a}{v^{3}}=-\frac{R_{g} T v-2 a(v-b)^{2}}{v^{3}(v-b)^{2}} \end{aligned}$

所以

$c_{p}-c_{v}=\frac{T \frac{R_{g}^{2}}{(v-b)^{2}} v^{3}(v-b)^{2}}{R_{g} T v^{3}-2 a(v-b)^{2}}=\frac{T R_{g}^{2} v^{3}}{R_{g} T v^{3}-2 a(v-b)^{2}}$

欲证明满足范德瓦尔方程的气体比定容热容不随气体比体积变化，即需证明 $\left(\frac{\partial c_{v}}{\partial v}\right)_{T}=0$，根据比定容热容与压力的一般关系

$\left(\frac{\partial c_{v}}{\partial v}\right)_{T}=T\left(\frac{\partial^{2} p}{\partial T^{2}}\right)_{v}$

对于范德瓦尔气体

$\left(\frac{\partial c_{v}}{\partial v}\right)_{T}=T\left(\frac{\partial^{2} p}{\partial T^{2}}\right)_{v}=T\left[\frac{\partial}{\partial T}\left(\frac{R_{g}}{v-b}\right)\right]_{v}=0$

点评 $\left(\frac{\partial c_{v}}{\partial v}\right)_{T}=0$，表明满足范德瓦尔方程的气体比定容热容不随气体比体积变化，这与实际情况不符，说明范德瓦尔方程并不能准确的描述实际气体的这方面的性质。
