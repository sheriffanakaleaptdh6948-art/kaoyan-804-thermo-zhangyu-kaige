解 已知 $p_{1}=0.45 \mathrm{MPa}, T_{1}=(37+273) \mathrm{K}=310 \mathrm{~K}, T_{0}=(17+273) \mathrm{K}=290 \mathrm{~K}$, $p_{2}=0.11 \mathrm{MPa}$ 。

过程绝热 $e_{x, Q}=0$, 不对外作功 $w_{u}=0$

$$\begin{aligned} & i=e_{x, 1}-e_{x, 2}+e_{x, Q}-w_{u} \\ & i=e_{x, 1}-e_{x, 2}=h_{1}-h_{2}-T_{0}\left(s_{1}-s_{2}\right)=T_{0}\left(s_{2}-s_{1}\right)=T_{0}\left(c_{p} \ln \frac{T_{2}}{T_{1}}-R_{g} \ln \frac{p_{2}}{p_{1}}\right) \\ & =-T_{0} R_{g} \ln \frac{p_{2}}{p_{1}} \\ & =-290 \mathrm{~K} \times 0.297 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times \ln \frac{0.11 \mathrm{MPa}}{0.45 \mathrm{MPa}}=121.34 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \end{aligned}$$
