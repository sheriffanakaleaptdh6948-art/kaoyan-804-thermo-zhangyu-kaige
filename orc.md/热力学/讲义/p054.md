$$ \frac{p_2}{p_1} = \left(\frac{v_1}{v_2}\right)^n \quad \frac{T_2}{T_1} = \left(\frac{p_2}{p_1}\right)^{\frac{n-1}{n}} \quad \frac{T_2}{T_1} = \left(\frac{v_1}{v_2}\right)^{n-1} $$

已知多变过程线上两点，可得多变指数为 $n = \ln \frac{p_2}{p_1} / \ln \frac{v_1}{v_2} = \frac{\ln p_2 - \ln p_1}{\ln v_1 - \ln v_2}$

如果各点没有在一条直线上，说明该过程不是多变过程。

【图 $\ln p - \ln v$ 图】

**理想气体的 $p-v$ 图和 $T-s$ 图**

状态参数的变化与过程无关

内能变化 $\Delta u = \int c_v \mathrm{d}T$

变化 $\Delta h = \int c_p \mathrm{d}T$

熵变化
$$ \begin{aligned} \Delta s &= \int_1^2 c_v \frac{\mathrm{d}T}{T} + R_g \ln \frac{v_2}{v_1} \\ &= \int_1^2 c_p \frac{\mathrm{d}T}{T} - R_g \ln \frac{p_2}{p_1} = \int_1^2 c_p \frac{\mathrm{d}v}{v} + \int_1^2 c_v \frac{\mathrm{d}p}{p} \end{aligned} $$

**理想气体 $n, w, w_t, q$ 的计算**

$$ \begin{aligned} w_{12} &= \int_1^2 p \mathrm{d}v = \int_1^2 \frac{C}{v^n} \mathrm{d}v = \int_1^2 p_1 v_1^n \frac{\mathrm{d}v}{v^n} \\ &= \frac{p_1 v_1}{n-1} \left[1 - \left(\frac{v_1}{v_2}\right)^{n-1}\right] = p_1 v_1^n \frac{1}{n-1} \left(\frac{1}{v_1^{n-1}} - \frac{1}{v_2^{n-1}}\right) \\ &= \frac{R_g T_1}{n-1} \left[1 - \left(\frac{T_2}{T_1}\right)\right] = \frac{R_g}{n-1} (T_1 - T_2) = \frac{1}{n-1} (p_1 v_1 - p_2 v_2) \end{aligned} $$

$$ p v^n = C \xrightarrow{\text{微分}} v^n \mathrm{d}p + n p v^{n-1} \mathrm{d}v = 0 \rightarrow -v \mathrm{d}p = n p \mathrm{d}v \rightarrow \delta w_t = n \delta w \rightarrow w_t = n w $$

$$ n = \frac{\delta w_t}{\delta w} = \frac{w_t}{w} = \frac{A_1}{A_2} $$

多变指数 $n$ 等于多变过程的技术功和体积功之比。

$$ \begin{aligned} w_{t12} &= - \int_1^2 v \mathrm{d}p = \frac{n R_g T_1}{n-1} \left[1 - \left(\frac{p_2}{p_1}\right)^{\frac{n-1}{n}}\right] = \frac{n R_g}{n-1} (T_1 - T_2) \\ &= \frac{n}{n-1} (p_1 v_1 - p_2 v_2) \end{aligned} $$
