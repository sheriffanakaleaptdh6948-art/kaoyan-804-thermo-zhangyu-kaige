余隙容积 $V_c$ 对理论压气功的影响

$$
\begin{aligned}
W_C &= W_{t12} + W_{t34} = W_{t12} - W_{t43} \\
&= \frac{n m_1 R_g T_1}{n-1} \left[ 1 - \left( \pi \right)^{\frac{n-1}{n}} \right] - \frac{n' m_4 R_g T_4}{n'-1} \left[ 1 - \left( \pi \right)^{\frac{n'-1}{n'}} \right] \\
&= - \frac{n p_1 V_1}{n-1} \left( \pi^{\frac{n-1}{n}} - 1 \right) + \frac{n' p_4 V_4}{n'-1} \left( \pi^{\frac{n'-1}{n'}} - 1 \right)
\end{aligned}
$$

由于是同一汽缸，冷却条件相同，故 $1 \to 2$ 过程 $n$ 和 $4 \to 3$ 过程 $n'$ 相同，则

$$
\begin{aligned}
W_C &= - \frac{n p_1 V_1}{n-1} \left( \pi^{\frac{n-1}{n}} - 1 \right) + \frac{n p_4 V_4}{n-1} \left( \pi^{\frac{n-1}{n}} - 1 \right) \\
&= - \frac{n p_1 (V_1 - V_4)}{n-1} \left( \pi^{\frac{n-1}{n}} - 1 \right)
\end{aligned}
$$

【图 P-V 图，标注点 1, 2, 3, 4 及体积 $V_1, V_3, V_4, V_1-V_4, V_1-V_3$】

$$
W_C = - \frac{n p_1 (V_1 - V_4)}{n-1} \left( \pi^{\frac{n-1}{n}} - 1 \right)
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
w_C &= \frac{W_C}{m_1 - m_4} \\
&= - \frac{n p_1 (V_1 - V_4)}{\left( \frac{p_1 V_1}{R_g T_1} - \frac{p_4 V_4}{R_g T_4} \right) (n-1)} \left( \pi^{\frac{n-1}{n}} - 1 \right)
\end{aligned}
$$

利用 $T_4 = T_1$，得

$$
w_C = - \frac{n R_g T_1}{n-1} \left( \pi^{\frac{n-1}{n}} - 1 \right)
$$

**结论：** 余隙对单位产气量耗功没有影响

余隙容积 $V_c$ 对产气量的影响

定义容积效率

$$
\begin{aligned}
\eta_v &= \frac{\text{有效容积}}{\text{活塞排量}} = \frac{V_1 - V_4}{V_1 - V_3} \\
&= 1 - \frac{V_4 - V_3}{V_1 - V_3} = 1 - \frac{V_3}{V_1 - V_3} \left( \frac{V_4}{V_3} - 1 \right)
\end{aligned}
$$

令 $c = \frac{V_c}{V_1 - V_3}$

工程上一般 $= 0.03 \sim 0.08$

$$
\eta_v = 1 - c \left[ \left( \frac{p_3}{p_4} \right)^{\frac{1}{n}} - 1 \right] = 1 - c \left[ \left( \frac{p_2}{p_1} \right)^{\frac{1}{n}} - 1 \right]
$$

讨论：$\eta_v = 1 - c (\pi^{\frac{1}{n}} - 1)$
