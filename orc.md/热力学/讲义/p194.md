向外部冷源的放热量为
$$ q_2 = h_{5'} - h_1 = [ h_4 - \sigma(h_4 - h_2) ] - h_1 $$

则回热循环的热效率为
$$ \eta_{t, \text{RG}} = 1 - \frac{q_2}{q_1} = 1 - \frac{c_p [ (T_4 - T_1) - \sigma(T_4 - T_2) ]}{c_p [ (T_3 - T_2) - \sigma(T_4 - T_2) ]} $$
$$ \eta_{t, \text{RG}} = 1 - \frac{(T_4 - T_1) - \sigma(T_4 - T_2)}{(T_3 - T_2) - \sigma(T_4 - T_2)} $$
$$ = \frac{(T_3 - T_2) - (T_4 - T_1)}{(T_3 - T_2) - \sigma(T_4 - T_2)} $$
$$ = \frac{(\frac{T_4}{T_3} \frac{T_3}{T_1} - 1)}{(\frac{T_4}{T_3} \frac{T_3}{T_1} - 1) - \sigma(\frac{T_4}{T_3} \frac{T_3}{T_1} \frac{T_1}{T_2} - 1)} (1 - \frac{T_1}{T_2}) $$
则回热循环的热效率决定于循环各转折点温度，循环热效率的本质仍然是温度。
$$ \eta_{t, \text{RG}} = \frac{\frac{\tau}{\pi^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}}} - 1}{(\frac{\tau}{\pi^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}}} - 1) - \sigma(\frac{\tau}{\pi^{\frac{2(\kappa-1)}{\kappa}}} - 1)} (1 - \frac{1}{\pi^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}}}) $$
$$ = \frac{1}{1 - \sigma \frac{\tau - \pi^{\frac{2(\kappa-1)}{\kappa}}}{\tau \pi^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}} - \pi^{\frac{2(\kappa-1)}{\kappa}}}} (1 - \frac{1}{\pi^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}}}) $$
可以证明：
$$ 0 < 1 - \sigma \frac{\tau - \pi^{\frac{2(\kappa-1)}{\kappa}}}{\tau \pi^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}} - \pi^{\frac{2(\kappa-1)}{\kappa}}} < 1 \text{ 必有 } \eta_{t, \text{RG}} > (1 - \frac{1}{\pi^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}}}) $$
回热必使循环热效率提高。可见，采用公式法分析的结果与前面采用热量法和温度法进行的定性分析结果是完全一致的。
