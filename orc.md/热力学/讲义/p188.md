### 三、考虑不可逆损失的实际燃气轮机循环

【图 实际燃气轮机循环T-s图】

燃气轮机的压气机和涡轮机往往是“速度式”叶轮机械，气流速度大，摩擦大，在压缩和膨胀过程中会产生不可逆增。从而产生功损。

该功损用绝热过程的定熵效率来考虑。或称为压气机效率 $\eta_{\text{c}}$ 和涡轮效率 $\eta_{\text{T}}$。

$$
|w'_{\text{c}}| = h'_2 - h_1 = \frac{w_{\text{c}}}{\eta_{\text{c}}} = \frac{h_2 - h_1}{\eta_{\text{c}}} = \frac{c_p(T_2 - T_1)}{\eta_{\text{c}}} = \frac{\kappa R_{\text{g}} T_1}{\eta_{\text{c}}(\kappa - 1)} \left[ \left( \frac{p_2}{p_1} \right)^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}} - 1 \right]
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
w'_{\text{T}} &= h_3 - h'_4 = w_{\text{T}} \eta_{\text{T}} \\
&= (h_3 - h_4) \eta_{\text{T}} \\
&= \eta_{\text{T}} c_p (T_3 - T_4) \\
&= \frac{\eta_{\text{T}} \kappa R_{\text{g}} T_3}{(\kappa - 1)} \left[ 1 - \left( \frac{1}{\pi} \right)^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}} \right]
\end{aligned}
$$

则实际循环功为

$$
\begin{aligned}
w'_0 &= w'_{\text{T}} - |w'_{\text{c}}| = (h_3 - h'_4) - (h'_2 - h_1) \\
&= w_{\text{T}} \eta_{\text{T}} - \frac{w_{\text{c}}}{\eta_{\text{c}}} = (h_3 - h_4) \eta_{\text{T}} - \frac{h_2 - h_1}{\eta_{\text{c}}} \\
&= \eta_{\text{T}} c_p (T_3 - T_4) - \frac{c_p(T_2 - T_1)}{\eta_{\text{c}}} \\
&= \frac{\eta_{\text{T}} \kappa R_{\text{g}} T_3}{\kappa - 1} \left[ 1 - \left( \frac{1}{\pi} \right)^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}} \right] - \frac{\kappa R_{\text{g}} T_1}{\eta_{\text{c}}(\kappa - 1)} \left[ \pi^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}} - 1 \right] \\
&= \frac{\kappa R_{\text{g}} T_1}{\kappa - 1} \left[ \eta_{\text{T}} \tau \left( 1 - \frac{1}{\pi^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}}} \right) - \frac{1}{\eta_{\text{c}}} \left( \pi^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}} - 1 \right) \right] \\
&= \frac{\kappa R_{\text{g}} T_1}{\kappa - 1} \left[ \eta_{\text{T}} \tau + \frac{1}{\eta_{\text{c}}} - \left( \frac{\eta_{\text{T}} \tau}{\pi^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}}} + \frac{\pi^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}}}{\eta_{\text{c}}} \right) \right]
\end{aligned}
$$

存在一个最佳增压比 $\pi'_{\text{opt}}$ 使 $w'_0 = w'_{\text{T}} - w'_{\text{c}}$ 最大。
