**例 6-3** 试推导热系数之间的关系。

循环关系

$$ \left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial T}\right)_v \left(\frac{\partial T}{\partial v}\right)_p \left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial p}\right)_T = -1 $$

$$ \downarrow \qquad \downarrow \qquad \downarrow $$

$$ \alpha_p = \frac{1}{p} \left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial T}\right)_v \quad \alpha_p p \quad 1/\alpha_v v \quad -v\kappa_T \quad \kappa_T = -\frac{1}{v} \left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial p}\right)_T $$

$$ \alpha_v = \frac{1}{v} \left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial T}\right)_p $$

$$ \frac{\alpha_v}{\kappa_T \alpha_p} = p $$

说明热系数之间也不是独立的，相互之间有一定的关系。实际上这也是由简单可压缩热力系只有两个独立自变量的本质所决定的。

Maxwell 关系 $\left(\frac{\partial s}{\partial p}\right)_T = -\left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial T}\right)_p = -v\alpha_v$

$$ \Delta s_T = \int \left(\frac{\partial s}{\partial p}\right)_T \mathrm{d}p = \int -\left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial T}\right)_p \mathrm{d}p = -\int v\alpha_v \mathrm{d}p $$

若工质为理想气体，则 $\alpha_v = \frac{1}{v} \left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial T}\right)_p = \frac{1}{pv}R_g = \frac{1}{R_g T}R_g = \frac{1}{T}$

$$ \Delta s_T = -\int \frac{v}{T} \mathrm{d}p = -\int \frac{R_g}{p} \mathrm{d}p = -R_g \ln \frac{p_2}{p_1} $$

若工质为液体，则 $\Delta s_T = -v\alpha_v \Delta p$
