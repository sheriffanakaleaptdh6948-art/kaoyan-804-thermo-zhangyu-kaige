沈维道《工程热力学》考点精讲及复习思路

$$
q = \Delta u + w = c_v(T_2 - T_1) - \frac{R_g}{n-1}(T_2 - T_1)
$$
$$
= \left(c_v - \frac{R_g}{n-1}\right)(T_2 - T_1) = \frac{n-\kappa}{n-1}c_v(T_2 - T_1) = c_n(T_2 - T_1)
$$

$$
c_n = \frac{n-\kappa}{n-1}c_v
$$

- $n=0 \quad c_n = \kappa c_v = c_p$
- $n=1 \quad c_n = \infty$
- $n=k \quad c_n = 0$
- $n=\infty \quad c_n = c_v$

比热容的确是个过程量，不同的热力过程具有不同的比热容。

$$
w_{12} = \int_1^2 p \mathrm{d}v = \frac{R_g T_1}{n-1}\left[1 - \left(\quad\right)^{\frac{n-1}{n}}\right] = \frac{R_g}{n-1}(T_1 - T_2)
$$

$$
w_{t12} = -\int_1^2 v \mathrm{d}p = \frac{n R_g T_1}{n-1}\left[1 - \left(\quad\right)^{\frac{n-1}{n}}\right] = \frac{n R_g}{n-1}(T_1 - T_2)
$$

$$
q = \Delta u + w = \frac{n-\kappa}{n-1}c_v(T_2 - T_1) = c_n(T_2 - T_1)
$$

对理想气体定温过程

$\Delta u = \Delta h = 0$，则 $q_{12} = w_{12} = w_{t12} = \int_1^2 p \mathrm{d}v = R_g T \int_1^2 \frac{\mathrm{d}v}{v}$
$= R_g T \ln \frac{v_2}{v_1} = R_g T \ln \frac{p_1}{p_2}$ 无定比热容要求

理想气体基本过程的 $p-v, T-s$ 图

首先，要知道一阶导数 $\frac{\partial T}{\partial s}, \frac{\partial p}{\partial v}$

理想气体多变过程

$$
pv^n = C, \mathrm{d}(pv^n) = 0, \mathrm{d}s = \frac{\delta q}{T} = \frac{c_n \mathrm{d}T}{T}
$$

$$
n p v^{n-1} \mathrm{d}v + v^n \mathrm{d}p = 0
$$

$$
\left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial v}\right)_n = -\frac{np}{v} \begin{cases} < 0 \\ = 0 \text{ 取决于 } n \\ > 0 \end{cases}
$$

$$
\left(\frac{\partial T}{\partial s}\right)_n = \frac{T}{c_n} \begin{cases} < 0 \\ = 0 \text{ 取决于 } c_n \\ > 0 \end{cases}
$$

曲线的形状和走向取决于 $p, v, T, n$ 和 $c_n$。

$$
\left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial v}\right)_n = -\frac{np}{v} \quad \left(\frac{\partial T}{\partial s}\right)_n = \frac{T}{c_n}
$$
