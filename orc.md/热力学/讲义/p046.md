【图 $C_p$ 随 $T$ 变化曲线及平均比热容示意图】

理想气体热力学能和焓的计算

1) 按定比热容计算

$$ \Delta u = \int_1^2 c_v \mathrm{d}T = c_v(T_2 - T_1) \quad \Delta h = \int_1^2 c_p \mathrm{d}T = c_p(T_2 - T_1) $$

2) 按真实比热容或者说变比热容计算

$$ \Delta h = \int_1^2 c_p \mathrm{d}T = \int_1^2 (a + bT + cT^2 + dT^3 + \cdots) R_g \mathrm{d}T $$

$$ \Delta u = \int_1^2 c_v \mathrm{d}T = \int_1^2 (a - 1 + bT + cT^2 + dT^3 + \cdots) R_g \mathrm{d}T $$

3) 按平均比热容计算

$$ \Delta h_{12} = \int_1^2 c_p \mathrm{d}T = \int_{t_1}^{t_2} c_p \mathrm{d}t = \int_{t_0}^{t_2} c_p \mathrm{d}t - \int_{t_0}^{t_1} c_p \mathrm{d}t = \overline{c_p}\bigg|_{t_0}^{t_2}(t_2 - t_0) - \overline{c_p}\bigg|_{t_0}^{t_1}(t_1 - t_0) $$

若 $t_0 = 0^\circ\text{C}$，则上式可写为 $\Delta h_{12} = \overline{c_p}\bigg|_{t_0}^{t_2} t_2 - \overline{c_p}\bigg|_{t_0}^{t_1} t_1$

同理可得 $\Delta u_{12} = \overline{c_v}\bigg|_{t_0}^{t_2} t_2 - \overline{c_v}\bigg|_{t_0}^{t_1} t_1$

迈尔公式对平均比热容也是成立的，即有 $\overline{c_p}\bigg|_0^t - \overline{c_v}\bigg|_0^t = R_g$

4) 按温表计算

$$ \Delta h = h_2(T_2) - h_1(T_1) $$
