证明：$\mathrm{d}f = -s\mathrm{d}T - p\mathrm{d}v \rightarrow \left(\frac{\partial s}{\partial v}\right)_T = \left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial T}\right)_v$

**例 6-8 推导题**：对于简单可压缩热力系统，已知以温度和压力为独立变量的比自由焓 $g(T,p)$，试导出比熵 $s(T,p)$、比焓 $h(T,p)$、比内能 $u(T,p)$、比自由能 $f(T,p)$ 各函数以及状态方程 $v(T,p)$。（重庆大学 2002 年考研试题）

答：$\mathrm{d}g = -s\mathrm{d}T + v\mathrm{d}p$ $g=g(T,p)$ 是特征函数

$$s = -\left(\frac{\partial g}{\partial T}\right)_p \quad v = \left(\frac{\partial g}{\partial p}\right)_T$$

$$h = g + Ts = g - T\left(\frac{\partial g}{\partial T}\right)_p, f = g - pv = g - p\left(\frac{\partial g}{\partial p}\right)_T$$

$$u = h - pv = g - T\left(\frac{\partial g}{\partial T}\right)_p - p\left(\frac{\partial g}{\partial p}\right)_T$$

**考点五：熵、热力学能、焓的一般关系式**

$\mathrm{d}s, \mathrm{d}u, \mathrm{d}h \rightarrow p, v, T + c_p, c_v$

**一、熵的一般关系式** 对理想气体

$$s = f(T,v) \qquad \mathrm{d}s = c_v \frac{\mathrm{d}T}{T} + R_g \frac{\mathrm{d}v}{v}$$

$$s = f(T,p) \qquad \mathrm{d}s = c_p \frac{\mathrm{d}T}{T} - R_g \frac{\mathrm{d}p}{p}$$

$$s = f(p,v) \qquad \mathrm{d}s = c_v \frac{\mathrm{d}p}{p} + c_p \frac{\mathrm{d}v}{v}$$

一般工质

$$s = f(T,v) \quad \mathrm{d}s = \left(\frac{\partial s}{\partial T}\right)_v \mathrm{d}T + \left(\frac{\partial s}{\partial v}\right)_T \mathrm{d}v$$

$$\text{Maxwell}: \left(\frac{\partial s}{\partial v}\right)_T = \left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial T}\right)_v$$

$$\left(\frac{\partial s}{\partial T}\right)_v = \frac{c_v}{T}$$

$$\mathrm{d}s = \frac{c_v}{T}\mathrm{d}T + \left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial T}\right)_v \mathrm{d}v \text{ 第一熵方程普遍适用}$$

第一熵方程 $\mathrm{d}s = \frac{c_v}{T}\mathrm{d}T + \left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial T}\right)_v \mathrm{d}v$

理想气体

$$pv = R_g T \quad \left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial T}\right)_v = \frac{R_g}{v}$$

$$\mathrm{d}s = c_v \frac{\mathrm{d}T}{T} + R_g \frac{\mathrm{d}v}{v}$$

可见与第三章理想气体的熵表达式是一样的。只是推导路径不同。此处先推一般，再到特殊，路径较长，较繁琐。
