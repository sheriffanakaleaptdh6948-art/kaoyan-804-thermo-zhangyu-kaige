· 定比热容时 $\Delta u_{12} = \frac{R_g}{\kappa - 1}(T_2 - T_1)$ (6a)
· 变比热容时 $\Delta h_{12} = \int_1^2 c_p \mathrm{d}T$ 或附录 (6a)

上述 7 个独立的方程中，共出现了 12 个参数，其中，2 个常数 $(\kappa, R_g)$，3 个已知 $(p_1, T_1, p_2)$，7 个未知 $(v_1, v_2, T_2, w_{12}, w_{t12}, \Delta u_{12}, \Delta h_{12})$，方程组封闭。

(3)方程求解
1)按定值比热容计算时
由(5b)解得：$T_2 = \left(\frac{p_2}{p_1}\right)^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}} T_1 = \left(\frac{0.1}{0.5}\right)^{\frac{1.40-1}{1.40}} \times (427 + 273) = 441.97\mathrm{K}$

由(4)得：$v_2 = \frac{R_g T_2}{p_2} = \frac{287 \times 441.97}{0.1 \times 10^6} = 1.2685\mathrm{m^3/kg}$

由(6b)得：$\Delta u_{12} = \frac{R_g}{\kappa - 1}(T_2 - T_1) = \frac{287}{1.40 - 1} \times (441.97 - 700) = -185.14\mathrm{kJ/kg}$

由(7b)得：$\Delta h_{12} = \frac{\kappa R_g}{\kappa - 1}(T_2 - T_1) = \frac{1.40 \times 287}{1.40 - 1} \times (441.97 - 700) = -259.19\mathrm{kJ/kg}$

由(1)得：$w_{12} = u_1 - u_2 = -\Delta u_{12} = 185.14\mathrm{kJ/kg}$
由(2)得：$w_{t12} = h_1 - h_2 = -\Delta h_{12} = 259.19\mathrm{kJ/kg}$

2)按变比热容计算时，由附录查得 $T_1=700\mathrm{K}$ 时
$h_1=713.27\mathrm{kJ/kg}, u_1=512.33\mathrm{kJ/kg}, p_{r1}=28.8$
$v_{r1}=67.76, s_{T1}^0=2.57277\mathrm{kJ/(kg \cdot K)}$

$p_{r2} = \frac{p_2}{p_1}p_{r1} = \frac{0.1}{0.5} \times 28.8 = 5.76$

再由附录插值查得 $p_{r1}=5.76$ 时：
$T_2 = 440 + \frac{5.76 - 5.332}{5.775 - 5.332} \times 10 = 449.66\mathrm{K}$ 时
类似插值查得 $h_2=451.45\mathrm{kJ/kg}, u_2=322.37\mathrm{kJ/kg}$
$v_{r2}=224.05, s_{T1}^0=2.11086\mathrm{kJ/(kg \cdot K)}$

$v_2 = \frac{v_{r2}}{v_{r1}}v_1 = \frac{224.05}{67.76} \times 0.4018 = 1.3286\mathrm{m^3/kg}$

也可直接由(4)得 $v_2 = \frac{R_g T_2}{p_2} = \frac{287 \times 449.66}{0.1 \times 10^6} = 1.2905\mathrm{m^3/kg}$

由(6a)得：$\Delta u_{12} = u_2 - u_1 = 322.37 - 512.33 = -189.96\mathrm{kJ/kg}$
由(7a)得：$\Delta h_{12} = h_2 - h_1 = 451.45 - 713.27 = -261.82\mathrm{kJ/kg}$
由(1)得：$w_{12} = u_1 - u_2 = -\Delta u_{12} = 189.96\mathrm{kJ/kg}$
由(2)得：$w_{t12} = h_1 - h_2 = -\Delta h_{12} = 261.82\mathrm{kJ/kg}$
