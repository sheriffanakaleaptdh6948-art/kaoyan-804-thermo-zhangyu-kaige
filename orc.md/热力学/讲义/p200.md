(3)最佳增压比下的循环净功和热效率。（北航 2007 年考研试题）

**解：**1. 物理模型：该燃气轮机理想循环中每一转折点均确定，因此该循环确定，可按照分析循环的方法求解。

2. 数学模型及求解：

$$
\begin{aligned}
\eta_t &= 1 - \frac{q_2}{q_1} = 1 - \frac{c_p(T_4 - T_1)}{c_p(T_3 - T_2)} \\
&= 1 - \frac{T_4 - T_1}{T_3 - T_2}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
T_2 &= T_1 (\pi)^{\frac{\kappa - 1}{\kappa}} = 300 \times 5.5^{\frac{1.4-1}{1.4}} \\
&= 488.3 K = 215.3^{\circ}\mathrm{C}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
T_4 = T_3 (\frac{1}{\pi})^{\frac{\kappa - 1}{\kappa}} = 1000 \times (\frac{1}{5.5})^{\frac{1.4-1}{1.4}} = 614.4 K = 341.4^{\circ}\mathrm{C}
$$
(注：原图 $T_4$ 算式处排版似有缺漏，根据物理意义补全底数)

$$
\eta_t = 1 - \frac{T_4 - T_1}{T_3 - T_2} = 1 - \frac{341.4 - 27}{727 - 215.3} = 38.56\%
$$

【图 布莱顿循环T-s图】

$$
\begin{aligned}
w_0 &= w_{t34} + w_{t12} = w_T - |w_c| \\
&= \frac{\kappa R_g T_3}{\kappa - 1} [1 - (\frac{1}{\pi})^{\frac{\kappa - 1}{\kappa}}] - \frac{\kappa R_g T_1}{\kappa - 1} [(\pi)^{\frac{\kappa - 1}{\kappa}} - 1] \\
&= \frac{\kappa R_g T_1}{\kappa - 1} [\tau (1 - \frac{1}{\pi^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}}}) - (\pi^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}} - 1)] \\
&= \frac{\kappa R_g T_1}{\kappa - 1} [\tau + 1 - (\frac{\tau}{\pi^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}}} + \pi^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}})] \quad \frac{\tau}{\pi^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}}} \cdot \pi^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}} = \tau = \text{常数}
\end{aligned}
$$

当 $\frac{\tau}{\pi^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}}} = \pi^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}}$ 时，循环功 $w_0$ 最大。

可得最佳增压比 $\pi_{\mathrm{opt}}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\pi_{\mathrm{opt}} &= \tau^{\frac{\kappa}{2(\kappa-1)}} \\
&= \sqrt{\tau^{\frac{\kappa}{\kappa-1}}} = \sqrt{(\frac{T_3}{T_1})^{\frac{\kappa}{\kappa-1}}} \\
&= \sqrt{(\frac{1000}{300})^{\frac{1.4}{1.4-1}}} = 8.223
\end{aligned}
$$

最佳增压比下的循环净功

$$
w_{0\max} = \frac{\kappa R_g T_3}{\kappa - 1} [1 - (\frac{1}{\pi_{\mathrm{opt}}})^{\frac{\kappa - 1}{\kappa}}] - \frac{\kappa R_g T_1}{\kappa - 1} [(\pi_{\mathrm{opt}})^{\frac{\kappa - 1}{\kappa}} - 1]
$$
