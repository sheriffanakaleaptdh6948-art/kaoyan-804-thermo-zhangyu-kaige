**例 9-1** 已知：$V_1 - V_3 = 0.4455 m^3, V_3 = 0.0223 m^3, p_1 = 1 \text{bar}, t_1 = 15^{\circ}\text{C}, p_2 = 3.5 \text{bar}, n = 1.2$
求(1) 有余隙时的排气量和耗功
(2) 无余隙时的排气量和耗功

【图 例9-1示功图】

**解：**(1) 有效容积
$$
\begin{aligned}
V &= V_1 - V_4 = V_1 - V_3 + V_3 - V_4 \\
&= V_1 - V_3 + V_3 - V_3 \left(\frac{p_2}{p_1}\right)^{\frac{1}{n}} \\
&= 0.4045 m^3
\end{aligned}
$$
$V = 0.4045 m^3$、$W_{\text{t}} = \frac{n}{n-1} p_1 V \left[ 1 - \left(\frac{p_2}{p_1}\right)^{\frac{n-1}{n}} \right] = -56.35 \text{kJ}$

$$w_{\text{t}} = \frac{W_{\text{t}}}{m} = -115 \text{kJ/kg}$$
