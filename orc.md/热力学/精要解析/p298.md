(2) 求循环吸热量和循环放热量

$h_{4}=h_{10^{\prime}}+\left(h_{4 s}-h_{10^{\prime}}\right) / \eta_{\mathrm{P}}=720.9+(726.8-720.9) / 0.8=728.3 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$
