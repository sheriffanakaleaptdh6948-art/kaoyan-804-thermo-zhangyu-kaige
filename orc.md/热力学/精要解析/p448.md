4. (12 分)

(1) 绝热混合过程: 由稳定流动系能量方程可知: $\Delta H=0$

即: $\left(q_{m_{1}}+q_{m_{2}}\right) \cdot h_{3}=q_{m_{1}} \cdot h_{1}+q_{m_{2}} \cdot h_{2} \Rightarrow\left(q_{m_{1}}+q_{m_{2}}\right) \cdot T_{3}=q_{m_{1}} \cdot T_{1}+q_{m_{2}} \cdot T_{2}$

$T_{3}=\frac{q_{m_{1}} \cdot T_{1}+q_{m_{2}} \cdot T_{2}}{q_{m_{1}}+q_{m_{2}}}=\frac{160 \times(400+273.15)+280 \times(150+273.15)}{160+280}=514.06 \mathrm{~K}$

(2) 混合过程的熵变为:

$\begin{aligned} \Delta S & =q_{m_{1}} \cdot c_{p} \cdot \ln \frac{T_{3}}{T_{1}}+q_{m_{2}} \cdot c_{p} \cdot \ln \frac{T_{3}}{T_{2}} \\ & =160 \times 1.004 \times \ln \frac{514.06}{673.15}+280 \times 1.004 \times \ln \frac{514.06}{423.15}=11.40 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{K} \cdot \mathrm{h}) \end{aligned}$

(3) 混合气流加热至 $t_{4}$ 所需加热功率为:

$P=q_{m_{3}} \cdot c_{p} \cdot\left(T_{4}-T_{3}\right)$

$=(160+280) \times 1.004 \times(723.15-514.06)=9.24 \times 10^{4} \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{h}=25.66 \mathrm{kW}$

(4) 加热过程不等温传热引起的有效能损失:

$\begin{aligned} I=T_{0}\left(\Delta S_{\text {工质 }}+\Delta S_{\mathrm{r}}\right)= & T_{0}\left(q_{m_{3}} \cdot c_{p} \cdot \ln \frac{T_{4}}{T_{3}}-\frac{Q}{T_{\mathrm{r}}}\right) \\ = & 300 \times\left(440 \times 1.004 \times \ln \frac{450+273.15}{514.06}-\frac{9.24 \times 10^{4}}{500+273.15}\right)=9.38 \times 10^{3} \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{h} \end{aligned}$
