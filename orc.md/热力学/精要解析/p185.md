### 8.5 典型题精解

例题 8-1 由不变气源来的压力 \(p_1 = 1.5\) MPa,温度 \(t_1 = 27^\circ \mathrm{C}\) 的空气,流经一喷管进入压力保持在 \(p_b = 0.6\) MPa 的某装置中,若流过喷管的流量为 \(3 \, \text{kg/s}\),来流速度可忽略不计,试设计该喷管?若来流速度 \(c_{\text{fl}} = 100 \, \text{m/s}\),其他条件不变,则喷管出口流速及截面积为多少?

解 (1) 这是一典型的喷管设计问题,可按设计步骤进行。

① 求滞止参数

因 \(c_{\text{fl}} = 0\),所以初始状态即可认为是滞止状态,则

$p_0 = p_1, T_0 = T_1 = (27 + 273) \, \text{K} = 300 \, \text{K}$

② 选型

$\frac{p_b}{p_0} = \frac{0.6 \, \text{MPa}}{1.5 \, \text{MPa}} = 0.4 < \nu_{\text{cr}} = 0.528$

所以,为了使气体在喷管内实现完全膨胀,应选缩放喷管,则 \(p_2 = p_b = 0.6 \, \text{MPa}\)。

③ 求临界截面及出口截面参数(状态参数及流速)

$p_{\text{cr}} = \nu_{\text{cr}} p_0 = 0.528 \times 1.5 \, \text{MPa} = 0.792 \, \text{MPa}$

$T_{\text{cr}} = T_0 \left( \frac{p_{\text{cr}}}{p_0} \right)^{(\kappa - 1)/\kappa} = 300 \, \text{K} \times \left( \frac{0.792 \, \text{MPa}}{1.5 \, \text{MPa}} \right)^{0.4/1.4} = 250.0 \, \text{K}$

$v_{\text{cr}} = \frac{R_g T_{\text{cr}}}{p_{\text{cr}}} = \frac{287 \, \text{J/(kg·K)} \times 250.0 \, \text{K}}{0.792 \times 10^6 \, \text{Pa}} = 0.090 \, \text{m}^3/\text{kg}$

$c_{\text{fl,cr}} = \sqrt{\kappa R_g T_{\text{cr}}} = \sqrt{1.4 \times 287 \, \text{J/(kg·K)} \times 250.0 \, \text{K}} = 316.9 \, \text{m/s}$

或

$c_{\text{t,cr}} = \sqrt{2 c_p (T_0 - T_{\text{cr}})}$

$p_2 = p_b = 0.6 \, \text{MPa}$

$T_2 = T_0 \left( \frac{p_2}{p_0} \right)^{(\kappa - 1)/\kappa} = 300 \, \text{K} \times \left( \frac{0.6 \, \text{MPa}}{1.5 \, \text{MPa}} \right)^{0.4/1.4} = 230.9 \, \text{K}$

$v_2 = \frac{R_g T_2}{p_2} = \frac{287 \, \text{J/(kg·K)} \times 230.0 \, \text{K}}{0.6 \times 10^6 \, \text{Pa}} = 0.110 \, \text{m}^3/\text{kg}$

$c_{\text{t2}} = \sqrt{2 c_p (T_0 - T_2)} = \sqrt{2 \times 1004 \, \text{J/(kg·K)} \times (300 - 230.9) \, \text{K}}$

$= 372.6 \, \text{m/s}$

(4) 求临界截面和出口截面面积及渐扩段长度

$\begin{aligned} A_{\text{cr}} & = \frac{q_m v_{\text{cr}}}{c_{\text{t,cr}}} = \frac{3 \, \text{kg/s} \times 0.090 \, 59 \, \text{m}^3/\text{kg}}{316.9 \, \text{m/s}} \\ & = 8.576 \times 10^{-4} \, \text{m}^2 = 8.576 \, \text{cm}^2 \\ A_2 & = \frac{q_m v_2}{c_{\text{t2}}} = \frac{3 \, \text{kg/s} \times 0.110 \, 4 \, \text{m}^3/\text{kg}}{372.6 \, \text{m/s}} \end{aligned}$
