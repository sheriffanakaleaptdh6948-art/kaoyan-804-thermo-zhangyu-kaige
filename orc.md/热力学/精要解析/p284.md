$\mathrm{CO}+\frac{1}{2} \mathrm{O}_{2} \longrightarrow \mathrm{CO}$

已知 $\Delta H_{\mathrm{f}, \mathrm{CO}}^{0}=-100603 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{mol}, \Delta H_{\mathrm{f}, \mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}(\mathrm{g})}^{0}=-241997 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{mol}, \Delta H_{\mathrm{f}, \mathrm{CO}_{2}}^{0}=-393791$ $\mathrm{J} / \mathrm{mol}$ 。

解 (1) 对于反应 $\mathrm{CO}+\mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}(\mathrm{g}) \longrightarrow \mathrm{CO}_{2}+\mathrm{H}_{2}$, 依题意有

$\begin{aligned} Q_{p} & =\Delta H^{0}=\sum_{\mathrm{P}}\left(n \Delta H_{\mathrm{f}}^{0}\right)-\sum_{\mathrm{R}}\left(n \Delta H_{\mathrm{f}}^{0}\right) \\ & =\Delta H_{\mathrm{f}, \mathrm{CO}_{2}}^{0}+\Delta H_{\mathrm{f}, \mathrm{H}_{2}}^{0}-\Delta H_{\mathrm{f}, \mathrm{CO}}^{0}-\Delta H_{\mathrm{f}, \mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}}^{0} \\ & =\left[-393791+0-(-110603)-(-241997)\right] \mathrm{J} / \mathrm{mol}=-41191 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{mol} \end{aligned}$

(2) 对于反应 $\mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}(\mathrm{g}) \longrightarrow \mathrm{H}_{2}+\frac{1}{2} \mathrm{O}_{2}$

对于反应

$\begin{gathered} Q_{p_{1}}=-\Delta H_{\mathrm{f}, \mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}(\mathrm{g})}^{0}=241997 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{mol} \\ \mathrm{CO}+\frac{1}{2} \mathrm{O}_{2} \longrightarrow \mathrm{CO}_{2} \end{gathered}$

于是

$\begin{gathered} Q_{p_{2}}=\Delta H_{\mathrm{f}, \mathrm{CO}_{2}}^{0}-\Delta H_{\mathrm{f}, \mathrm{CO}}^{0}=-283188 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{mol} \\ Q_{p_{1}}+Q_{p_{2}}=(241997-283188) \mathrm{J} / \mathrm{mol}=-41191 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{mol} \end{gathered}$

可见

$Q_{p}=Q_{p_{1}}+Q_{p_{2}}$

例题 $11-5400 \mathrm{~K}$ 甲烷气 $\mathrm{CH}_{4}(\mathrm{~g})$ 与 $500 \mathrm{~K}$ 的空气在燃烧室内完全燃烧, 过量空气系数 $\alpha=1.5$, 生成物温度为 $1800 \mathrm{~K}$, 试计算燃烧 $1 \mathrm{~mol}$ 甲烷燃烧室与外界传递的热量。已知 $\mathrm{CH}_{4}(\mathrm{~g})$ 的真实摩尔热容为

$C_{p, \mathrm{~m}}=\left(14.16+75.55 \times 10^{-3} T-18.00 \times 10^{-6} T^{2}\right) \mathrm{J} /(\mathrm{mol} \cdot \mathrm{K})$

设空气中 $\mathrm{N}_{2}$ 与 $\mathrm{O}_{2}$ 的物质的量之比为 $3.76: 1$, 一些物质的标准生成焓如下:

$\Delta H_{\mathrm{f}, \mathrm{CO}_{2}}^{0}=-393522 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{mol}, \Delta H_{\mathrm{f}, \mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}(\mathrm{g})}^{0}=-241827 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{mol}, \Delta H_{\mathrm{f}, \mathrm{CH}_{4}(\mathrm{~g})}^{0}=-74873 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{mol}$

另外, 一些物质从 $298 \mathrm{~K}$ 到 $1800 \mathrm{~K}$ 或到 $550 \mathrm{~K}$ 的焓变分别为

$\begin{aligned} & \Delta H_{\mathrm{m}, \mathrm{O}_{2}}\left|\frac{1800 \mathrm{~K}}{298 \mathrm{~K}}=51689 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{mol}, \quad \Delta H_{\mathrm{m}, \mathrm{O}_{2}}\left|\frac{500 \mathrm{~K}}{298 \mathrm{~K}}=6088 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{mol},\right.\right. \\ & \Delta H_{\mathrm{m}, \mathrm{CO}_{2}}\left|\frac{1800 \mathrm{~K}}{298 \mathrm{~K}}=79442 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{mol}, \quad \Delta H_{\mathrm{m}, \mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}(\mathrm{g})}\left|\frac{1800 \mathrm{~K}}{298 \mathrm{~K}}=62609 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{mol},\right. \\ & \Delta H_{\mathrm{m}, \mathrm{N}_{2}}\left|\frac{1800 \mathrm{~K}}{298 \mathrm{~K}}=48982 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{mol}, \quad \Delta H_{\mathrm{m}, \mathrm{N}_{2}}\left|\frac{500 \mathrm{~K}}{298 \mathrm{~K}}=5912 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{mol}\right. \end{aligned}$

解 $\mathrm{CH}_{4}$ 完全燃烧的反应式为

$\mathrm{CH}_{4}(\mathrm{~g})+2 \mathrm{O}_{2}(\mathrm{~g})=\mathrm{CO}_{2}(\mathrm{~g})+2 \mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}(\mathrm{g})$

在过量空气系数 $\alpha=1.5$ 下的反应式为

$\begin{aligned} & \mathrm{CH}_{4}(\mathrm{~g})+1.5 \times 2 \mathrm{O}_{2}(\mathrm{~g})+1.5 \times 2 \times 3.76 \mathrm{~N}_{2}(\mathrm{~g})=\mathrm{CO}_{2}(\mathrm{~g})+2 \mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}(\mathrm{g})+1.5 \times 2 \times 3.76 \mathrm{~N}_{2}(\mathrm{~g}) \\ & \text { 于是 } \quad Q_{p}=\left[\Delta H_{\mathrm{f}, \mathrm{CO}_{2}}^{0}+2 \Delta H_{\mathrm{f}, \mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}(\mathrm{g})}^{0}-\Delta H_{\mathrm{f}, \mathrm{CH}_{4}(\mathrm{~g})}^{0}\right]+ \\ & {\left[\Delta H_{\mathrm{m}, \mathrm{CO}_{2}}\left|\frac{1800 \mathrm{~K}}{298 \mathrm{~K}}+2 \Delta H_{\mathrm{m}, \mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}(\mathrm{g})}^{0}\right| \frac{1800 \mathrm{~K}}{298 \mathrm{~K}}+\Delta H_{\mathrm{m}, \mathrm{O}_{2}}\left|\frac{1800 \mathrm{~K}}{298 \mathrm{~K}}+11.28 \Delta H_{\mathrm{m}, \mathrm{N}_{2}}\left|\frac{1800 \mathrm{~K}}{298 \mathrm{~K}}\right]-\right. \\ & {\left[\Delta H_{\mathrm{m}, \mathrm{CH}_{4}}\left|\frac{400 \mathrm{~K}}{298 \mathrm{~K}}+1.5 \times 2 \Delta H_{\mathrm{m}, \mathrm{O}_{2}}\left|\frac{500 \mathrm{~K}}{298 \mathrm{~K}}+1.5 \times 2 \times 3.76 \Delta H_{\mathrm{m}, \mathrm{N}_{2}}\left|\frac{500 \mathrm{~K}}{298 \mathrm{~K}}\right]\right.\right.}

其中 $\left.\Delta H_{\mathrm{m}, \mathrm{CH}_{4}}\right|_{298 \mathrm{~K}}^{400 \mathrm{~K}}=\int_{298 \mathrm{~K}}^{400 \mathrm{~K}} C_{p, \mathrm{~m}} d T$

$=\int_{298 \mathrm{~K}}^{400 \mathrm{~K}}\left(14.16+75.55 \times 10^{-3} T-18.00 \times 10^{-6} T^{2}\right) d T$
