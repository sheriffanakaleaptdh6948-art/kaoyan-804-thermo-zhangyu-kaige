3. 解: (1) 依题意, 取热源 A, B, C, D 及热机为孤立系, 则

$\left\{\begin{array}{l} W_{\text {net }}=Q_{A}-Q_{B}-Q_{C}-Q_{D} \\ \frac{Q_{A}}{T_{A}}+\frac{Q_{B}}{T_{B}}+\frac{Q_{C}}{T_{C}}+\frac{Q_{D}}{T_{D}}=0 \\ \frac{Q_{B}}{T_{B}}=\frac{Q_{C}}{T_{C}} \end{array}\right.$

代入, 求解可得:

$\left\{\begin{array}{l} Q_{\mathrm{B}}=300 \mathrm{~kJ} \\ Q_{\mathrm{C}}=225 \mathrm{~kJ} \\ Q_{\mathrm{D}}=-175 \mathrm{~kJ} \end{array}\right.$

(2) 4 个热源的熵变量:

$\begin{aligned} & \Delta S_{\mathrm{A}}=\frac{-500}{800}=-0.625 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{K} \\ & \Delta S_{\mathrm{B}}=\frac{Q_{\mathrm{B}}}{T_{\mathrm{B}}}=\frac{300}{400}=0.75 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{K} \\ & \Delta S_{\mathrm{C}}=\Delta S_{\mathrm{B}}=0.75 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{K} \\ & \Delta S_{\mathrm{D}}=\frac{Q_{\mathrm{D}}}{T_{\mathrm{D}}}=\frac{-175}{200}=-0.875 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{K} \end{aligned}$
