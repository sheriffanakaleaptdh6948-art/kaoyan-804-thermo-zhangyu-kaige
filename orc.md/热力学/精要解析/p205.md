例题 9-2 压气机中气体压缩后的温度不宜过高，取极限值为 $150 \, ^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$，吸入空气的压力和温度为 $p_{1} = 0.1 \, \text{MPa}$，$t_{1} = 20 \, ^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$。若压气机缸套中流过 $465 \, \text{kg/h}$ 的冷却水，在气缸套中的水温升高 $14 \, ^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$。求在单级压气机中压缩 $250 \, \text{m}^{3}/\text{h}$ 进气状态下空气可能达到的最高压力，及压气机必需的功率。

解法 1

(1) 压气机的产气量为

$q_{m} = \frac{p_{1} q_{V}}{R_{g} T_{1}} = \frac{0.1 \times 10^{6} \, \text{Pa} \times 250 \, \text{m}^{3}/\text{h}}{287 \, \text{J/(kg·K)} \times 293 \, \text{K}} = 297.3 \, \text{kg/h}$

(2) 求多变压缩过程的多变指数

根据能量守恒有 $Q_{\text{气}} = -Q_{\text{水}}$

即

$q_{m} c_{n} (T_{2} - T_{1}) = -q_{m, 水} c_{水} \Delta t_{水}$

$c_{n} = \frac{-q_{m, 水} c_{水} \Delta t_{水}}{q_{m} (T_{2} - T_{1})} = \frac{-465 \, \text{kg/h} \times 4 \, 187 \, \text{J/(kg·K)} \times 14 \, \text{K}}{297.3 \, \text{kg/h} (150-20) \, \text{K}}$

$= -702.5 \, \text{J/(kg·K)}$

又因

$c_{n} = \frac{n-\kappa}{n-1} c_{V} = \frac{n-\kappa}{n-1} \frac{5}{2} R_{g}$

即

$-702.5 \, \text{J/(kg·K)} = \frac{n-1.4}{n-1} \times \frac{5}{2} \times 287 \, \text{J/(kg·K)}$

解得

$n = 1.20$

(3) 求压气机的终压

$\begin{aligned}p_{2} &= p_{1} \left( \frac{T_{2}}{T_{1}} \right)^{n/(n-1)} = 0.1 \times 10^{6} \, \text{Pa} \times \left( \frac{423 \, \text{K}}{293 \, \text{K}} \right)^{1.20/0.20} \\&= 0.905 \times 10^{6} \, \text{Pa} = 0.905 \, \text{MPa}\end{aligned}$

(4) 求压气机的耗功

$\begin{aligned}\dot{W}_{\mathrm{t}} &= \frac{n}{n-1} q_{m} R_{\mathrm{g}} (T_{1} - T_{2}) = \frac{1.20}{1.20-1} \times 297.3 \, \text{kg/h} \times \frac{1}{3 \times 600} \, \text{h/s} \times \\&287 \, \text{J/(kg·K)} \times (293-423) \, \text{K} \\&= -18.45 \times 10^{3} \, \text{W} = -18.45 \, \text{kW}\end{aligned}$

解法 2

在求得压气机产气量 $q_{m}$ 后，再求压气机的耗功量为

$\begin{aligned}W_{\mathrm{t}} &= Q - \Delta H = -Q_{\text{水}} - \Delta H = -q_{m, 水} c_{\text{水}} \Delta t_{\text{水}} - q_{m} c_{p} (T_{2} - T_{1}) \\&= -465 \, \text{kg/h} \times \frac{1}{3 \times 600} \, \text{h/s} \times 4 \, 187 \, \text{J/(kg·K)} \times 14 \, \text{K} - \\&297 \, \text{kg/h} \times \frac{1}{3 \times 600} \, \text{h/s} \times 1004 \, \text{J/(kg·K)} \times (150-20) \, \text{K} \\&= -18.34 \times 10^{3} \, \text{W} = -18.34 \, \text{kW}\end{aligned}$

由

$W_{\mathrm{t}} = \frac{n}{n-1} q_{m} R_{\mathrm{g}} (T_{1} - T_{2})$
