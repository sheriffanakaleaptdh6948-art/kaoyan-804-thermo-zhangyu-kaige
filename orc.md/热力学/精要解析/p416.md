(1) 因 $\frac{p_{\mathrm{b}}}{p_{0}}=\frac{p_{\mathrm{b}}}{p_{1}}=\frac{0.2}{0.6}=0.33<\nu_{\mathrm{cr}}=0.528$

所以, 出口压力 $p_{2}=p_{\mathrm{cr}}=0.528 p_{1}=0.3168 \mathrm{MPa}$

$\begin{aligned} & T_{2}=T_{1}\left(\frac{p_{2}}{p_{1}}\right)^{\left(\kappa-1\right) / \kappa}=600 \times 0.528^{0.4 / 1.4}=500 \mathrm{~K} \\ & c_{12}=\sqrt{2 c_{p}\left(T_{1}-T_{2}\right)}=\sqrt{2 \times 1004(600-500)}=448.1 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s} \end{aligned}$

(2) $v_{2}=\frac{R_{\mathrm{g}} T_{2}}{p_{2}}=0.453 \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{kg}$

$q_{m}=\frac{c_{12} A_{2}}{v_{2}}=\frac{448.1 \times 25 \times 10^{-4}}{0.453}=2.48 \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{s}$

(2) (1) 绝热混合过程: 由稳定流动能量方程得

$\Delta H=0$

即

$\left(q_{m 1}+q_{m 2}\right) h=q_{m 1} h_{1}+q_{m 2} h_{2}$

$T=\frac{q_{m 1} T_{1}+q_{m 2} T_{2}}{q_{m 1}+q_{m 2}}=\frac{120 \times(400+273)+210 \times(150+273)}{120+210}=514 \mathrm{~K}$

(2) $\Delta S=q_{m 1} c_{p} \ln \frac{T}{T_{1}}+q_{m 2} c_{p} \ln \frac{T}{T_{2}}$

$\begin{aligned} & =120 \times 1.004 \ln \frac{514}{673}+210 \times 1.004 \ln \frac{514}{423}=8.62 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{K} \cdot \mathrm{h}) \end{aligned}$

(3) $\dot{Q}=q_{m} c_{p} \Delta T=(120+210) \times 1.004 \times[(400+273)+514]$

$=5.27 \times 10^{4} \mathrm{~kJ/h}=14.63 \mathrm{kW}$

(4) $\Delta S_{\text {工质 }}=q_{m} c_{p} \ln \frac{T_{4}}{T_{1}}=330 \times 1.004 \ln \frac{674}{514}=89.3 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{K} \cdot \mathrm{h})$

$\Delta S_{\mathrm{r}}=-\frac{\dot{Q}}{T_{\mathrm{r}}}=-\frac{5.27 \times 10^{4}}{500+273}=-68.22 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{K} \cdot \mathrm{h})$

$I=T_{0} \Delta S_{\text {iso }}=T_{0}\left(\Delta S_{\text {工质 }}+\Delta S_{\mathrm{r}}\right)=300 \times(89.3-68.22)=2.68 \times 10^{3} \mathrm{~kJ/h}$

(3) (1) 若压缩机出口温度为 $180^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$, 则绝热压缩过程的熵产

$\Delta s_{\mathrm{g}}=c_{p} \ln \frac{T_{2}}{T_{1}}-R_{\mathrm{g}} \ln \frac{p_{2}}{p_{1}}=1.004 \ln \frac{180+273}{20+273}-0.287 \ln \frac{0.6}{0.1}=-0.077 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})<0$
