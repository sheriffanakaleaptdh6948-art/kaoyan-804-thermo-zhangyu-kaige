水在高压锅炉的吸热量：

$\begin{aligned}Q_{\text {高 }} & =1 \times\left(h_{\mathrm{a}}-h_{3}^{\prime}\right)+1 \times\left(h_{1}-h_{\mathrm{b}}\right) \\& =1 \times\left(3415.9-121.3\right)+1 \times\left(3343-2918.5\right)=3719.1 \mathrm{~kJ}\end{aligned}$

水在低压锅炉的吸热量：

$Q_{\text {低 }}=1 \times\left(h_{1}-h_{3}^{\prime}\right)=1 \times\left(3343-121.3\right)=3221.7 \mathrm{~kJ}$

b 点: $s_{\mathrm{a}}=s_{\mathrm{b}}=6.4049, p_{\mathrm{b}}=3 \mathrm{MPa}, h_{\mathrm{b}}=2918.5 \mathrm{~kJ/kg}$

2 点: $s_{2}=s_{1}=7.0817 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})$

$\begin{aligned}& x_{2}=\frac{s_{2}-s_{2}^{\prime}}{s_{2}^{\prime \prime}-s_{2}}=\frac{7.0817-0.4221}{8.4725-0.4221}=0.827 \\& h_{2}=h_{2}^{\prime}+\left(h_{2}^{\prime \prime}-h_{2}^{\prime}\right) x_{2}=121.3+\left(2553.5-121.3\right) \times 0.827=2132.73 \mathrm{~kJ/kg}\end{aligned}$

高压汽轮机作功量: $W_{\text {高 }}=m_{\text {高 }}\left(h_{\mathrm{a}}-h_{\mathrm{b}}\right)=1 \times\left(3415.9-2918.5\right)=497.4 \mathrm{~kJ}$

低压汽轮机作功量: $W_{\text {低 }}=\left(m_{\text {高 }}+m_{\text {低 }}\right)\left(h_{1}-h_{2}\right)=2 \times\left(3343-2132.73\right)=2420.54 \mathrm{~kJ}$

(4) 整个循环的放热量:

$q_{\text {放 }}=2 \times\left(h_{2}-h_{3}\right)=2 \times(2132.73-121.3)=4022.86 \mathrm{~kJ}$

(5) 整个循环的热效率:

$\eta=\frac{W_{\text {net }}}{Q_{\text {吸 }}}=\frac{W_{\text {高 }}+W_{\text {低 }}}{Q_{\text {高 }}+Q_{\text {低 }}}=\frac{497.4+2420.54}{3719.1+3221.7}=42.04 \%$
