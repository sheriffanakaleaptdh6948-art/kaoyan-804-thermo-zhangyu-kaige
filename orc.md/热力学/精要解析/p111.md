对于 1 kg 的燃气，$\Delta e_{x \text {,iso }}=e_{x \text {,H }_{2}}-e_{x \text {,H }_{1}}+\omega_{1}$

其中

$\begin{aligned} e_{x, H_{2}}-e_{x, H_{1}} & =\left(h_{2}-h_{1}\right)-T_{0}\left(s_{2}-s_{1}\right) \\ & =c_{p}\left(T_{2}-T_{1}\right)-T_{0}\left(c_{p} \ln \frac{T_{2}}{T_{1}}-R_{g} \ln \frac{p_{2}}{p_{1}}\right) \\ & =1.004 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})\left(495-800\right) \mathrm{K}-300 \mathrm{~K}\left[1.004 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \ln \frac{495}{800}\right. \\ & \left.-0.287 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \ln \frac{0.7 \mathrm{MPa}}{5.5 \mathrm{MPa}}\right] \\ & =-339.1 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \\ w_{1} & =-\Delta h=c_{p}\left(T_{1}-T_{2}\right)=306.2 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \end{aligned}$

于是 $\Delta e_{x \text {,iso }}=(-399.1+306.2) \mathrm{kJ} / \mathrm{kg}=-32.9 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}<0$

所以第二组参数是正确的。
