空气在回热器中定压加热，则 \(q_{p} = \int_{T_{1}}^{T_{2}} c_{p} \, \mathrm{d}T = \int_{T_{1}}^{T_{2}} \frac{C_{p,\text{m}}}{M} \, \mathrm{d}T\)

又

$$C_{p,\text{m}} = a_{0} + a_{1} T + a_{2} T^{2}$$

据空气的摩尔定压热容公式，得

$$a_{0} = 28.15, \quad a_{1} = 1.967 \times 10^{-3}, \quad a_{2} = 4.801 \times 10^{-6}$$

故

$\begin{aligned}q_{p} & = \int_{T_{1}}^{T_{2}} \frac{C_{p,\text{m}}}{M} \, \mathrm{d}T = \frac{1}{M} \int_{T_{1}}^{T_{2}} (a_{0} + a_{1} T + a_{2} T^{2}) \, \mathrm{d}T \\& = \left. \left( \frac{1}{M} (a_{0} T + \frac{a_{1}}{2} T^{2} + \frac{a_{2}}{3} T^{3}) \right) \right|_{T_{1}}^{T_{2}} \\& = \frac{1}{28.97} \left[ 28.15 \times (600 - 400) + \frac{1.967 \times 10^{-3}}{2} (600^{2} - 400^{2}) + \frac{4.801 \times 10^{-6}}{3} (600^{3} - 400^{3}) \right] = 209.53 \, \text{kJ/kg}\end{aligned}$

(2) 按平均比热容计算

$q_{p} = \left. c_{p} \right|_{0} ^{t_{2}} t_{2} - c_{p} \left. \right|_{0} ^{t_{1}} t_{1}$

查平均比热容表

$t = 100^{\circ} \mathrm{C}, c_{p} = 1.006 \, \text{kJ/(kg·K)}$

$t = 200^{\circ} \mathrm{C}, c_{p} = 1.012 \, \text{kJ/(kg·K)}$

$t = 300^{\circ} \mathrm{C}, c_{p} = 1.019 \, \text{kJ/(kg·K)}$

$t = 400^{\circ} \mathrm{C}, c_{p} = 1.028 \, \text{kJ/(kg·K)}$

用线性内插法，得

$\begin{aligned}c_{p} \left|_{0}^{127} = c_{p} \right|_{0}^{100} + \frac{c_{p} \left|_{0}^{200} - c_{p} \right|_{0}^{100}}{200 - 100} \times (127 - 100) \\= 1.006 + \frac{1.012 - 1.006}{100} \times 27 \\= 1.0076 \, \text{kJ/(kg·K)}\\c_{p} \left|_{0}^{327} = c_{p} \right|_{0}^{300} + \frac{c_{p} \left|_{0}^{400} - c_{p} \right|_{0}^{300}}{400 - 300} \times (327 - 300) \\= 1.019 + \frac{1.028 - 1.019}{100} \times 27 \\= 1.0214 \, \text{kJ/(kg·K)}\end{aligned}$

故

$q_{p} = 1.0214 \times 327 - 1.0076 \times 127 = 206.03 \, \text{kJ/kg}$
