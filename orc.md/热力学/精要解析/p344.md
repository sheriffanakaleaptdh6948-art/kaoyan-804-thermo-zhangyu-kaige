2. 解: 参见图所示, 燃气轮机的理想出口温度:

$T_{2 s}=T_{1}\left(\frac{p_{2}}{p_{1}}\right)^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}}=1000 \times\left(\frac{0.1}{1}\right)^{\frac{1.4-1}{1.4}}=518.0 \mathrm{~K}$

燃气轮机的实际出口温度:

根据

$\eta_{\mathrm{T}}=\frac{h_{1}-h_{2}}{h_{1}-h_{2 s}}=\frac{T_{1}-T_{2}}{T_{1}-T_{2 s}}$

所以 $T_{2}=T_{1}-\eta_{\mathrm{T}}\left(T_{1}-T_{2 s}\right)=1000-0.8 \times(1000-518.0)=614.4 \mathrm{~K}$

(1)燃气轮机对外作功量:

$W_{\mathrm{T}}=m c_{p}\left(T_{1}-T_{2}\right)=1 \times 1.004 \times(1000-614.4)=387.14 \mathrm{~kJ}$

(2)系统总熵增:

$\begin{aligned} \Delta S & =m\left(c_{p} \ln \frac{T_{2}}{T_{1}}-R_{\mathrm{s}} \ln \frac{p_{2}}{p_{1}}\right)=1 \times(1.004 \ln \frac{614.4}{1000}-0.287 \ln \frac{0.1}{1}) \\ & =0.1717 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{K} \end{aligned}$

(3)系统作功能力损失:

$I=T_{0} \Delta S=300 \times 0.1717=51.51 \mathrm{~kJ}$
