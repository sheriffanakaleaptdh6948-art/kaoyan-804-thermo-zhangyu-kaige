若以质量为单位, 则按质量分数 $\omega_{i}$ 加权平均, 即

$\begin{aligned} & u=\sum_{i} \omega_{i} u_{i}(T) \\ & h=\sum_{i} \omega_{i} h_{i}(T) \\ & c_{p}=\sum_{i} \omega_{i} c_{p, i}(T) \\ & c_{V}=\sum_{i} \omega_{i} c_{V, i}(T) \\ & s=\sum_{i} \omega_{i} s_{i}\left(T, p_{i}\right) \\ & e_{x}=\sum_{i} \omega_{i} e_{x, i}\left(T, p_{i}\right) \end{aligned}$

若以摩尔为单位, 则按摩尔分数 $x_{i}$ 加权平均, 即

$\begin{aligned} & U_{\mathrm{m}}=\sum_{i} x_{i} U_{\mathrm{m}, i}(T) \\ & H_{\mathrm{m}}=\sum_{i} x_{i} H_{\mathrm{m}, i}(T) \\ & C_{p, \mathrm{~m}}=\sum_{i} x_{i} C_{p, \mathrm{~m}, i}(T) \\ & C_{V, \mathrm{~m}}=\sum_{i} x_{i} C_{V, \mathrm{~m}, i}(T) \\ & S_{\mathrm{m}}=\sum_{i} x_{i} S_{\mathrm{m}, i}\left(T, p_{i}\right) \\ & E_{\mathrm{x}, \mathrm{m}}=\sum_{i} x_{i} E_{\mathrm{x}, \mathrm{m}, i}\left(T, p_{i}\right) \end{aligned}$

在成分无变化的混合气体进行的热力过程中, 其热力学能、焓和熵的变化为

$\begin{aligned} & \mathrm{d} u=\sum_{i} w_{i} c_{V i} \mathrm{~d} T \\ & \mathrm{~d} h=\sum_{i} w_{i} c_{p i} \mathrm{~d} T \\ & \mathrm{~d} s=\sum_{i} w_{i} c_{p i} \frac{\mathrm{d} T}{T}-\sum_{i} w_{i} R_{\mathrm{g}, i} \frac{\mathrm{d} p_{i}}{p_{i}} \end{aligned}$

或

$\begin{aligned} & \mathrm{d} U_{\mathrm{m}}=\sum_{i} x_{i} C_{\mathrm{V}, \mathrm{m}, i} \mathrm{~d} T \\ & \mathrm{~d} H_{\mathrm{m}}=\sum_{i} x_{i} C_{p, \mathrm{~m}, i} \mathrm{~d} T \\ & \mathrm{~d} S_{\mathrm{m}}=\sum_{i} x_{i} C_{p, \mathrm{~m}, i} \frac{\mathrm{d} T}{T}-\sum_{i} x_{i} R \frac{\mathrm{d} p_{i}}{p} \end{aligned}$

值得指出的是, 公式中各组元气体的熵和焓是在各自分压力 $p_{i}$ 下计算的, 不要误以为是混合物总压力 $p$ 下的计算值。其分压力计算式为

$p_{i}=x_{i} p$
