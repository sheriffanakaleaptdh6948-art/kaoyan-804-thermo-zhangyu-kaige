$$\begin{aligned}P &= \sum_{i} q_{m} \omega_{1,i} = 2 \frac{n}{n-1} p_{1} \dot{V}_{1} \left[ 1 - \left( \frac{p_{2}}{p_{1}} \right)^{(n-1)/n} \right] \\&= 2 \frac{n}{n-1} p_{3} \dot{V}_{3} \left[ 1 - \left( \frac{p_{4}}{p_{3}} \right)^{(n-1)/n} \right]\end{aligned}$$

因为

$$\frac{p_{4}}{p_{3}} \dot{V}_{4} = \frac{T_{4}}{T_{3}} = \left( \frac{p_{4}}{p_{3}} \right)^{(n-1)/n}$$

故

$$p_{3} \dot{V}_{3} = \frac{p_{4} \dot{V}_{4}}{\left( p_{4} / p_{3} \right)^{(n-1)/n}}$$

所以

$\begin{aligned}P &= 2 \frac{n}{n-1} p_{4} \dot{V}_{4} \left[ \left( \frac{p_{4}}{p_{3}} \right)^{(1-n)/n} - 1 \right] \\&= 2 \frac{1.2}{1.2-1} 6.4 \times 10^{6} \text{Pa} \times 4 \text{m}^{3} / \text{h} \left[ \left( \frac{6.4 \times 10^{6} \text{Pa}}{0.8 \times 10^{6} \text{Pa}} \right)^{(1-1.2)/1.2} - 1 \right] \\&= -899 \, 74 \times 10^{3} \, \text{J/h} = -25.0 \times 10^{3} \, \text{W} = -25.0 \, \text{kW}\end{aligned}$

(2) 总的冷却水量

空气的终温 \(T_{4}\)

$\begin{aligned}T_{3} &= T_{1} = 300 \, \text{K} \\T_{4} &= T_{3} \left( \frac{p_{4}}{p_{3}} \right)^{(n-1)/n} = 300 \, \text{K} \times 8^{(1.2-1)/1.2} = 424.3 \, \text{K}\end{aligned}$

空气质量流量为

$q_{m} = \frac{p_{4} \dot{V}_{4}}{R_{\text{g}} T_{4}} = \frac{6.4 \times 10^{6} \text{Pa} \times 4 \, \text{m}^{3} / \text{h}}{287 \, \text{J/(kg·K)} \times 424.3 \, \text{K}} = 210.2 \, \text{kg/h}$

压缩空气在多变压缩过程中，对冷却水放出的热流量 \(\dot{Q}_{n}\)（也是冷却水流经压气机水套时带走的热流量）

$\begin{aligned}\dot{Q}_{n} &= 2 \Delta \dot{H} + \dot{W}_{\mathrm{t}} = 2 q_{m} c_{p} (T_{2} - T_{1}) + P \\&= 2 \times 210.2 \, \text{kg/h} \times 1004 \, \text{J/(kg·K)} (424.3 - 300) \, \text{K} - 899 \, 74 \times 10^{3} \, \text{J/h} \\&= -37.51 \times 10^{6} \, \text{J/h}\end{aligned}$

压缩空气在中间冷器中，对冷却水放出的热流量 \(\dot{Q}_{p}\)（也就是冷却水流经中间冷器时带走的热流量）

$\begin{aligned}\dot{Q}_{p} &= q_{m} c_{p} (T_{3} - T_{2}) = 210.2 \, \text{kg/h} \times 1004 \, \text{J/(kg·K)} (300 - 424.3) \, \text{K} \\&= -26.23 \times 10^{6} \, \text{J/h}\end{aligned}$

冷却水流经压气机水套及中间冷器时，带走总的热流量 \(\dot{Q}\)

$\begin{aligned}\dot{Q} &= (\dot{Q}_{n} + \dot{Q}_{p}) = (37.51 \times 10^{6} + 26.23 \times 10^{6}) \, \text{J/h} \\&= 63.74 \times 10^{6} \, \text{J/h}\end{aligned}$

每小时流经压气机水套及中间冷器总的冷却水量为

$q_{m,\text{H}_{2}\text{O}} = \frac{\dot{Q}}{c_{p,\text{H}_{2}\text{O}} \Delta t} = \frac{63.74 \times 10^{6} \, \text{J/h}}{4187 \, \text{J/(kg·K)} \times 15 \, \text{K}} = 1014.9 \, \text{kg/h}$
