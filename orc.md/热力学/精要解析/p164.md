(2) 若再热器热源温度为 \(t_{\mathrm{HR}}=900 \mathrm{~K}\)，试求不可逆传热引起的熵产；

(3) 在 \(T-S\) 图上定性画出过程。

按定值比热容计算，\(c_{p, \mathrm{CO}_{2}}=0.845 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})\)，\(c_{p, \mathrm{~N}_{2}}=1.04 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})\)。

解 (1) 先求混合气体的物性参数

$\begin{aligned} c_{p} & =\sum_{i} w_{i} c_{p, i}=w_{\mathrm{CO}_{2}} c_{p, \mathrm{CO}_{2}}+w_{\mathrm{N}_{2}} c_{p, \mathrm{~N}_{2}} \\ & =\left(0.4 \times 0.845+0.6 \times 1.04\right) \mathrm{kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \\ & =0.962 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \end{aligned}$

$\begin{aligned} R_{\mathrm{g}} & =\sum_{i} w_{i} R_{\mathrm{g}, i}=w_{\mathrm{CO}_{2}} R_{\mathrm{g}, \mathrm{CO}_{2}}+w_{\mathrm{N}_{2}} R_{\mathrm{R}, \mathrm{N}_{2}} \\ & =0.4 \times \frac{8.314 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{mol} \cdot \mathrm{K})}{44 \times 10^{-3} \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{mol}}+0.6 \times \frac{8.314 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{mol} \cdot \mathrm{K})}{28 \times 10^{-3} \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{mol}} \\ & =0.254 \times 10^{3} \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})=0.254 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \\ c_{V} & =c_{p}-R_{\mathrm{g}}=(0.962-0.254) \mathrm{kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})=0.708 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \\ & \kappa=\frac{c_{p}}{c_{V}}=1.36 \end{aligned}$

再求状态 2 点的温度，由

$Q_{23}=m c_{p}\left(T_{3}-T_{2}\right)=m c_{p}\left(T_{1}-T_{2}\right)$

得

$T_{2}=T_{1}-\frac{Q}{m c_{p}}=1000 \mathrm{~K}-\frac{460 \times 10^{3} \mathrm{~J}}{2 \mathrm{~kg} \cdot 962 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})}=760.9 \mathrm{~K}$

则

$\begin{aligned} p_{2} & =p_{1}\left(\frac{T_{2}}{T_{1}}\right)^{\kappa(\kappa-1)}=0.8 \times 10^{6} \mathrm{~Pa}\left(\frac{760.9 \mathrm{~K}}{1000 \mathrm{~K}}\right)^{1.36 / 0.36}=0.285 \times 10^{6} \mathrm{~Pa} \\ T_{4} & =T_{3}\left(\frac{p_{4}}{p_{3}}\right)^{(\kappa-1) / \kappa}=1000 \mathrm{~K} \times\left(\frac{0.1 \times 10^{6} \mathrm{~Pa}}{0.285 \times 10^{6} \mathrm{~Pa}}\right)^{0.36 / 1.36}=757.9 \mathrm{~K} \end{aligned}$

于是，两个可逆绝热膨胀过程共作技术功为

$\begin{aligned} W_{\mathrm{t}} & =W_{\mathrm{t}, 12}+W_{\mathrm{t}, 34}=m c_{p}\left[\left(T_{1}-T_{2}\right)+\left(T_{3}-T_{4}\right)\right] \\ & =2 \times 962 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})[(1000-760.9) \mathrm{K}+(1000-757.9) \mathrm{K}] \\ & =925.8 \times 10^{3} \mathrm{~J}=925.8 \mathrm{~kJ} \end{aligned}$

(2) 求传热过程 \(2-3\) 引起的熵产

热源熵的变化

$\Delta S_{\mathrm{HR}}=\frac{Q}{T_{\mathrm{HR}}}=\frac{-460 \mathrm{~kJ}}{(900+273) \mathrm{K}}=-0.392 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{K}$

过程 \(2-3\) 混合气体熵的变化

$\begin{aligned} \Delta S & =m c_{p} \ln \frac{T_{3}}{T_{2}}=2 \mathrm{~kg} \times 962 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \ln \frac{1000 \mathrm{~K}}{760.9 \mathrm{~K}} \\ & =0.526 \times 10^{3} \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{K}=0.526 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{K} \end{aligned}$

故不可逆传热引起的熵产

$\Delta S_{\mathrm{g}}=\Delta S_{\mathrm{HR}}+\Delta S=0.134 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{K}$
