2. (1) 当可逆机在 A, B 两物体间工作时, 它们的温度在不断变化, 设两物体的平衡温度为 \(T_{\mathrm{m}}\) 。根据孤立系熵增原理, 有

$\begin{aligned} \Delta S_{\text {iso }} & =\Delta S_{\mathrm{A}}+\Delta S_{\mathrm{B}}+\Delta S_{\mathrm{E}} \\ & =\int_{T_{\mathrm{A}}}^{T_{\mathrm{m}}} \frac{m_{\mathrm{A}} c \mathrm{~d} T}{T}+\int_{T_{\mathrm{B}}}^{T_{\mathrm{m}}} \frac{m_{\mathrm{B}} c \mathrm{~d} T}{T}+0=0 \end{aligned}$

即 \(2 m_{\mathrm{A}} \mathrm{cln} \frac{T_{\mathrm{m}}}{T_{\mathrm{A}}}+m_{\mathrm{B}} \mathrm{cln} \frac{T_{\mathrm{m}}}{T_{\mathrm{B}}}=0\)

$\begin{aligned} & \ln \left(\frac{T_{\mathrm{m}}}{T_{\mathrm{A}}}\right)^{2}\left(\frac{T_{\mathrm{m}}}{T_{\mathrm{B}}}\right)=0 \\ & \frac{T_{\mathrm{m}}^{3}}{T_{\mathrm{A}}^{2} T_{\mathrm{B}}}=1 \quad \text { 即 } T_{\mathrm{m}}=\sqrt[3]{T_{\mathrm{A}}^{2} T_{\mathrm{B}}} \end{aligned}$

于是可逆机输出的最大功为

$\begin{aligned} W_{\max } & =Q_{1}-Q_{2}=m_{\mathrm{A}} c\left(T_{\mathrm{A}}-T_{\mathrm{m}}\right)-m_{\mathrm{B}} c\left(T_{\mathrm{m}}-T_{\mathrm{B}}\right) \\ & =m_{\mathrm{B}} c\left[2\left(T_{\mathrm{A}}-T_{\mathrm{m}}-T_{\mathrm{m}}+T_{\mathrm{B}}\right)\right]=m_{\mathrm{B}} c\left(2 T_{\mathrm{A}}+T_{\mathrm{B}}-3 T_{\mathrm{m}}\right) \\ & =m_{\mathrm{B}} c\left(2 T_{\mathrm{A}}+T_{\mathrm{B}}-3 \sqrt[3]{T_{\mathrm{A}}^{2} T_{\mathrm{B}}}\right) \end{aligned}$

(2) \(\mathrm{d} h=c_{p} \mathrm{~dT}-\left[T\left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial T}\right)_{p}-v\right] \mathrm{dp}\)

对于等温过程, 则

$\mathrm{d} h=\left[v-T\left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial T}\right)_{p}\right] \mathrm{dp}$

由所给状态方程得

$\left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial T}\right)_{p}=\frac{R_{\mathrm{g}}}{p}-2 C T^{-3}$

代入式 A

$\begin{aligned} \mathrm{d} h & =\left[v-\frac{R_{\mathrm{g}} T}{p}+2 C T^{-2}\right] \mathrm{dp}=\left[v-\left(\frac{R_{\mathrm{g}} T}{p}+\frac{\mathrm{C}}{T^{2}}\right)+\frac{3 \mathrm{C}}{T^{2}}\right] \mathrm{dp} \\ & =\left(v-v+\frac{3 \mathrm{C}}{T^{2}}\right) \mathrm{dp}=\frac{3 \mathrm{C}}{T^{2}} \mathrm{dp} \end{aligned}$
