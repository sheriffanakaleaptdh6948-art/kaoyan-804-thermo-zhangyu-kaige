解 (1) 压气机的耗功率

$\begin{aligned}& \pi_{\text {opt }}=\sqrt{\frac{6.4 \mathrm{MPa}}{1.0 \mathrm{MPa}}}=8 \\& P=\frac{2 q_{m} n R_{\mathrm{g}} T_{1}}{n-1}\left[1-\pi^{\left(\frac{n-1}{n}\right)}\right]=\frac{2 q_{m} n R T_{1}}{(n-1) M}\left[1-\pi^{\left(\frac{n-1}{n}\right)}\right] \\& \frac{2 \times 2.16 \times 10^{3} \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{h} \times \frac{1}{3600} \mathrm{~h} / \mathrm{s} \times 1.3 \times 8.314 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{mol} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times 300 \mathrm{~K} \\& =\frac{(1.3-1) \times 28 \times 10^{-3} \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{mol}}{\times} \\& {\left[1-8^{\left(\frac{1}{3}-1\right) / 1.3}\right]} \\& =-285.3 \times 10^{3} \mathrm{~W}=-285.3 \mathrm{kW}\end{aligned}$

(2) 压气机的排气温度

$T_{2}=T_{1} \pi^{\left(\frac{n-1}{n}\right)}=300 \mathrm{~K} \times 8^{\left(\frac{1}{3}-1\right) / 1.3}=484.8 \mathrm{~K}$

对中间冷却器列出稳定流动能量方程

$\Delta \dot{H}_{\mathrm{N}_{2}}+\Delta \dot{\mathrm{H}}_{\mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}}=0$

$q_{m} c_{p}\left(T_{3}-T_{2}\right)+q_{m, \mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}} c_{p, \mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}}\left(T_{2, \mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}}-T_{1, \mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}}\right)=0$

则中冷器的出口水温

$\begin{aligned}T_{2, \mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}} & =\frac{q_{m} c_{p}\left(T_{2}-T_{3}\right)}{q_{m, \mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}} c_{p, \mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}}+T_{1, \mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}}} \\& \frac{\left(\frac{2.16 \times 10^{3}}{3600} \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{s} \times \frac{7}{2} \times \frac{8.314 \mathrm{~J}}{\mathrm{~mol} \cdot \mathrm{K}}\right)\left(484.8-300\right) \mathrm{K}}{1.2 \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{s} \times 4187 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})} \\& +(24+273) \mathrm{K}=319.9 \mathrm{~K}\end{aligned}$

水在冷却器中的熵增

$\begin{aligned}\Delta \dot{S}_{\mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}} & =\int_{\mathrm{T}_{1}}^{\mathrm{T}_{2}} \frac{\partial \mathrm{Q}_{\mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}}}{\mathrm{T}}=\int_{\mathrm{T}_{1}}^{\mathrm{T}_{2}} \frac{q_{m, \mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}} c_{p, \mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}} \mathrm{dT}}{\mathrm{T}}=q_{m, \mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}} c_{p, \mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}} \ln \frac{\mathrm{T}_{2, \mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}}}{\mathrm{T}_{1, \mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}}} \\& =1.2 \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{s} \times 4187 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \ln \frac{319.9 \mathrm{~K}}{297 \mathrm{~K}} \\& =373.2 \mathrm{~W} / \mathrm{K}\end{aligned}$

氮气在冷却器中的熵增

$\begin{aligned}\Delta \dot{S} & =q_{m} c_{p} \ln \left(\frac{T_{3}}{T_{2}}\right) \\& =\left(\frac{2.16 \times 10^{3}}{3600}\right) \mathrm{kg} / \mathrm{s} \times \frac{7}{2} \times \frac{8.314 \mathrm{~J}}{28 \times 10^{-3} \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{mol}} \ln \frac{300 \mathrm{~K}}{484.8 \mathrm{~K}} \\& =-298.8 \mathrm{~W} / \mathrm{K}\end{aligned}$

冷却器中传热过程引起的熵产

$\Delta \dot{S}_{\mathrm{g}}=\Delta \dot{\mathrm{S}}+\Delta \dot{\mathrm{S}}_{\mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}}=(373.2-298.8) \mathrm{W} / \mathrm{K}=74.4 \mathrm{~W} / \mathrm{K}$

有效能损失

$I=T_{0} \Delta \dot{S}_{\mathrm{g}}=297 \mathrm{~K} \times 74.4 \mathrm{~W} / \mathrm{K}=22.1 \times 10^{3} \mathrm{~W}=22.1 \mathrm{kW}$
