点 2: $p_{2}=\pi p_{1}=6 \times 10^{5} \mathrm{~Pa}$

$\begin{aligned}T_{2} & =T_{1}\left(\frac{p_{2}}{p_{1}}\right)^{\left(\kappa-1\right) / \kappa}=T_{1} \pi^{\left(\kappa-1\right) / \kappa} \\& =293 \mathrm{~K} \times 6^{0.4 / 1.4}=488.9 \mathrm{~K} \\T_{2^{\prime}} & =\frac{T_{2}-T_{1}}{\eta_{c, s}}+T_{1} \\& =\frac{488.9 \mathrm{~K}-293 \mathrm{~K}}{0.82}+293 \mathrm{~K}=531.9 \mathrm{~K}\end{aligned}$

点 3: $p_{3}=6 \times 10^{5} \mathrm{~Pa}, T_{3}=1073 \mathrm{~K}$

点 4: $T_{4}=T_{3} \frac{1}{\pi^{(\kappa-1) / \kappa}}=1073 \mathrm{~K} \times \frac{1}{6^{0.4 / 1.4}}=643.1 \mathrm{~K}$

$T_{4^{\prime}}=T_{3}-\eta_{\mathrm{T}}\left(T_{3}-T_{4}\right)=1073 \mathrm{~K}-0.85(1073 \mathrm{~K}-643.1 \mathrm{~K})=707.6 \mathrm{~K}$

(1) 装置的净功比 $R$

$\begin{aligned}R & =\frac{w_{\text {net }}}{w_{T}^{\prime}}=\frac{\left(T_{3}-T_{4^{\prime}}\right)-\left(T_{2^{\prime}}-T_{1}\right)}{T_{3}-T_{4^{\prime}}} \\& =\frac{(1073 \mathrm{~K}-707.6 \mathrm{~K})-(531.9 \mathrm{~K}-293 \mathrm{~K})}{1073 \mathrm{~K}-707.6 \mathrm{~K}}=0.3462\end{aligned}$

(2) 装置的热效率

$\eta=1-\frac{T_{4^{\prime}}-T_{1}}{T_{3}-T_{2^{\prime}}}=1-\frac{707.6 \mathrm{~K}-293 \mathrm{~K}}{1073 \mathrm{~K}-531.9 \mathrm{~K}}=23.4 \%$

(3) 吸入热量 $q_{1}$ 中的可用能

$\begin{aligned}e_{\mathrm{x}, q} & =q_{1}-T_{0}\left(s_{3}-s_{2^{\prime}}\right) \leqslant c_{p}\left(T_{3}-T_{2^{\prime}}\right)-T_{0} c_{p} \ln \frac{T_{3}}{T_{2^{\prime}}} \\& =1004 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})[(1073 \mathrm{~K}-531.9 \mathrm{~K})-293 \mathrm{~K} \times \ln \frac{1073 \mathrm{~K}}{531.9 \mathrm{~K}}] \\& =336.82 \times 10^{3} \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{kg}=336.82 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}\end{aligned}$

(4) 压缩机引起的可用能损失

$\begin{aligned}i_{\mathrm{c}} & =T_{0} \Delta s_{\mathrm{g}, 12^{\prime}}=T_{0}\left(s_{2^{\prime}}-s_{1}\right)=T_{0}\left(s_{2^{\prime}}-s_{2}\right)=T_{0} c_{p} \ln \frac{T_{2^{\prime}}}{T_{2}} \\& =293 \mathrm{~K} \times 1004 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \ln \frac{531.9 \mathrm{~K}}{488.9 \mathrm{~K}} \\& =24.80 \times 10^{3} \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{kg}=24.80 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}\end{aligned}$

燃气轮机引起的可用能损失为

$\begin{aligned}i_{\mathrm{T}} & =T_{0} \Delta s_{\mathbf{g}, 34^{\prime}}=T_{0}\left(s_{4^{\prime}}-s_{3}\right)=T_{0}\left(s_{4^{\prime}}-s_{4}\right)=T_{0} c_{p} \ln \frac{T_{4^{\prime}}}{T_{4}} \\& =293 \mathrm{~K} \times 1004 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \ln \frac{707.6 \mathrm{~K}}{643.1 \mathrm{~K}}=28.12 \times 10^{3} \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{kg}=28.12 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}\end{aligned}$
