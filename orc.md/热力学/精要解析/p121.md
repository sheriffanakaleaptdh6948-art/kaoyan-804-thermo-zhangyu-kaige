4 个基本关系式

$\mathrm{d} u=T \mathrm{~d} s-p \mathrm{~d} v$

$\mathrm{d} h=T \mathrm{~d} s+v \mathrm{~d} p$

$\mathrm{df}=-\mathrm{s} \mathrm{dT}-p \mathrm{~d} v$

$\mathrm{dg}=-\mathrm{s} \mathrm{dT}+v \mathrm{~d} p$

麦克斯韦方程

$\left(\frac{\partial T}{\partial v}\right)_{s}=-\left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial s}\right)_{v},\left(\frac{\partial T}{\partial p}\right)_{v}=\left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial s}\right)_{p}$

$\left(\frac{\partial s}{\partial p}\right)_{T}=\left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial T}\right)_{v},\left(\frac{\partial s}{\partial p}\right)_{T}=-\left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial T}\right)_{p}$

$\text { 麦克斯韦方程建立了不可测量的熵 } s \text { 与可测量参数 } p 、 v 、 T \text { 间的偏导数关系式 }$

熵方程

$\begin{aligned} & \mathrm{d} \mathrm{s}=c_{\mathrm{v}} \frac{\mathrm{dT}}{\mathrm{T}}+\left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial T}\right)_{\mathrm{v}} \mathrm{dv} \\ & \mathrm{d} \mathrm{s}=c_{p} \frac{\mathrm{dT}}{\mathrm{T}}-\left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial T}\right)_{p} \mathrm{~dp} \end{aligned}$

热力学能方程

$\mathrm{d} u=c_{\mathrm{v}} \mathrm{dT}+\left[T\left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial T}\right)_{\mathrm{v}}-p\right] \mathrm{dv}$

$\text { 热力学能方程建立了不可测量的热力学能 } u \text { 与可测量参数 } p 、$

焓方程

$\mathrm{d} h=c_{p} \mathrm{dT}+\left[v-T\left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial T}\right)\right]_{p} \mathrm{dp}$

给出了不可测量的焓 $h$ 与可测量参数 $p 、 v 、 T$ 间的关系式

比热容方程

$\begin{aligned} & \left(\frac{\partial c_{\mathrm{v}}}{\partial v}\right)_{\mathrm{T}}=T\left(\frac{\partial^{2} p}{\partial T^{2}}\right)_{\mathrm{v}} \\ & \left(\frac{\partial c_{p}}{\partial p}\right)_{\mathrm{T}}=-T\left(\frac{\partial^{2} v}{\partial T^{2}}\right)_{p} \\ & c_{p}-c_{\mathrm{v}}=T\left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial T}\right)_{\mathrm{v}}\left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial T}\right)_{p} \\ & c_{p}-c_{\mathrm{v}}=-T\left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial T}\right)_{p}^{2}\left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial v}\right)_{\mathrm{T}} \end{aligned}$

$\left(\frac{\partial c_{\mathrm{v}}}{\partial v}\right)_{\mathrm{T}}=T\left(\frac{\partial^{2} p}{\partial T^{2}}\right)_{\mathrm{v}}$

给出了比热容与状态方程式间的关系式

$c_{p}$ 与 $c_{v}$ 之间的关系式

克拉贝龙方程

$\begin{aligned} \frac{\mathrm{d} p_{\mathrm{s}}}{\mathrm{dT}_{\mathrm{s}}} & =\frac{\mathrm{s}^{\prime \prime}-\mathrm{s}^{\prime}}{\mathrm{v}^{\prime \prime}-\mathrm{v}^{\prime}}=\frac{\mathrm{h}^{\prime \prime}-\mathrm{h}^{\prime}}{\mathrm{T}_{\mathrm{s}}\left(\mathrm{v}^{\prime \prime}-\mathrm{v}^{\prime}\right)} \\ & =\frac{\mathrm{r}}{\mathrm{T}_{\mathrm{s}}\left(\mathrm{v}^{\prime \prime}-\mathrm{v}^{\prime}\right)} \end{aligned}$

$\frac{\mathrm{d} p_{\mathrm{s}}}{\mathrm{dT}_{\mathrm{s}}}=\frac{\mathrm{s}^{\prime \prime}-\mathrm{s}^{\prime}}{\mathrm{v}^{\prime \prime}-\mathrm{v}^{\prime}}=\frac{\mathrm{h}^{\prime \prime}-\mathrm{h}^{\prime}}{\mathrm{T}_{\mathrm{s}}\left(\mathrm{v}^{\prime \prime}-\mathrm{v}^{\prime}\right)}$
