参见图, 现有卡诺循环 $1-2-3-4-1$, 其热效率为

$\eta_{\mathrm{v}, \mathrm{c}}=1-\frac{T_{2}}{T_{1}}$

如用逆卡诺循环 $1^{\prime}-2-3-4^{\prime}-1$ 降低冷源温度 $T_{2}$ 至 $T_{1}^{\prime}$, 与热源构成的联合循环 $1-4-4^{\prime}-1$ 的净功为

$\omega_{\text {net }}=q_{41}-q_{4^{\prime} 1^{\prime}}-\left(q_{23}-q_{4^{\prime} 1^{\prime}}\right)=q_{41}-q_{23} \text {, 不变 }$

吸热量: $q_{1}=q_{41}$

热效率: $\dot{\eta}_{1}^{\prime}=\frac{w_{\text {net }}}{q_{1}}=\frac{q_{41}-q_{23}}{q_{41}}=1-\frac{T_{2}}{T_{1}}$, 不变
