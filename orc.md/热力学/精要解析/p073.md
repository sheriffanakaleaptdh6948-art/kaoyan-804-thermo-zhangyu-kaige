(4) 因 $\eta_{\mathrm{T}}=0.90$, 说明此过程是不可逆的绝热过程, 透平实际输出的功率为

$P^{\prime}=P \eta_{\mathrm{T}}=1812.5 \mathrm{kW} \times 0.90=1631.3 \mathrm{kW}$

由热力学第一定律得

$\Delta \dot{H}+P^{\prime}=0$

即

$\begin{aligned} & q_{m} c_{p}\left(T_{2}^{\prime}-T_{1}\right)+P^{\prime}=0 \\ & T_{2}^{\prime}=-\frac{P^{\prime}}{q_{m} c_{p}}+T_{1}=-\frac{P^{\prime}}{q_{m} \times \frac{7}{2} R_{k}}+T_{1} \\ & =-\frac{1631.3 \times 10^{3} W}{5 \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{s} \times \frac{7}{2} \times 287 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})}=900 \mathrm{~K}=575.2 \mathrm{~K} \end{aligned}$
