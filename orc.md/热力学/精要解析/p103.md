例题 4-4 5 kg 的水起初与温度为 295 K 的大气处于热平衡状态。用一制冷机在这 5 kg 水与大气之间工作, 使水定压冷却到 280 K, 求所需的最小功是多少?

解 方法 1

根据题意画出示意图如图 4-9 所示, 由大气、水、制冷机、功源组成了孤立系, 则熵变

$\Delta S_{\text {iso }}=\Delta S_{\mathrm{H}}+\Delta S_{\mathrm{L}}+\Delta S_{\mathrm{R}}+\Delta S_{\mathrm{W}}$

其中 $\Delta S_{\mathrm{R}}=0$

$\Delta S_{\mathrm{W}}=0$

$\begin{aligned} \Delta S_{\mathrm{L}} & =\int_{295 \mathrm{~K}}^{280 \mathrm{~K}} \frac{\partial Q_{2}}{T_{2}}=\int_{295 \mathrm{~K}}^{280 \mathrm{~K}} \frac{m c d T_{2}}{T_{2}}=m c \ln \frac{280 \mathrm{~K}}{295 \mathrm{~K}} \\ & =5 \mathrm{~kg} \times 4180 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \ln \frac{280 \mathrm{~K}}{295 \mathrm{~K}}=-1090.7 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{K} \\ \Delta S_{\mathrm{H}} & =\frac{Q_{1}}{T_{0}}=\frac{\left|Q_{2}\right|+|W|}{T_{0}} \\ & =\frac{5 \mathrm{~kg} \times 4180 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})(295-280) \mathrm{K}+|W|}{295 \mathrm{~K}} \\ & =\frac{313500 \mathrm{~J}+|W|}{295 \mathrm{~K}} \end{aligned}$

于是

$\Delta S_{\text {iso }}=-1090.7 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{K}+\frac{313500 \mathrm{~J}}{295 \mathrm{~K}}+\frac{|\mathrm{W}|}{295 \mathrm{~K}}$

因可逆时所需的功最小, 所以令 $\Delta S_{\text {iso }}=0$, 可解得

$\left|\boldsymbol{W}_{\min }\right|=8256 \mathrm{~J}=8.256 \mathrm{~kJ}$

方法 2

制冷机为一可逆机时需功最小, 由卡诺定理得

即

$\varepsilon=\frac{\partial Q_{2}}{\partial W}=\frac{T_{2}}{T_{0}-T_{2}}$

$\begin{aligned} \delta W & =\delta Q_{2}\left(\frac{T_{0}-T_{2}}{T_{2}}\right)=\frac{T_{0}-T_{2}}{T_{2}} m c \mathrm{~d} T_{2} \\ W & =\int_{295 \mathrm{~K}}^{280 \mathrm{~K}} T_{0} m c \frac{\mathrm{d} T_{2}}{T_{2}}-\int_{295 \mathrm{~K}}^{280 \mathrm{~K}} m c \mathrm{~d} T_{2} \\ & =T_{0} m c \ln \frac{280 \mathrm{~K}}{295 \mathrm{~K}}-m c(280-295) \mathrm{K} \\ & =295 \mathrm{~K} \times 5 \mathrm{~kg} \times 4180 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \ln \frac{280 \mathrm{~K}}{295 \mathrm{~K}} \\ & -5 \mathrm{~kg} \times 4180 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})(280-295) \mathrm{K} \\ & =-8251.2 \mathrm{~J}=-8.251 \mathrm{~kJ} \end{aligned}$
