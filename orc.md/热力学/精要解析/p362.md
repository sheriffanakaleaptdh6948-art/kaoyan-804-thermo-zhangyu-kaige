计算题

4. 解：

湍止参数：$T_{0}=T_{1}+\frac{c_{1}^{2}}{2 c_{p}}=\frac{773+\frac{312^{2}}{2 \times 1004}}{821.54} \mathrm{~K}$

$p_{0}=p_{1}\left(\frac{T_{0}}{T_{1}}\right)^{\frac{k-1}{k-1}}=0.5 \times\left(\frac{821.54}{773}\right)^{\frac{1.44}{0.4}}=0.6187 \mathrm{MPa}$

(1) 为使喷管的流量达到最大，则：

$p_{2}=p_{\mathrm{cr}}=v_{\mathrm{cr}} p_{0}=0.528 \times 0.6187=0.3267 \mathrm{MPa}$

为使喷管达到最大质量流量，选择背压为：

$p_{\mathrm{b}} \leqslant p_{2}=0.3267 \mathrm{MPa}(2) T_{2}=T_{0} v_{\mathrm{cr}}^{\frac{1}{k}}=821.5 \times 0.528^{\frac{0.44}{0.4}}=684.4 \mathrm{~K}$

$v_{2}=R_{\mathrm{g}} \frac{T_{2}}{p_{2}}=0.287 \times \frac{684.4}{0.3267 \times 10^{6}}=0.6012 \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{kg}$

$c_{\mathrm{f} 2}=\sqrt{2 c_{p}\left(T_{0}-T_{2}\right)}=\sqrt{2 \times 1004 \times(821.5-684.4)}=524.7 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$

或者 $c_{\mathrm{f} 2}=c_{\mathrm{f}, \max }=c_{\mathrm{f}, \mathrm{cr}}=\sqrt{\frac{2 \kappa}{\kappa+1} R_{\mathrm{g}} T_{0}}=524.7 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$

则空气流经喷管的最大质量流量为：

$q_{m}=\frac{A_{2} \cdot c_{\mathrm{f} 2}}{v_{2}}=\frac{40 \times 10^{-6} \times 524.7}{0.6012}=0.03491 \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{s}$
