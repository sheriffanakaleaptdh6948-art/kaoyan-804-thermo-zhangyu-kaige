3. (12 分)

解:

先求滞止参数:

$T_{0}=T_{1}+\frac{c_{\mathrm{f}}^{2}}{2 c_{p}}=673.15+\frac{180^{2}}{2 \times 1004}=689.29 \mathrm{~K}$

$p_{0}=p_{1}\left(\frac{T_{0}}{T_{1}}\right)^{\frac{k}{k-1}}=0.6 \times\left(\frac{689.29}{673.15}\right)^{\frac{1.4}{1.4-1}}=0.65 \mathrm{MPa}$

$v_{0}=\frac{R_{\mathrm{g}} T_{0}}{p_{0}}=\frac{287 \times 689.29}{0.65 \times 10^{6}}=0.3043 \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{kg}$

$p_{\mathrm{cr}}=v_{\mathrm{cr}} \times p_{0}=0.528 \times 0.65=0.34 \mathrm{MPa}>p_{\mathrm{b}}=0.25 \mathrm{MPa}$

故: $p_{2}=0.34 \mathrm{MPa}$

(1) 喷管出口截面上的流速:

$p_{2}=0.34 \mathrm{MPa}, T_{2}=T_{0}\left(\frac{p_{2}}{p_{0}}\right)^{\frac{\kappa-1}{\kappa}}=689.29 \times\left(\frac{0.34}{0.65}\right)^{\frac{1.4-1}{1.4}}=572.79 \mathrm{~K}$

$c_{\mathrm{t} 2}=\sqrt{2\left(h_{0}-h_{2}\right)}=\sqrt{2 c_{p}\left(T_{0}-T_{2}\right)}=\sqrt{2 \times 1004 \times(689.29-572.79)}=483.67 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$

$c_{\mathrm{t} 2}^{\prime}=c_{\mathrm{t} 2} \varphi=483.67 \times 0.92=444.98 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$

(2) 喷管出口截面上的温度:

$T_{2^{\prime}}=T_{0}-\frac{c^{2} T_{\mathrm{t} 2}^{\prime}}{2 c_{p}}=689.29-\frac{444.98^{2}}{2 \times 1004}=590.68 \mathrm{~K}$

(3) 质量流量:

$p_{2^{\prime}}=p_{2}=0.34 \mathrm{MPa}$

$v_{2^{\prime}}=\frac{R_{\mathrm{g}} T_{2^{\prime}}}{p_{2}}=\frac{287 \times 590.68}{0.34 \times 10^{6}}=0.4986 \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{kg}$

$q_{m}=\frac{A_{2} c_{\mathrm{t} 2}^{\prime}}{v_{2^{\prime}}}=\frac{2.5 \times 10^{-3} \times 444.98}{0.4986}=2.23 \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{s}$

(4) 摩擦引起的有效能损失:

$\begin{aligned} I & =q_{m} \cdot T_{0} \cdot \Delta s=q_{m} \cdot T_{0} \cdot\left(c_{p} \ln \frac{T_{2^{\prime}}}{T_{1}}-R_{\mathrm{g}} \ln \frac{p_{2^{\prime}}}{p_{1}}\right) \\ & =2.23 \times 300 \times\left(1004 \times \ln \frac{590.68}{673.15}-287 \times \ln \frac{0.34}{0.6}\right)=21.27 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{s} \end{aligned}$
