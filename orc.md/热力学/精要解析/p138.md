(2) 查饱和表得 $p=9 \mathrm{MPa}$ 时, $v^{\prime}=0.001477 \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{kg}, v^{\prime \prime}=0.020500 \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{kg}$, 可见 $v^{\prime}<v<$ $v^{\prime \prime}$, 该状态为湿蒸汽, 其干度为

$x=\frac{v-v^{\prime}}{v^{\prime \prime}-v^{\prime}}=\frac{(0.017-0.0014177) \mathrm{m}^{3} / \mathrm{kg}}{(0.020500-0.0014177) \mathrm{m}^{3} / \mathrm{kg}}=0.8166$

又查饱和表得 $p=9 \mathrm{MPa}$ 时

$\begin{aligned} & h^{\prime}=1363.1 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \quad h^{\prime \prime}=2741.9 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \\ & s^{\prime}=3.2854 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \quad s^{\prime \prime}=5.6771 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \end{aligned}$

按湿蒸汽的参数计算式得

$\begin{aligned} & h=h^{\prime}+x\left(h^{\prime \prime}-h^{\prime}\right) \\ & =1363.1 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}+0.8166(2741.9-1363.1) \mathrm{kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \\ & =2489.0 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \\ & s=s^{\prime}+x\left(s^{\prime \prime}-s^{\prime}\right) \\ & =3.2854 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})+0.8166(5.6771-3.2854) \mathrm{kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \\ & =5.238 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \end{aligned}$

(3) 显然, 该状态为湿蒸汽状态。由已知参数查饱和水和饱和蒸汽表得

$\begin{aligned} & h^{\prime}=640.35 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \quad h^{\prime \prime}=2748.6 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \\ & s^{\prime}=1.8610 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \quad s^{\prime \prime}=6.8214 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \end{aligned}$

按湿蒸汽的参数计算公式得

$\begin{aligned} & h=h^{\prime}+x\left(h^{\prime \prime}-h^{\prime}\right) \\ & =640.35 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}+0.9(2748.6-640.35) \mathrm{kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \\ & =2537.8 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \\ & s=s^{\prime}+x\left(s^{\prime \prime}-s^{\prime}\right) \\ & =1.8610 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})+0.9(6.8214-1.8610) \mathrm{kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \\ & =6.325 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \end{aligned}$

(4) 由饱和水和饱和蒸汽表查得

当 $p=1.0 \mathrm{MPa}$ 时, $t_{\mathrm{s}}=179.9^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$, 显然 $t<t_{\mathrm{s}}$, 所以该状态为未饱和水。通常 $t=175^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ 的状态参数可利用 $t=170^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ 与 $t=180^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ 的对应状态参数内插得到, 但此处 $t=170^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ 与 $t=$ $180^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ 跨越了未饱和表中的黑粗线, 说明它们分别处于不同相区。应使内插在未饱和水区内进行, 选取离 $t=175^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ 最接近的 $t=170^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ 与 $t_{\mathrm{s}}=179.9^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ 的未饱和水参数内插。

查未饱和水和过热蒸汽表得

$\begin{aligned} & p=10 \mathrm{MPa}, t=170^{\circ} \mathrm{C} \text { 时 } \quad h=719.36 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \\ & s=2.0418 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \\ & p=1.0 \mathrm{MPa}, t=179.9^{\circ} \mathrm{C} \text { 时 } \quad h=762.84 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \\ & s=2.1388 \mathrm{~kJ} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \end{aligned}$

于是 $t=175^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ 时

$h=719.36 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \times(762.84-719.36) \mathrm{kJ} / \mathrm{kg} \times \frac{(175-170) \mathrm{K}}{(179.9-170) \mathrm{K}}=741.3 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$
