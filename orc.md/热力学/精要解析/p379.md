(2) 朗肯循环 2 点的状态参数：

$x_{2}=\frac{6.5865-0.42239}{8.4734-0.42239}=\frac{6.16411}{8.05101}=0.7656$

$h_{2}=\left(1-x_{2}\right) h^{\prime}+x_{2} h^{\prime \prime}$

$=0.2344 \times 121.39+0.7656 \times 2553.7=1983.6 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$

循环的净功量：

$w_{\text {net }}=h_{1}-h_{2}=3446.1-1983.6=1482.5 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$

循环吸热量：

$q_{1}=h_{1}-h_{3}=3466.1-121.39=3344.7 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$

循环的热效率：

$\eta=\frac{w_{\text {net }}}{q_{1}}=\frac{1482.5}{3344.7}=44.32 \%$
