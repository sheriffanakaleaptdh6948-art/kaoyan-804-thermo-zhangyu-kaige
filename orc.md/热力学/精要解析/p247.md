$$p_{4}=p_{3}\left(\frac{v_{3}}{v_{4}}\right)^{\kappa}=p_{3}\left(\frac{\rho}{\varepsilon}\right)^{\kappa}$$

式中应用了 \(v_{1}=v_{4}\) 的关系。

$\begin{aligned} \eta_{1} & =1-\frac{q_{2}}{q_{1}}=1-\frac{c_{V}\left(T_{4}-T_{1}\right)}{c_{p}\left(T_{3}-T_{2}\right)} \\ & =1-\frac{717 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})\left(646.5 \mathrm{~K}-293 \mathrm{~K}\right)}{1004 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})\left(1709.1 \mathrm{~K}-971 \mathrm{~K}\right)}=65.8 \% \end{aligned}$

或用式 \(\eta_{1}=1-\frac{\rho^{\kappa}-1}{\varepsilon^{\kappa-1} \kappa(\rho-1)}\) 计算。

平均有效压力为

$\begin{aligned} p_{\mathrm{m}} & =\frac{\mathrm{w}_{\text {net }}}{v_{\mathrm{h}}}=\frac{\eta_{1} q_{1}}{v_{1}-v_{2}} \\ & =\frac{\eta_{1} \cdot c_{p}\left(T_{3}-T_{2}\right)}{v_{1}-v_{2}} \\ & =\frac{0.658 \times 1004 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times\left(1709.1 \mathrm{~K}-971.1 \mathrm{~K}\right)}{(0.841-0.042) \mathrm{m}^{3} / \mathrm{kg}} \\ & =610.2 \times 10^{3} \mathrm{~Pa}=610.2 \mathrm{kPa} \end{aligned}$
