(1) 各状态点的温度和压力

因

余隙比 \( = \frac{V_2}{V_1 - V_2} = \frac{1}{\frac{V_1}{V_2} - 1} = \frac{1}{\varepsilon - 1} = 0.087 \)

故有

$$\varepsilon = \frac{1.087}{0.087} = 12.5$$

于是

$$\begin{aligned}& p_2 = p_1 \varepsilon^\kappa = 100 \, \text{kPa} \times 12.5^{1.4} = 3433 \, \text{kPa} \\& T_2 = T_1 \varepsilon^{(\kappa - 1)} = (273 + 20) \, \text{K} \times 12.5^{0.4} = 804.7 \, \text{K} \\& \dot{Q}_{23} = (42000 \times 10^3) \, \text{J/kg} \times \frac{0.2 \, \text{kg/s}}{28} = 300 \times 10^3 \, \text{J/s}\end{aligned}$$

工质的质量流量为

$$\begin{aligned}& q_m = 0.2 \, \text{kg/s} + \frac{0.2 \, \text{kg/s}}{28} = 0.207 \, 14 \, \text{kg/s} \\& T_3 = T_2 + \frac{\dot{Q}_{23}}{q_m c_v} \\& = 804.7 \, \text{K} + \frac{300 \times 10^3 \, \text{J/s}}{0.207 \, 14 \, \text{kg/s} \times 717 \, \text{J/(kg·K)}} = 2825 \, \text{K} \\& p_3 = p_2 \frac{T_3}{T_2} = 3433 \, \text{kPa} \times \frac{2825 \, \text{K}}{804.7 \, \text{K}} = 12052 \, \text{kPa} \\& T_4 = T_3 \frac{1}{\varepsilon^{\kappa - 1}} = 2825 \, \text{K} \times \frac{1}{12.5^{0.4}} = 1028.6 \, \text{K} \\& p_4 = p_3 \frac{1}{\varepsilon^\kappa} = 12052 \, \text{kPa} \times \frac{1}{12.5^{1.4}} = 351.1 \, \text{kPa}\end{aligned}$$

(2) 循环作出的功率

循环放热量

$$\begin{aligned}\dot{Q}_2 &= Q_{41} = q_m c_v (T_4 - T_1) \\&= 0.207 \, 14 \, \text{kg/s} \times 717 \, \text{J/(kg·K)} \times (1028.6 - 293) \, \text{K} \\&= 109.3 \times 10^3 \, \text{J/s}\end{aligned}$$

循环净功率

$$\begin{aligned}W_{\text{net}} &= \dot{Q}_1 - \dot{Q}_2 = \dot{Q}_{23} - \dot{Q}_{41} = 300 \times 10^3 \, \text{J/s} - 109.3 \times 10^3 \, \text{J/s} \\&= 190.7 \times 10^3 \, \text{J/s} = 190.7 \, \text{kW}\end{aligned}$$

(3) 循环热效率

$$\eta_1 = 1 - \frac{\dot{Q}_2}{\dot{Q}_1} = 1 - \frac{109.3 \times 10^3 \, \text{W}}{300 \times 10^3 \, \text{W}} = 63.6\%$$

或

$$\eta_1 = 1 - \frac{1}{\varepsilon^{\kappa - 1}} = 1 - \frac{1}{12.5^{(1.4 - 1)}} = 63.6\%$$

(4) 平均有效压力

$$\dot{V}_1 = \frac{q_m R_g T_1}{p_1} = \frac{0.207 \, 14 \, \text{kg/s} \times 287 \, \text{J/(kg·K)} \times 293 \, \text{K}}{10^5 \, \text{Pa}} = 0.174 \, 19 \, \text{m}^3/\text{s}$$
