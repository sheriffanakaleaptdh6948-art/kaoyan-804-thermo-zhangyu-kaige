(3) 假设每千克水中，有 $x$ 千克从 $60^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ 热水变成 $90^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ 水，由能量守恒可得：

$q=x \Delta T_{1}+(1-x) \Delta T_{2}$

$\begin{aligned}& =x \times 4.1868 \times(90-60)+(1-x) \times 4.1868 \times(15-60) \\& =125.604 x+188.406 x-188.406 \\& =(314.01 x-188.406) \mathrm{kJ} / \mathrm{kg}\end{aligned}$

由孤立系统熵增原理，可得：

$\Delta s_{\mathrm{iso}}=\int_{60+273}^{90+273} \frac{x \mathrm{~dT}}{T}+\int_{60+273}^{15+273} \frac{(1-x) \times c \mathrm{~dT}}{T}-\left(\frac{314.01 x-188.406}{15+273}\right) \geqslant 0$

即

$\begin{aligned}\Delta s_{\mathrm{iso}} & =x \times 4.1868 \times \ln \frac{363}{333}+(1-x) \times 4.1868 \times \ln \frac{288}{333}-\left(\frac{314.01 x-188.406}{15+273}\right) \\& =0.361 x-0.608+0.608 x-1.09 x+0.654 \\& =0.046-0.121 x \geqslant 0\end{aligned}$

即，$x \leqslant 0.38 \mathrm{~kg}$
