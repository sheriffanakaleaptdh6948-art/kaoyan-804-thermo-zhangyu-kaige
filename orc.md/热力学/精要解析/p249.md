$c_{V} \ln \frac{T_{3}}{T_{2}}+c_{p} \ln \frac{T_{4}}{T_{3}}=c_{V} \ln \frac{T_{5}}{T_{1}}$

则

$\begin{aligned} T_{4} & =T_{3}\left(\frac{T_{5}}{T_{1}}\right)^{1 / \kappa}\left(\frac{T_{2}}{T_{3}}\right)^{1 / \kappa} \\ & =873 \mathrm{~K}\left(\frac{573 \mathrm{~K}}{293 \mathrm{~K}}\right)^{1.4}\left(\frac{633 \mathrm{~K}}{873 \mathrm{~K}}\right)^{1.4} \\ & =1120.3 \mathrm{~K} \end{aligned}$

热效率为

$\begin{aligned} \eta_{\mathrm{r}} & =1-\frac{q_{2}}{q_{1}}=1-\frac{T_{5}-T_{1}}{\left(T_{3}-T_{2}\right)+\kappa\left(T_{4}-T_{3}\right)} \\ & =1-\frac{573 \mathrm{~K}-293 \mathrm{~K}}{(873 \mathrm{~K}-633 \mathrm{~K})+1.4(1120.3 \mathrm{~K}-873 \mathrm{~K})}=52.2 \% \end{aligned}$

同温限卡诺循环热效率为

$\eta_{\mathrm{r}, \mathrm{c}}=1-\frac{T_{1}}{T_{4}}=1-\frac{293 \mathrm{~K}}{1120.3 \mathrm{~K}}=73.8 \%$

讨论

求 4 点的温度也可以用下面方法导出

$\begin{aligned} T_{4} & =T_{3} \frac{v_{4}}{v_{3}} \cdot \frac{v_{4}}{v_{4}}=\frac{T_{3}}{v_{3}} \cdot \frac{v_{4}}{v_{4}}=\frac{v_{4}}{v_{1}}\left(\frac{T_{1}}{T_{2}}\right)^{1 /(\kappa-1)} \\ & =T_{3}\left(\frac{T_{2}}{T_{1}}\right)^{\frac{v_{4}}{v_{1}}}=T_{3}\left(\frac{T_{2}}{T_{1}}\right)^{1 /(\kappa-1)}\left(\frac{v_{4}}{v_{5}}\right) \\ & =T_{3}\left(\frac{T_{2}}{T_{1}}\right)^{1 /(\kappa-1)}\left(\frac{T_{5}}{T_{4}}\right)^{1 /(\kappa-1)} \end{aligned}$

所以

$T_{4}=\left(\frac{T_{2}}{T_{1}}\right)^{1 / \kappa} T_{3}^{(k-1) / k} T_{5}^{1 / k}$

代入已知数值即可求得。这种方法利用了循环各组成过程参数间的关系推导而得，显然较繁琐。
