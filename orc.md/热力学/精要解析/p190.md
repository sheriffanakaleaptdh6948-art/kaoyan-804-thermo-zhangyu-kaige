(1) 喷管出口处空气的温度和流速

$$T_{2}=T_{1}\left(\frac{p_{2}}{p_{1}}\right)^{(-1) / \kappa}=(77+273) \mathrm{K}\left(\frac{0.28 \times 10^{6} \mathrm{~Pa}}{0.45 \times 10^{6} \mathrm{~Pa}}\right)^{0.4 / 1.4}=305.6 \mathrm{~K}$$

$c_{\mathrm{f} 2}=\sqrt{2 c_{p}\left(T_{1}-T_{2}\right)}=\sqrt{2 \times 1004 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K})}(350-305.6) \mathrm{K}=298.6 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$

(2) 平直管道进出口的比体积

$$v_{2}=\frac{R_{\mathrm{g}} T_{2}}{p_{2}}=\frac{287 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{K}) \times 305.6 \mathrm{~K}}{0.28 \times 10^{6} \mathrm{~Pa}}=0.3132 \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{kg}$$
