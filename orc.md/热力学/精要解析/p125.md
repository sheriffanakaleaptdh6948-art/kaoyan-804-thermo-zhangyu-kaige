$d S_{m}=C_{V, m} \frac{d T}{T}+\frac{R}{V_{m}-b} d V_{m}$

$\mathrm{d} S_{\mathrm{m}}=\frac{R}{V_{\mathrm{m}}-b} \mathrm{~d} V_{\mathrm{m}}$

例题 5-3 对于符合范德瓦尔方程的气体, 求

(1) 比定压热容与比定容热容之差: $c_{p}-c_{v}$;

(2) 焦耳-汤姆逊系数。

解 (1) $c_{p}-c_{v}=T\left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial T}\right)_{p}\left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial T}\right)_{v}$

而

$\begin{aligned} \left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial T}\right)_{p} & =-\frac{1}{\left(\frac{\partial T}{\partial p}\right)_{v}\left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial v}\right)_{T}}=-\frac{\left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial T}\right)_{v}}{\left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial v}\right)_{T}} \\ & =\frac{\frac{R_{g}}{V-b}}{\frac{R_{g} T}{(v-b)^{2}}}-\frac{2 a}{v^{3}}=\frac{R_{g} v^{3}(v-b)}{R_{g} T v^{3}-2 a(v-b)^{2}} \\ \left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial v}\right)_{v} & =\frac{R_{g}}{v-b} \end{aligned}$

于是

$c_{p}-c_{v}=T \frac{R_{g}}{v-b} \frac{R_{g} v^{3}(v-b)}{R_{g} T v^{3}-2 a(v-b)^{2}}=\frac{R_{g}^{2} T v^{3}}{R_{g} T v^{3}-2 a(v-b)^{2}}$

(2) $\mu=\frac{T\left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial T}\right)_{p}-v}{c_{p}}=\frac{1}{c_{p}}\left[\frac{T R_{g} v^{3}(v-b)}{R_{g} T v^{3}-2 a(v-b)^{2}}-v\right]$

$=\frac{v}{c_{p}} \frac{2 a(v-b)^{2}-R_{g} T b v^{2}}{R_{g} T v^{3}-2 a(v-b)^{2}}$
