5. 解:

(1) 热力学第一定律的验证:

进入系统的能量: $q_{m, 1} c_{p} T_{1}=1 \times 1.046 \times(21+273)=307.5 \mathrm{kW}$

离开系统的能量:

$q_{m, 2} c_{p} T_{2}+q_{m, 3} c_{p} T_{3}=0.5 \times 1.046 \times(82+273)+0.5 \times 1.046 \times(-40+273)=307.6 \mathrm{kW}$可见,两者相等,说明满足热力学第一定律。

再验证是否满足第二定律。

取整体系统为孤立系,则:

$\begin{aligned} \Delta S_{\text {iso }} & =\Delta S_{12}+\Delta S_{13} \\ & =0.5 q_{m, 1}\left(c_{p} \ln \frac{T_{2}}{T_{1}}-R_{\mathrm{g}} \ln \frac{p_{2}}{p_{1}}\right)+0.5 q_{m, 1}\left(c_{p} \ln \frac{T_{3}}{T_{1}}-R_{\mathrm{g}} \ln \frac{p_{3}}{p_{1}}\right) \\ & =0.5 \times 1 \times(1.046 \ln \frac{82+273}{21+273}-0.297 \ln \frac{1}{6})+0.5 \times 1 \times(1.046 \ln \frac{-40+273}{21+273}) \\ & -0.297 \ln \frac{1}{6}) \\ & =0.509 \mathrm{kJ} / \mathrm{K}>0 \end{aligned}$

所以,满足第二定律。故此系统可以实现。

(2) 设最低入口压力为 $p_{\min }$, 则有

$\begin{aligned} & \Delta S_{\text {iso }}=\Delta S_{12}+\Delta S_{13}=0 \\ & 0.5 q_{m 1}\left(c_{p} \ln \frac{T_{2}}{T_{1}}-R_{\mathrm{g}} \ln \frac{p_{2}}{p_{\min }}\right)+0.5 q_{m 1}\left(c_{p} \ln \frac{T_{3}}{T_{1}}-R_{\mathrm{g}} \ln \frac{p_{3}}{p_{\min }}\right)=0 \\ & \ln \frac{p_{2}}{p_{\min }}=\frac{0.5}{R_{\mathrm{g}}}\left(c_{p} \ln \frac{T_{2}}{T_{1}}+c_{p} \ln \frac{T_{3}}{T_{1}}\right) \\ & \ln \frac{1}{p_{\min }}=\frac{0.5}{0.297} \times(1.046 \ln \frac{82+273}{21+273}+1.046 \ln \frac{-40+273}{21+273})=-0.0775 \end{aligned}$

可得: $p_{\min }=1.08 \mathrm{MPa}$
