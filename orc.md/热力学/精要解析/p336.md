7. 求滞止参数

$T_{0}=T_{1}+\frac{c_{\mathrm{r}}^{2}}{2 c_{p}}=973+\frac{312^{2}}{2 \times 1004}=1021.5 \mathrm{~K}$

$p_{0}=p_{1}\left(\frac{T_{0}}{T_{1}}\right)^{\kappa /(\kappa-1)}=0.6\left(\frac{1021.5}{973}\right)^{1.4 / 0.4}=0.7114 \mathrm{MPa}$

为使流经喷管的空气能达到最大质量流量，则

$p_{2}=p_{\mathrm{cr}}=\nu_{\mathrm{cr}} p_{0}=0.528 p_{0}=0.3756 \mathrm{MPa}$

$T_{2}=T_{0} \nu_{\mathrm{cr}}^{(\kappa-1) / \kappa}=1021.5 \times 0.528^{0.4 / 1.4}=851.0 \mathrm{~K}$

$v_{2}=\frac{R_{\mathrm{g}} T_{2}}{p_{2}}=\frac{0.287 \times 851.0}{0.3756 \times 10^{3}}=0.6503 \mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{kg}$

$c_{\mathrm{f} 2}=\sqrt{2 c_{p}}\left(T_{0}-T_{2}\right)=\sqrt{2 \times 1004 \times(1021.5-851.0)}=585.1 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$

则空气流经喷管的最大质量流量为

$q_{m}=\frac{A_{2} c_{\mathrm{f} 2}}{v_{2}}=\frac{30 \times 10^{-6} \times 585.1}{0.6503}=0.02699 \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{s}$

达到最大质量时的背压应满足

$p_{\mathrm{b}} \leqslant p_{2}=0.3756 \mathrm{MPa}$
