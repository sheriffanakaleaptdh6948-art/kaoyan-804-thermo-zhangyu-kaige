$$T_{4}=T_{3}\left(\frac{p_{4}}{p_{3}}\right)^{\left(\gamma-1\right) / \gamma}=288 \times\left(\frac{1}{4}\right)^{\left(1.4-1\right) / 1.4}=193.81(\mathrm{~K})$$
