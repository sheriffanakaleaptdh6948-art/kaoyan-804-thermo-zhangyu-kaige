$$14.175 \mathrm{~d} \tau+0.6 \mathrm{~d} \tau-0.315 T \mathrm{~d} \tau-7.6 \mathrm{~d}_{\tau}=189 \mathrm{~dT}$$

化简得:

$$\frac{\mathrm{d} \tau}{189}=\frac{\mathrm{dT}}{7.175-0.315 T}$$

所以两边同时积分后, 可求得温度表达式为:

$$T=22.778+\kappa \mathrm{e}^{-\frac{0.315}{189} \tau}$$
