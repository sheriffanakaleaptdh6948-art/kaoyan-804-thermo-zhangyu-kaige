这样, 可求得水和金属棒的平衡温度为:

$$T_{\mathrm{f}}=\frac{m_{\mathrm{w}}\left(c_{\mathrm{w}} / c_{\mathrm{m}}\right) T_{\mathrm{wi}}+m_{\mathrm{m}} T_{\mathrm{mi}}}{m_{\mathrm{w}}\left(c_{\mathrm{w}} / c_{\mathrm{m}}\right)+m_{\mathrm{m}}}=\frac{9 \times 10 \times 300+0.3 \times 1200}{9 \times 10+0.3}=303(\mathrm{~K})$$

(2) 熵方程为:

$$\Delta S|_{\text {water }}+\Delta S|_{\text {metal }}=\Delta S_{\mathrm{g}}$$

即熵产为:

$$S_{\mathrm{g}}=m_{\mathrm{w}} c_{\mathrm{w}} \ln \frac{T_{\mathrm{f}}}{T_{\mathrm{wi}}}+m_{\mathrm{m}} c_{\mathrm{m}} \ln \frac{T_{\mathrm{f}}}{T_{\mathrm{mi}}}$$

代入数据即可求得熵产为:

$$\dot{S}_{\mathrm{g}}=9 \times 4.2 \times \ln \frac{303}{300}+0.3 \times 0.42 \times \ln \frac{303}{1200}=0.203(\mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{K})$$
