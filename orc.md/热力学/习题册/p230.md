$$\dot{w}_{\mathrm{T}}=-\Delta h=h_{3}-h_{4^{\prime}}=c_{\mathrm{p}}\left(T_{3}-T_{4^{\prime}}\right)=1.005 \times\left(1500-870.92\right)=632.23(\mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg})$$

压气机的轴功率为：

$$\dot{w}_{\mathrm{p}}=\Delta h=h_{2^{\prime}}-h_{1}=c_{\mathrm{p}}\left(T_{2^{\prime}}-T_{1}\right)=1.005 \times\left(607.53-290\right)=319.12(\mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg})$$

燃气轮机的工质流率 $\dot{m}\left(\dot{w}_{\mathrm{T}}-\dot{w}_{\mathrm{c}}\right)=\dot{w}_{\mathrm{g}}$，所以：
