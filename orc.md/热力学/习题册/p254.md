```latex
\begin{equation}
\textbf{(1)} \quad \frac{\dot{T}_{2}}{\dot{s}_{2}}=\frac{\dot{w}_{\mathrm{net }}}{\dot{q}_{1}}, \quad (2) \quad \frac{\dot{q}_{2}}{\dot{q}_{1}}=-\frac{\dot{T}_{2}}{\dot{s}_{2}}+\frac{\dot{w}_{\mathrm{net }}}{{c_{v}}}
\end{equation}

所以 $\dot{T}_{2}/\dot{s}_{2}=T_{2}/s_{2}$, $\dot{q}_{2}/\dot{q}_{1}=(T_{2}-T_{1})/(T_{2}-T_{1})$, $\dot{w}_{\mathrm{net }}=C_{V}(T_{2}-T_{1})$.

$\begin{aligned} & \therefore \quad \frac{\dot{T}_{2}}{\dot{s}_{2}}=\frac{\dot{Q}_{2}}{\dot{Q}_{1}} \\ & =\left(\frac{\dot{Q}_{2}}{\dot{Q}_{1}}+1\right)\frac{\dot{T}_{2}}{\dot{s}_{2}}-\frac{\dot{Q}_{1}}{\dot{Q}_{2}} \end{aligned}

因为 $\dot{Q}_{1}=\dot{Q}_{2}$, $\dot{Q}_{2}=\dot{Q}_{1}$, 上式中第一个因子等于零; 第二个因子等于 $-(T_{2}-T_{1})(T_{2}-T_{1})<0$, 故

\[

\[

\frac{\dot{Q}_{2}}{\dot{Q}_{1}}=\frac{\dot{Q}_{1}}{\dot{Q}_{2}}-\frac{\dot{Q}_{1}}{\dot{Q}_{2}}+x
\]

\[
x=0
\]

即

\[
\frac{\dot{Q}_{2}}{\dot{Q}_{1}}=\frac{\dot{Q}_{1}}{\dot{Q}_{2}}
\]

\[
\boxed{\frac{\dot{Q}_{2}}{\dot{Q}_{1}}=\frac{\dot{Q}_{1}}{\dot{Q}_{2}}}
