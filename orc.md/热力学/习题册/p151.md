$$\frac{T_{1}}{T_{2}}=\frac{p_{1} v_{1}}{p_{2} v_{2}}=\varepsilon^{1-\kappa}$$

$2-3$ 为等压过程: $p_{2}=p_{3}, \frac{T_{3}}{T_{2}}=\frac{v_{3}}{v_{2}} ; 4-1$ 为等容过程: $v_{4}=v_{1}, \frac{T_{4}}{T_{1}}=\frac{p_{4}}{p_{1}}$ 。所以有:

$$\frac{p_{1}}{p_{4}}=\left(\frac{v_{2}}{v_{3}}\right)^{\kappa}$$

综上,可得:

$$\eta=1-\frac{T_{1}}{\kappa T_{2}}\left(\frac{T_{4}}{T_{3}}-\frac{1}{T_{2}-1}\right)$$

$$\eta_{\mathrm{t}}=1-\frac{\rho^{\kappa}-1}{\varepsilon^{\kappa-1} \kappa(\rho-1)}$$
