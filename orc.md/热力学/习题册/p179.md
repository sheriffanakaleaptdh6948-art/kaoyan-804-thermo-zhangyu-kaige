解: 由题意可知, 水蒸气的初始干度为:

$x_{1}=\frac{1}{1+9}=0.1$

初始比体积为:

$v_{1}=\left(1-x_{1}\right) v^{\prime}+x_{1} v^{\prime \prime}=\left(1-0.1\right) \times 0.0012346+0.1 \times 0.057154$

$=0.0068265\left(\mathrm{~m}^{3} / \mathrm{kg}\right)$

初始比焓为

$h_{1}=\left(1-x_{1}\right) h^{\prime}+x_{1} h^{\prime \prime}=\left(1-0.1\right) \times 1049.1+0.1 \times 2802.52$
