例：…they are usually concerned at the prospect of their best and brightest departure to Silicon Valley or to hospitals and universities in the developed world. (2012, Translation)

译：……他们（指发展中国家的人）通常担心的是本国最优秀的人才可能会移民到硅谷或发达国家的医院和大学工作。

1. 找到谓语动词，确定是否为简单句（一件事）

找到句中的谓语动词 are，确定只有一个谓语动词后，就可以判断出这是一个简单句，只表述了一件事。

2. 去扩展，找核心

…they are usually concerned at the prospect of their best and brightest departure to Silicon Valley or to hospitals and universities in the developed world.

句中的扩展成分（非核心的修饰限定和解释说明的成分）有副词 usually 和多个介词短语表示补充说明，如 at the prospect, of their best and brightest departure, to Silicon Valley or to hospitals and universities, in the developed world。

去掉了如上的扩展成分之后，自然就找到了句子的核心内容：…they are… concerned…整个句子的核心内容就是“……他们……担心……。

例：In a series of four experiments, behavioral scientists at the University of Chicago and the Wisconsin School of Business tested students’ willingness to expose themselves to unpleasant stimuli in an effort to satisfy curiosity. (2018, Use of English)

译：在一系列的四项实验中，芝加哥大学和威斯康星商学院的行为科学家测试学生们为了满足好奇心而遭受令人不快的刺激的意愿。

1. 找到谓语动词，确定是否为简单句（一件事）

找到句中的谓语动词 tested，确定只有一个谓语动词后，就可以判断出这是一个简单句，只表述了一件事。注意：其中的 to expose 和 to satisfy 是非谓语动词（会在下一个章节中详细讲到），在本句中不是句子的核心，只是扩展成分。

2. 去扩展，找核心

In a series of four experiments, behavioral scientists at the University of Chicago and the Wisconsin School of Business tested students’ willingness to expose themselves to unpleasant stimuli in an effort to satisfy curiosity.
