1. 先行词是事或物, 关系词选择 which/that

例: The key messages that have been put together for World Environment Day do include a call for governments to enact legislation to curb single-use plastics. (2019, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 4)

译: 为世界环保日收集的关键信息确实包括呼吁各国政府立法来限制一次性塑料的使用。

例: One thing that does seem to work is requiring drivers to report in person for license renewal. (2024, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 3)

译: 有一种方法似乎确实有效, 那就是要求驾驶员亲自办理驾照延期手续。

例: But that’s exactly the kind of false cheerfulness that positive psychologists wouldn’t recommend. (2014, Translation)

译: 但这恰恰不是真正的快乐, 积极心理学家们并不提倡。

例: The BIO recently held a convention which included sessions to coach lawyers on the shifting landscape for patents. (2012, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 3)

译: 生物技术工业组织最近召开了一次大会, 其中包括就专利形势的变化对律师进行培训的系列会议。

2. 先行词是人, 关系词选择 who/whom/that

例: The teenagers who were more rebellious were also more likely to help others. (2023, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 4)

译: 越叛逆的青少年也越有可能帮助别人。

例: ...normal-weight people are in fact at higher risk of some diseases compared to those who are overweight. (2014, Use of English)

译: ……事实上, 和超重的人相比, 体重正常的人患某些疾病的风险更高。

例: ...you have limited time with the person that you want to talk with and you want to make this talk memorable. (2018, Reading Comprehension, Part B)

译: ……你只有有限的时间和想要交谈的人（谈话），而你想让这次交谈留下深刻印象。
