8. To read such books today is to marvel at the fact… (英语一, 2010, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 1)

我是一条答案的分隔线，请先不要偷看呦！

1. Planning ahead should eliminate wastage… (2013, Reading Comprehension, Part B)

译：提前计划可以杜绝浪费……

非谓语动词 doing 的词组 Planning ahead 作主语。

2. It is far better to spend money on experiences… (2014, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 1)

译：把钱花在体验上会更好……

非谓语动词 to do 的词组 to spend money on experiences 作真正的主语，由于较长而被后置，it 放在句首作形式主语。

3. Working with your hands is seen as almost a mark of inferiority. (2018, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 1)

译：用双手劳动几乎被视为低人一等的标志。

非谓语动词 doing 的词组 Working with your hands 作主语。

4. It is too soon to write off the EU. (2011, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 4)

译：现在放弃欧盟还为时过早。

非谓语动词 to do 的词组 to write off the EU 作真正的主语后置，it 放在句首作形式主语。

5. For these reasons, I stopped weighing myself every day… (2019, Use of English)

译：因为这些原因，我停止每天称体重……

及物动词 stopped 后，非谓语动词 doing 的词组 weighing myself every day 作宾语（其中再细分的话，myself 作动词 weigh 的宾语，但没必要细分至此）。

6. I want to talk with this person… (2018, Reading Comprehension, Part B)

译：我想和这个人说话……

及物动词 want 后，非谓语动词 to do 的词组 to talk with this person 作宾语（其中再细分的话，this person 作介词 with 的宾语，但没必要细分至此）。

7. Creating positive habits is a huge part of improving our consumers’ lives… (2010, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 3)

译：创造积极的习惯是改善消费者生活的重要部分……

非谓语动词 doing 的词组 Creating positive habits，作主语；介词 of 后，非谓语动词
