考研真题中的插入语一般有几种常用的形式, 如下。

1. 主谓结构作插入语, 表示 “某人说, 某人认为……”

例: Elegance, he believed, did not derive from abundance. (2011, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 3)

译: 他认为优雅未必来源于繁多。

例: The answer, says Dr. Kroo, lies with birds. (2010, Reading Comprehension, Part B)

译: 克鲁博士说, 答案就在鸟类身上。

例: Visual recognition, reads the study, is “an automatic psychological process, occurring rapidly and intuitively with little or no apparent conscious deliberation”. (2014, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 2)

译: 研究显示, 视觉识别是 “一个自动的心理过程, 迅速且本能地发生, 很少有或没有明显的有意识思考”。

2. 副词作插入语

例: Some environmentalists, however, were disappointed. (2016, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 2)

译: 然而, 一些环境保护主义者（对此决定）感到失望。

例: Facebook, therefore, is a self-enhancer’s paradise… (2014, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 2)

译: 脸书因此成了一个自我拔高者的天堂……

3. 介词短语作插入语

例: Guilt, in other words, can help hold a cooperative species together. (2019, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 1)

译: 换言之, 内疚感有助于将一个相互协作的物种凝聚在一起。

例: The Boyle Family, on the other hand, stand for its dirty, urban aspect. (2014, Reading Comprehension, Part B)

译: 另一方面, 博伊尔家族则代表着它肮脏、都市化的一面。
