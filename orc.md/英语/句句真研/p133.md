3. 先行词是人/物, 表示人或物的所有关系, 关系词选择 whose

例: Users could select which system to join, and only registered users whose identities have been authenticated could navigate those systems. (2011, Use of English)

译: 用户可以选择加入哪个系统, 而且只有身份得到验证的注册用户才能浏览这些系统。

例: On display here are various fantasy elements whose reference, at some basic level, seems to be the natural world. (英语一, 2013, Translation)

译: 这里展示的是各式各样的幻想元素, 它们的参照——在一些基本层面上——似乎是自然世界。

4. 先行词是时间, 关系词选择 when

例: It is difficult to the point of impossibility for the average reader under the age of forty to imagine a time when high-quality arts criticism could be found in most big-city newspapers. (英语一, 2010, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 1)

译: 对于 40 岁以下的普通读者来说, (他们) 很难甚至无法想象一个在大多数大城市的报纸上可以读到高质量艺术评论的时代。

5. 先行词是地点, 关系词选择 where

例: The relationship was also stronger in places where happiness was spread more equally. (2016, Use of English)

译: 这种关系（相关性）在幸福感分布更为均等的地方也更显著。

例: He is a teacher at a New Hampshire high school where learning is not something of books and tests and mechanical memorization, but practical. (2018, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 1)

译: 他是新罕布什尔州一所高中的老师, 在那里, 学习并不关乎书本、考试和机械记忆, 而（在于）实践。
