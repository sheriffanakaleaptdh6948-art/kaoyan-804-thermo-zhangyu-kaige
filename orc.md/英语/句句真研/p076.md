内容要点

(1) 同位语：解释说明前面的名词。

插入语：后插入句子中的成分，表示补充说明。

(2) 同位语和插入语都是对句子的补充说明，因此都是非核心部分，在考研的长难句分析当中可以先略去不看。尤其是两边有逗号或破折号隔开的时候，可以率先省掉，降低句子难度。

真题演练

请用下划线标出同位语或插入语的部分，并理解其含义。

1. Jealousy and anger, for example, may have evolved to alert us to important inequalities. (2019, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 1)

2. There will always be change—new jobs, new products, new services. (2013, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 1)

3. Cargo aircraft, in contrast, might be easier to reschedule... (2010, Reading Comprehension, Part B)

4. Within weeks the world’s two biggest auction houses, Sotheby’s and Christie’s, had to pay out nearly $200m in guarantees to clients... (2010, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 1)

5. It is far better to spend money on experiences, say Ms Dumn and Mr Norton, like interesting trips, unique meals or even going to the cinema. (2014, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 1)

6. The demands follow comments made last week by the health secretary, Andrew Lansley... (2011, Reading Comprehension, Part B)

7. But the epidemic is “moderate” in severity, according to Margaret Chan, the organization’s director general... (2010, Use of English)

8. The apartments in the elegant towers... on Chicago’s Lake Shore Drive, for example, were smaller—two bedroom units under 1,000 square feet—than those in their older neighbors along the city’s Gold Coast. (2011, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 3)
