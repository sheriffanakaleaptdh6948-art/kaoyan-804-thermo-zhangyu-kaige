11. ……technology might change the way we react… (2013, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 3)

译: ……科技可能会改变我们的反应方式……

此句为省略关系词的定语从句，关系词被省略，先行词为 the way。

12. The outbreak came to global notice in late April 2009, when Mexican authorities noted an unusually large number of hospitalizations and deaths among healthy adults. (2010, Use of English)

译：(猪流感的) 暴发在 2009 年 4 月末引起了全世界的关注，当时墨西哥当局注意到在健康的成年人之中，住院治疗和死亡人数异常多。

此句为非限定性定语从句，关系词为 when，先行词为 late April 2009。

13. …the euro zone’s economies… will one day converge thanks to the discipline of sharing a single currency, which denies uncompetitive members the quick fix of devaluation. (2011, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 4)

译: …欧元区的各经济体……总有一天会由于使用单一货币的准则而走向聚和，这（指单一货币）使得竞争力不足的成员国无法采取货币贬值这一权宜之计。

此句为特殊的非限定性定语从句，补充说明名词词组 the discipline of sharing a single currency，关系词为 which。

14. …most of those initial doses were of the FluMist nasal spray type, which is not recommended for pregnant women… (2010, Use of English)

译: …这批初始的药剂中大部分是 FluMist 鼻用喷雾剂型疫苗，(此疫苗) 不建议孕妇……使用。

此句为非限定性定语从句，关系词为 which，先行词为 the FluMist nasal spray type。

15. As such, it needed to appreciate the economic realities, the social contexts and power relations in which each epoch stood. (2012, Reading Comprehension, Part B)

译: 因此，它（指历史）需要了解每一个时代的经济现实和社会背景，以及所牵扯的权力关系。

此句为介词提前的限定性定语从句，关系词为 which，先行词为 the economic realities, the social contexts and power relations。
