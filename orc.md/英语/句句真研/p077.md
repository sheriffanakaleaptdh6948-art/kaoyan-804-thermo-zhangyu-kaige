我是一条答案的分隔线，请先不要偷看哟!

1. Jealousy and anger, for example, may have evolved to alert us to important inequalities. (2019, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 1)

译：例如，嫉妒和愤怒可能已经演变成对严重的不平等现象的提醒。

介词短语 for example 作插入语，且两端有逗号隔开。

2. There will always be change new jobs, new products, new services. (2013, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 1)

译：变化将永远存在——新工作、新产品、新服务。

new jobs, new products, new services 为同位语，解释说明前面的名词 change，且前面有破折号提示。

3. Cargo aircraft, in contrast, might be easier to reschedule... (2010, Reading Comprehension, Part B)

译：相比之下，货运飞机或许更容易重新协调安排……

介词短语 in contrast 作插入语，且两端有逗号隔开。

4. Within weeks the world’s two biggest auction houses, Sotheby’s and Christie’s, had to pay out nearly $200m in guarantees to clients... (2010, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 1)

译：几周之内，世界上最大的两家拍卖行，苏富比和佳士得，不得不支付近 2 亿美元的保证金给客户……

Sotheby’s and Christie’s 为同位语，解释说明前面的名词词组 the world’s two biggest auction houses。

5. It is far better to spend money on experiences, say Ms Dunn and Mr Norton, like interesting trips, unique meals or even going to the cinema. (2014, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 1)

译：邓恩女士和诺顿先生说，把钱花在体验上会更好，比如有趣的旅行、不同寻常的一顿饭，甚至是去看电影。

主谓结构 say Ms Dunn and Mr Norton 作插入语，且两端有逗号隔开。

6. The demands follow comments made last week by the health secretary, Andrew Lansley... (2011, Reading Comprehension, Part B)

译：这些要求是在卫生部部长安德鲁·兰斯利上周发表意见之后提出的……

Andrew Lansley 为同位语，解释说明前面的名词 the health secretary。
