| --- | --- | --- |
| when while as | 当……时候 | 例：But as companies continue their attempts at personalized medicine, the courts will remain rather busy. (2012, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 3) |
| 译：但是随着公司继续尝试个性化医疗，法院仍会相当忙碌。 |  |  |
| before | 在……之前 | 例：Even dairy farms, where robots do a small share of milking, have a long way to go before they're automated. (2019, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 3) |
| 译：即使是机器人完成少部分挤奶工作的奶牛场，要实现自动化也有很长的路要走。 |  |  |
| after | 在……之后 | 例：...we quickly “thin slice” information reliably only after we ground such snap reactions in “thick sliced” long-term study. (2013, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 3) |
| 译：……只有在对“厚片”的长期研究中建立了这种快速反应的基础后，我们才会迅速可靠地（进行）信息“薄片”化。 |  |  |
| since | 自从…… | 例：The current downturn in the art market is the worst since the Japanese stopped buying Impressionists at the end of 1989. (2010, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 1) |
| 译：当前艺术品市场的衰退是自1989年年底日本人停止购买印象派画家（作品）以来最糟糕的一次。 |  |  |
| until | 直到…… | 例：...the corporate workplace will never be completely family-friendly until women are part of senior management decisions... (2013, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 4) |
| 译：...企业工作场所永远不会完全是家庭友好型的，除非女性能参与高级管理层决策…… |  |  |

personalized medicine 个性化医疗

dairy /ˈdeəri/ adj. 生产乳品的；牛奶的

a small share of… 一小部分的……

automated /ˈɑːtəmɪtʊd/ adj. 自动化的

thin slice/thick slice 切成薄片 / 厚片

reliably /rɪˈlaɪəblɪ/ adv. 可靠地，可信赖地；确实地

ground /ɡraʊnd/ v. 打基础；使接触地面 n. 地面，土地；范围；

根据

the current downturn in the art market 当前艺术品市场的衰退

current /ˈkʌrənt/ adj. 当前的，现行的；最近的；通用的，流行的

downturn /ˈdaʊntən/ n. 衰退，下降

Impressionist /ɪmˈprəˈfʌnst/ n. 印象派画家；印象派（文中指“印象派画家的作品”）

corporate /ˈkɔːporət/ adj. 公司的；法人的

workplace /ˈwːkpleɪs/ n. 工作场所

family-friendly 家庭友好型

senior management decision 高层管理决策
