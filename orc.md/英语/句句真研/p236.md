wanted

例: But successive governments have presided over selling green spaces, squeezing money from local authorities and declining attention on sport in education. (2017, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 1)

译：但历届政府都在负责出售绿地，从地方政府榨取资金，并减少对体育教育的关注。

三个 doing 词组平行并列，位于介词 over 后作宾语，中间由逗号和并列连词 and 连接。平行并列结构参见下图：

But successive governments have presided over

1 selling green spaces

2, squeezing money from local authorities

3 and declining attention on sport in education.

(三) 介词短语的平行并列

例: Information flows from the media to the influencers and from them to everyone else. (英语一, 2010, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 3)

译：信息从媒体流向有影响力的人，又从有影响力的人流向其他所有人。

两个 from...to... 介词短语平行并列，由并列连词 and 连接，表示“信息流动从……到……和从……到……”。平行并列结构参见下图。

Information flows

1 from the media to the influencers

2 and from them to everyone else.

successive governments 历届政府
