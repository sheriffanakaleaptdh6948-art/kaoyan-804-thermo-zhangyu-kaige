2. 表示转折的并列连词

| 并列连词 | 含义 |
| --- | --- |
| but | ……但是… |
| yet | ……但是… |

例: They didn’t remember the information, but they remembered how to find the folders. (2023, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 3)

译: 他们不记得这些信息, 但记得如何找到这些文件夹。

例: Money may not quite buy happiness, but people in wealthier countries are generally happier than those in poor ones. (2014, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 1)

译: 金钱可能不会真正买到幸福, 但是身处富裕国家的人通常比身处贫穷国家的人更幸福。

例: ...adult women do not remember being so obsessed with the colour, yet it is pervasive in our young girls’ lives. (2012, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 2)

译: …成年女性不记得自己曾对这种颜色如此着迷过, 然而它（指粉色）却充斥在我们年轻女孩的生活中。

3. 表示选择的并列连词

| 并列连词 | 含义 |
| --- | --- |
| or | ……或者… |
| either...or... | (二选一) |
| neither...nor... | 既不……也不…… |
|  | (两个都不选) |

例: In effect, the U.S. can import food or it can import the workers... (2019, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 3)

译: 实际上, 美国可以进口食物, 或者它可以引进工人……

例: Either Energy never really intended to live by those commitments, or it simply didn’t foresee what would happen next. (英语一, 2012, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 2)

译: 要么是安特吉公司从来就没有真正想要信守那些承诺, 要么就是它仅仅没有预见接下来会发生什么事情。

be obsessed with... 痴迷于……

intend to do sth. 打算做某事

obsessed /ə'b səs/ adj. 着迷的, 痴迷的; 受困扰的

live by those commitments 遵守那些承诺

pervasive /pə'veivsv/ adj. 无处不在的, 遍布的

foresee /fo: 'si:/ v. 预见, 预知

in effect 实际上
