9. It’s true that high-school coding classes aren’t essential for learning computer science in college. (2016, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 1)

译: 的确, 高中编码课程对于在大学学习计算机科学来说并不是必不可少的。

that 引出主语从句, 位于句尾, 句首用形式主语 It 来占位置。

10. But most people will come away from this book believing it was money well spent. (2014, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 1)

译: 但大部分人读完这本书都会觉得钱花得值。

非谓语动词 believing (原形是及物动词 believe) 后, 是省略连接词 that 的动宾结构的宾语从句。

11. In an essay entitled “Making It in America,” the author Adam Davidson relates a joke from cotton country about just how much a modern textile mill has been automated… (2013, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 1)

译: 在一篇标题为《在美国制造》的文章中, 作者亚当・戴维森讲述了一个来自棉花之乡的笑话, 讲的是现代纺织厂的自动化程度有多高…

介词 about 后, how 引出介宾结构的宾语从句。

12. One survey found that bureaucratic delays led the average H-2A worker to arrive on the job 22 days late. (2019, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 3)

译: 一项调查发现, 官僚主义的拖延导致 H-2A 签证工人平均晚 22 天到岗。

及物动词 found 后, that 引出动宾结构的宾语从句。

13. Lansley has alarmed health campaigners by suggesting he wants industry rather than government to take the lead. (2011, Reading Comprehension, Part B)

译: 兰斯利已经给健康活动家敲响了警钟, 他表示希望 (能够) 由企业而不是政府来发挥带头作用。

非谓语动词 suggesting (原形是及物动词 suggest) 后, 是省略连接词 that 的动宾结构的宾语从句。

14. For the low-sympathy kids, how much they shared appeared to turn on how inclined they were to feel guilty. (2019, Reading Comprehension, Part A Text 1)

译: 对于缺乏同情心的孩子, 他们分享多少似乎取决于他们有多大的内疚倾向。

how 引出主语从句, 位于句首。介词 on 后, how 引出介宾结构的宾语从句。
