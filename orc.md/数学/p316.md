考研数学基础30讲·高等数学分册

(A) $\left. \frac{\partial f}{\partial x} \right|_{(0, 1)}$ 不存在，$\left. \frac{\partial f}{\partial y} \right|_{(0, 1)}$ 存在
(B) $\left. \frac{\partial f}{\partial x} \right|_{(0, 1)}$ 存在，$\left. \frac{\partial f}{\partial y} \right|_{(0, 1)}$ 不存在
(C) $\left. \frac{\partial f}{\partial x} \right|_{(0, 1)}$，$\left. \frac{\partial f}{\partial y} \right|_{(0, 1)}$ 均存在
(D) $\left. \frac{\partial f}{\partial x} \right|_{(0, 1)}$，$\left. \frac{\partial f}{\partial y} \right|_{(0, 1)}$ 均不存在

**分析** $\left. \frac{\partial f}{\partial x} \right|_{(0, 1)} = \lim_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{f(0+\Delta x, 1) - f(0, 1)}{\Delta x} = \lim_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{\ln(1+|\Delta x \cdot \sin 1|) - 0}{\Delta x} \xrightarrow{\to 0} \text{利用当 } x \to 0 \text{ 时，} \ln(1+x) \sim x$

$\left. \frac{\partial f}{\partial y} \right|_{(0, 1)} = \lim_{\Delta y \to 0} \frac{f(0, 1+\Delta y) - f(0, 1)}{\Delta y} = \lim_{\Delta y \to 0} \frac{\ln(1+\Delta y) - 0}{\Delta y}$.

**解** 应选 (A).

因为 $f(x, y) = \ln(y + |x \sin y|)$，所以
$$f(0, 1) = \ln(1+0) = 0, \quad f(x, 1) = \ln(1+|x \sin 1|), \quad f(0, y) = \ln(y+0) = \ln y,$$
因此
$$ \left. \frac{\partial f}{\partial x} \right|_{(0, 1)} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(x, 1) - f(0, 1)}{x - 0} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\ln(1+|x \sin 1|)}{x} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{|x \sin 1|}{x}, $$
$$ \left. \frac{\partial f}{\partial y} \right|_{(0, 1)} = \lim_{y \to 1} \frac{f(0, y) - f(0, 1)}{y - 1} = \lim_{y \to 1} \frac{\ln y}{y - 1} = \lim_{y \to 1} \frac{1}{y} = 1, $$
所以 $\left. \frac{\partial f}{\partial x} \right|_{(0, 1)}$ 不存在，$\left. \frac{\partial f}{\partial y} \right|_{(0, 1)}$ 存在.

**注** $f'_x(x_0, y_0) = \lim_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{f(x_0+\Delta x, y_0) - f(x_0, y_0)}{\Delta x} \longrightarrow \text{增量式}$
$$ \underset{x_0+\Delta x = x}{=} \lim_{x \to x_0} \frac{f(x, y_0) - f(x_0, y_0)}{x - x_0} \longrightarrow \text{函数差值式} $$
同理，$f'_y(x_0, y_0) = \lim_{\Delta y \to 0} \frac{f(x_0, y_0+\Delta y) - f(x_0, y_0)}{\Delta y} \underset{y_0+\Delta y = y}{=} \lim_{y \to y_0} \frac{f(x_0, y) - f(x_0, y_0)}{y - y_0}$.
