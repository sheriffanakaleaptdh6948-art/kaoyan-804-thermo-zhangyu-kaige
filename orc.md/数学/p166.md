考研数学基础30讲·高等数学分册

$$
\frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} = \frac{y'_t}{x'_t} = \frac{3\sin^2 t \cos t}{-3\cos^2 t \sin t} = -\tan t, \quad \left. \frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} \right|_{t=\frac{\pi}{4}} = -1,
$$

$$
\frac{\mathrm{d}^2y}{\mathrm{d}x^2} = (-\tan t)' \frac{\mathrm{d}t}{\mathrm{d}x} = -\frac{1}{\cos^2 t} \cdot \frac{1}{-3\cos^2 t \sin t} = \frac{1}{3\cos^4 t \sin t}, \quad \left. \frac{\mathrm{d}^2y}{\mathrm{d}x^2} \right|_{t=\frac{\pi}{4}} = \frac{4}{3}\sqrt{2} \ .
$$

按曲率公式，$t=\frac{\pi}{4}$对应点处的曲率为

$$
k = \left. \frac{\left| \frac{\mathrm{d}^2y}{\mathrm{d}x^2} \right|}{\left[ 1 + \left( \frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} \right)^2 \right]^{3/2}} \right|_{t=\frac{\pi}{4}} = \frac{\frac{4}{3}\sqrt{2}}{2^{3/2}} = \frac{2}{3} \ .
$$
