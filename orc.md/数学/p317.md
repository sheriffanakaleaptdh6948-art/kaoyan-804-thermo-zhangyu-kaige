第13讲 多元函数微分学

$\xrightarrow{\text{视}y\text{为常数}}$
$$ \frac{\partial F}{\partial x} = \int_0^{x-y} f(t)\mathrm{d}t + xf(x-y) - yf(x-y) - (x-y)f(x-y) = \int_0^{x-y} f(t)\mathrm{d}t, $$
$$ \frac{\partial^2 F}{\partial x^2} = f(x-y), $$

$\xrightarrow{\text{视}x\text{为常数}}$
$$ \frac{\partial F}{\partial y} = -xf(x-y) - \int_0^{x-y} f(t)\mathrm{d}t + yf(x-y) + (x-y)f(x-y) = -\int_0^{x-y} f(t)\mathrm{d}t, $$
$$ \frac{\partial^2 F}{\partial y^2} = f(x-y), $$

所以 $\frac{\partial F}{\partial x} = -\frac{\partial F}{\partial y}$，$\frac{\partial^2 F}{\partial x^2} = \frac{\partial^2 F}{\partial y^2}$.

**例 13.5** 设函数 $f(x, y) = \int_0^{xy} \mathrm{e}^{-xt^2}\mathrm{d}t$，则 $f'_x(1, +\infty) = \underline{\hspace{2cm}}$.

**解** 应填 $-\frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{4}$.

当 $x>0$ 时，
$$ f(x, y) = \int_0^{xy} \mathrm{e}^{-xt^2}\mathrm{d}t \xrightarrow{\text{令} \frac{u}{\sqrt{x}}=t, u=\sqrt{x}t} \frac{1}{\sqrt{x}} \int_0^{x^{\frac{3}{2}}y} \mathrm{e}^{-u^2}\mathrm{d}u, $$

于是 $f(x, +\infty) = \lim_{y \to +\infty} f(x, y) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{x}} \int_0^{+\infty} \mathrm{e}^{-u^2}\mathrm{d}u = \frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{\sqrt{x}}$，故
$$ f'_x(1, +\infty) = \frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2} \left( \frac{1}{\sqrt{x}} \right)' \Bigg|_{x=1} = \frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2} \left( -\frac{1}{2} \right) \frac{1}{x^{\frac{3}{2}}} \Bigg|_{x=1} = -\frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{4}. $$

(右侧图示公式)
$$ \int_0^{+\infty} \mathrm{e}^{-x}\mathrm{d}x = 1. $$
$$ \int_0^{+\infty} \mathrm{e}^{-x^2}\mathrm{d}x = \frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2}. $$

**注** 此题先计算 $f(x, +\infty)$，再计算 $f'_x(1, +\infty)$ 是简便的.
若先计算 $f'_x(x, y)$，再代入 $(1, +\infty)$，过程如下：

$\xrightarrow{\text{将}y\text{当作常数}}$
$$ f'_x(x, y) = -\frac{1}{2}x^{-\frac{3}{2}} \cdot \int_0^{x^{\frac{3}{2}}y} \mathrm{e}^{-u^2}\mathrm{d}u + \frac{1}{\sqrt{x}} \mathrm{e}^{-x^3 y^2} \cdot \frac{3}{2}\sqrt{x}y, \xrightarrow{\text{前面求导，后面不动} + \text{前面不动，后面求导}} $$

于是 $f'_x(1, +\infty) = -\frac{1}{2} \int_0^{+\infty} \mathrm{e}^{-u^2}\mathrm{d}u = -\frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{4}$. 略复杂.

两种方式计算 $f'_x(x_0, y_0)$，得 $f'_x(x_0, y_0) = \begin{cases} f'_x(x, y_0)\big|_{x=x_0}, & \text{先代值再求导} \\ f'_x(x, y)\big|_{\substack{x=x_0 \\ y=y_0}}. & \text{先求导再代值} \end{cases}$
