**9.17** 设 $f(x) = \int_0^1 t|t-x| \mathrm{d}t$，求 $f'(x)$.

解答

**9.1** $-\frac{1}{3}(1-x^2)^{\frac{3}{2}} + C_1$

**解** 由 $\int x f(x) \mathrm{d}x = \arcsin x + C$ 求导可得 $x f(x) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}$，则 $\frac{1}{f(x)} = x\sqrt{1-x^2}$，故
$$ \int \frac{1}{f(x)} \mathrm{d}x = \int x\sqrt{1-x^2} \mathrm{d}x = -\frac{1}{3}(1-x^2)^{\frac{3}{2}} + C_1. $$
