考研数学基础30讲 · 高等数学分册

$$
\begin{aligned}
\iint_{D} \frac{1}{3 x^{2}+y^{2}} \mathrm{~d} x \mathrm{~d} y &= \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{3}} \mathrm{~d} \theta \int_{\sqrt{\frac{1}{1-\cos \theta \sin \theta}}}^{\sqrt{\frac{2}{1-\cos \theta \sin \theta}}} \frac{1}{r^{2}\left(3 \cos ^{2} \theta+\sin ^{2} \theta\right)} r \mathrm{~d} r \\
&= \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{3}} \mathrm{~d} \theta \int_{\sqrt{\frac{1}{1-\cos \theta \sin \theta}}}^{\sqrt{\frac{2}{1-\cos \theta \sin \theta}}} \frac{1}{3 \cos ^{2} \theta+\sin ^{2} \theta} \cdot \frac{1}{r} \mathrm{~d} r \quad \xrightarrow{\ln r \Big|_{\sqrt{\frac{1}{1-\cos \theta \sin \theta}}}^{\sqrt{\frac{2}{1-\cos \theta \sin \theta}}}=\frac{\ln 2}{2}} \\
&= \frac{\ln 2}{2} \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{3}} \frac{1}{3 \cos ^{2} \theta+\sin ^{2} \theta} \mathrm{d} \theta \\
&= \frac{\ln 2}{2} \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{3}} \frac{1}{3+\tan ^{2} \theta} \mathrm{d}(\tan \theta) \quad \xleftarrow{\int \frac{\mathrm{d} x}{a^{2}+x^{2}}=\frac{1}{a} \arctan \frac{x}{a}+C(a>0)} \\
&= \frac{\ln 2}{2}\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} \arctan \frac{\tan \theta}{\sqrt{3}}\right)\bigg|_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{3}} \\
&= \frac{\sqrt{3} \ln 2}{24} \pi .
\end{aligned}
$$

（右侧图形标注：$y$轴，$x$轴，$O$，$y=\sqrt{3}x$，$y=x$，$\sqrt{2}$，$1$，$1$，$\sqrt{2}$）

> **注** 本题有两个办法画出 $D$ 的边界图形.
>
> 第一，描点法. 显然，$x^{2}+y^{2}-x y=1$ 与 $x^{2}+y^{2}-x y=2$ 分别与 $x$ 轴、$y$ 轴和 $y=x$ 交于 $(1,0)$ 与 $(\sqrt{2}, 0)$，$(0,1)$ 与 $(0, \sqrt{2})$，$(1,1)$ 与 $(\sqrt{2}, \sqrt{2})$，将这些点连起来即可得到其大致图形.
>
> 第二，事实上，$x^{2}+y^{2}-x y=[x, y]\left[\begin{array}{cc}1 & -\frac{1}{2} \\ -\frac{1}{2} & 1\end{array}\right]\left[\begin{array}{l}x \\ y\end{array}\right]$，其二次型矩阵为 $\boldsymbol{A}=\left[\begin{array}{cc}1 & -\frac{1}{2} \\ -\frac{1}{2} & 1\end{array}\right]$，$|\lambda \boldsymbol{E}-\boldsymbol{A}|=\left|\begin{array}{cc}\lambda-1 & \frac{1}{2} \\ \frac{1}{2} & \lambda-1\end{array}\right|=0$，得 $\lambda_{1}=\frac{1}{2}, \lambda_{2}=\frac{3}{2}$（或用配方法 $x^{2}+y^{2}-x y=\left(x-\frac{1}{2} y\right)^{2}+\frac{3}{4} y^{2}$）. 故 $x^{2}+y^{2}-x y$ 可经正交变换化为 $\frac{1}{2} y_{1}^{2}+\frac{3}{2} y_{2}^{2}$，于是 $\frac{1}{2} y_{1}^{2}+\frac{3}{2} y_{2}^{2}=1$ 与 $\frac{1}{2} y_{1}^{2}+\frac{3}{2} y_{2}^{2}=2$ 均为椭圆，即可画出图形.
>

**方法总结** 当积分区域给出的不是常见曲线时，可考虑描点法画积分区域图.
