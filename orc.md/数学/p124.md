$$ \lim_{x\to 0}\frac{\ln(1-2x)+2xf(x)}{x^{2}}=\lim_{x\to 0}\frac{-2x-\frac{1}{2}\cdot 4x^{2}+o(x^{2})+2xf(x)}{x^{2}}=2\lim_{x\to 0}\frac{f(x)-1}{x}-2+0=0, $$
