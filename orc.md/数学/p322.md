考研数学基础30讲·高等数学分册

(C) $z_1$ 在点 $(0,0)$ 处可微，$z_2$ 在点 $(0,0)$ 处不可微

(D) $z_1$ 在点 $(0,0)$ 处可微，$z_2$ 在点 $(0,0)$ 处可微

**分析**
$$
\Delta z_1 = z_1(0+\Delta x, 0+\Delta y) - z_1(0,0) = |\Delta x \Delta y|,
$$
$$
\text{d}z_1 = z'_{1x}(0,0) \cdot \Delta x + z'_{1y}(0,0) \cdot \Delta y,
$$
其中
$$
z'_{1x}(0,0) = \lim_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{z_1(0+\Delta x, 0) - z_1(0,0)}{\Delta x} = 0,
$$
同理
$$
z'_{1y}(0,0) = 0.
$$
$$
\Delta z_2 = \frac{\Delta x \cdot |\Delta y|}{\sqrt{(\Delta x)^2 + (\Delta y)^2}},
$$
$$
\text{d}z_2 = z'_{2x}(0,0)\Delta x + z'_{2y}(0,0) \cdot \Delta y,
$$
其中
$$
z'_{2x}(0,0) = \lim_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{z_2(0+\Delta x, 0) - z_2(0,0)}{\Delta x} = 0,
$$
同理
$$
z'_{2y}(0,0) = 0.
$$

**解** 应选 (C).

$$
\left. \frac{\partial z_1}{\partial x} \right|_{(0,0)} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{z_1(x,0) - z_1(0,0)}{x-0} = 0,
$$
$$
\left. \frac{\partial z_1}{\partial y} \right|_{(0,0)} = \lim_{y \to 0} \frac{z_1(0,y) - z_1(0,0)}{y-0} = 0,
$$
$$
\frac{z_1(x,y) - z_1(0,0) - \left. \frac{\partial z_1}{\partial x} \right|_{(0,0)} \cdot x - \left. \frac{\partial z_1}{\partial y} \right|_{(0,0)} \cdot y}{\sqrt{x^2+y^2}} = \frac{|xy|}{\sqrt{x^2+y^2}},
$$
由例 13.1 结合可微的判别步骤可知，$z_1$ 在点 $(0,0)$ 处可微.

$$
\left. \frac{\partial z_2}{\partial x} \right|_{(0,0)} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{z_2(x,0) - z_2(0,0)}{x-0} = 0,
$$
$$
\left. \frac{\partial z_2}{\partial y} \right|_{(0,0)} = \lim_{y \to 0} \frac{z_2(0,y) - z_2(0,0)}{y-0} = 0,
$$
$$
\frac{z_2(x,y) - z_2(0,0) - \left. \frac{\partial z_2}{\partial x} \right|_{(0,0)} \cdot x - \left. \frac{\partial z_2}{\partial y} \right|_{(0,0)} \cdot y}{\sqrt{x^2+y^2}} = \frac{x|y|}{x^2+y^2},
$$
由例 13.1 结合可微的判别步骤可知，$z_2$ 在点 $(0,0)$ 处不可微.
