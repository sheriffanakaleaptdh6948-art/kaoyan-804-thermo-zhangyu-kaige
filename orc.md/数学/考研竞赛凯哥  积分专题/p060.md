解：原式$=-\int x\mathrm{e}^x\mathrm{d}\frac{1}{1+x}=-\frac{x\mathrm{e}^x}{1+x}+\int \frac{1+x}{1+x}\mathrm{e}^x\mathrm{d}x=\frac{\mathrm{e}^x}{1+x}+C$

**类题 1** $\int \frac{(1+\sin x)\mathrm{e}^x}{1+\cos x}\mathrm{d}x$

解：原式$=\int \left(\frac{1}{1+\cos x}+\frac{\sin x}{1+\cos x}\right)\mathrm{e}^x\mathrm{d}x=\frac{\sin x}{1+\cos x}\mathrm{e}^x+C$

**类题 2** $\int \mathrm{e}^x\left(\frac{1-x}{1+x^2}\right)^2\mathrm{d}x$

解：原式$=\int \mathrm{e}^x\frac{x^2-2x+1}{(x^2+1)^2}=\int \mathrm{e}^x\left[\frac{1}{x^2+1}-\frac{2x}{(x^2+1)^2}\right]\mathrm{d}x=\frac{\mathrm{e}^x}{x^2+1}+C$

**类题 3** $\int \frac{x^2\mathrm{e}^x}{(x+2)^2}\mathrm{d}x$

解：原式$=-\int x^2\mathrm{e}^x\mathrm{d}\frac{1}{2+x}=-\frac{x^2\mathrm{e}^x}{x+2}+\int \frac{\mathrm{e}^x(2x+x^2)}{x+2}\mathrm{d}x=-\frac{x^2\mathrm{e}^x}{x+2}+(x-1)\mathrm{e}^x+C$

**例题 2** $\int \frac{\mathrm{e}^{-\sin x}\cdot \sin 2x}{\sin^4\left(\frac{\pi}{4}-\frac{x}{2}\right)}\mathrm{d}x$

解：令$\sin x=t$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式}&=8\int \frac{\sin x\cos x\mathrm{e}^{-\sin x}}{(1-\sin x)^2}\mathrm{d}x=8\int \frac{t\mathrm{e}^{-t}}{(t-1)^2}\mathrm{d}t=-8\int t\mathrm{e}^{-t}\mathrm{d}\frac{1}{t-1}=-8\frac{t\mathrm{e}^{-t}}{t-1}+8\mathrm{e}^{-t}+C \\
&=-8\frac{\sin x\mathrm{e}^{-\sin x}}{\sin x-1}+8\mathrm{e}^{-\sin x}+C
\end{aligned}
$$

**例题 3** $\int \mathrm{e}^{-\frac{x}{2}}\frac{\cos x-\sin x}{\sqrt{\sin x}}\mathrm{d}x$

解：原式$=\int \mathrm{e}^{-\frac{x}{2}}\frac{\cos x}{\sqrt{\sin x}}\mathrm{d}x-\int \mathrm{e}^{-\frac{x}{2}}\sqrt{\sin x}\mathrm{d}x=2\int \mathrm{e}^{-\frac{x}{2}}\mathrm{d}\sqrt{\sin x}-\int \mathrm{e}^{-\frac{x}{2}}\sqrt{\sin x}\mathrm{d}x=2\mathrm{e}^{-\frac{x}{2}}\sqrt{\sin x}+C$

**类题 1** $\int \mathrm{e}^{\sin x}\frac{x\cos^3 x-\sin x}{\cos^2 x}\mathrm{d}x$

解：原式$=\int x\mathrm{d}\mathrm{e}^{\sin x}-\int \mathrm{e}^{\sin x}\mathrm{d}\frac{1}{\cos x}=x\mathrm{e}^{\sin x}-\int \mathrm{e}^{\sin x}\mathrm{d}x-\frac{\mathrm{e}^{\sin x}}{\cos x}+\int \frac{\cos x}{\cos x}\mathrm{e}^{\sin x}\mathrm{d}x=x\mathrm{e}^{\sin x}-\frac{\mathrm{e}^{\sin x}}{\cos x}+C$

**类题 2** $\int \left(1+x-\frac{1}{x}\right)\mathrm{e}^{x+\frac{1}{x}}\mathrm{d}x$

解：原式$=\int \mathrm{e}^{x+\frac{1}{x}}\mathrm{d}x+\int \left(x-\frac{1}{x}\right)\mathrm{e}^{x+\frac{1}{x}}\mathrm{d}x=x\mathrm{e}^{x+\frac{1}{x}}-\int x\mathrm{e}^{x+\frac{1}{x}}\left(1-\frac{1}{x^2}\right)\mathrm{d}x+\int \left(x-\frac{1}{x}\right)\mathrm{e}^{x+\frac{1}{x}}\mathrm{d}x$
$$=x\mathrm{e}^{x+\frac{1}{x}}+C$$

**例题 4** $\int \left(\ln\ln x+\frac{1}{\ln x}\right)\mathrm{d}x$

解：原式$=x\ln\ln x-\int \frac{1}{\ln x}\mathrm{d}x+\int \frac{1}{\ln x}\mathrm{d}x=x\ln\ln x+C$
