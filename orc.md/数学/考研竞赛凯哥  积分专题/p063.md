类题 $\int_{0}^{+\infty} \frac{1}{\left(1+x^{2}\right)\left(1+x^{\alpha}\right)} \mathrm{d} x$

解：事实上做变换 $x \mapsto \tan x$ 后跟例题 2 是一样的。

这里我们用倒代换：$\int_{0}^{+\infty} \frac{\mathrm{d} x}{\left(1+x^{2}\right)\left(1+x^{\alpha}\right)} \xlongequal{x \mapsto \frac{1}{x}} \int_{0}^{+\infty} \frac{x^{\alpha}}{\left(1+x^{2}\right)\left(1+x^{\alpha}\right)} \mathrm{d} x=\frac{1}{2} \int_{0}^{+\infty} \frac{\mathrm{d} x}{1+x^{2}}=\frac{\pi}{4}$.

**例题 3** $\int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\left(\arctan e^{x}\right) \cdot \sin ^{2} x \mathrm{~d} x$

解：$\int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \arctan \left(e^{x}\right) \cdot \sin ^{2} x \mathrm{~d} x=\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\left[\arctan \left(e^{x}\right)+\arctan \left(e^{-x}\right)\right] \sin ^{2} x \mathrm{~d} x=\frac{\pi}{2} \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \sin ^{2} x \mathrm{~d} x=\frac{\pi^{2}}{8}$.

类题 $\int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{\sin ^{4} x}{1+\mathrm{e}^{-2 x}} \mathrm{~d} x$

解：$\int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{\sin ^{4} x}{1+\mathrm{e}^{-2 x}} \mathrm{~d} x=\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\left(\frac{\sin ^{4} x}{1+\mathrm{e}^{-2 x}}+\frac{\sin ^{4} x}{1+\mathrm{e}^{2 x}}\right) \mathrm{d} x=\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \sin ^{4} x \mathrm{~d} x=\frac{3 \pi}{16}$.

**例题 4** $\int_{-2}^{2} x \ln \left(1+\mathrm{e}^{x}\right) \mathrm{d} x$

解：$\int_{-2}^{2} x \ln \left(1+\mathrm{e}^{x}\right) \mathrm{d} x=\int_{0}^{2}\left[x \ln \left(1+\mathrm{e}^{x}\right)-x \ln \left(1+\mathrm{e}^{-x}\right)\right] \mathrm{d} x=\int_{0}^{2} x^{2} \mathrm{~d} x=\frac{8}{3}$.

**例题 5** $\int_{\frac{\pi}{6}}^{\frac{\pi}{3}} \frac{\cos ^{2} x}{x(\pi-2 x)} \mathrm{d} x$

解：$\int_{\frac{\pi}{6}}^{\frac{\pi}{3}} \frac{\cos ^{2} x}{x(\pi-2 x)} \mathrm{d} x=\int_{\frac{\pi}{6}}^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\left[\frac{\cos ^{2} x}{x(\pi-2 x)}+\frac{\sin ^{2} x}{x(\pi-2 x)}\right] \mathrm{d} x=\int_{\frac{\pi}{6}}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \frac{\mathrm{d} x}{x(\pi-2 x)}=\frac{\ln 2}{\pi}$.

**例题 6** $\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \frac{x}{\cos \left(\frac{\pi}{4}-x\right) \cos x} \mathrm{~d} x$

提示：请思考一切形如 $\int \frac{1}{\sin (x+a) \sin (x+b)} \mathrm{d} x$ （其中 $\sin (a-b) \neq 0$）的积分应该如何计算

解：$\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \frac{x}{\cos \left(\frac{\pi}{4}-x\right) \cos x} \mathrm{~d} x=\frac{\pi}{8} \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \frac{\mathrm{d} x}{\cos \left(\frac{\pi}{4}-x\right) \cos x}=\frac{\pi}{4 \sqrt{2}} \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \frac{\sin \left(\frac{\pi}{4}-x+x\right)}{\cos \left(\frac{\pi}{4}-x\right) \cos x} \mathrm{~d} x$

$=\frac{\pi}{4 \sqrt{2}} \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\left[\tan \left(\frac{\pi}{4}-x\right)+\tan x\right] \mathrm{d} x=\frac{\pi}{2 \sqrt{2}} \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \tan x \mathrm{~d} x=\frac{\pi}{4 \sqrt{2}} \ln 2$.

**例题 7** $\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \ln \sin x \mathrm{~d} x$

解：$\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \ln (\sin x) \mathrm{d} x=\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \ln \left(\frac{1}{2} \sin 2 x\right) \mathrm{d} x \xlongequal{2 x \mapsto x}-\frac{\pi}{4} \ln 2+\frac{1}{2} \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \ln (\sin x) \mathrm{d} x=-\frac{\pi}{2} \ln 2$.
