**例题 2** 计算定积分 $\displaystyle \int_{0}^{2\pi} \frac{1}{1+\cos^2 x} \mathrm{d}x$

**解：** $\displaystyle \int_{0}^{2\pi} \frac{1}{1+\cos^2 x} \mathrm{d}x = 4 \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{1}{1+\cos^2 x} \mathrm{d}x = 4 \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{\sec^2 x}{\sec^2 x + 1} \mathrm{d}x$
$$
= 4 \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{\sec^2 x}{\sec^2 x + 1} \mathrm{d}x = 4 \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{1}{\tan^2 x + 2} \mathrm{d}\tan x = 2\sqrt{2} \arctan \left( \frac{\tan x}{\sqrt{2}} \right) \Bigg|_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} = \sqrt{2}\pi;
$$

**类题 1** 计算定积分 $\displaystyle \int_{0}^{\pi} \frac{1}{1+\sin^2 x} \mathrm{d}x$

**解：** $\displaystyle \int_{0}^{\pi} \frac{1}{1+\sin^2 x} \mathrm{d}x = 2 \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{1}{1+\sin^2 x} \mathrm{d}x \xrightarrow{t=\frac{\pi}{2}-x} 2 \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{1}{1+\cos^2 x} \mathrm{d}x = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$

**类题 2** 以下计算是否正确？为什么？如果错了，请将其更正。
$$
\int_{-1}^{1} \left( \arctan \frac{1}{x} \right)' \mathrm{d}x = \arctan \frac{1}{x} \Bigg|_{-1}^{1} = \arctan 1 - \arctan(-1) = \frac{\pi}{4} - \left( -\frac{\pi}{4} \right) = \frac{\pi}{2}
$$
**解：** 错误，积分区间存在无定义的点。
正确做法：$\displaystyle \int_{-1}^{1} \left( \arctan \frac{1}{x} \right)' \mathrm{d}x = \int_{-1}^{0} \left( \arctan \frac{1}{x} \right)' \mathrm{d}x + \int_{0}^{1} \left( \arctan \frac{1}{x} \right)' \mathrm{d}x$
$$
= \lim_{x \to 0^-} \arctan \frac{1}{x} + \frac{\pi}{4} + \frac{\pi}{4} - \lim_{x \to 0^+} \arctan \frac{1}{x} = -\frac{\pi}{2};
$$
