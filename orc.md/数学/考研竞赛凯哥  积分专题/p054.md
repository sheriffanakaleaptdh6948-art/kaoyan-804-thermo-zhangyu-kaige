NO.4-2 不定积分解题方法（下）

**套路三** 换元法的基本套路

**（1）整体换元**

**例题 1** $\int \sqrt{\frac{x}{x+1}} \mathrm{d}x$

**解：** 令 $\sqrt{\frac{x}{x+1}}=t$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= \int t \mathrm{d} \frac{1}{1-t^2} = \frac{t}{1-t^2} + \int \frac{1}{t^2-1} \mathrm{d}t = \frac{t}{1-t^2} + \frac{1}{2} \ln \left| \frac{t-1}{t+1} \right| + C \\
&= (x+1)\sqrt{\frac{x}{1+x}} + \frac{1}{2} \ln \left| \frac{\sqrt{x}-\sqrt{x+1}}{\sqrt{x}+\sqrt{x+1}} \right| + C
\end{aligned}
$$

**类题 1** $\int \frac{1}{x} \sqrt{\frac{x+1}{x}} \mathrm{d}x$

**解：** 令 $\sqrt{\frac{x+1}{x}}=t$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= \int (t^2-1)t \mathrm{d} \frac{1}{t^2-1} = t - \int \frac{1}{t^2-1}(3t^2-3)+2t \mathrm{d}t \\
&= t - \left[ 3t + 2 \int \frac{1}{t^2-1} \mathrm{d}t \right] \\
&= -2t - \ln \left| \frac{t-1}{t+1} \right| + C \\
&= -2\sqrt{\frac{x+1}{x}} - \ln \left| \frac{\sqrt{x+1}-\sqrt{x}}{\sqrt{x}+\sqrt{x+1}} \right| + C
\end{aligned}
$$

**类题 2** 请计算 $\int \sqrt{\frac{1-x}{1+x}} \mathrm{d}x$ 和 $\int \left( \sqrt{\frac{1-x}{1+x}} + \sqrt{\frac{1+x}{1-x}} \right) \mathrm{d}x$ 两个积分

**解：** (1) $\text{原式} = \int \frac{1-x}{\sqrt{1-x^2}} \mathrm{d}x = \arcsin x - \int \frac{x}{\sqrt{1-x^2}} \mathrm{d}x = \arcsin x + \sqrt{1-x^2} + C$

(2) $\text{原式} = \int \left( \frac{1-x}{\sqrt{1-x^2}} + \frac{1+x}{\sqrt{1-x^2}} \right) \mathrm{d}x = \int \frac{2}{\sqrt{1-x^2}} \mathrm{d}x = 2\arcsin x + C$

**类题 3** $\int \frac{x \mathrm{e}^x}{\sqrt{\mathrm{e}^x-2}} \mathrm{d}x$

**解：** 令 $\sqrt{\mathrm{e}^x-2}=t$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= \int \frac{\ln(2+t^2)}{t} (2+t^2) \frac{2t}{(2+t^2)} \mathrm{d}t = 2 \int \ln(2+t^2) \mathrm{d}t = 2t \ln(2+t^2) - 2 \int t \cdot \frac{2t}{(2+t^2)} \mathrm{d}t \\
&= 2t \ln(2+t^2) - 4t + 2\sqrt{2} \arctan \frac{t}{\sqrt{2}} + C = 2x\sqrt{\mathrm{e}^x-2} - 4\sqrt{\mathrm{e}^x-2} + 2\sqrt{2} \arctan \frac{\sqrt{\mathrm{e}^x-2}}{\sqrt{2}} + C
\end{aligned}
$$
