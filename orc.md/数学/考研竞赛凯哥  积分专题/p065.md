③ 考虑 $\arcsin x$ 的级数展开式 $(-1 \leqslant x \leqslant 1)$：
$$
\begin{aligned}
\arcsin x &= \int_{0}^{x} \frac{\mathrm{d}t}{\sqrt{1-t^2}} = \int_{0}^{x} (1-t^2)^{-\frac{1}{2}} \mathrm{d}t = \int_{0}^{x} \left[ 1 + \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} -\frac{1}{2}\left(-\frac{1}{2}-1\right)\cdots\left(-\frac{1}{2}-n+1\right) \frac{(-t^2)^n}{n!} \right] \mathrm{d}t \\
&= \int_{0}^{x} \left[ 1 + \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{(2n-1)!!}{2^n n!} t^{2n} \right] \mathrm{d}t = \int_{0}^{x} \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{(2n)!}{2^{2n} n! n!} t^{2n} \mathrm{d}t = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{(2n)!}{2^{2n}(2n+1)n!n!} x^{2n+1}, \quad x \in [-1, 1]
\end{aligned}
$$
代换 $x \mapsto \sin x$，并从 $0$ 到 $\frac{\pi}{2}$ 积分： $\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} x \mathrm{d}x = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{(2n)!}{2^{2n}(2n+1)n!n!} \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \sin^{2n+1}x \mathrm{d}x$，运用 Wallis 公式计算： $\frac{\pi^2}{8} = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{(2n)!}{2^{2n}(2n+1)n!n!} \frac{(2n)!!}{(2n+1)!!} = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{1}{(2n+1)^2}$，同样得到 $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^2} = \frac{\pi^2}{6}$.

**例题 10** $\int_{0}^{1} \frac{\arcsin\sqrt{x}}{\sqrt{x^2-x+1}} \mathrm{d}x$

**解：** $\int_{0}^{1} \frac{\arcsin\sqrt{x}}{\sqrt{1-x+x^2}} \mathrm{d}x = \int_{0}^{\frac{1}{2}} \left( \frac{\arcsin\sqrt{x}}{\sqrt{1-x+x^2}} + \frac{\arcsin\sqrt{1-x}}{\sqrt{1-x+x^2}} \right) \mathrm{d}x = \frac{\pi}{2} \int_{0}^{\frac{1}{2}} \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\sqrt{1-x+x^2}}$
$$
= \frac{\pi}{2} \int_{0}^{\frac{1}{2}} \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\sqrt{\frac{3}{4} + \left(\frac{1}{2}-x\right)^2}} \xlongequal{x \mapsto \frac{1}{2} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\tan x} \frac{\pi}{2} \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{6}} \sec x \mathrm{d}x = \frac{\pi}{4} \ln 3.
$$

**例题 11** $\int_{0}^{1} x \cdot \arcsin\left(2\sqrt{x-x^2}\right) \mathrm{d}x$

**解：** $\int_{0}^{1} x \cdot \arcsin\left(2\sqrt{x-x^2}\right) \mathrm{d}x \xlongequal{x \mapsto 1-x} \int_{0}^{1} (1-x) \cdot \arcsin\left(2\sqrt{x-x^2}\right) \mathrm{d}x = \int_{0}^{\frac{1}{2}} \arcsin\left(2\sqrt{x-x^2}\right) \mathrm{d}x$
$$
\xlongequal{\text{分部积分}} \frac{\pi}{4} - \int_{0}^{\frac{1}{2}} \frac{x}{\sqrt{x-x^2}} \mathrm{d}x \xlongequal{x \mapsto \frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{2}\sin x} \frac{\pi}{4} - \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \left(\frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{2}\sin x\right) \mathrm{d}x = \frac{1}{2}.
$$

**例题 12** 计算积分 $\int_{0}^{1} (1-x)^{100} x \mathrm{d}x$

**解：** $\int_{0}^{1} x(1-x)^{100} \mathrm{d}x = \int_{0}^{1} x^{100}(1-x) \mathrm{d}x = \frac{1}{101} - \frac{1}{102} = \frac{1}{10302}$.

**例题 13** 计算积分 $\int_{0}^{2} x(x-1)(x-2) \mathrm{d}x$

**解：** $\int_{0}^{2} x(x-1)(x-2) \mathrm{d}x \xlongequal{x \mapsto 2-x} \int_{0}^{1} [x(x-1)(x-2) - x(x-1)(x-2)] \mathrm{d}x = 0$.

**类题** 计算积分 $\int_{0}^{2n} x(x-1)(x-2)\cdots(x-n)\cdots[x-(2n-1)](x-2n) \mathrm{d}x$

**解：** 记 $I = \int_{0}^{2n} x(x-1)(x-2)\cdots(x-n)\cdots(x-2n) \mathrm{d}x$，则 $I \xlongequal{x \mapsto 2n-x} (-1)^{2n+1} I = 0$.
