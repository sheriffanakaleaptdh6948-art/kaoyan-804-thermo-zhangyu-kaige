NO.5-3 定积分计算（下）

**综合题型一 被积函数中含有变限积分函数的定积分计算**

**例题 1** 设 $f(x)=\int_{0}^{x} \frac{\sin t}{\pi-t} \mathrm{d} t$，计算 $\int_{0}^{\pi} f(x) \mathrm{d} x$（三个方法）

**解 1：**
$$ \int_{0}^{\pi} f(x) \mathrm{d} x=\pi f(\pi)-\int_{0}^{\pi} x \mathrm{d} f(x)=\int_{0}^{\pi} \frac{\pi \sin t}{\pi-t} \mathrm{d} t-\int_{0}^{\pi} \frac{x \sin x}{\pi-x} \mathrm{d} x=\int_{0}^{\pi} \sin x \mathrm{d} x=2 . $$

**解 2：**
$$ \int_{0}^{\pi} f(x) \mathrm{d} x=\int_{0}^{\pi} \int_{0}^{x} \frac{\sin t}{\pi-t} \mathrm{d} t \mathrm{d} x \xrightarrow{\text { 交换积分次序 }} \int_{0}^{\pi} \int_{t}^{\pi} \frac{\sin t}{\pi-t} \mathrm{d} x \mathrm{d} t=\int_{0}^{\pi} \sin t \mathrm{d} t=2 . $$

**类题 1** 设 $f(x)=\int_{1}^{x} \frac{\ln (1+t)}{t} \mathrm{d} t$，计算 $\int_{0}^{1} \frac{f(x)}{\sqrt{x}} \mathrm{d} x$

**解 1：**
$$ \begin{aligned} \int_{0}^{1} \frac{f(x)}{\sqrt{x}} \mathrm{d} x &=2 \int_{0}^{1} f(x) \mathrm{d} \sqrt{x}=-2 \int_{0}^{1} \sqrt{x} \mathrm{d} f(x)=-2 \int_{0}^{1} \frac{\ln (1+x)}{\sqrt{x}} \mathrm{d} x \xrightarrow{x \mapsto x^{2}}-4 \int_{0}^{1} \ln \left(1+x^{2}\right) \mathrm{d} x \\ &=-4 \ln 2+8 \int_{0}^{1} \frac{x^{2}}{1+x^{2}} \mathrm{d} x=8-2 \pi-4 \ln 2 . \end{aligned} $$

**解 2：**
$$ \begin{aligned} \int_{0}^{1} \frac{f(x)}{\sqrt{x}} \mathrm{d} x &=-\int_{0}^{1} \frac{1}{\sqrt{x}} \int_{x}^{1} \frac{\ln (1+t)}{t} \mathrm{d} t \mathrm{d} x=-\int_{0}^{1} \frac{\ln (1+t)}{t} \int_{0}^{t} \frac{\mathrm{d} x}{\sqrt{x}} \mathrm{d} t=-2 \int_{0}^{1} \frac{\ln (1+t)}{\sqrt{t}} \mathrm{d} t \\ &=8-2 \pi-4 \ln 2 . \end{aligned} $$

**类题 2** 设 $f^{\prime}(x)=\arctan (x-1)^{2}$，且 $f(0)=0$，求 $I=\int_{0}^{1} f(x) \mathrm{d} x$

**解 1：**
$$ \begin{aligned} \int_{0}^{1} f(x) \mathrm{d} x &=f(1)-\int_{0}^{1} x \mathrm{d} f(x)=\int_{0}^{1} \arctan (x-1)^{2} \mathrm{d} x-\int_{0}^{1} x \arctan (x-1)^{2} \mathrm{d} x \xrightarrow{(x-1)^{2} \mapsto x} \frac{1}{2} \int_{0}^{1} \arctan x \mathrm{d} x \\ &=\frac{\pi}{8}-\frac{1}{2} \int_{0}^{1} \frac{x}{1+x^{2}} \mathrm{d} x=\frac{\pi}{8}-\frac{1}{4} \ln 2 . \end{aligned} $$

**解 2：**
$$ \begin{aligned} \int_{0}^{1} f(x) \mathrm{d} x &=\int_{0}^{1} \int_{0}^{x} \arctan (t-1)^{2} \mathrm{d} t \mathrm{d} x=\int_{0}^{1} \int_{t}^{1} \arctan (t-1)^{2} \mathrm{d} x \mathrm{d} t=\int_{0}^{1}(1-t) \arctan (t-1)^{2} \mathrm{d} t \\ &=\frac{\pi}{8}-\frac{1}{4} \ln 2 . \end{aligned} $$

(1) 求 $f(x)$ 在 $\left[0, \frac{3}{2} \pi\right]$ 上的平均值

(2) 证明 $f(x)$ 在 $\left(0, \frac{3}{2} \pi\right)$ 内有唯一零点

**解：** (1)
