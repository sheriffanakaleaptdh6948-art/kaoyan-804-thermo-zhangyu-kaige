类题 1 $\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{x}{\tan x} \mathrm{d} x$

解：$\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{x}{\tan x} \mathrm{d} x=\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} x \mathrm{d} \ln (\sin x)=-\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \ln (\sin x) \mathrm{d} x=\frac{\pi}{2} \ln 2$.

类题 2 $\int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{\cos x \cdot \ln \cos x}{1+\sin x+\cos x} \mathrm{d} x$ （套娃题）

解：$\int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{\cos x \cdot \ln (\cos x)}{1+\sin x+\cos x} \mathrm{d} x=\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\left[\frac{\cos x \cdot \ln (\cos x)}{1+\sin x+\cos x}+\frac{\cos x \cdot \ln (\cos x)}{1-\sin x+\cos x}\right] \mathrm{d} x=\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \ln (\cos x) \mathrm{d} x=-\frac{\pi}{2} \ln 2$.

例题 8 计算 $\int_{0}^{1} \frac{\ln (1+x)}{1+x^{2}} \mathrm{d} x$ 和 $\int_{0}^{1} \frac{\arctan x}{1+x} \mathrm{d} x$

解：$\int_{0}^{1} \frac{\ln (1+x)}{1+x^{2}} \mathrm{d} x \xrightarrow{x \mapsto \tan x} \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \ln (1+\tan x) \mathrm{d} x=\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \ln \left(\sqrt{2} \sin \left(x+\frac{\pi}{4}\right)\right) \mathrm{d} x-\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \ln (\cos x) \mathrm{d} x$
$$ \xrightarrow{x+\frac{\pi}{4} \mapsto x, x \mapsto \frac{\pi}{2}-x} \frac{\pi}{8} \ln 2+\int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \ln (\sin x) \mathrm{d} x-\int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \ln (\sin x) \mathrm{d} x=\frac{\pi}{8} \ln 2 ; $$
$$ \int_{0}^{1} \frac{\arctan x}{1+x} \mathrm{d} x=\int_{0}^{1} \arctan x \mathrm{d} \ln (1+x)=\frac{\pi}{4} \ln 2-\int_{0}^{1} \frac{\ln (1+x)}{1+x^{2}} \mathrm{d} x=\frac{\pi}{8} \ln 2 . $$

例题 9 计算 $\int_{0}^{n \pi} x|\sin x| \mathrm{d} x$

解：$\int_{0}^{n \pi} x|\sin x| \mathrm{d} x=\sum_{k=1}^{n} \int_{(k-1) \pi}^{k \pi} x|\sin x| \mathrm{d} x \xrightarrow{x \mapsto x+(k-1) \pi} \sum_{k=1}^{n} \int_{0}^{\pi}[x+(k-1) \pi] \sin x \mathrm{d} x$
$$ =\frac{\pi}{2} \sum_{k=1}^{n} \int_{0}^{\pi} \sin x \mathrm{d} x+\pi \sum_{k=1}^{n}(k-1) \int_{0}^{\pi} \sin x \mathrm{d} x=n^{2} \pi . $$

类题 设 $a_{n}=\int_{0}^{n \pi} x|\sin x| \mathrm{d} x$，则 $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{a_{n}}=\_\_\_$ （仅数一、数三）

解：$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{a_{n}}=\frac{1}{\pi} \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^{2}}=\frac{\pi}{6}$. 对于 $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^{2}}=\frac{\pi^{2}}{6}$ 我们给出如下证明：

① 考虑 $f(x)=x, x \in(-\pi, \pi)$ 的 Fourier 展开式
$$ f(x) \text { 是奇函数，故 } f(x)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} b_{n} \sin n x, b_{n}=\frac{1}{\pi} \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} x \sin n x \mathrm{d} x=\frac{1}{\pi}\left[\frac{\sin n x}{n^{2}}-\frac{x \cos n x}{n}\right]_{-\pi}^{\pi}=2 \frac{(-1)^{n-1}}{n}, $$
$$ x=2 \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^{n-1}}{n} \sin n x . \text { 运用帕塞瓦尔恒等式： } \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^{2}}=\frac{1}{4 \pi} \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} x^{2} \mathrm{d} x=\frac{\pi^{2}}{6} ; $$
② 考虑 $h(x)=x^{2}, x \in(-\pi, \pi)$ 的 Fourier 展开式。
$h(x)$ 是偶函数，故
$$ h(x)=\frac{a_{0}}{2}+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_{n} \cos n x, a_{n}=\frac{1}{\pi} \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} x^{2} \cos n x \mathrm{d} x=4 \frac{(-1)^{n}}{n^{2}}, a_{0}=\frac{1}{\pi} \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} x^{2} \mathrm{d} x=\frac{2 \pi^{2}}{3}, $$
