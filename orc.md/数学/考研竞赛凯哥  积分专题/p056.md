**例题 7** $\displaystyle \int \frac{1}{x^2\sqrt{x^2-1}} \, dx$

解：令 $x=\sec t$
$$
\text{原式} = \int \frac{\sec t \tan t}{\sec^2 t \tan t} \, dt = \int \frac{1}{\sec t} \, dt = \int \cos t \, dt = \sin t + C = \sqrt{1-\frac{1}{x^2}} + C
$$

**类题 1** $\displaystyle \int \frac{1}{x\sqrt{2x^2+2x+1}} \, dx$

解：令 $x=\frac{1}{t}$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= -\int \frac{1}{\sqrt{t^2+2t+2}} \, dt = -\int \frac{1}{\sqrt{1+(t+1)^2}} \, d(t+1) \\
&= -\ln\left|t+1+\sqrt{1+(t+1)^2}\right| + C = -\ln\left|\frac{1}{x}+1+\sqrt{1+\left(\frac{1}{x}+1\right)^2}\right| + C
\end{aligned}
$$

**类题 2** $\displaystyle \int \frac{1}{x^2\sqrt{2x^2+2x+1}} \, dx$

解：令 $x=\frac{1}{t}$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= -\int \frac{t+1-1}{\sqrt{t^2+2t+2}} \, dt = \int \frac{1}{\sqrt{1+(t+1)^2}} \, d(t+1) - \int \frac{t+1}{\sqrt{t^2+2t+2}} \, dt \\
&= -\ln\left|t+1+\sqrt{1+(t+1)^2}\right| - \sqrt{t^2+2t+2} + C \\
&= -\ln\left|\frac{1}{x}+1+\sqrt{1+\left(\frac{1}{x}+1\right)^2}\right| - \frac{\sqrt{2x^2+2x+1}}{x} + C
\end{aligned}
$$
