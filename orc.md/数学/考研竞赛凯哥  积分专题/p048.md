**类题 2** $\int \frac{1}{1+x^6} \mathrm{d}x$

**解：** $I = \int \frac{1+x^2-x^2}{1+x^6} \mathrm{d}x = \int \frac{1}{1+x^4-x^2} \mathrm{d}x - \frac{1}{3} \arctan x^3 = \frac{1}{2} \int \frac{1+x^2+1-x^2}{1+x^4-x^2} \mathrm{d}x - \frac{1}{3} \arctan x^3$
$$ = \frac{1}{2} \int \frac{1+\frac{1}{x^2}}{x^2+\frac{1}{x^2}-1} \mathrm{d}x + \frac{1}{2} \int \frac{\frac{1}{x^2}-1}{x^2+\frac{1}{x^2}-1} \mathrm{d}x - \frac{1}{3} \arctan x^3 $$
$$ = \frac{1}{2} \int \frac{\mathrm{d}(x-\frac{1}{x})}{(x-\frac{1}{x})^2+1} - \frac{1}{2} \int \frac{\mathrm{d}(x+\frac{1}{x})}{(x+\frac{1}{x})^2-3} - \frac{1}{3} \arctan x^3 $$
$$ = \frac{1}{2} \arctan(x-\frac{1}{x}) - \frac{1}{4\sqrt{3}} \ln \left| \frac{x+\frac{1}{x}-\sqrt{3}}{x+\frac{1}{x}+\sqrt{3}} \right| - \frac{1}{3} \arctan x^3 + C $$

**套路二 三角有理函数的积分**

**（一）三角有理函数积分的通用方法**

**例题 1** $\int \frac{1}{3+5\cos x} \mathrm{d}x$

**解：** 使用万能公式，令 $u = \tan \frac{x}{2}$
$$ I = \int \frac{1}{3+5\frac{1-u^2}{1+u^2}} \cdot \frac{2}{1+u^2} \mathrm{d}u = \int \frac{1}{4-u^2} \mathrm{d}u = \frac{1}{4} \int \frac{2+u+2-u}{4-u^2} \mathrm{d}u = \frac{1}{4} \int \left( \frac{1}{2-u} + \frac{1}{2+u} \right) \mathrm{d}u $$
$$ = \frac{1}{4} \ln \left| \frac{u+2}{u-2} \right| + C = \frac{1}{4} \ln \left| \frac{\tan \frac{x}{2} + 2}{\tan \frac{x}{2} - 2} \right| + C $$

**例题 2** $\int \frac{1}{1+\sin x + \cos x} \mathrm{d}x$

**解：** 使用万能公式，令 $u = \tan \frac{x}{2}$
$$ I = \int \frac{1}{1+\frac{2u}{1+u^2}+\frac{1-u^2}{1+u^2}} \cdot \frac{2}{1+u^2} \mathrm{d}u = \int \frac{1}{u+1} \mathrm{d}u = \ln|u+1| + C = \ln \left| \tan \frac{x}{2} + 1 \right| + C $$
