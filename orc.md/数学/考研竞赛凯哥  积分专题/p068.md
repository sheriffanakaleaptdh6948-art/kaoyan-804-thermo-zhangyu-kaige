**例题 3** 设 $I_{n}=\int_{0}^{2 \pi} \sin ^{n} x \mathrm{~d} x$ ，$J_{n}=\int_{0}^{2 \pi} \cos ^{n} x \mathrm{~d} x$ ，请背住下列常用结论。

(1) 对于任意正整数 $n$ ，均有 $I_{n}=J_{n}$

(2) 当 $n$ 是偶数时，$I_{n}=4 \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \sin ^{n} x \mathrm{~d} x=4 \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \cos ^{n} x \mathrm{~d} x$

(3) 当 $n$ 是奇数时，$I_{n}=0$

**例题 4** 计算 $\int \frac{\sin 10 x}{\sin x} \mathrm{~d} x$

**解：** $\int \frac{\sin 10 x}{\sin x} \mathrm{~d} x=\int 2 \sum_{k=1}^{5} \cos (2 k-1) x \mathrm{~d} x=2 \sum_{k=1}^{5} \frac{\sin (2 k-1) x}{2 k-1}+C$ ;

**例题 5** 计算 $I_{n}=\int_{0}^{\pi} \frac{\sin (2 n+1) x}{\sin x} \mathrm{~d} x$

**解：** $I_{n}-I_{n-1}=\int_{0}^{\pi} \frac{\sin (2 n+1) x}{\sin x} \mathrm{~d} x-\int_{0}^{\pi} \frac{\sin (2 n-1) x}{\sin x} \mathrm{~d} x$

$$ =\int_{0}^{\pi} \frac{\sin (2 n+1) x-\sin (2 n-1) x}{\sin x} \mathrm{~d} x=\int_{0}^{\pi} \frac{2 \cos 2 n x \cdot \sin x}{\sin x} \mathrm{~d} x=\int_{0}^{\pi} 2 \cos 2 n x \mathrm{~d} x=\left.\frac{\sin 2 n x}{n}\right|_{0} ^{\pi}=0 $$

所以 $I_{n}=I_{0}=\int_{0}^{\pi} \frac{\sin x}{\sin x} \mathrm{~d} x=\pi$ ;

**例题 6** 若 $a_{n}=\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\left(\frac{\sin n x}{\sin x}\right)^{2} \mathrm{~d} x$ ，计算 $a_{n}$ ;

**解：** $a_{n+1}-a_{n}=\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{\sin ^{2}(n+1) x-\sin ^{2} n x}{\sin ^{2} x} \mathrm{~d} x$

$$ =\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{(\sin (n+1) x+\sin n x)(\sin (n+1) x-\sin n x)}{\sin ^{2} x} \mathrm{~d} x $$

$$ =\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{2 \sin \frac{2 n+1}{2} x \cos \frac{x}{2} \cdot 2 \cos \frac{2 n+1}{2} x \sin \frac{x}{2}}{\sin ^{2} x} \mathrm{~d} x $$

$$ =\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{\sin x \sin (2 n+1) x}{\sin ^{2} x} \mathrm{~d} x=\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{\sin (2 n+1) x}{\sin x} \mathrm{~d} x $$

令 $I_{n}=\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{\sin (2 n+1) x}{\sin x} \mathrm{~d} x$ ，$I_{n}-I_{n-1}=\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{\sin (2 n+1) x}{\sin x} \mathrm{~d} x-\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{\sin (2 n-1) x}{\sin x} \mathrm{~d} x$

$$ =\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{\sin (2 n+1) x-\sin (2 n-1) x}{\sin x} \mathrm{~d} x=\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{2 \cos 2 n x \cdot \sin x}{\sin x} \mathrm{~d} x=\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} 2 \cos 2 n x \mathrm{~d} x=\left.\frac{\sin 2 n x}{n}\right|_{0} ^{\frac{\pi}{2}}=0 $$
