第13讲 多元函数微分学

$$
F(x, y, \lambda) = x^2 - y^2 + 2 + \lambda \left( x^2 + \frac{y^2}{4} - 1 \right).
$$

令 $\begin{cases} \frac{\partial F}{\partial x} = 2x + 2\lambda x = 0, \\ \frac{\partial F}{\partial y} = -2y + \frac{\lambda}{2} y = 0, \\ \frac{\partial F}{\partial \lambda} = x^2 + \frac{y^2}{4} - 1 = 0, \end{cases}$ 解得 $M_1(0, 2), M_2(0, -2), M_3(1, 0), M_4(-1, 0)$. 此时

$$
f(M_1) = f(M_2) = -2, f(M_3) = f(M_4) = 3,
$$

故在边界 $x^2 + \frac{y^2}{4} = 1$ 上的最大值是 3，最小值是 $-2$.
