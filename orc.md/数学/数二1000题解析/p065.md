基础篇
第9章 一元函数积分学的计算

$$ \int_1^2 f(x)\mathrm{d}x = \int_0^1 f(t+1)\mathrm{d}t = \int_0^1 [f(t) + t\ln t]\,\mathrm{d}t = \int_0^1 f(t)\mathrm{d}t + \int_0^1 t\ln t\mathrm{d}t, $$

其中
$$ \int_0^1 t\ln t\mathrm{d}t \xlongequal{t=\mathrm{e}^u} \int_{-\infty}^0 u\mathrm{e}^{2u}\mathrm{d}u = \frac{1}{2}\int_{-\infty}^0 u\mathrm{d}(\mathrm{e}^{2u}) $$
$$ = \frac{1}{2}u\mathrm{e}^{2u}\bigg|_{-\infty}^0 - \frac{1}{2}\int_{-\infty}^0 \mathrm{e}^{2u}\mathrm{d}u = -\frac{1}{4}\mathrm{e}^{2u}\bigg|_{-\infty}^0 = -\frac{1}{4}, $$

故 $\int_1^2 f(x)\mathrm{d}x = -\frac{1}{4}$.

**10.【答案】** $\frac{\pi}{12}$

**【解析】** 由 $y^2 = \frac{x^2}{1+x^2}$，得 $x = \frac{y}{\sqrt{1-y^2}}$，故
$$ \int_{\frac{1}{2}}^{\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}} xy\mathrm{d}y = \int_{\frac{1}{2}}^{\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}} \frac{y^2}{\sqrt{1-y^2}}\,\mathrm{d}y \xlongequal{y=\sin t} \int_{\frac{\pi}{6}}^{\frac{\pi}{3}} \frac{\sin^2 t}{\cos t}\cos t\mathrm{d}t = \int_{\frac{\pi}{6}}^{\frac{\pi}{3}} \sin^2 t\mathrm{d}t = \frac{\pi}{12}. $$

**11.【答案】** (A)

**【解析】** 由于 $f(x) = (\mathrm{e}^{-x})' = -\mathrm{e}^{-x}$，因此 $f(\ln x) = -\mathrm{e}^{-\ln x} = -\frac{1}{x}$，从而
$$ \int_1^{\sqrt{2}} \frac{1}{x^2}f(\ln x)\mathrm{d}x = -\int_1^{\sqrt{2}} \frac{1}{x^3}\mathrm{d}x = \frac{1}{2}\frac{1}{x^2}\bigg|_1^{\sqrt{2}} = \frac{1}{4} - \frac{1}{2} = -\frac{1}{4}. $$

**12.【答案】** (A)

**【解析】** 在 $g(x) = \int_0^{2x} f\left(x+\frac{t}{2}\right)\mathrm{d}t$ 中，令 $x+\frac{t}{2}=u$，则
$$ g(x) = \int_0^{2x} f\left(x+\frac{t}{2}\right)\mathrm{d}t = 2\int_x^{2x} f(u)\mathrm{d}u, $$
$$ \lim_{x\to 0^+} \frac{g(x)}{\sqrt{x}} = \lim_{x\to 0^+} \frac{2\int_x^{2x} f(u)\mathrm{d}u}{\sqrt{x}} \xlongequal{\text{洛必达法则}} 2\lim_{x\to 0^+} \frac{2f(2x)-f(x)}{\frac{1}{2\sqrt{x}}} $$
$$ = 4\lim_{x\to 0^+} \sqrt{x}[2f(2x)-f(x)] = 0, $$
这表明当 $x\to 0^+$ 时，$g(x)$ 是 $\sqrt{x}$ 的高阶无穷小.

**13.【答案】** (D)

**【解析】** $\lim\limits_{x\to 0} \varphi(x) = \lim\limits_{x\to 0} \frac{\int_0^x tf(t)\mathrm{d}t}{x^2} \xlongequal{\text{洛必达法则}} \lim\limits_{x\to 0} \frac{xf(x)}{2x} = \frac{1}{2}f(0) = 0 = \varphi(0)$，因此 $\varphi(x)$ 在 $x=0$ 处连续.
