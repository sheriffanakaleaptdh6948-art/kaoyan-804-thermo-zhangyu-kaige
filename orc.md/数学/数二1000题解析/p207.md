第6章 一元函数微分学的应用（二）——中值定理、微分等式与微分不等式

**【解析】** $f(x)$在$[0,1]$上二阶可导，则由带拉格朗日余项的泰勒公式，有
$$f(x)=f\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)+f^{\prime}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)\left(x-\frac{1}{2}\right)+\frac{1}{2} f^{\prime \prime}(\xi)\left(x-\frac{1}{2}\right)^{2},$$
其中$\xi$介于$x$与$\frac{1}{2}$之间.在$[0,1]$上取积分，得
$$
\begin{aligned}
\int_{0}^{1} f(x) \mathrm{d} x &=\int_{0}^{1} f\left(\frac{1}{2}\right) \mathrm{d} x+\int_{0}^{1} f^{\prime}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)\left(x-\frac{1}{2}\right) \mathrm{d} x+\frac{1}{2} \int_{0}^{1} f^{\prime \prime}(\xi)\left(x-\frac{1}{2}\right)^{2} \mathrm{~d} x \\
&=f\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)+f^{\prime}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right) \cdot \frac{1}{2}\left(x-\frac{1}{2}\right)^{2}\bigg|_{0}^{1}+\frac{1}{2} \int_{0}^{1} f^{\prime \prime}(\xi)\left(x-\frac{1}{2}\right)^{2} \mathrm{~d} x=0,
\end{aligned}
$$
于是
$$f\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)=-\frac{1}{2} \int_{0}^{1} f^{\prime \prime}(\xi)\left(x-\frac{1}{2}\right)^{2} \mathrm{~d} x .$$
故当$f^{\prime \prime}(x)>0$时，有$f^{\prime \prime}(\xi)>0$，则$f\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)<0$.因此选(D).

**14.【解析】** (1)记$x_{0}$为$[0,1]$上$f(x)$的最大值点，又$f(0)=f(1)=0$，则$M=f\left(x_{0}\right)>0, x_{0} \in(0,1)$，且由费马定理，有$f^{\prime}\left(x_{0}\right)=0$.

在$\left[0, x_{0}\right]$上对$f(x)$应用拉格朗日中值定理，得$f^{\prime}(\xi)=\frac{f\left(x_{0}\right)-f(0)}{x_{0}-0}=\frac{M}{x_{0}}, \xi \in\left(0, x_{0}\right)$.

对任意正整数$n, 0<\frac{M}{n}<\frac{M}{x_{0}}$，即$f^{\prime}\left(x_{0}\right)<\frac{M}{n}<f^{\prime}(\xi)$.又由于$f^{\prime}(x)$在$\left[\xi, x_{0}\right]$上连续，故由介值定理，知存在$x_{n} \in\left(\xi, x_{0}\right) \subset(0,1)$，使得$f^{\prime}\left(x_{n}\right)=\frac{M}{n}$.又$f^{\prime \prime}(x)<0, f^{\prime}(x)$严格单调减少，故$x_{n}$唯一.

(2)由于$f^{\prime}\left(x_{n}\right)=\frac{M}{n}>\frac{M}{n+1}=f^{\prime}\left(x_{n+1}\right)$，且$f^{\prime}(x)$严格单调减少，故$x_{n}<x_{n+1}$，即$\left\{x_{n}\right\}$严格单调增加，且有界，故$\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} x_{n}$存在.又
$$\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} f^{\prime}\left(x_{n}\right)=\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} \frac{M}{n}=0 \text {, 即 } f^{\prime}\left(\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} x_{n}\right)=0,$$
由于$f^{\prime}\left(x_{0}\right)=0$，且导数零点唯一，故$\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} x_{n}=x_{0}$，所以$\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} f\left(x_{n}\right)=f\left(x_{0}\right)=M$.
