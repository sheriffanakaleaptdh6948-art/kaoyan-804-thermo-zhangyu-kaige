方法二 将 $f(x)=\int_{1}^{x} \sqrt{1+t^{3}} \mathrm{~d} t$ 代入所求表达式，得 $\int_{0}^{1} x f(x) \mathrm{d} x=-\int_{0}^{1} x \mathrm{~d} x \int_{x}^{1} \sqrt{1+t^{3}} \mathrm{~d} t$，交换积分次序并计算，得
$$
\begin{aligned}
\int_{0}^{1} x f(x) \mathrm{d} x & =-\int_{0}^{1} \sqrt{1+t^{3}} \mathrm{~d} t \int_{0}^{t} x \mathrm{~d} x \\
& =-\frac{1}{2} \int_{0}^{1} t^{2} \sqrt{1+t^{3}} \mathrm{~d} t=-\frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{2}{9}\left(1+t^{3}\right)^{\frac{3}{2}} \bigg|_{0}^{1} \\
& =\frac{1}{9}(1-2 \sqrt{2}) .
\end{aligned}
$$

**19.【答案】e**

**【解析】** 令 $F(x)=\int_{0}^{x} f(t) \mathrm{d} t=x \mathrm{e}^{x}$，则
$$
\int_{1}^{\mathrm{e}} \frac{f(\ln x)}{x} \mathrm{~d} x=\int_{1}^{\mathrm{e}} f(\ln x) \mathrm{d}(\ln x)=F(\ln x) \bigg|_{1}^{\mathrm{e}}=F(1)-F(0)=\mathrm{e} .
$$

**20.【答案】$\mathrm{e}^{-3}$**

**【解析】** 当 $n \geqslant 2$ 时，因为
$$
\begin{aligned}
a_{n} & =\int_{0}^{1} x^{n} \sqrt{1-x^{2}} \mathrm{~d} x \\
& =-\frac{1}{3} x^{n-1}\left(1-x^{2}\right)^{\frac{3}{2}} \bigg|_{0}^{1}+\frac{n-1}{3} \int_{0}^{1} x^{n-2}\left(1-x^{2}\right)^{\frac{3}{2}} \mathrm{~d} x \\
& =\frac{n-1}{3} \int_{0}^{1} x^{n-2} \sqrt{1-x^{2}} \mathrm{~d} x-\frac{n-1}{3} \int_{0}^{1} x^{n} \sqrt{1-x^{2}} \mathrm{~d} x \\
& =\frac{n-1}{3} a_{n-2}-\frac{n-1}{3} a_{n},
\end{aligned}
$$
所以 $a_{n}=\frac{n-1}{n+2} a_{n-2}(n=2,3, \cdots)$，故
$$
\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty}\left(\frac{a_{n}}{a_{n-2}}\right)^{n}=\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty}\left(\frac{n-1}{n+2}\right)^{n}=\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty}\left(1+\frac{-3}{n+2}\right)^{n}=\mathrm{e}^{-3} .
$$

**21.【答案】$\frac{\pi}{2}-\arcsin \frac{\sqrt{5}}{3}+\ln 3$**

**【解析】**
$$
\begin{aligned}
\int_{\sqrt{5}}^{5} \frac{1}{\sqrt{\left|x^{2}-9\right|}} \mathrm{d} x & =\int_{\sqrt{5}}^{3} \frac{1}{\sqrt{9-x^{2}}} \mathrm{~d} x+\int_{3}^{5} \frac{1}{\sqrt{x^{2}-9}} \mathrm{~d} x \\
& =\arcsin \frac{x}{3} \bigg|_{\sqrt{5}}^{3}+\ln \left|x+\sqrt{x^{2}-9}\right| \bigg|_{3}^{5}
\end{aligned}
$$
