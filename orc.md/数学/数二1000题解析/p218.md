对于(C)选项，
$$ \int_1^{+\infty} \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x^2\sqrt{x^2-1}} = \int_1^{+\infty} \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x^3\sqrt{1-\frac{1}{x^2}}} = \frac{1}{2}\int_1^{+\infty} \frac{1}{\sqrt{1-\frac{1}{x^2}}} \mathrm{d}\left(1-\frac{1}{x^2}\right) = \sqrt{1-\frac{1}{x^2}}\bigg|_1^{+\infty} = 1, $$
