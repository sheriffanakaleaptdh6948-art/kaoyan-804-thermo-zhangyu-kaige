**7.【解析】方法一** 在极坐标系中，区域 $D$ 可表示为 $\left\{(r,\theta)\left|0\leqslant r\leqslant 1,0\leqslant \theta\leqslant \frac{\pi}{4}\right.\right\}$，所以
$$
\begin{aligned}
\iint_{D}\mathrm{e}^{(x+y)^{2}}(x^{2}-y^{2})\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y &=\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\mathrm{d}\theta\int_{0}^{1}\mathrm{e}^{r^{2}(\sin\theta+\cos\theta)^{2}}r^{3}(\cos^{2}\theta-\sin^{2}\theta)\mathrm{d}r \\
&=\int_{0}^{1}\mathrm{d}r\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\mathrm{e}^{r^{2}(\sin\theta+\cos\theta)^{2}}r^{3}(\cos^{2}\theta-\sin^{2}\theta)\mathrm{d}\theta=\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{1}r\mathrm{d}r\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\mathrm{e}^{r^{2}(\sin\theta+\cos\theta)^{2}}\mathrm{d}\left[r^{2}(\sin\theta+\cos\theta)^{2}\right] \\
&=\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{1}r\left[\mathrm{e}^{r^{2}(\sin\theta+\cos\theta)^{2}}\right]_{\theta=0}^{\theta=\frac{\pi}{4}}\mathrm{d}r=\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{1}r(\mathrm{e}^{2r^{2}}-\mathrm{e}^{r^{2}})\mathrm{d}r=\frac{(\mathrm{e}-1)^{2}}{8}.
\end{aligned}
$$

**方法二** 令 $\begin{cases}u=x-y,\\ v=x+y,\end{cases} D_{uv}=\left\{(u,v)\left|u^{2}+v^{2}\leqslant 2,v\geqslant u\geqslant 0\right.\right\}$.
因为 $\begin{cases}x=\frac{u+v}{2},\\ y=\frac{v-u}{2},\end{cases} (u,v)\in D_{uv}, \left\|\begin{matrix}\frac{\partial x}{\partial u}&\frac{\partial x}{\partial v}\\ \frac{\partial y}{\partial u}&\frac{\partial y}{\partial v}\end{matrix}\right\|=\frac{1}{2}$，所以
$$
\begin{aligned}
\iint_{D}\mathrm{e}^{(x+y)^{2}}(x^{2}-y^{2})\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y &=\iint_{D_{uv}}\frac{1}{2}\mathrm{e}^{v^{2}}uv\mathrm{d}u\mathrm{d}v=\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{1}u\mathrm{d}u\int_{u}^{\sqrt{2-u^{2}}}\mathrm{e}^{v^{2}}v\mathrm{d}v \\
&=\frac{1}{4}\int_{0}^{1}(\mathrm{e}^{2-u^{2}}-\mathrm{e}^{u^{2}})u\mathrm{d}u=\frac{(\mathrm{e}-1)^{2}}{8}.
\end{aligned}
$$
