$$ \lim_{x \to 1} f(x) = \lim_{x \to 1} (x^2 + \mathrm{e}^x \cdot a) = 1 + a\mathrm{e} = a \text{，} $$
从而 $a = \frac{1}{1-\mathrm{e}}$，故 $f(x) = x^2 + \frac{1}{1-\mathrm{e}} \mathrm{e}^x$。

**19.【答案】** $\frac{3}{2}$

**【解析】** 利用等价无穷小代换，$\mathrm{e}^x - 1 \sim x (x \to 0)$，则
$$ \lim_{x \to 0} \left( \frac{\mathrm{e}^x + x\mathrm{e}^x}{\mathrm{e}^x - 1} - \frac{1}{x} \right) = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{x\mathrm{e}^x(1+x) + 1 - \mathrm{e}^x}{x(\mathrm{e}^x - 1)} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{x\mathrm{e}^x(1+x) + 1 - \mathrm{e}^x}{x^2} $$
$$ = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{3x\mathrm{e}^x + x^2\mathrm{e}^x}{2x} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{3\mathrm{e}^x + x\mathrm{e}^x}{2} = \frac{3}{2} \text{.} $$

**【注】** 下面的做法是错误的：
$$ \lim_{x \to 0} \left( \frac{\mathrm{e}^x + x\mathrm{e}^x}{\mathrm{e}^x - 1} - \frac{1}{x} \right) = \lim_{x \to 0} \left( \frac{\mathrm{e}^x + x\mathrm{e}^x}{x} - \frac{1}{x} \right) $$
$$ = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\mathrm{e}^x + x\mathrm{e}^x - 1}{x} = \lim_{x \to 0} (2\mathrm{e}^x + x\mathrm{e}^x) = 2 \text{.} $$

**20.【答案】** $-\frac{1}{2} + \frac{3}{2} \ln 2 - \ln^2 2$

**【解析】** 由连续的定义，
$$ f(1) = \lim_{x \to 1^+} \frac{1 - x \cdot 2^{1-x}}{(2-x)(1-x)} \overset{“0”}{\underset{0}{=}} \lim_{x \to 1^+} \frac{-2^{1-x} + x \cdot 2^{1-x} \ln 2}{-(1-x) - (2-x)} $$
$$ = \frac{-1 + \ln 2}{-1} = 1 - \ln 2 \text{，} $$
$$ f(2) = \lim_{x \to 2^-} \frac{1 - x \cdot 2^{1-x}}{(2-x)(1-x)} \overset{“0”}{\underset{0}{=}} \lim_{x \to 2^-} \frac{-2^{1-x} + x \cdot 2^{1-x} \ln 2}{-(1-x) - (2-x)} $$
$$ = \frac{-\frac{1}{2} + 2 \cdot \frac{1}{2} \cdot \ln 2}{1} = -\frac{1}{2} + \ln 2 \text{.} $$
故 $f(1)f(2) = (1 - \ln 2)\left( -\frac{1}{2} + \ln 2 \right) = -\frac{1}{2} + \frac{3}{2} \ln 2 - \ln^2 2$。
