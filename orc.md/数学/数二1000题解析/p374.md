**18.【解析】** 当 $x \to 0$ 时， $x\left[(1+x)^{\frac{1}{x}} - \mathrm{e}\right] \sim x\mathrm{e}\left(-\frac{x}{2}\right) = -\frac{\mathrm{e}}{2}x^2$， $\quad \cdots\cdots 2\text{分}$

故
$$ \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{xf(x) - \mathrm{e}^{2\ln(1+x)} + 1}{x^2} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{xf(x) - (1+x)^2 + 1}{x^2} = 1, \quad \cdots\cdots 4\text{分} $$
可得
$$ xf(x) - (1+x)^2 + 1 = x^2 + o(x^2) (x \to 0\text{时}), $$
进而可得
$$ f(x) = \frac{(1+x)^2 - 1}{x} + x + o(x) = 2x + 2 + o(x) (x \to 0\text{时}). \quad \cdots\cdots 7\text{分} $$
由 $f(x)$ 连续，可得
$$ f(0) = \lim_{x \to 0} f(x) = 2, \quad \cdots\cdots 8\text{分} $$
故
$$ f'(0) = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(x) - f(0)}{x - 0} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{2x + 2 + o(x) - 2}{x} \quad \cdots\cdots 10\text{分} $$
$$ = 2. \quad \cdots\cdots 12\text{分} $$
