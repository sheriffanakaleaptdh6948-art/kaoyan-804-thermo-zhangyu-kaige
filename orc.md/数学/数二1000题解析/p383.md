测试卷三

假设 $r(\boldsymbol{P}) \neq r$，则 $\boldsymbol{P}$ 的 $r$ 个行向量 $\boldsymbol{p}_1, \boldsymbol{p}_2, \cdots, \boldsymbol{p}_r$ 线性相关，于是存在不全为零的数 $k_1, k_2, \cdots, k_r$，使得
$$ k_1 \boldsymbol{p}_1 + k_2 \boldsymbol{p}_2 + \cdots + k_r \boldsymbol{p}_r = \mathbf{0} \text{，} $$
即
$$ [k_1, k_2, \cdots, k_r] \boldsymbol{P} = \mathbf{0} \text{.} $$
分别在等式两端右边乘 $\begin{bmatrix} \boldsymbol{\alpha}_1 \\ \boldsymbol{\alpha}_2 \\ \vdots \\ \boldsymbol{\alpha}_s \end{bmatrix}$，有
$$ [k_1, k_2, \cdots, k_r] \boldsymbol{P} \begin{bmatrix} \boldsymbol{\alpha}_1 \\ \boldsymbol{\alpha}_2 \\ \vdots \\ \boldsymbol{\alpha}_s \end{bmatrix} = \mathbf{0} \text{，} $$
于是
$$ [k_1, k_2, \cdots, k_r] \begin{bmatrix} \boldsymbol{\beta}_1 \\ \boldsymbol{\beta}_2 \\ \vdots \\ \boldsymbol{\beta}_r \end{bmatrix} = \mathbf{0} \text{，} $$
即
$$ k_1 \boldsymbol{\beta}_1 + k_2 \boldsymbol{\beta}_2 + \cdots + k_r \boldsymbol{\beta}_r = \mathbf{0} \text{，} $$
其中 $k_1, k_2, \cdots, k_r$ 不全为零，于是 $\boldsymbol{\beta}_1, \boldsymbol{\beta}_2, \cdots, \boldsymbol{\beta}_r$ 线性相关，这与 $\boldsymbol{\beta}_1, \boldsymbol{\beta}_2, \cdots, \boldsymbol{\beta}_r$ 线性无关矛盾，假设错误，故 $r(\boldsymbol{P}) = r$。充分性得证。

**二、填空题**

**11.【答案】** $\mathrm{e}^{-1}$

**【解析】** 先求 $y = \tan^n x (n \geqslant 1)$ 在点 $\left( \frac{\pi}{4}, 1 \right)$ 处的切线方程。$y'\left( \frac{\pi}{4} \right) = n \tan^{n-1} x \cdot \sec^2 x \Big|_{x=\frac{\pi}{4}} = 2n$，故切线方程为 $y - 1 = 2n \left( x - \frac{\pi}{4} \right)$，切线在 $x$ 轴上的截距为 $x_n = \frac{\pi}{4} - \frac{1}{2n}$，故
$$ \lim_{n \to \infty} y(x_n) = \lim_{n \to \infty} \tan^n \left( \frac{\pi}{4} - \frac{1}{2n} \right) = \lim_{n \to \infty} \left( \frac{1 - \tan \frac{1}{2n}}{1 + \tan \frac{1}{2n}} \right)^n $$
$$ = \lim_{n \to \infty} \left[ \left( 1 - \frac{2 \tan \frac{1}{2n}}{1 + \tan \frac{1}{2n}} \right)^{\frac{1 + \tan \frac{1}{2n}}{-2 \tan \frac{1}{2n}}} \right]^{\frac{-2n \tan \frac{1}{2n}}{1 + \tan \frac{1}{2n}}} = \mathrm{e}^{-1} \text{.} $$
