**14.【解析】**(1)二次型 $f\left(x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3}\right)$ 与 $g\left(y_{1}, y_{2}, y_{3}\right)$ 的矩阵分别为

$$
\boldsymbol{A}=\left[\begin{array}{ccc} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & -\frac{1}{2} \\ 0 & -\frac{1}{2} & 0 \end{array}\right], \boldsymbol{B}=\left[\begin{array}{ccc} 0 & \frac{1}{2} & 0 \\ \frac{1}{2} & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & a \end{array}\right],
$$

且 $\boldsymbol{Q}^{\mathrm{T}} \boldsymbol{A} \boldsymbol{Q}=\boldsymbol{B}$. 因为 $\boldsymbol{Q}$ 为正交矩阵，所以矩阵 $\boldsymbol{A}$ 与 $\boldsymbol{B}$ 相似，故 $\operatorname{tr}(\boldsymbol{A})=\operatorname{tr}(\boldsymbol{B})$，从而 $a=1$.

(2)由于 $|\lambda \boldsymbol{E}-\boldsymbol{A}|=(\lambda-1)\left(\lambda-\frac{1}{2}\right)\left(\lambda+\frac{1}{2}\right)$，因此矩阵 $\boldsymbol{A}$ 与 $\boldsymbol{B}$ 的特征值均为 $1, \frac{1}{2},-\frac{1}{2}$，易求得矩阵 $\boldsymbol{A}$ 对应于特征值 $1, \frac{1}{2},-\frac{1}{2}$ 的特征向量依次为 $\left[\begin{array}{l}1 \\ 0 \\ 0\end{array}\right],\left[\begin{array}{c}0 \\ -1 \\ 1\end{array}\right],\left[\begin{array}{l}0 \\ 1 \\ 1\end{array}\right]$，又因为实对称矩阵属于不同特征值的特征向量相互正交，故单位化即可，对应依次为 $\left[\begin{array}{l}1 \\ 0 \\ 0\end{array}\right],\left[\begin{array}{c}0 \\ -\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \\ \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\end{array}\right],\left[\begin{array}{c}0 \\ \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \\ \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\end{array}\right]$.

令 $\boldsymbol{Q}_{1}=\left[\begin{array}{ccc}1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & -\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \\ 0 & \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\end{array}\right]$，则 $\boldsymbol{Q}_{1}$ 为正交矩阵，且 $\boldsymbol{Q}_{1}^{\mathrm{T}} \boldsymbol{A} \boldsymbol{Q}_{1}=\left[\begin{array}{ccc}1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & \frac{1}{2} & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & -\frac{1}{2}\end{array}\right]$.
