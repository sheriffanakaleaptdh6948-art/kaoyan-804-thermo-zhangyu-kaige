$$
\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(x)}{\sin^3 x} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(x)}{x^3} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\mathrm{e}^{x^2[o(1)-1]} - \cos x}{x^2} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\mathrm{e}^{x^2[o(1)-1]} - 1}{x^2} + \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{1 - \cos x}{x^2}
$$
$$
= \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\mathrm{e}^{x^2[o(1)-1]} - 1}{x^2[o(1)-1]} \cdot \lim_{x \to 0} [o(1)-1] + \frac{1}{2} = -1 + \frac{1}{2} = -\frac{1}{2}.
$$

**【注】** ①由于此题未注明 $f(x)$ 可导，因此不能直接利用洛必达法则进行计算，而是利用等价无穷小计算.

②此题也可由 $\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\ln\left[\cos x + \frac{f(x)}{x}\right]}{x^2} = -1$，利用 $\ln(1+x) \sim x (x \to 0)$，得
$$
\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\cos x + \frac{f(x)}{x} - 1}{x^2} = -1,
$$
从而得到 $\lim_{x \to 0} \left[\frac{f(x)}{x^3} - \frac{1 - \cos x}{x^2}\right] = -1$. 求出 $\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(x)}{x^3} = -\frac{1}{2}$，也就得到所求结果了.

**12.【答案】** 3

**【解析】** 由积分中值定理，知
$$
\int_0^{2x} \cos t^2 \mathrm{d}t = 2x \cos \xi^2 \quad (\xi \text{介于} 0, 2x \text{之间；当} x \to 0 \text{时，有} \xi \to 0).
$$
故
$$
\text{原式} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{xf(x) + g(x) \cdot 2x \cos \xi^2}{xg(x)} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(x) + g(x) \cdot 2\cos \xi^2}{g(x)} = 3.
$$

**【注】** 这里用到 $g(x)$ 在点 $x=0$ 处的连续性，$\lim_{x \to 0} g(x) = \frac{1}{2} = g(0)$.

**13.【答案】** (A)

**【解析】**
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(x) &= \lim_{n \to \infty} n^x \left[\mathrm{e}^{n \ln\left(1+\frac{1}{n}\right)} - \mathrm{e}\right] = \lim_{n \to \infty} n^x \left[\mathrm{e}^{n \ln\left(1+\frac{1}{n}\right) - 1} - 1\right] \cdot \mathrm{e} \\
&= \lim_{n \to \infty} n^x \left\{\mathrm{e}^{n\left[\frac{1}{n} - \frac{1}{2n^2} + o\left(\frac{1}{n^2}\right)\right] - 1} - 1\right\} \cdot \mathrm{e} = \lim_{n \to \infty} n^x \left[\mathrm{e}^{-\frac{1}{2n} + o\left(\frac{1}{n}\right)} - 1\right] \cdot \mathrm{e} \\
&= \lim_{n \to \infty} n^x \left[-\frac{1}{2n} + o\left(\frac{1}{n}\right)\right] \cdot \mathrm{e} = \lim_{n \to \infty} \left[-\frac{\mathrm{e}}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{n^{1-x}} + \mathrm{e} \cdot n^x \cdot o\left(\frac{1}{n}\right)\right] \\
&= \begin{cases} 0, & x < 1, \\ -\frac{\mathrm{e}}{2}, & x = 1, \\ -\infty, & x > 1, \end{cases}
\end{aligned}
$$
