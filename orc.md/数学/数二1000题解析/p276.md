$$ =-\int_{0}^{1} y \mathrm{e}^{-y^{2}} \mathrm{~d} y-\int_{0}^{1} y \mathrm{e}^{y} \sin y \mathrm{~d} y $$
$$ =\frac{1}{2} \mathrm{e}^{-y^{2}} \bigg|_{0}^{1}-\int_{0}^{1} y \mathrm{e}^{y} \sin y \mathrm{~d} y $$
$$ =\frac{1}{2} \mathrm{e}^{-1}-\frac{1}{2}-\int_{0}^{1} y \mathrm{e}^{y} \sin y \mathrm{~d} y . $$

其中，
$$ \begin{aligned} \int_{0}^{1} y \mathrm{e}^{y} \sin y \mathrm{~d} y &=\int_{0}^{1} y \sin y \mathrm{~d}\left(\mathrm{e}^{y}\right) \\ &=y \sin y \cdot \mathrm{e}^{y} \bigg|_{0}^{1}-\int_{0}^{1} \mathrm{e}^{y}(\sin y+y \cos y) \mathrm{d} y \\ &=\mathrm{e} \sin 1-\int_{0}^{1} \mathrm{e}^{y} \sin y \mathrm{~d} y-\int_{0}^{1} y \cos y \mathrm{~d}\left(\mathrm{e}^{y}\right) \\ &=\mathrm{e} \sin 1-\int_{0}^{1} \mathrm{e}^{y} \sin y \mathrm{~d} y-y \cos y \cdot \mathrm{e}^{y} \bigg|_{0}^{1}+\int_{0}^{1} \mathrm{e}^{y}(\cos y-y \sin y) \mathrm{d} y \\ &=\mathrm{e}(\sin 1-\cos 1)+\mathrm{e}^{y} \cos y \bigg|_{0}^{1}-\int_{0}^{1} y \mathrm{e}^{y} \sin y \mathrm{~d} y \\ &=\mathrm{e} \sin 1-1-\int_{0}^{1} y \mathrm{e}^{y} \sin y \mathrm{~d} y, \end{aligned} $$

则
$$ \int_{0}^{1} y \mathrm{e}^{y} \sin y \mathrm{~d} y=\frac{1}{2}(\mathrm{e} \sin 1-1) . $$

故
$$ \begin{aligned} \text { 原式 } &=\frac{1}{2} \mathrm{e}^{-1}-\frac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{2}(\mathrm{e} \sin 1-1) \\ &=\frac{\mathrm{e}^{-1}-\mathrm{e} \sin 1}{2} . \end{aligned} $$

**33.** 【解析】转化为直角坐标系再计算.
由 $r \leqslant 1$，知 $x^{2}+y^{2} \leqslant 1$；由 $r \leqslant 2 \cos \theta$，知 $(x-1)^{2}+y^{2} \leqslant 1$；由 $\sin \theta \geqslant 0$，知 $y \geqslant 0$. 积分区域 $D_{x y}$ 如图所示.

$$ \begin{aligned} \text { 原式 } &=\iint_{D} r \cos \theta(1+r \sin \theta) r \mathrm{~d} r \mathrm{~d} \theta \\ &=\iint_{D_{x y}} x(1+y) \mathrm{d} x \mathrm{~d} y \\ &=\iint_{D_{x y}} x \mathrm{~d} x \mathrm{~d} y+\iint_{D_{x y}} x y \mathrm{~d} x \mathrm{~d} y \end{aligned} $$
