**12. 【答案】** $\frac{11}{9}$

【解析】方程 $3y^3 = x^5 + 2x^3$ 两边同时对 $x$ 求导得 $9y^2 \cdot \frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} = 5x^4 + 6x^2$.

当 $y=1$ 时，$x=1$，代入上式得 $\left. \frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} \right|_{x=1} = \frac{11}{9}$，即曲线在 $y=1$ 对应点处的切线斜率为 $\frac{11}{9}$.

**13. 【答案】** $y=x-1$

【解析】
$$ \lim_{x \to \infty} \frac{y}{x} = \lim_{x \to \infty} \mathrm{e}^{-\frac{1}{x+1}} = 1 $$
$$ \lim_{x \to \infty} (y-x) = \lim_{x \to \infty} \left( x\mathrm{e}^{-\frac{1}{x+1}} - x \right) = \lim_{x \to \infty} x \left( \mathrm{e}^{-\frac{1}{x+1}} - 1 \right) $$
$$ = \lim_{x \to \infty} \frac{-x}{x+1} = -1 $$
故斜渐近线方程为
$$ y=x-1 $$

**14. 【答案】** $-(x+y) + \frac{f(y)-f(x)}{2}$

【解析】令 $x+y-t=u$，得 $x^2+y^2+z^2 = \int_x^y f(u)\mathrm{d}u$. 等式两端分别对 $x, y$ 求偏导，得
$$ 2x + 2z\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} = -f(x), \quad 2y + 2z\frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = f(y) $$
所以
$$ z\left( \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} \right) = -(x+y) + \frac{f(y)-f(x)}{2} $$

**15. 【答案】** $\ln\left[ (x-1)^2 + 4y^2 \right] + \arctan \frac{2y}{x-1} = C$，$C$ 为任意常数

【解析】将原方程写成
$$ \frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} = \frac{-(x-1)+y}{(x-1)+4y} $$
令 $X=x-1$，$Y=y$，则 $\mathrm{d}x=\mathrm{d}X$，$\mathrm{d}y=\mathrm{d}Y$，原方程化为 $\frac{\mathrm{d}Y}{\mathrm{d}X} = \frac{-X+Y}{X+4Y}$，即
$$ \frac{\mathrm{d}Y}{\mathrm{d}X} = \frac{\frac{Y}{X}-1}{\frac{4Y}{X}+1} $$
