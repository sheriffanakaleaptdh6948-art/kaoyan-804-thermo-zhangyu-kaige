**24.**【解析】令 $\begin{cases} x=r \cos \theta, \\ y=r \sin \theta, \end{cases}$ 其中 $\begin{cases} 0 \leqslant \theta \leqslant \frac{\pi}{2} \\ 0 \leqslant r \leqslant 1, \end{cases}$ 则有

$$
\iint_{D} \sqrt{\frac{1-x^{2}-y^{2}}{1+x^{2}+y^{2}}} \mathrm{d} x \mathrm{d} y=\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \mathrm{d} \theta \int_{0}^{1} \sqrt{\frac{1-r^{2}}{1+r^{2}}} r \mathrm{d} r
$$

$$
=\frac{\pi}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{2} \int_{0}^{1} \sqrt{\frac{1-r^{2}}{1+r^{2}}} \mathrm{d}\left(r^{2}\right) \stackrel{\text { 令 } r^{2}=t}{=} \frac{\pi}{4} \int_{0}^{1} \sqrt{\frac{1-t}{1+t}} \mathrm{d} t
$$

$$
=\frac{\pi}{4} \int_{0}^{1} \frac{1-t}{\sqrt{1-t^{2}}} \mathrm{d} t=\frac{\pi}{4}\left(\int_{0}^{1} \frac{\mathrm{d} t}{\sqrt{1-t^{2}}}-\int_{0}^{1} \frac{t}{\sqrt{1-t^{2}}} \mathrm{d} t\right)
$$

$$
=\frac{\pi}{4}\left(\arcsin t+\sqrt{1-t^{2}}\right)\bigg|_{0}^{1}=\frac{\pi}{8}(\pi-2) .
