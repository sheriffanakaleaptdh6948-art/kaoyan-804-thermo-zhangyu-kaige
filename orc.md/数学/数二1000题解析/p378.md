# 测试卷三

**一、选择题**

**1.【答案】(B)**

**【解析】**
$$f(x)=\int_{0}^{x}(x-2 t) \mathrm{e}^{-t^{2}} \mathrm{~d} t=x \int_{0}^{x} \mathrm{e}^{-t^{2}} \mathrm{~d} t-\int_{0}^{x} 2 t \mathrm{e}^{-t^{2}} \mathrm{~d} t,$$
求导得
$$f^{\prime}(x)=\int_{0}^{x} \mathrm{e}^{-t^{2}} \mathrm{~d} t+x \mathrm{e}^{-x^{2}}-2 x \mathrm{e}^{-x^{2}}=\int_{0}^{x} \mathrm{e}^{-t^{2}} \mathrm{~d} t-x \mathrm{e}^{-x^{2}},$$
易知 $f^{\prime}(0)=0$.

又 $f^{\prime \prime}(x)=\mathrm{e}^{-x^{2}}-\left(\mathrm{e}^{-x^{2}}-2 x^{2} \mathrm{e}^{-x^{2}}\right)=2 x^{2} \mathrm{e}^{-x^{2}} \geqslant 0$, 故 $f^{\prime}(x)$ 单调增加, 进而有 $f^{\prime}(x) \geqslant f^{\prime}(0)=0$, 则 $f(x)$ 单调增加.

$$g(x)=\int_{0}^{x}(x-2 t) \sin t^{2} \mathrm{~d} t=x \int_{0}^{x} \sin t^{2} \mathrm{~d} t-\int_{0}^{x} 2 t \sin t^{2} \mathrm{~d} t,$$
由于 $\int_{0}^{x} \sin t^{2} \mathrm{~d} t$ 为奇函数, $\int_{0}^{x} 2 t \sin t^{2} \mathrm{~d} t$ 为偶函数, 故 $g(x)$ 为偶函数.

**【注】** 对 $g(x)$ 亦可求导, 得
$$g^{\prime}(x)=x \sin x^{2}+\int_{0}^{x} \sin t^{2} \mathrm{~d} t-2 x \sin x^{2}=\int_{0}^{x} \sin t^{2} \mathrm{~d} t-x \sin x^{2},$$
$$g^{\prime \prime}(x)=\sin x^{2}-\left(\sin x^{2}+2 x^{2} \cos x^{2}\right)=-2 x^{2} \cos x^{2}.$$
因为 $g^{\prime \prime}(x)$ 为偶函数, 且 $g^{\prime}(0)=0$, 故 $g^{\prime}(x)$ 为奇函数, 进而可知 $g(x)$ 为偶函数.
