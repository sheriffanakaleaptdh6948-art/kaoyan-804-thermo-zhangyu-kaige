**21.【解析】方法一** 用极坐标系.

将 $(x-1)^{2}+y^{2} \leqslant 1$, 即 $x^{2}+y^{2} \leqslant 2 x$, 转化成 $r^{2} \leqslant 2 r \cos \theta$, 即 $r \leqslant 2 \cos \theta$. 由此, $\cos \theta \geqslant 0$, 从而 $-\frac{\pi}{2} \leqslant \theta \leqslant \frac{\pi}{2}$, 所求积分

$$
\begin{aligned}
I &=\iint_{D}\left(x^{2}-3 y^{2}\right) \mathrm{d} x \mathrm{~d} y=\int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \mathrm{~d} \theta \int_{0}^{2 \cos \theta}\left(r^{2} \cos ^{2} \theta-3 r^{2} \sin ^{2} \theta\right) r \mathrm{~d} r \\
&=4 \int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\left(\cos ^{2} \theta-3 \sin ^{2} \theta\right) \cos ^{4} \theta \mathrm{d} \theta=4 \int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\left[4 \cos ^{2} \theta-\left(3 \cos ^{2} \theta+3 \sin ^{2} \theta\right)\right] \cos ^{4} \theta \mathrm{d} \theta \\
&=4 \int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\left(4 \cos ^{6} \theta-3 \cos ^{4} \theta\right) \mathrm{d} \theta=8 \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\left(4 \cos ^{6} \theta-3 \cos ^{4} \theta\right) \mathrm{d} \theta \\
&=8\left(4 \times \frac{5}{6} \times \frac{3}{4} \times \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{\pi}{2}-3 \times \frac{3}{4} \times \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{\pi}{2}\right)=\frac{\pi}{2}.
\end{aligned}
$$
