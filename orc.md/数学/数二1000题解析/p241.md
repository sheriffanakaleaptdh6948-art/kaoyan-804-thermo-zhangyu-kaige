第 11 章 一元函数积分学的应用（二）——积分等式与积分不等式

**4.【解析】** $\int_0^1 \left( \int_x^{\sqrt{x}} \frac{\sin t}{t} \mathrm{d}t \right) \mathrm{d}x \xlongequal{\text{分部积分}} \left( x \int_x^{\sqrt{x}} \frac{\sin t}{t} \mathrm{d}t \right) \Bigg|_0^1 - \int_0^1 x \mathrm{d} \left( \int_x^{\sqrt{x}} \frac{\sin t}{t} \mathrm{d}t \right)$
$$ = - \int_0^1 x \left( \frac{\sin \sqrt{x}}{\sqrt{x}} \cdot \frac{1}{2\sqrt{x}} - \frac{\sin x}{x} \right) \mathrm{d}x = \int_0^1 \left( \sin x - \frac{\sin \sqrt{x}}{2} \right) \mathrm{d}x $$
$$ \xlongequal{t=\sqrt{x}} - \int_0^1 t \sin t \mathrm{d}t + \int_0^1 \sin x \mathrm{d}x = 1 - \sin 1 \ . $$

**5.【解析】** 令 $F(x) = f(x) - g(x) (x \in [a, b])$，$G(x) = \int_a^x F(t) \mathrm{d}t (x \in [a, b])$。易见 $G'(x) = F(x)$，且 $G(x) \geqslant 0$。从而
$$ \int_a^b x F(x) \mathrm{d}x = \int_a^b x \mathrm{d}[G(x)] = x G(x) \Bigg|_a^b - \int_a^b G(x) \mathrm{d}x = - \int_a^b G(x) \mathrm{d}x \leqslant 0 \ , $$
移项即得待证不等式.

**6.【解析】** 由柯西积分不等式，得
$$ \left[ \int_a^b \varphi(x) \psi(x) \mathrm{d}x \right]^2 \leqslant \int_a^b \varphi^2(x) \mathrm{d}x \int_a^b \psi^2(x) \mathrm{d}x \ . $$
令 $\varphi(x) = 1$，$\psi(x) = f'(x)$，有
$$ \left[ \int_a^t 1 \cdot f'(x) \mathrm{d}x \right]^2 \leqslant \int_a^t 1^2 \mathrm{d}x \int_a^t [f'(x)]^2 \mathrm{d}x (a \leqslant t \leqslant b) \ , $$
$$ [f(t) - f(a)]^2 \leqslant (t-a) \int_a^t [f'(x)]^2 \mathrm{d}x \ , $$
则
$$ f^2(t) \leqslant (t-a) \int_a^b [f'(x)]^2 \mathrm{d}x \ , $$
可得
$$ \int_a^b f^2(t) \mathrm{d}t \leqslant \int_a^b (t-a) \mathrm{d}t \int_a^b [f'(x)]^2 \mathrm{d}x \ , $$
故
$$ \int_a^b f^2(t) \mathrm{d}t \leqslant \frac{(b-a)^2}{2} \int_a^b [f'(x)]^2 \mathrm{d}x \ , $$
即
$$ \int_a^b f^2(x) \mathrm{d}x \leqslant \frac{(b-a)^2}{2} \int_a^b [f'(x)]^2 \mathrm{d}x \ . $$

【注】柯西积分不等式(也称柯西-施瓦茨不等式):
若函数 $\varphi(x)$ 与 $\psi(x)$ 在 $[a, b]$ 上均连续，则有
$$ \left[ \int_a^b \varphi(x) \psi(x) \mathrm{d}x \right]^2 \leqslant \int_a^b [\varphi(x)]^2 \mathrm{d}x \int_a^b [\psi(x)]^2 \mathrm{d}x \ . $$
