**14.【解析】** 由轮换对称性，得
$$ \iint_{D} \frac{x \cos \sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}}}{x+y} \mathrm{~d} x \mathrm{~d} y=\iint_{D} \frac{y \cos \sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}}}{x+y} \mathrm{~d} x \mathrm{~d} y $$
$$ =\frac{1}{2} \iint_{D} \cos \sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}} \mathrm{~d} x \mathrm{~d} y=\frac{1}{2} \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \mathrm{~d} \theta \int_{1}^{2} r \cos r \mathrm{~d} r $$
$$ =\frac{\pi}{4}(2 \sin 2+\cos 2-\sin 1-\cos 1) . $$

**15.【解析】** 由对称性，得
$$ \iint_{D} \frac{x^{2}-x \cos y-y^{2}}{x^{2}+y^{2}} \mathrm{~d} x \mathrm{~d} y=\iint_{D} \frac{x^{2}-y^{2}}{x^{2}+y^{2}} \mathrm{~d} x \mathrm{~d} y=2 \int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \mathrm{~d} \theta \int_{0}^{\frac{1}{\sin \theta}} \frac{r^{2} \cos ^{2} \theta-r^{2} \sin ^{2} \theta}{r^{2}} r \mathrm{~d} r $$
$$ =\int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\left(\cos ^{2} \theta-\sin ^{2} \theta\right) \frac{1}{\sin ^{2} \theta} \mathrm{~d} \theta=\int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \cot ^{2} \theta \mathrm{~d} \theta-\int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} 1 \mathrm{~d} \theta $$
$$ =\int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \csc ^{2} \theta \mathrm{~d} \theta-\int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} 2 \mathrm{~d} \theta=-\cot \theta \mid_{\frac{\pi}{4}} ^{\frac{\pi}{2}}-\frac{\pi}{2}=1-\frac{\pi}{2} . $$

**16.【解析】** 积分区域如图所示，由对称性，用极坐标，得
$$ \iint_{D} \sqrt{1-x^{2}-y^{2}} \mathrm{~d} \sigma=2 \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \mathrm{~d} \theta \int_{0}^{\sin \theta} \sqrt{1-r^{2}} r \mathrm{~d} r $$
$$ =-\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \mathrm{~d} \theta \int_{0}^{\sin \theta}\left(1-r^{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}} \mathrm{~d}\left(1-r^{2}\right) $$
$$ =-\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\left[\frac{2}{3}\left(1-r^{2}\right)^{\frac{3}{2}} \mid_{0} ^{\sin \theta}\right] \mathrm{~d} \theta $$
$$ =-\frac{2}{3} \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\left(\cos ^{3} \theta-1\right) \mathrm{~d} \theta $$
$$ =-\frac{2}{3}\left(\frac{2}{3}-\frac{\pi}{2}\right)=\frac{\pi}{3}-\frac{4}{9} . $$

**17.【解析】** 由于 $f(x)+f(-x)$ 为偶函数，因而 $x[f(x)+f(-x)]$ 为奇函数，又 $f(x)-f(-x)$ 为奇函数，从而 $(x+1) f(x)+(x-1) f(-x)=x[f(x)+f(-x)]+[f(x)-f(-x)]$ 为奇函数. 故
$$ \text { 原式 }=\int_{-1}^{1}[(x+1) f(x)+(x-1) f(-x)] \mathrm{~d} x \int_{x^{3}}^{1} \sin y \mathrm{~d} y $$
$$ =\int_{-1}^{1}\left(\cos x^{3}-\cos 1\right)[(x+1) f(x)+(x-1) f(-x)] \mathrm{~d} x=0 . $$
