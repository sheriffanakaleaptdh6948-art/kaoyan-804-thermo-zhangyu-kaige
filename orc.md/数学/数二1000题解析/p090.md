$$P(x)\frac{\partial z}{\partial y} + P(y)\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} = P(x)f'(u)\frac{\partial u}{\partial y} + P(y)f'(u)\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}$$
$$= -\frac{P(x)P(y)f'(u)}{1-\varphi'(u)} + \frac{P(x)P(y)f'(u)}{1-\varphi'(u)} = 0.$$

**12.【答案】** $f'_2 + 2xf''_{11} + (2x^2+y)f''_{12} + xyf''_{22}$

**【解析】** 由题可得
$$u'_x = 2xf'_1 + yf'_2,$$
$$u''_{xy} = 2xf''_{11} + 2x^2 f''_{12} + f'_2 + yf''_{21} + xyf''_{22} = f'_2 + 2xf''_{11} + (2x^2+y)f''_{12} + xyf''_{22},$$
因为 $f$ 具有二阶连续偏导数，所以 $f''_{21} = f''_{12}$。

**13.【解析】** 函数复合关系如图所示，所以
$$\frac{\partial g}{\partial x} = y - f'_u \cdot \left(-\frac{y}{x^2}\right) - f'_v \cdot \frac{1}{y}, \quad \frac{\partial g}{\partial y} = x - f'_u \cdot \frac{1}{x} - f'_v \cdot \left(-\frac{x}{y^2}\right),$$
$$\frac{\partial^2 g}{\partial x^2} = \frac{y}{x^2} \left[ f''_{uu} \cdot \left(-\frac{y}{x^2}\right) + f''_{uv} \cdot \frac{1}{y} \right] + f'_u \cdot \left(-\frac{2y}{x^3}\right) - \frac{1}{y} \left[ f''_{vu} \cdot \left(-\frac{y}{x^2}\right) + f''_{vv} \cdot \frac{1}{y} \right]$$
$$= -\frac{y^2}{x^4} f''_{uu} + \frac{2}{x^2} f''_{uv} - \frac{1}{y^2} f''_{vv} - \frac{2y}{x^3} f'_u,$$
$$\frac{\partial^2 g}{\partial x \partial y} = 1 + f'_u \cdot \frac{1}{x^2} + \frac{y}{x^2} \left[ f''_{uu} \cdot \frac{1}{x} + f''_{uv} \cdot \left(-\frac{x}{y^2}\right) \right] + \frac{1}{y^2} f'_v - \frac{1}{y} \left[ f''_{vu} \cdot \frac{1}{x} + f''_{vv} \cdot \left(-\frac{x}{y^2}\right) \right]$$
$$= 1 + \frac{y}{x^3} f''_{uu} - \frac{2}{xy} f''_{uv} + \frac{x}{y^3} f''_{vv} + \frac{1}{x^2} f'_u + \frac{1}{y^2} f'_v,$$
$$\frac{\partial^2 g}{\partial y^2} = -\frac{1}{x} \left[ f''_{uu} \cdot \frac{1}{x} + f''_{uv} \cdot \left(-\frac{x}{y^2}\right) \right] - \frac{2x}{y^3} f'_v + \frac{x}{y^2} \left[ f''_{vu} \cdot \frac{1}{x} + f''_{vv} \cdot \left(-\frac{x}{y^2}\right) \right]$$
$$= -\frac{1}{x^2} f''_{uu} + \frac{2}{y^2} f''_{uv} - \frac{x^2}{y^4} f''_{vv} - \frac{2x}{y^3} f'_v,$$
故
$$x^2 \frac{\partial^2 g}{\partial x^2} + 2xy \frac{\partial^2 g}{\partial x \partial y} + y^2 \frac{\partial^2 g}{\partial y^2} = 2xy.$$
