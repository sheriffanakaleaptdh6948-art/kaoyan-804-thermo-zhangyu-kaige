**21.【解析】** (1) 由题意知，$dy = \frac{4y^3}{4-y^6} dx$，$\frac{dx}{dy} = \frac{4-y^6}{4y^3} = \frac{1}{y^3} - \frac{1}{4}y^3$，$-2 \leqslant y \leqslant -1$. 故
$$ \sqrt{1+\left(\frac{dx}{dy}\right)^2} = \sqrt{1+\left(\frac{1}{y^3}-\frac{1}{4}y^3\right)^2} = \sqrt{\left(\frac{1}{y^3}+\frac{1}{4}y^3\right)^2} $$
$$ = -\left(\frac{1}{y^3}+\frac{1}{4}y^3\right), -2 \leqslant y \leqslant -1, $$
则
$$ s = \int_{-2}^{-1} \sqrt{1+\left(\frac{dx}{dy}\right)^2} dy = -\int_{-2}^{-1} \left(\frac{1}{y^3}+\frac{1}{4}y^3\right) dy $$
$$ = \left(\frac{1}{2y^2} - \frac{1}{16}y^4\right)\bigg|_{-2}^{-1} = \frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{16} - \frac{1}{8} + 1 = \frac{21}{16}. $$

(2) 由(1)知，$x = \int \left(\frac{1}{y^3} - \frac{1}{4}y^3\right) dy = -\frac{1}{2y^2} - \frac{1}{16}y^4 + C$，且 $x(-1) = -\frac{9}{16} + C = -\frac{9}{16}$，则 $C=0$，
故 $x = -\frac{1}{2y^2} - \frac{1}{16}y^4$.
当 $\frac{dx}{dy} = 0$ 时，$y^6=4$，得 $y=-\sqrt[6]{4}$. 当 $-2 \leqslant y < -\sqrt[6]{4}$ 时，$\frac{dx}{dy} > 0$，当 $-\sqrt[6]{4} < y \leqslant -1$ 时，$\frac{dx}{dy} < 0$. 故 $y=-\sqrt[6]{4}$ 为最大值点，最大值为 $x_{\max} = -\frac{3}{4^{\frac{4}{3}}}$.

**22.【解析】** (1) $y' + \frac{t^2}{y} x^3 = \frac{y}{x}$ 整理为 $y' - \frac{1}{x} y = -t^2 x^3 y^{-1}$，由 $z=y^2$，得 $\frac{1}{2} \frac{dz}{dx} - \frac{z}{x} = -t^2 x^3$，即
$$ \frac{dz}{dx} - \frac{2}{x} z = -2t^2 x^3, $$
根据一阶线性微分方程的求解公式，解得
$$ z = e^{\int \frac{2}{x} dx} \left( -2 \int e^{-\int \frac{2}{x} dx} t^2 x^3 dx + C \right) = x^2 (-x^2 t^2 + C), $$
即 $y^2 = x^2 (-x^2 t^2 + C)$，根据初始条件 $y(1) = \sqrt{9-t^2}$，可得 $C=9$. 故 $y = x \sqrt{9-x^2 t^2}$.

(2) 由(1)得
$$ f(x) = \frac{1}{9} \int_0^3 y(x) dt = \frac{1}{9} \int_0^3 x \sqrt{9-x^2 t^2} dt. $$
