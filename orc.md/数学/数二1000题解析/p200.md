**28.【答案】(B)**

**【解析】**
$$ \lim_{x\to\infty} \frac{y}{x} = \lim_{x\to\infty} \ln\left(2+\frac{1}{x-1}\right) = \ln 2, $$
$$ \lim_{x\to\infty} (y - x\ln 2) = \lim_{x\to\infty} \left[ x\ln\left(2+\frac{1}{x-1}\right) - x\ln 2 \right] = \lim_{x\to\infty} x \left[ \ln\left(2+\frac{1}{x-1}\right) - \ln 2 \right] $$
$$ = \lim_{x\to\infty} x \ln\left[ 1 + \frac{1}{2(x-1)} \right] = \lim_{x\to\infty} x \cdot \frac{1}{2(x-1)} = \frac{1}{2}. $$
故曲线的斜渐近线方程为 $y = x\ln 2 + \frac{1}{2}$.

**29.【解析】** 先看无定义点 $x=0$，因
$$ \lim_{x\to 0} y = \lim_{x\to 0} x^3 \left( \mathrm{e}^{\frac{1}{x}} + \mathrm{e}^{-\frac{1}{x}} - 2 \right) $$
$$ \overset{\frac{1}{x}=t}{=} \lim_{t\to\infty} \frac{\mathrm{e}^t + \mathrm{e}^{-t} - 2}{t^3} = \lim_{t\to\infty} \frac{\mathrm{e}^t - \mathrm{e}^{-t}}{3t^2} $$
$$ = \lim_{t\to\infty} \frac{\mathrm{e}^t + \mathrm{e}^{-t}}{6t} = \lim_{t\to\infty} \frac{\mathrm{e}^t - \mathrm{e}^{-t}}{6} = \infty, $$
故 $x=0$ 为曲线的铅直渐近线.

再看
$$ \lim_{x\to\infty} y = \lim_{x\to\infty} x^3 \left( \mathrm{e}^{\frac{1}{x}} + \mathrm{e}^{-\frac{1}{x}} - 2 \right) \overset{\frac{1}{x}=t}{=} \lim_{t\to 0} \frac{\mathrm{e}^t + \mathrm{e}^{-t} - 2}{t^3} $$
$$ = \lim_{t\to 0} \frac{1+t+\frac{t^2}{2} + 1-t+\frac{t^2}{2} - 2 + o(t^2)}{t^3} $$
$$ = \lim_{t\to 0} \frac{t^2 + o(t^2)}{t^3} = \infty, $$
故曲线无水平渐近线.

$$ = \lim_{t\to 0} \frac{1+t+\frac{t^2}{2} + 1-t+\frac{t^2}{2} - 2 + o(t^2)}{t^2} = \lim_{t\to 0} \frac{t^2 + o(t^2)}{t^2} = 1, $$
