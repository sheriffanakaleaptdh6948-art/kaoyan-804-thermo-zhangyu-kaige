对(**)式两边乘 $|A|$，得
$$P^{-1} |A| A^{-1} P = P^{-1} A^* P = |A| B^{-1} = |B| B^{-1} = B^*,$$
从而 $A^* \sim B^*$，故③正确.

因 $A$ 可逆，故
$$BA = EBA = A^{-1}ABA = A^{-1}(AB)A,$$

**20.【解析】** (1)
$$
\begin{aligned}
A^2 &= \left( E - \frac{3}{\alpha^{\mathrm{T}} \alpha} \alpha \alpha^{\mathrm{T}} \right) \left( E - \frac{3}{\alpha^{\mathrm{T}} \alpha} \alpha \alpha^{\mathrm{T}} \right) \\
&= E - \frac{6}{\alpha^{\mathrm{T}} \alpha} \alpha \alpha^{\mathrm{T}} + 9 \frac{1}{\alpha^{\mathrm{T}} \alpha (\alpha^{\mathrm{T}} \alpha)} \alpha (\alpha^{\mathrm{T}} \alpha) \alpha^{\mathrm{T}} \\
&= E - \frac{6}{\alpha^{\mathrm{T}} \alpha} \alpha \alpha^{\mathrm{T}} + 9 \frac{1}{\alpha^{\mathrm{T}} \alpha} \alpha \alpha^{\mathrm{T}} = E + \frac{3}{\alpha^{\mathrm{T}} \alpha} \alpha \alpha^{\mathrm{T}} \\
&= - \left( E - \frac{3}{\alpha^{\mathrm{T}} \alpha} \alpha \alpha^{\mathrm{T}} \right) + 2E = -A + 2E,
\end{aligned}
$$
即
$$A^2 + A = A(A+E) = 2E,$$
故 $A$ 可逆，且
$$
\begin{aligned}
A^{-1} &= \frac{1}{2}(A+E) = \frac{1}{2} \left[ \left( E - \frac{3}{\alpha^{\mathrm{T}} \alpha} \alpha \alpha^{\mathrm{T}} \right) + E \right] \\
&= \frac{1}{2} \left( 2E - \frac{3}{\alpha^{\mathrm{T}} \alpha} \alpha \alpha^{\mathrm{T}} \right) = E - \frac{3}{2\alpha^{\mathrm{T}} \alpha} \alpha \alpha^{\mathrm{T}}.
\end{aligned}
$$

(2)
$$A\alpha = \left( E - \frac{3}{\alpha^{\mathrm{T}} \alpha} \alpha \alpha^{\mathrm{T}} \right) \alpha = \alpha - \frac{3}{\alpha^{\mathrm{T}} \alpha} \alpha (\alpha^{\mathrm{T}} \alpha) = \alpha - 3\alpha = -2\alpha,$$
故 $\alpha$ 是 $A$ 的特征向量，对应的特征值为 $-2$.
