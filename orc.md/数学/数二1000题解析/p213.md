**4.【答案】(C)**

**【解析】**
$$ I_1 = \int_0^\pi \frac{\sin x}{x} \mathrm{d}x + \int_\pi^{2\pi} \frac{\sin x}{x} \mathrm{d}x $$
$$ = \int_0^\pi \frac{\sin x}{x} \mathrm{d}x + \int_0^\pi \frac{\sin(\pi+t)}{\pi+t} \mathrm{d}t $$
$$ = \int_0^\pi \frac{\sin x}{x} \mathrm{d}x - \int_0^\pi \frac{\sin t}{\pi+t} \mathrm{d}t = \int_0^\pi \left( \frac{\sin x}{x} - \frac{\sin x}{\pi+x} \right) \mathrm{d}x $$
$$ = \int_0^\pi \frac{\pi \sin x}{x(\pi+x)} \mathrm{d}x > 0, $$
$$ I_2 = \int_0^{2\pi} \frac{\sin x}{2\pi-x} \mathrm{d}x \xrightarrow{2\pi-x=t} \int_0^{2\pi} \frac{\sin(2\pi-t)}{t} \mathrm{d}t $$
$$ = -\int_0^{2\pi} \frac{\sin t}{t} \mathrm{d}t = -I_1 < 0, $$
$$ I_3 = \int_0^{2\pi} \frac{\sin x}{x(2\pi-x)} \mathrm{d}x = \frac{1}{2\pi} \left( \int_0^{2\pi} \frac{\sin x}{x} \mathrm{d}x + \int_0^{2\pi} \frac{\sin x}{2\pi-x} \mathrm{d}x \right) $$
$$ = \frac{1}{2\pi} \left( \int_0^{2\pi} \frac{\sin x}{x} \mathrm{d}x - \int_0^{2\pi} \frac{\sin t}{t} \mathrm{d}t \right) = 0, $$
故 $I_2 < I_3 < I_1$.

**5.【答案】(A)**

**【解析】**
$$ I_1 \xrightarrow{t=x^2} \int_0^{2\pi} \frac{\sin t}{2\sqrt{t}} \mathrm{d}t = \int_0^\pi \frac{\sin t}{2\sqrt{t}} \mathrm{d}t + \int_\pi^{2\pi} \frac{\sin t}{2\sqrt{t}} \mathrm{d}t $$
$$ = \int_0^\pi \sin u \cdot \left( \frac{1}{2\sqrt{u}} - \frac{1}{2\sqrt{u+\pi}} \right) \mathrm{d}u \overset{(*)}{>} 0, $$
$$ I_2 = \int_{-\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{1+\sin x} = \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \left[ \frac{1}{1+\sin x} + \frac{1}{1+\sin(-x)} \right] \mathrm{d}x $$
$$ = \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \frac{2}{\cos^2 x} \mathrm{d}x = 2 \tan x \bigg|_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}} = 2 > 0. $$

**【注】**①(*)处，除区间端点外，$I_1$ 的被积函数严格大于0.
②因
$$ \int_{-a}^a f(x)\mathrm{d}x = \int_{-a}^a \left[ \frac{f(x)+f(-x)}{2} + \frac{f(x)-f(-x)}{2} \right] \mathrm{d}x $$
$$ = \frac{1}{2} \int_{-a}^a [f(x)+f(-x)] \mathrm{d}x = \int_0^a [f(x)+f(-x)] \mathrm{d}x (a>0), $$
故 $I_2 = \int_{-\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{1+\sin x} = \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \left[ \frac{1}{1+\sin x} + \frac{1}{1+\sin(-x)} \right] \mathrm{d}x$.
