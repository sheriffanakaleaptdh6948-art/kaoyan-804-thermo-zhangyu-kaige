第1章 行列式

$$
\begin{aligned}
D_4 &= (3+4x)\left[(2+3x) \cdot 2x^2 - 4x^2\right] - 9x \cdot 2x^2 \\
&= (3+4x) \cdot 6x^3 - 18x^3 = 24x^4.
\end{aligned}
$$

**【注】①**
$$
D_n = \begin{vmatrix}
a_1 & b_1 & & & & \\
c_2 & a_2 & b_2 & & & \\
& c_3 & a_3 & b_3 & & \\
& & \ddots & \ddots & \ddots & \\
& & & c_{n-1} & a_{n-1} & b_{n-1} \\
& & & & c_n & a_n
\end{vmatrix} = a_n D_{n-1} - b_{n-1}c_n D_{n-2},
$$
这可以作为递推的开端，往下就方便多了.

②注意 $D_n$ 元素分布规律为“左上至右下”阶数增加，即 $D_1=a_1$，$D_2=\begin{vmatrix} a_1 & b_1 \\ c_2 & a_2 \end{vmatrix}$，$D_3=\begin{vmatrix} a_1 & b_1 & 0 \\ c_2 & a_2 & b_2 \\ 0 & c_3 & a_3 \end{vmatrix}$，$\cdots$，别弄反了.

**11. 【答案】-4**

**【解析】** 由题设条件 $A\alpha_1 = \alpha_1 + 2\alpha_2 + \alpha_3$，$A\alpha_2 = 2\alpha_1 + \alpha_2 + \alpha_3$，$A\alpha_3 = \alpha_1 + \alpha_2 + 2\alpha_3$，故
$$
\begin{aligned}
A[\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3] &= [\alpha_1 + 2\alpha_2 + \alpha_3, 2\alpha_1 + \alpha_2 + \alpha_3, \alpha_1 + \alpha_2 + 2\alpha_3] \\
&= [\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3] \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & 1 \\ 2 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & 2 \end{bmatrix}.
\end{aligned}
$$

**方法一** 两边取行列式，得
$$
|A[\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3]| = |A| |[\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3]| = \left| [\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3] \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & 1 \\ 2 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & 2 \end{bmatrix} \right| = |[\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3]| \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 2 & 1 \\ 2 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & 2 \end{vmatrix},
$$
因为 $\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3$ 线性无关，所以 $|[\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3]| \neq 0$，可得 $|A| = \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 2 & 1 \\ 2 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & 2 \end{vmatrix} = -4$.

**方法二** 因为 $\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3$ 线性无关，记 $P=[\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3]$，则 $P$ 是可逆矩阵，从而可得
$$
AP = P \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & 1 \\ 2 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & 2 \end{bmatrix}, \text{即 } P^{-1}AP = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & 1 \\ 2 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & 2 \end{bmatrix},
$$
又因为相似矩阵有相同的行列式，所以 $|A| = \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 2 & 1 \\ 2 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & 2 \end{vmatrix} = -4$.
