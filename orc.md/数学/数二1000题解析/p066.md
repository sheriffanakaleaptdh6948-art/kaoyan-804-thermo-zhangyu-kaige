当 $x \neq 0$ 时，
$$ \varphi'(x) = \frac{x^3 f(x) - 2x \int_0^x t f(t) \mathrm{d}t}{x^4} = \frac{f(x)}{x} - \frac{2 \int_0^x t f(t) \mathrm{d}t}{x^3} \text{；} $$
当 $x=0$ 时，
$$ \varphi'(0) = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\varphi(x) - \varphi(0)}{x-0} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\int_0^x t f(t) \mathrm{d}t}{x^3} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{x f(x)}{3x^2} $$
$$ = \frac{1}{3} \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(x)}{x} = \frac{1}{3} \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(x) - f(0)}{x} = \frac{1}{3} f'(0) \text{.} $$
$$ \lim_{x \to 0} \varphi'(x) = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(x)}{x} - \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{2 \int_0^x t f(t) \mathrm{d}t}{x^3} = f'(0) - \frac{2}{3} f'(0) = \frac{1}{3} f'(0) = \varphi'(0) \text{，} $$
这表明 $\varphi'(x)$ 在 $x=0$ 处连续，故选(D).

**14.【答案】(C)**

**【解析】** $\int_{-1}^{x+6} f(t) \mathrm{d}t + \int_{x-3}^4 f(t) \mathrm{d}t = 14$ 两边分别求导，得 $f(x+6) - f(x-3) = 0$，即 $f(x+6) = f(x-3)$.
令 $x-3=t$，则 $x=t+3$，代入 $f(x+6)=f(x-3)$ 中得 $f(t+9)=f(t)$，所以 $f(x)$ 的周期为 9.
故正确选项为(C).

**【解析】**
$$ g(x) = \int_{-a}^a |x-t| f(t) \mathrm{d}t = \int_{-a}^x (x-t) f(t) \mathrm{d}t + \int_x^a (t-x) f(t) \mathrm{d}t $$
$$ = x \int_{-a}^x f(t) \mathrm{d}t - \int_{-a}^x t f(t) \mathrm{d}t + \int_x^a t f(t) \mathrm{d}t - x \int_x^a f(t) \mathrm{d}t \text{.} $$
因为 $f(x)$ 在 $[-a, a]$ 上连续，故上式中的变限积分函数均可导，求导得
$$ g'(x) = \int_{-a}^x f(t) \mathrm{d}t + x f(x) - x f(x) - x f(x) - \int_x^a f(t) \mathrm{d}t + x f(x) $$
$$ = \int_{-a}^x f(t) \mathrm{d}t - \int_x^a f(t) \mathrm{d}t \text{，} $$
其中，$\int_{-a}^x f(t) \mathrm{d}t \xlongequal{t=-u} \int_{-x}^a f(-u) \mathrm{d}u \xlongequal{f(x)\text{为偶函数}} \int_{-x}^a f(u) \mathrm{d}u = \int_{-x}^a f(t) \mathrm{d}t$.
故
$$ g'(x) = \int_{-x}^a f(t) \mathrm{d}t - \int_x^a f(t) \mathrm{d}t = \int_{-x}^a f(t) \mathrm{d}t + \int_a^x f(t) \mathrm{d}t = \int_{-x}^x f(t) \mathrm{d}t \text{，} $$
