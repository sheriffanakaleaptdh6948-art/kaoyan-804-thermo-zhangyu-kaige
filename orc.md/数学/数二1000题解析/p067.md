第9章 一元函数积分学的计算

$$ \xlongequal{\text{令}t=-u} \int_{a}^{-a} |x-u| f(-u)\mathrm{d}(-u) $$
$$ = \int_{-a}^{a} |x-u| f(u)\mathrm{d}u = g(x) \text{，} $$

故可知 $g(x)$ 是偶函数，所以选(C).

**16.【答案】(A)**
**【解析】** 因为
$$ F(x) = \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} |x-t| \sin t \mathrm{d}t = \int_{-\pi}^{x} (x-t) \sin t \mathrm{d}t + \int_{x}^{\pi} (t-x) \sin t \mathrm{d}t $$
$$ = x \int_{-\pi}^{x} \sin t \mathrm{d}t - \int_{-\pi}^{x} t \sin t \mathrm{d}t + \int_{x}^{\pi} t \sin t \mathrm{d}t - x \int_{x}^{\pi} \sin t \mathrm{d}t \text{，} $$
所以
$$ F'(x) = \int_{-\pi}^{x} \sin t \mathrm{d}t + x \sin x - x \sin x - x \sin x - \int_{x}^{\pi} \sin t \mathrm{d}t + x \sin x $$
$$ = \int_{-\pi}^{x} \sin t \mathrm{d}t - \int_{x}^{\pi} \sin t \mathrm{d}t \text{，} $$
于是
$$ F'(0) = \int_{-\pi}^{0} \sin t \mathrm{d}t - \int_{0}^{\pi} \sin t \mathrm{d}t = -\cos t \bigg|_{-\pi}^{0} + \cos t \bigg|_{0}^{\pi} = -4 \text{.} $$

**17.【答案】(A)**
**【解析】** 由于本题是求在 $x=-1$ 处的二阶导数值，不妨设 $x<0$.
$$ \frac{\mathrm{d}[y(x)]}{\mathrm{d}x} = \mathrm{e}^{-\sqrt{x^2}} (x^2)' = 2x\mathrm{e}^{-\sqrt{x^2}} = 2x\mathrm{e}^x \text{，} \frac{\mathrm{d}^2[y(x)]}{\mathrm{d}x^2} = (2x\mathrm{e}^x)' = 2\mathrm{e}^x + 2x\mathrm{e}^x = 2(1+x)\mathrm{e}^x \text{，} $$
所以
$$ \frac{\mathrm{d}^2[y(x)]}{\mathrm{d}x^2} \Bigg|_{x=-1} = 2(1+x)\mathrm{e}^x \bigg|_{x=-1} = 0 \text{.} $$

**18.【答案】** $\frac{1}{9}(1-2\sqrt{2})$

**【解析】** 方法一 由 $f(x) = \int_{1}^{x} \sqrt{1+t^3} \mathrm{d}t$，可得 $f'(x) = \sqrt{1+x^3}$，且 $f(1)=0$，从而
$$ \int_{0}^{1} x f(x) \mathrm{d}x = \frac{1}{2} \int_{0}^{1} f(x) \mathrm{d}(x^2) = \frac{1}{2} \left[ x^2 f(x) \Big|_{0}^{1} - \int_{0}^{1} x^2 f'(x) \mathrm{d}x \right] $$
$$ = -\frac{1}{2} \int_{0}^{1} x^2 \sqrt{1+x^3} \mathrm{d}x = -\frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{2}{9} (1+x^3)^{\frac{3}{2}} \Bigg|_{0}^{1} $$
$$ = \frac{1}{9}(1-2\sqrt{2}) \text{.} $$
