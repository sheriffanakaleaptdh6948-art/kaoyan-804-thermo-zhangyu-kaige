**方法二** 用直角坐标.

$$
\iint_{D} (x+y)\mathrm{d}\sigma = \iint_{D} [(x-1)+(y-1)+2] \mathrm{d}\sigma
$$
$$
= \iint_{D} (x-1)\mathrm{d}\sigma + \iint_{D} (y-1)\mathrm{d}\sigma + 2\iint_{D} \mathrm{d}\sigma \ .
$$
$$
\iint_{D} (x-1)\mathrm{d}\sigma = \int_{1-\sqrt{2}}^{1+\sqrt{2}} \mathrm{d}x \int_{1-\sqrt{2-(x-1)^2}}^{1+\sqrt{2-(x-1)^2}} (x-1)\mathrm{d}y
$$
$$
= \int_{1-\sqrt{2}}^{1+\sqrt{2}} 2(x-1)\sqrt{2-(x-1)^2} \mathrm{d}x \xlongequal{x-1=t} \int_{-\sqrt{2}}^{\sqrt{2}} 2t\sqrt{2-t^2} \mathrm{d}t = 0 \ .
$$

同理，$\iint_{D} (y-1)\mathrm{d}\sigma = 0$，而 $2\iint_{D} \mathrm{d}\sigma = 2 \cdot 2\pi = 4\pi$，所以原式 $= 4\pi$.

**30.【解析】** 如图所示，将 $D$ 分成 $D_1 \cup D_2$，$D_1$ 与 $D_2$ 除边界之外无公共部分.

**[图]** 直角坐标系中，$x$轴与$y$轴交于原点$O$。曲线 $y=\mathrm{e}^x$ 经过第一象限。矩形区域由 $x=0, x=1, y=0$ 及 $y=2\mathrm{e}$ 围成（右上角标注点 $(1, 2\mathrm{e})$）。曲线 $y=\mathrm{e}^x$ 将该矩形区域分为两部分：上方阴影区域标记为 $D_1$，下方区域标记为 $D_2$。$x$轴上标有刻度 $1$。

$$
\iint_{D} x\left|y-\mathrm{e}^x\right| \mathrm{d}\sigma = \iint_{D_1} x\left|y-\mathrm{e}^x\right| \mathrm{d}\sigma + \iint_{D_2} x\left|y-\mathrm{e}^x\right| \mathrm{d}\sigma
$$
$$
= \int_{0}^{1} \mathrm{d}x \int_{\mathrm{e}^x}^{2\mathrm{e}} x(y-\mathrm{e}^x) \mathrm{d}y + \int_{0}^{1} \mathrm{d}x \int_{0}^{\mathrm{e}^x} x(\mathrm{e}^x-y) \mathrm{d}y
$$
$$
= \int_{0}^{1} \left[ \left( \frac{xy^2}{2} - yx\mathrm{e}^x \right) \bigg|_{y=\mathrm{e}^x}^{y=2\mathrm{e}} \right] \mathrm{d}x + \int_{0}^{1} \left[ \left( yx\mathrm{e}^x - \frac{xy^2}{2} \right) \bigg|_{y=0}^{y=\mathrm{e}^x} \right] \mathrm{d}x
$$
$$
= \int_{0}^{1} \left( 2\mathrm{e}^2 x - 2\mathrm{e}x\mathrm{e}^x + \frac{1}{2}x\mathrm{e}^{2x} \right) \mathrm{d}x + \int_{0}^{1} \frac{x}{2} \mathrm{e}^{2x} \mathrm{d}x
$$
$$
= \frac{5}{4}\mathrm{e}^2 - 2\mathrm{e} + \frac{1}{4} \ .
$$
