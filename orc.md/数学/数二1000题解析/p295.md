强化篇
第15章 微分方程

$$ \ln \frac{p^2}{1+p^2} = 2x + C_1 \text{.} $$

当 $x=0$ 时，$y'(0)=p=1$，将 $p=1, x=0$ 代入上式得 $C_1 = \ln \frac{1}{2}$，于是有

$$ y' = p = \frac{\frac{\mathrm{e}^x}{\sqrt{2}}}{\sqrt{1-\frac{1}{2}\mathrm{e}^{2x}}} \text{,} $$

两边积分得

$$ y = \int \frac{\frac{\mathrm{e}^x}{\sqrt{2}}}{\sqrt{1-\left(\frac{\mathrm{e}^x}{\sqrt{2}}\right)^2}} \mathrm{d}x = \arcsin \frac{\mathrm{e}^x}{\sqrt{2}} + C_2 \text{.} $$

又 $y(0)=0$，将 $y=0, x=0$ 代入上式得 $C_2 = -\frac{\pi}{4}$，故 $y = \arcsin \frac{\mathrm{e}^x}{\sqrt{2}} - \frac{\pi}{4}$.

**36.【解析】** (1) 由题可得

$$ \int_0^x \sqrt{1+[y'(t)]^2} \mathrm{d}t = y'(x) \text{,} $$

对等式两端关于 $x$ 求导，得 $\sqrt{1+[y'(x)]^2} = y''(x)$.

令 $y'(x)=w$，则 $y''(x)=w'$，即 $\frac{\mathrm{d}w}{\mathrm{d}x} = \sqrt{1+w^2}$，分离变量，两边积分有 $\int \mathrm{d}x = \int \frac{1}{\sqrt{1+w^2}} \mathrm{d}w$，解得

$$ x = \ln(\sqrt{1+w^2}+w) + C_1 = \ln \left[ \mathrm{e}^{C_1} (\sqrt{1+w^2}+w) \right] \text{,} $$

即 $\sqrt{1+[y'(x)]^2} + y'(x) = C_2 \mathrm{e}^x$，两端取倒数，有理化得 $\sqrt{1+[y'(x)]^2} - y'(x) = \frac{1}{C_2} \mathrm{e}^{-x}$，两式相减，得

$$ y'(x) = \frac{1}{2} \left( C_2 \mathrm{e}^x - \frac{1}{C_2} \mathrm{e}^{-x} \right) \text{.} $$

由 $y'(0)=0$，得 $C_2 = \pm 1$，故 $y'(x) = \frac{1}{2}(\mathrm{e}^x - \mathrm{e}^{-x})$ ($y' = \frac{1}{2}(-\mathrm{e}^x + \mathrm{e}^{-x})$ 舍去)，积分得

$$ y = \frac{1}{2}(\mathrm{e}^x + \mathrm{e}^{-x}) + C_3 \text{.} $$

代入 $y(0)=1$，得 $C_3=0$，即 $y = \frac{1}{2}(\mathrm{e}^x + \mathrm{e}^{-x}) (x>0)$.

(2)
$$ \bar{x} = \frac{\int_0^{\ln 2} xy \mathrm{d}x}{\int_0^{\ln 2} y \mathrm{d}x} = \frac{\frac{1}{2} \int_0^{\ln 2} x(\mathrm{e}^x + \mathrm{e}^{-x}) \mathrm{d}x}{\frac{1}{2} \int_0^{\ln 2} (\mathrm{e}^x + \mathrm{e}^{-x}) \mathrm{d}x} = \frac{\frac{3}{4} \ln 2 - \frac{1}{4}}{\frac{3}{4}} = \ln 2 - \frac{1}{3} \text{,} $$
