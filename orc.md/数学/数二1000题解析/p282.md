(2) 由积分保号性可得

$$
\begin{aligned}
a & =\iint_{D} \frac{f(x, y)}{\sqrt{1-x^{2}}} \mathrm{~d} \sigma=\left|\iint_{D} \frac{f(x, y)}{\sqrt{1-x^{2}}} \mathrm{~d} \sigma\right| \leqslant \iint_{D} \frac{|f(x, y)|}{\sqrt{1-x^{2}}} \mathrm{~d} \sigma \\
& \stackrel{(*)}{=} |f(\xi, \eta)| \iint_{D} \frac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^{2}}} \mathrm{~d} \sigma \\
& =\left(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \pi-\sqrt{2} \arctan \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right)|f(\xi, \eta)|,
\end{aligned}
$$

其中 $(\xi, \eta) \in D$，即存在 $(\xi, \eta) \in D$，使得 $a \leqslant\left(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \pi-\sqrt{2} \arctan \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right)|f(\xi, \eta)|$，即

$$
|f(\xi, \eta)| \geqslant \frac{a}{\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \pi-\sqrt{2} \arctan \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}} \geqslant \frac{a}{\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \pi}=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{\pi} a .
$$

【注】(*)处利用了二重积分的中值定理.
设 $f(x, y)$ 和 $g(x, y)$ 都在闭区域 $D$ 上连续，且 $g(x, y)$ 在 $D$ 上不变号，则至少存在一点 $(\xi, \eta) \in D$，使得 $\iint_{D} f(x, y) \cdot g(x, y) \mathrm{d} x \mathrm{~d} y=f(\xi, \eta) \iint_{D} g(x, y) \mathrm{d} x \mathrm{~d} y$.
