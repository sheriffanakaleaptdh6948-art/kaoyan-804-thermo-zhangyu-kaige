第5章 特征值与特征向量

$$
= \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 0 & \frac{1}{\sqrt{14}} \\ 0 & 0 & \frac{2}{\sqrt{14}} \\ 0 & 0 & \frac{3}{\sqrt{14}} \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} -\frac{2}{\sqrt{5}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{5}} & 0 \\ -\frac{3}{\sqrt{70}} & -\frac{6}{\sqrt{70}} & \frac{5}{\sqrt{70}} \\ \frac{1}{\sqrt{14}} & \frac{2}{\sqrt{14}} & \frac{3}{\sqrt{14}} \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \frac{1}{14} & \frac{1}{7} & \frac{3}{14} \\ \frac{1}{7} & \frac{2}{7} & \frac{3}{7} \\ \frac{3}{14} & \frac{3}{7} & \frac{9}{14} \end{bmatrix}.
$$

令 $\xi = \begin{bmatrix} \frac{1}{\sqrt{14}} \\ \frac{2}{\sqrt{14}} \\ \frac{3}{\sqrt{14}} \end{bmatrix}$，则 $A=\xi\xi^{\mathrm{T}}$，且 $\xi^{\mathrm{T}}\xi=1$，故

$$
A^n = \xi(\xi^{\mathrm{T}}\xi)^{n-1}\xi^{\mathrm{T}} = 1^{n-1}(\xi\xi^{\mathrm{T}}) = A = \begin{bmatrix} \frac{1}{14} & \frac{1}{7} & \frac{3}{14} \\ \frac{1}{7} & \frac{2}{7} & \frac{3}{7} \\ \frac{3}{14} & \frac{3}{7} & \frac{9}{14} \end{bmatrix}.
$$
