**16.【答案】(B)**

**【解析】**
$$ \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} = f_1' \cdot y + f_2' \cdot \left( \frac{1}{x} + g' \cdot y \right), \quad \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = f_1' \cdot x + f_2' \cdot g' \cdot x, $$
故 $x \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} - y \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = f_2'$.

**17.【答案】(B)**

**【解析】**
$$ \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} = - \frac{\frac{\partial F}{\partial x}}{\frac{\partial F}{\partial z}} = - \frac{F_1' \cdot \frac{1}{z}}{F_1' \cdot \left( - \frac{x}{z^2} \right) + F_2' \cdot y}, $$
$$ \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = - \frac{\frac{\partial F}{\partial y}}{\frac{\partial F}{\partial z}} = - \frac{F_2' \cdot z}{F_1' \cdot \left( - \frac{x}{z^2} \right) + F_2' \cdot y}, $$
则
$$ x \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} - y \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = z. $$

**18.【答案】-2**

**【解析】** 令 $F(x,y,z) = 3x + xyz + z^3 - 1$，则 $F_x' = 3 + yz$，$F_z' = xy + 3z^2$. 当 $x=0$，$y=0$ 时，$z=1$，于是，
$$ \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} = - \frac{F_x'}{F_z'} = - \frac{3+yz}{xy+3z^2}, \quad \left. \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} \right|_{\substack{x=0 \\ y=0}} = - \left. \frac{3+yz}{xy+3z^2} \right|_{\substack{x=0 \\ y=0 \\ z=1}} = -1, $$
$$ \left. \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x^2} \right|_{\substack{x=0 \\ y=0}} = - \left. \frac{y \frac{\partial z}{\partial x}(xy+3z^2) - (3+yz)\left( y + 6z \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} \right)}{(xy+3z^2)^2} \right|_{\substack{x=0 \\ y=0 \\ z=1}} = -2. $$

**19.【解析】** 设 $F(x,y,z) = 2z - \mathrm{e}^z + 1 + \int_y^{x^2} \sin(t^2) \mathrm{d}t$，则
$$ F_x' = 2x \sin(x^4), \quad F_y' = -\sin(y^2), \quad F_z' = 2 - \mathrm{e}^z, $$
$$ \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} = - \frac{F_x'}{F_z'} = - \frac{2x \sin(x^4)}{2 - \mathrm{e}^z}, \quad \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = - \frac{F_y'}{F_z'} = \frac{\sin(y^2)}{2 - \mathrm{e}^z}. $$
将 $x=1, y=1, z=0$ 代入，得 $\left. \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} \right|_{(1,1)} = -2\sin 1$，$\left. \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} \right|_{(1,1)} = \sin 1$，则
$$ \left. \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x \partial y} \right|_{(1,1)} = \left. \frac{-2x \sin(x^4) \cdot \mathrm{e}^z \cdot \frac{\partial z}{\partial y}}{(2 - \mathrm{e}^z)^2} \right|_{(1,1,0)} = -2\sin^2 1. $$
