# 测试卷一

**一、选择题**

**1.【答案】(D)**

**【解析】** 当 $x \to 0^+$ 时，

$$ \int_0^{x^2} (\mathrm{e}^{t^2}+1)\mathrm{d}t \sim \int_0^{x^2} 2\mathrm{d}t = 2x^2, $$

$$ \int_0^x \ln(1+\sqrt{t^3})\mathrm{d}t \sim \int_0^x t^{\frac{3}{2}}\mathrm{d}t = \frac{2}{5}x^{\frac{5}{2}}, $$

$$ \int_0^{\sin x} \cos t^2 \mathrm{d}t \sim \int_0^{\sin x} 1\mathrm{d}t = \sin x \sim x, $$

$$ \int_0^{1-\cos x} \sqrt{\arcsin^3 t}\mathrm{d}t \sim \int_0^{1-\cos x} t^{\frac{3}{2}}\mathrm{d}t = \frac{2}{5}(1-\cos x)^{\frac{5}{2}} \sim \frac{\sqrt{2}}{20}x^5. $$

**2.【答案】(C)**

**【解析】** 原式 $= \lim_{n \to \infty} \sum_{k=1}^n \frac{1}{\left(1+\frac{k}{n}\right)^2} \cdot \frac{1}{n} = \int_0^1 \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{(1+x)^2} = -\frac{1}{1+x}\bigg|_0^1 = \frac{1}{2}$.

**3.【答案】(B)**

**【解析】** 显然，曲线没有铅直渐近线. 当 $x \to +\infty$ 时，$y \to +\infty$，所以沿 $x \to +\infty$ 方向不存在水平渐近线.

$$ \lim_{x \to +\infty} \frac{y}{x} = \lim_{x \to +\infty} \frac{x+\sqrt{x^2-x+1}}{x} = \lim_{x \to +\infty} \frac{x\left(1+\sqrt{1-\frac{1}{x}+\frac{1}{x^2}}\right)}{x} = 2, $$

$$ \lim_{x \to +\infty} (y-2x) = \lim_{x \to +\infty} (-x+\sqrt{x^2-x+1}) = \lim_{x \to +\infty} \frac{x^2-x+1-x^2}{\sqrt{x^2-x+1}+x} = -\frac{1}{2}. $$

故曲线有斜渐近线 $y=2x-\frac{1}{2}$. 再看沿 $x \to -\infty$ 方向：

$$ \lim_{x \to -\infty} y = \lim_{x \to -\infty} (x+\sqrt{x^2-x+1}) = \lim_{x \to -\infty} \frac{x^2-x+1-x^2}{\sqrt{x^2-x+1}-x} = \frac{1}{2}. $$

故曲线有水平渐近线 $y=\frac{1}{2}$. 综上，曲线有2条渐近线.
