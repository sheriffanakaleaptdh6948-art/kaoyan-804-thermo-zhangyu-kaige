**14.【答案】** $(-\infty, 2) \cup (2, +\infty)$

**【解析】** 令
$$ \boldsymbol{A} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & a & 2 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \\ -1 & 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix}, \boldsymbol{B} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & a \\ 1 & 3 & 0 \\ 2 & 7 & -a \end{bmatrix}, $$
若 $\boldsymbol{\alpha}_i$ 可由 $\boldsymbol{\beta}_1, \boldsymbol{\beta}_2, \boldsymbol{\beta}_3$ 线性表示，$i=1,2,3$，即 $\boldsymbol{BX}=\boldsymbol{A}$ 有解，则 $r(\boldsymbol{B}) = r([\boldsymbol{B}|\boldsymbol{A}])$.

又 $\boldsymbol{B} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & a \\ 1 & 3 & 0 \\ 2 & 7 & -a \end{bmatrix} \to \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & a \\ 0 & 1 & -a \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix}$，$r(\boldsymbol{B})=2$，于是 $r([\boldsymbol{B}|\boldsymbol{A}])=2$.

$$ [\boldsymbol{B}|\boldsymbol{A}] = \left[ \begin{array}{ccc:ccc} 1 & 2 & a & 1 & a & 2 \\ 1 & 3 & 0 & 0 & 1 & 1 \\ 2 & 7 & -a & -1 & 1 & 1 \end{array} \right] \to \left[ \begin{array}{ccc:ccc} 1 & 2 & a & 1 & a & 2 \\ 0 & 1 & -a & -1 & 1-a & -1 \\ 0 & 3 & -3a & -3 & 1-2a & -3 \end{array} \right] $$
$$ \to \left[ \begin{array}{ccc:ccc} 1 & 2 & a & 1 & a & 2 \\ 0 & 1 & -a & -1 & 1-a & -1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 & -2+a & 0 \end{array} \right], $$
故 $a=2$. 由题意，则 $a$ 的取值范围为 $(-\infty, 2) \cup (2, +\infty)$.
