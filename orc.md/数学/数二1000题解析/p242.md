**解析** **方法一** 令 $F(t)=\int_{a}^{t}[\varphi(x)]^{2} \mathrm{~d} x \cdot \int_{a}^{t}[\psi(x)]^{2} \mathrm{~d} x-\left[\int_{a}^{t} \varphi(x) \psi(x) \mathrm{d} x\right]^{2}$，则当 $t \geqslant a$ 时，有

由此可知 $F(t)$ 单调不减，又 $F(a)=0$，所以 $F(b) \geqslant 0$，即
$$\left[\int_{a}^{b} \varphi(x) \psi(x) \mathrm{d} x\right]^{2} \leqslant \int_{a}^{b}[\varphi(x)]^{2} \mathrm{~d} x \cdot \int_{a}^{b}[\psi(x)]^{2} \mathrm{~d} x \text {.}$$

**方法二** 由 $0 \leqslant \int_{a}^{b}[\varphi(x)-t \psi(x)]^{2} \mathrm{~d} x=\int_{a}^{b}[\varphi(x)]^{2} \mathrm{~d} x-2 t \int_{a}^{b} \varphi(x) \psi(x) \mathrm{d} x+t^{2} \int_{a}^{b}[\psi(x)]^{2} \mathrm{~d} x$，则
$$\Delta=4\left[\int_{a}^{b} \varphi(x) \psi(x) \mathrm{d} x\right]^{2}-4 \int_{a}^{b}[\varphi(x)]^{2} \mathrm{~d} x \int_{a}^{b}[\psi(x)]^{2} \mathrm{~d} x \leqslant 0 \text {.}$$

**【解析】** 令
$$F(x)=\int_{0}^{\int_{0}^{x} \mathrm{e}^{-t^{2}} \mathrm{~d} t} f(u) \mathrm{d} u-\int_{0}^{x} f(t) \mathrm{e}^{-t^{2}} \mathrm{~d} t, x \in[0,1] \text {，}$$
则
$$F^{\prime}(x)=f\left(\int_{0}^{x} \mathrm{e}^{-t^{2}} \mathrm{~d} t\right) \cdot \mathrm{e}^{-x^{2}}-f(x) \mathrm{e}^{-x^{2}}=\mathrm{e}^{-x^{2}}\left[f\left(\int_{0}^{x} \mathrm{e}^{-t^{2}} \mathrm{~d} t\right)-f(x)\right] \text {.}$$
由 $0<\mathrm{e}^{-x^{2}} \leqslant 1$ 知，$\int_{0}^{x} \mathrm{e}^{-t^{2}} \mathrm{~d} t \leqslant \int_{0}^{x} 1 \mathrm{~d} t=x$，且 $f(x)$ 单调递增，故 $F^{\prime}(x) \leqslant 0$，又 $F(0)=0$，故 $F(1) \leqslant 0$，
即 $\int_{0}^{\int_{0}^{1} \mathrm{e}^{-t^{2}} \mathrm{~d} t} f(x) \mathrm{d} x \leqslant \int_{0}^{1} f(x) \mathrm{e}^{-x^{2}} \mathrm{~d} x$. 故选项(B)正确.
