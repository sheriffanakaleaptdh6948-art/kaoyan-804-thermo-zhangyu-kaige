**6.【答案】(A)**

**【解析】** 令 $2x-3y+z-t=u$，则 $\int_{2x-3y}^{z} f(2x-3y+z-t) \mathrm{d}t = \int_{2x-3y}^{z} f(u) \mathrm{d}u$.

再令 $F(x,y,z) = \int_{2x-3y}^{z} f(u) \mathrm{d}u - \sin(2x-3y+z)$，则
$$
\begin{aligned}
F'_x &= -2f(2x-3y) - 2\cos(2x-3y+z), \\
F'_y &= 3f(2x-3y) + 3\cos(2x-3y+z), \\
F'_z &= f(z) - \cos(2x-3y+z).
\end{aligned}
$$
故
$$
\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} = -\frac{F'_x}{F'_z} = \frac{2[f(2x-3y) + \cos(2x-3y+z)]}{f(z) - \cos(2x-3y+z)},
$$
$$
\frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = -\frac{F'_y}{F'_z} = \frac{-3[f(2x-3y) + \cos(2x-3y+z)]}{f(z) - \cos(2x-3y+z)}.
$$
于是，$3\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} + 2\frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = 0$.

**7.【答案】(B)**

**【解析】** $\int_{0}^{+\infty} \frac{x^a(1-\mathrm{e}^{-x})}{(1+x)^b} \mathrm{d}x = \int_{0}^{1} \frac{x^a(1-\mathrm{e}^{-x})}{(1+x)^b} \mathrm{d}x + \int_{1}^{+\infty} \frac{x^a(1-\mathrm{e}^{-x})}{(1+x)^b} \mathrm{d}x = I_1 + I_2$.

对于
$$
I_1 = \int_{0}^{1} \frac{x^a(1-\mathrm{e}^{-x})}{(1+x)^b} \mathrm{d}x,
$$
当 $x \to 0^+$ 时，$\frac{x^a(1-\mathrm{e}^{-x})}{(1+x)^b} \sim x^{a+1} = \frac{1}{x^{-(a+1)}}$，故等价于讨论 $\int_{0}^{1} \frac{1}{x^{-(a+1)}} \mathrm{d}x$ 的敛散性，则当 $-(a+1) < 1$，即 $a > -2$ 时，$I_1$ 收敛；

对于
$$
I_2 = \int_{1}^{+\infty} \frac{x^a(1-\mathrm{e}^{-x})}{(1+x)^b} \mathrm{d}x,
$$
当 $x \to +\infty$ 时，$\frac{x^a(1-\mathrm{e}^{-x})}{(1+x)^b} \sim x^{a-b} = \frac{1}{x^{b-a}}$，故等价于讨论 $\int_{1}^{+\infty} \frac{1}{x^{b-a}} \mathrm{d}x$ 的敛散性，则当 $b-a > 1$，即 $b > a+1$ 时，$I_2$ 收敛.

综上，当 $a > -2, b > a+1$ 时，反常积分 $\int_{0}^{+\infty} \frac{x^a(1-\mathrm{e}^{-x})}{(1+x)^b} \mathrm{d}x$ 收敛.
