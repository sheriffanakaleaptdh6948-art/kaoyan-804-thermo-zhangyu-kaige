**5.【答案】** $\frac{1}{\ln 2}+\frac{1}{\ln^2 2}$

**【解析】**
$$
\text{原式}=\int_1^{+\infty}2^{-\sqrt{x}}\mathrm{d}x\xlongequal{\sqrt{x}=t}\int_1^{+\infty}2^{-t}\cdot 2t\mathrm{d}t
$$
$$
=\frac{-2}{\ln 2}\int_1^{+\infty}t\mathrm{d}(2^{-t})=-\frac{2}{\ln 2}\left(t\cdot 2^{-t}\bigg|_1^{+\infty}-\int_1^{+\infty}2^{-t}\mathrm{d}t\right)
$$
$$
=-\frac{2}{\ln 2}\left(-\frac{1}{2}+\frac{2^{-t}}{\ln 2}\bigg|_1^{+\infty}\right)=\frac{1}{\ln 2}+\frac{1}{\ln^2 2}.
$$

$$
\int_0^2 xf(1-x)\mathrm{d}x=\int_{-1}^1 (1+t)f(-t)\mathrm{d}t=2\int_0^1 f(t)\mathrm{d}t=4.
$$

**7.【答案】** $\frac{5}{3}$

**【解析】** 令 $F(x)=\int_{x^2}^{x^2+1}f(t)\mathrm{d}t(x>0)$，则 $F'(x)=2x\left[f(x^2+1)-f(x^2)\right]=2x^2$，则
$$
\int_{x^2}^{x^2+1}f(t)\mathrm{d}t=F(x)=\frac{2}{3}x^3+C.
$$
取极限 $x\to 0^+$，得 $\int_0^1 f(t)\mathrm{d}t=C$，即 $C=1$，则 $F(x)=\frac{2}{3}x^3+1(x>0)$，故
$$
\int_1^2 f(x)\mathrm{d}x=F(1)=\frac{2}{3}+1=\frac{5}{3}.
$$
