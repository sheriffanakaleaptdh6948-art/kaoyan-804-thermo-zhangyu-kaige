**40. 【解析】**
$$ 2\int_{-\frac{1}{2}}^1 \left( \int_0^x \mathrm{e}^{-y^2} \mathrm{d}y \right) \mathrm{d}x = 2 \left( -\int_{-\frac{1}{2}}^0 \mathrm{d}x \int_x^0 \mathrm{e}^{-y^2} \mathrm{d}y + \int_0^1 \mathrm{d}x \int_0^x \mathrm{e}^{-y^2} \mathrm{d}y \right) $$
$$ = 2 \left( - \iint_{-\frac{1}{2} \leqslant x \leqslant y \leqslant 0} \mathrm{e}^{-y^2} \mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y + \iint_{0 \leqslant y \leqslant x \leqslant 1} \mathrm{e}^{-y^2} \mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y \right) $$
$$ = 2 \left( -\int_{-\frac{1}{2}}^0 \mathrm{d}y \int_{-\frac{1}{2}}^y \mathrm{e}^{-y^2} \mathrm{d}x + \int_0^1 \mathrm{d}y \int_y^1 \mathrm{e}^{-y^2} \mathrm{d}x \right) $$
$$ = 2 \left[ \int_0^{-\frac{1}{2}} \left( y + \frac{1}{2} \right) \mathrm{e}^{-y^2} \mathrm{d}y + \int_0^1 (1-y) \mathrm{e}^{-y^2} \mathrm{d}y \right] $$
$$ = -\mathrm{e}^{-y^2} \bigg|_0^{-\frac{1}{2}} + \int_0^{-\frac{1}{2}} \mathrm{e}^{-y^2} \mathrm{d}y + 2\int_0^1 \mathrm{e}^{-y^2} \mathrm{d}y + \mathrm{e}^{-y^2} \bigg|_0^1 $$
$$ = 2\int_0^1 \mathrm{e}^{-y^2} \mathrm{d}y - \int_0^{\frac{1}{2}} \mathrm{e}^{-y^2} \mathrm{d}y + \frac{1}{\mathrm{e}} - \frac{1}{\sqrt[4]{\mathrm{e}}} $$
$$ = 2a - b + \frac{1}{\mathrm{e}} - \frac{1}{\sqrt[4]{\mathrm{e}}}, $$
所以 $m=2, n=1$.
