**13.【答案】** $\frac{2\pi}{3} \left[ \sqrt{(2h+1)^3} - 1 \right]$

【解析】由题知，第一象限内的抛物线方程为 $y = \sqrt{2x}$，$0 \leqslant x \leqslant h$. 由 $y = \sqrt{2x}$ 知，
$$ y' = \sqrt{2} \cdot \frac{1}{2\sqrt{x}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2x}} \text{，} $$
因此
$$ \sqrt{1 + (y')^2} = \sqrt{1 + \frac{1}{2x}} \text{，} $$
故所求旋转曲面的面积为
$$ \begin{aligned} S &= 2\pi \int_{0}^{h} \sqrt{2x} \cdot \sqrt{1 + \frac{1}{2x}} \mathrm{d}x = 2\pi \int_{0}^{h} \sqrt{2x} \cdot \frac{\sqrt{2x+1}}{\sqrt{2x}} \mathrm{d}x = 2\pi \int_{0}^{h} \sqrt{2x+1} \mathrm{d}x \\ &= \frac{2}{3}\pi (2x+1)^{\frac{3}{2}} \bigg|_{0}^{h} = \frac{2\pi}{3} \left[ \sqrt{(2h+1)^3} - 1 \right]. \end{aligned} $$

**14.【答案】** $\frac{1}{\sin x - 1}$

【解析】由 $\int_{0}^{y} \mathrm{e}^t \mathrm{d}t = \mathrm{e}^y - 1$，$\int_{0}^{x} \cos t \mathrm{d}t = \sin x$ 可将题干方程变形为 $\mathrm{e}^y + \sin x - 1 = 0$，等式两边同时对 $x$ 求导，得
$$ \mathrm{e}^y \cdot \frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} + \cos x = 0 \text{，} $$
于是
$$ \frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} = -\frac{\cos x}{\mathrm{e}^y} = \frac{\cos x}{\sin x - 1} \text{.} $$
上式两边再同时对 $x$ 求导，得
$$ \frac{\mathrm{d}^2y}{\mathrm{d}x^2} = \frac{-\sin x(\sin x - 1) - \cos^2 x}{(\sin x - 1)^2} = \frac{1}{\sin x - 1} \text{.} $$
