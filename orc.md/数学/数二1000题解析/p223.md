第9章 一元函数积分学的计算

**12.【解析】** $\int_{-1}^{1} x \ln(1+\mathrm{e}^{x}) \mathrm{d} x=\int_{-1}^{0} x \ln(1+\mathrm{e}^{x}) \mathrm{d} x+\int_{0}^{1} x \ln(1+\mathrm{e}^{x}) \mathrm{d} x$
$$
\begin{aligned}
& =\int_{1}^{0}(-x) \ln(1+\mathrm{e}^{-x})(-\mathrm{d} x)+\int_{0}^{1} x \ln(1+\mathrm{e}^{x}) \mathrm{d} x \\
& =-\int_{0}^{1} x \ln(1+\mathrm{e}^{-x}) \mathrm{d} x+\int_{0}^{1} x \ln(1+\mathrm{e}^{x}) \mathrm{d} x \\
& =\int_{0}^{1} x \ln \frac{1+\mathrm{e}^{x}}{1+\mathrm{e}^{-x}} \mathrm{~d} x=\int_{0}^{1} x \ln \mathrm{e}^{x} \mathrm{~d} x=\int_{0}^{1} x^{2} \mathrm{~d} x=\frac{1}{3} .
\end{aligned}
$$

【注】原函数不易求的非奇非偶函数在对称区间上的积分，可采用此题方法处理.

**13.【解析】** 注意到 $\frac{1}{(x+1)\left(x^{2}+1\right)}=\frac{1}{2(x+1)}-\frac{x-1}{2\left(x^{2}+1\right)}$，故
$$
\begin{aligned}
\int_{0}^{1} \frac{\mathrm{d} x}{(x+1)\left(x^{2}+1\right)} & =\frac{1}{2} \int_{0}^{1} \frac{\mathrm{d} x}{x+1}-\frac{1}{4} \int_{0}^{1} \frac{\mathrm{d}\left(x^{2}+1\right)}{x^{2}+1}+\frac{1}{2} \int_{0}^{1} \frac{\mathrm{d} x}{x^{2}+1} \\
& =\left.\frac{1}{2} \ln (x+1)\right|_{0} ^{1}-\left.\frac{1}{4} \ln \left(x^{2}+1\right)\right|_{0} ^{1}+\left.\frac{1}{2} \arctan x\right|_{0} ^{1}=\frac{\pi}{8}+\frac{1}{4} \ln 2 .
\end{aligned}
$$

**14.【解析】** 分离变量得 $F(x) \mathrm{d}[F(x)]=\frac{\mathrm{d}(\sin x)}{1+\sin ^{2} x}$，故 $F^{2}(x)=2 \arctan (\sin x)+C$. 将 $F(0)=\sqrt{\pi}$ 代入得 $C=\pi$，又 $F(x)>0$，故 $F(x)=\sqrt{2 \arctan (\sin x)+\pi}$.

**15.【解析】** $\int_{0}^{1} \frac{\arcsin \sqrt{x}}{\sqrt{x(1-x)}} \mathrm{d} x=2 \int_{0}^{1} \frac{\arcsin \sqrt{x}}{\sqrt{1-x}} \mathrm{~d}(\sqrt{x})=2 \int_{0}^{1} \arcsin \sqrt{x} \mathrm{~d}(\arcsin \sqrt{x})=\frac{\pi^{2}}{4}$.

**16.【解析】** $\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \frac{\mathrm{d} x}{\sin ^{2} x+3 \cos ^{2} x}=\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \frac{\mathrm{d} x}{\cos ^{2} x\left(3+\tan ^{2} x\right)} \stackrel{t=\tan x}{=} \int_{0}^{1} \frac{\mathrm{d} t}{3+t^{2}}$
$$
=\left.\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} \arctan \frac{t}{\sqrt{3}}\right|_{0} ^{1}=\frac{\pi}{6 \sqrt{3}} .
$$

**17.【解析】** $x=2$ 为奇点，应将积分区间分为两部分，即
$$
\begin{aligned}
\int_{1}^{3} \frac{\mathrm{d} x}{\sqrt{\left|2 x-x^{2}\right|}} & =\int_{1}^{2} \frac{\mathrm{d} x}{\sqrt{2 x-x^{2}}}+\int_{2}^{3} \frac{\mathrm{d} x}{\sqrt{x^{2}-2 x}} \\
& =\int_{1}^{2} \frac{\mathrm{d} x}{\sqrt{1-(x-1)^{2}}}+\int_{2}^{3} \frac{\mathrm{d} x}{\sqrt{(x-1)^{2}-1}} \\
& =\left.\arcsin (x-1)\right|_{1} ^{2}+\left.\ln \left[x-1+\sqrt{(x-1)^{2}-1}\right]\right|_{2} ^{3}=\frac{\pi}{2}+\ln (2+\sqrt{3}) .
\end{aligned}
$$
