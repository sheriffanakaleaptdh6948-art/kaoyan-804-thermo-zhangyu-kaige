易知$f(x)$在$x=1$处左极限存在，右极限不存在.

**14.【解析】**
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lim_{x \to +\infty} \left[ \frac{x^{x+1}}{(1+x)^x} - \frac{x}{\text{e}} \right] &= \lim_{x \to +\infty} \left[ \frac{x}{\left(1+\frac{1}{x}\right)^x} - \frac{x}{\text{e}} \right] = \lim_{x \to +\infty} \frac{x\left[\text{e} - \left(1+\frac{1}{x}\right)^x\right]}{\text{e}\left(1+\frac{1}{x}\right)^x} \\
&= \frac{1}{\text{e}^2} \lim_{x \to +\infty} \frac{\text{e} - \left(1+\frac{1}{x}\right)^x}{\frac{1}{x}} \xlongequal{\frac{1}{x}=u} \frac{1}{\text{e}^2} \lim_{u \to 0^+} \frac{\text{e} - (1+u)^{\frac{1}{u}}}{u} \\
&= -\frac{1}{\text{e}^2} \lim_{u \to 0^+} \frac{\text{e}^{\frac{1}{u}\ln(1+u)} - \text{e}}{u} = -\frac{\text{e}}{\text{e}^2} \lim_{u \to 0^+} \frac{\text{e}^{\frac{1}{u}\ln(1+u)-1} - 1}{u} \\
&= -\frac{1}{\text{e}} \lim_{u \to 0^+} \frac{\frac{1}{u}\ln(1+u)-1}{u} = -\frac{1}{\text{e}} \lim_{u \to 0^+} \frac{\ln(1+u)-u}{u^2} \\
&\xlongequal{\text{洛必达法则}} -\frac{1}{\text{e}} \lim_{u \to 0^+} \frac{\frac{1}{1+u}-1}{2u} = -\frac{1}{\text{e}} \lim_{u \to 0^+} \frac{-u}{2u(1+u)} = \frac{1}{2\text{e}}.
\end{aligned}
$$

**【注】** 应随时注意化简：将幂指函数化为指数函数，能求出极限的因子（其极限值不为零的因子）可利用乘积极限先求出，作变量替换将形式化简，以及用等价无穷小替换等.不要一上来就用洛必达法则.

**16.【答案】** $\text{e}^{-1}$

**【解析】** 易知，$f(x) = \frac{x+|x|}{2} = \begin{cases} x, & x \geqslant 0, \\ 0, & x < 0, \end{cases}$ 故
$$
f(1-x) = \begin{cases} 1-x, & x \leqslant 1, \\ 0, & x > 1, \end{cases} \quad f(1+x) = \begin{cases} 1+x, & x \geqslant -1, \\ 0, & x < -1, \end{cases}
$$
$$
f(1-x)f(1+x) = \begin{cases} 1-x^2, & -1 \leqslant x \leqslant 1, \\ 0, & \text{其他}, \end{cases}
$$
于是
$$
\lim_{x \to 0} [f(1-x)f(1+x)]^{\frac{1}{x^2}} = \lim_{x \to 0} (1-x^2)^{\frac{1}{x^2}} = \text{e}^{\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\ln(1-x^2)}{x^2}} = \text{e}^{\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{1}{x^2}(-x^2)} = \text{e}^{-1}.
$$
