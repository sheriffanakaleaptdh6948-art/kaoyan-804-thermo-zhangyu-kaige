# 第7章 一元函数微积分学的应用（三）
## ——物理应用

**1.** 【答案】 $\frac{19\sqrt{10}}{12}$ cm/s

【解析】 $P$到原点$O$的距离$s=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}$，于是
$$ \frac{\mathrm{d}s}{\mathrm{d}t} = \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}(\sqrt{x^2+y^2}) = \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x}(\sqrt{x^2+x}) \cdot \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t} = \frac{5(2x+1)}{2\sqrt{x^2+x}}. $$
当$x=9$时，$\left. \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}(\sqrt{x^2+y^2}) \right|_{x=9} = \left. \frac{5(2x+1)}{2\sqrt{x^2+x}} \right|_{x=9} = \frac{19\sqrt{10}}{12}$ (cm/s).

**2.** 【解析】 表面积为$S=4\pi r^2$，
$$ \frac{\mathrm{d}S}{\mathrm{d}t} = 8\pi r \frac{\mathrm{d}r}{\mathrm{d}t} = 8\pi \cdot 50 \cdot 5 = 2000\pi (\text{cm}^2/\text{s}). $$
体积为$V=\frac{4}{3}\pi r^3$，
$$ \frac{\mathrm{d}V}{\mathrm{d}t} = 4\pi r^2 \frac{\mathrm{d}r}{\mathrm{d}t} = 4\pi \cdot 50^2 \cdot 5 = 50000\pi (\text{cm}^3/\text{s}). $$

**3.** 【解析】 (1) 由$y=\ln\sqrt{x}=\frac{1}{2}\ln x$得$y'=\frac{1}{2x}$，曲线$L$在点$P(x,y)$处的切线方程为
$$ Y - \frac{1}{2}\ln x = \frac{1}{2x}(X-x), $$
于是
$$ \begin{aligned} S &= \int_2^4 \left( \frac{1}{2}\ln x + \frac{1}{2x}X - \frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{2}\ln X \right) \mathrm{d}X \\ &= \frac{1}{2}\ln x \cdot (4-2) + \frac{1}{4x}X^2 \bigg|_2^4 - \frac{1}{2}(4-2) - \frac{1}{2}(X\ln X - X)\bigg|_2^4 \\ &= \ln x + \frac{3}{x} - 1 - \frac{1}{2}(4\ln 4 - 4 - 2\ln 2 + 2) = \frac{3}{x} + \ln x - 3\ln 2. \end{aligned} $$
由$\frac{\mathrm{d}S}{\mathrm{d}x} = -\frac{3}{x^2} + \frac{1}{x} = 0$，得$x_0=3$，故$P_0\left(3, \frac{1}{2}\ln 3\right)$.

(2) $\left. \frac{\mathrm{d}S}{\mathrm{d}t} \right|_{x=\mathrm{e}} = \left. \frac{\mathrm{d}S}{\mathrm{d}x} \right|_{x=\mathrm{e}} \cdot \left. \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t} \right|_{x=\mathrm{e}} = \left( -\frac{3}{\mathrm{e}^2} + \frac{1}{\mathrm{e}} \right) \cdot 1 = \frac{\mathrm{e}-3}{\mathrm{e}^2}$.
