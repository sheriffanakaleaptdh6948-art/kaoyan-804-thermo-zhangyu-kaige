**方法二** 令 $\begin{cases} y=\xi\sqrt{x}, \\ x+y=\eta, \end{cases}$ 从中解出 $\xi, \eta$，即得变量代换 $\begin{cases} \xi=\frac{y}{\sqrt{x}}, \\ \eta=x+y. \end{cases}$

在这个变换下，$xOy$ 平面上的区域 $D$ 变成 $\xi O \eta$ 平面上的矩形区域 $D'$，其中 $D' = \{(\xi, \eta) | -\sqrt{2} \leqslant \xi \leqslant \sqrt{2}, 4 \leqslant \eta \leqslant 12\}$，其雅可比行列式为
$$
J = \frac{\partial(x,y)}{\partial(\xi,\eta)} = \frac{1}{\frac{\partial(\xi,\eta)}{\partial(x,y)}} = \frac{1}{\begin{vmatrix} \frac{\partial \xi}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial \xi}{\partial y} \\ \frac{\partial \eta}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial \eta}{\partial y} \end{vmatrix}} = \frac{1}{\begin{vmatrix} -\frac{y}{2x^{\frac{3}{2}}} & \frac{1}{x^{\frac{1}{2}}} \\ 1 & 1 \end{vmatrix}} = \frac{1}{-\frac{2x+y}{2x^{\frac{3}{2}}}} = -\frac{2x^{\frac{3}{2}}}{2x+y},
$$
于是
$$
\iint_D \frac{2x+y}{x^{\frac{3}{2}}} \mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y = \iint_{D'} \frac{2x+y}{x^{\frac{3}{2}}} \left| \frac{\partial(x,y)}{\partial(\xi,\eta)} \right| \mathrm{d}\xi\mathrm{d}\eta
$$
$$
= \iint_{D'} 2\mathrm{d}\xi\mathrm{d}\eta = 32\sqrt{2}.
$$
