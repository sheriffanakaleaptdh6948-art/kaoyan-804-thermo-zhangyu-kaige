**7.【答案】** $\frac{-6}{(2y-x)^{3}}$

【解析】对 $x^{2}-xy+y^{2}=1$ 两边关于 $x$ 求导，得 $2x-xy^{\prime}-y+2yy^{\prime}=0$，则 $y^{\prime}=\frac{y-2x}{2y-x}$，故
$$
\begin{aligned}
y^{\prime \prime} &=\frac{(2y-x)\left(y^{\prime}-2\right)-(y-2x)\left(2y^{\prime}-1\right)}{(2y-x)^{2}} \\
&=\frac{(2y-x)(y-2x-4y+2x)-(y-2x)(2y-4x-2y+x)}{(2y-x)^{3}} \\
&=\frac{-6\left(x^{2}-xy+y^{2}\right)}{(2y-x)^{3}}=\frac{-6}{(2y-x)^{3}} .
\end{aligned}
$$

**8.【答案】** $-2\pi$

【解析】由 $x=\int_{1}^{y-x} \sin ^{2}\left(\frac{\pi}{4} t\right) \mathrm{d}t$，将 $x=0$ 代入，有 $y=1$．再将所给方程两边对 $x$ 求导，得
$$
1=\sin ^{2}\left[\frac{\pi}{4}(y-x)\right] \cdot\left(y^{\prime}-1\right),
$$
于是 $y^{\prime}=\csc ^{2}\left[\frac{\pi}{4}(y-x)\right]+1$．从而 $y^{\prime \prime}=-2 \csc ^{2}\left[\frac{\pi}{4}(y-x)\right] \cot \left[\frac{\pi}{4}(y-x)\right] \cdot \frac{\pi}{4}\left(y^{\prime}-1\right)$．
将 $x=0, y=1$ 代入，得 $\left.y^{\prime}\right|_{x=0}=3,\left.y^{\prime \prime}\right|_{x=0}=-2 \pi$．

**9.【答案】** $\frac{2 \mathrm{e}^{2}+\mathrm{e}}{4}$

【解析】易知 $\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t}=2t-2, \frac{\mathrm{d}^{2}x}{\mathrm{d}t^{2}}=2$．对 $\mathrm{e}^{y} \sin t-y+1=0$ 的两边关于 $t$ 连续求导两次，得
$$
\mathrm{e}^{y} \sin t \frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}t}+\mathrm{e}^{y} \cos t-\frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}t}=0, \quad \mathrm{e}^{y} \sin t\left(\frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}t}\right)^{2}+2 \mathrm{e}^{y} \cos t \frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}t}+\mathrm{e}^{y} \sin t \frac{\mathrm{d}^{2}y}{\mathrm{d}t^{2}}-\mathrm{e}^{y} \sin t-\frac{\mathrm{d}^{2}y}{\mathrm{d}t^{2}}=0 .
$$
注意到 $t=0$ 时，$x=y=1$，一并代入上述式子，得 $\left.\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t}\right|_{t=0}=-2,\left.\frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}t}\right|_{t=0}=\mathrm{e},\left.\frac{\mathrm{d}^{2}y}{\mathrm{d}t^{2}}\right|_{t=0}=2 \mathrm{e}^{2}$．
