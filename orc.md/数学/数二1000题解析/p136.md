$$
-E-A=\left[\begin{array}{ccc}
-4 & -a & -4 \\
-2 & 0 & -2 \\
2 & a & 2
\end{array}\right] \rightarrow\left[\begin{array}{ccc}
-2 & 0 & -2 \\
-2 & 0 & -2 \\
2 & a & 2
\end{array}\right] \rightarrow\left[\begin{array}{ccc}
1 & 0 & 1 \\
0 & a & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 0
\end{array}\right],
$$
故 $r(-E-A)=\left\{\begin{array}{ll}2, & a \neq 0, \\ 1, & a=0 .\end{array}\right.$

当 $a \neq 0$ 时，$r(-E-A)=2$，则 $A$ 对应于 $\lambda_{1}=\lambda_{2}=-1$ 的线性无关的特征向量只有一个，故 $A$ 不能相似于对角矩阵.

当 $a=0$ 时，$r(-E-A)=1$，则 $A$ 对应于 $\lambda_{1}=\lambda_{2}=-1$ 的线性无关的特征向量有两个，故 $A$ 相似于对角矩阵.

当 $a=0, \lambda=-1$ 时，由 $(-E-A) x=\left[\begin{array}{ccc}-4 & 0 & -4 \\ -2 & 0 & -2 \\ 2 & 0 & 2\end{array}\right]\left[\begin{array}{l}x_{1} \\ x_{2} \\ x_{3}\end{array}\right]=\mathbf{0}$，解得 $\xi_{1}=\left[\begin{array}{c}1 \\ 0 \\ -1\end{array}\right], \xi_{2}=\left[\begin{array}{l}0 \\ 1 \\ 0\end{array}\right]$.

当 $a=0, \lambda=1$ 时，由 $(E-A) x=\left[\begin{array}{ccc}-2 & 0 & -4 \\ -2 & 2 & -2 \\ 2 & 0 & 4\end{array}\right]\left[\begin{array}{l}x_{1} \\ x_{2} \\ x_{3}\end{array}\right]=\mathbf{0}$，解得 $\xi_{3}=\left[\begin{array}{c}2 \\ 1 \\ -1\end{array}\right]$.

故存在可逆矩阵 $P=\left[\xi_{1}, \xi_{2}, \xi_{3}\right]$，使得 $P^{-1} A P=\left[\begin{array}{ccc}-1 & & \\ & -1 & \\ & & 1\end{array}\right]$.

**16.【答案】** $\left[\begin{array}{ccc}2 & & \\ & -1 & \\ & & \frac{1}{3}\end{array}\right]$

**【解析】** 因 $\lambda_{1}=-1, \lambda_{2}=2, \lambda_{3}=\frac{1}{3}$ 对应的特征向量分别是 $\xi_{1}, \xi_{2}, \xi_{3}$，即
$$
A \xi_{1}=-\xi_{1}, A \xi_{2}=2 \xi_{2}, A \xi_{3}=\frac{1}{3} \xi_{3},
$$
则
$$
\begin{aligned}
A\left(-\xi_{1}\right) & =-\left(-\xi_{1}\right), A\left(2 \xi_{2}\right)=2\left(2 \xi_{2}\right), A\left(3 \xi_{3}\right)=\frac{1}{3}\left(3 \xi_{3}\right), \\
& {\left[A\left(2 \xi_{2}\right), A\left(-\xi_{1}\right), A\left(3 \xi_{3}\right)\right]} \\
= & A\left[2 \xi_{2},-\xi_{1}, 3 \xi_{3}\right]=\left[2\left(2 \xi_{2}\right),-\left(-\xi_{1}\right), \frac{1}{3}\left(3 \xi_{3}\right)\right] \\
= & {\left[2 \xi_{2},-\xi_{1}, 3 \xi_{3}\right]\left[\begin{array}{ccc}
2 & & \\
& -1 & \\
& & \frac{1}{3}
\end{array}\right].}
\end{aligned}
$$
