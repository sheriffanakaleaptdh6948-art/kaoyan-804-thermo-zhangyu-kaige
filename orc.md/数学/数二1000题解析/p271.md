【解析】交换积分次序，得
$$ \int_0^1 \mathrm{d}y \int_{\sqrt{y}}^1 \sqrt{x^4-y^2} \mathrm{d}x = \int_0^1 \mathrm{d}x \int_0^{x^2} \sqrt{x^4-y^2} \mathrm{d}y $$

**23.** 【答案】 $\frac{1}{6} - \frac{1}{3\mathrm{e}}$

【解析】交换积分次序，得
$$ \int_0^1 x^2 \mathrm{d}x \int_x^1 \mathrm{e}^{-y^2} \mathrm{d}y = \int_0^1 \mathrm{e}^{-y^2} \mathrm{d}y \int_0^y x^2 \mathrm{d}x $$
$$ = \int_0^1 \mathrm{e}^{-y^2} \cdot \frac{y^3}{3} \mathrm{d}y = \frac{1}{3} \int_0^1 y^3 \cdot \mathrm{e}^{-y^2} \mathrm{d}y $$
$$ = \frac{1}{3} \cdot \left(-\frac{1}{2}\right) \int_0^1 y^2 \mathrm{d}(\mathrm{e}^{-y^2}) \overset{y^2=t}{=} -\frac{1}{6} \int_0^1 t \mathrm{d}(\mathrm{e}^{-t}) $$
$$ = -\frac{1}{6} (t\mathrm{e}^{-t} + \mathrm{e}^{-t}) \bigg|_0^1 = \frac{1}{6} - \frac{1}{3\mathrm{e}} . $$
