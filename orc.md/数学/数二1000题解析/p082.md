令 $f(x)=x$，$g(x)=-x$，亦满足题干条件，但
$$ \int_{-1}^{0} f[f(x)] \mathrm{d}x = \int_{-1}^{0} x \mathrm{d}x = \frac{1}{2} x^2 \bigg|_{-1}^{0} = -\frac{1}{2}, $$
$$ \int_{0}^{1} g[g(x)] \mathrm{d}x = \int_{0}^{1} x \mathrm{d}x = \frac{1}{2} x^2 \bigg|_{0}^{1} = \frac{1}{2}, $$
所以选项(D)错误.
对于选项(C)，
$$ \int_{-1}^{0} f[g(x)] \mathrm{d}x - \int_{0}^{1} f[g(x)] \mathrm{d}x = \int_{0}^{1} f[g(-x)] \mathrm{d}x - \int_{0}^{1} f[g(x)] \mathrm{d}x = \int_{0}^{1} \{f[g(-x)] - f[g(x)]\} \mathrm{d}x. $$
因为 $f'(x)>0$，$g'(x)<0$，所以 $f(x)$ 单调增加，$g(x)$ 单调减少，所以当 $x>0$ 时，有
$$ g(-x) > g(x), f[g(-x)] > f[g(x)], $$
故
$$ \int_{-1}^{0} f[g(x)] \mathrm{d}x - \int_{0}^{1} f[g(x)] \mathrm{d}x > 0, $$
即
$$ \int_{-1}^{0} f[g(x)] \mathrm{d}x > \int_{0}^{1} f[g(x)] \mathrm{d}x. $$

**8. 【答案】(C)**

**【解析】** 令 $F(x) = x \int_{0}^{x} f(t)g(t) \mathrm{d}t - \int_{0}^{x} f(t) \mathrm{d}t \int_{0}^{x} g(t) \mathrm{d}t$，$x \geqslant 0$，则 $F(0)=0$，
$$ F'(x) = \int_{0}^{x} f(t)g(t) \mathrm{d}t + x f(x)g(x) - f(x) \int_{0}^{x} g(t) \mathrm{d}t - g(x) \int_{0}^{x} f(t) \mathrm{d}t $$
$$ = \int_{0}^{x} [f(t)g(t) + f(x)g(x) - f(x)g(t) - g(x)f(t)] \mathrm{d}t $$
$$ = \int_{0}^{x} [f(x)-f(t)][g(x)-g(t)] \mathrm{d}t. $$
当 $f(x), g(x)$ 单调性相同时，$[f(x)-f(t)][g(x)-g(t)] \geqslant 0$，$F'(x) \geqslant 0$，有 $F(x) \geqslant 0$，故 $F(1) \geqslant 0$.
当 $f(x), g(x)$ 单调性相反时，$[f(x)-f(t)][g(x)-g(t)] \leqslant 0$，$F'(x) \leqslant 0$，有 $F(x) \leqslant 0$，故 $F(1) \leqslant 0$.
综上，选(C).

**【注】** 构造辅助函数时，为了前后统一量纲，令 $F(x) = x \int_{0}^{x} f(t)g(t) \mathrm{d}t - \int_{0}^{x} f(t) \mathrm{d}t \int_{0}^{x} g(t) \mathrm{d}t$，而不构造为 $F(x) = \int_{0}^{x} f(t)g(t) \mathrm{d}t - \int_{0}^{x} f(t) \mathrm{d}t \int_{0}^{x} g(t) \mathrm{d}t$.
