$$ \xlongequal{\frac{1}{x}=t} \lim_{t \to 0} \frac{\ln(\sin t + \cos t) - t}{t^2} = \lim_{t \to 0} \frac{\frac{\cos t - \sin t}{\sin t + \cos t} - 1}{2t} = \lim_{t \to 0} \frac{-\sin t - \sin t}{2t(\sin t + \cos t)} = -1 \text{，} $$

所以有斜渐近线 $y=x-1$ .

**19.【解析】**
$$ a = \lim_{x \to +\infty} \frac{y}{x} = \lim_{x \to +\infty} x \left[ \frac{\left(1+\frac{1}{x}\right)^x}{\text{e}} - 1 \right] $$
$$ \xlongequal{x=\frac{1}{t}} \lim_{t \to 0^+} \frac{\text{e}^{\frac{\ln(1+t)}{t}-1}-1}{t} = \lim_{t \to 0^+} \frac{\ln(1+t)-t}{t^2} = -\frac{1}{2} \text{，} $$
$$ b = \lim_{x \to +\infty} \left( y + \frac{1}{2}x \right) \xlongequal{x=\frac{1}{t}} \lim_{t \to 0^+} \frac{2\text{e}^{\frac{\ln(1+t)-t}{t}} - 2 + t}{2t^2} = \lim_{t \to 0^+} \frac{2\left[ \text{e}^{-\frac{t}{2} + \frac{t^2}{3} + o(t^2)} - 1 \right] + t}{2t^2} $$
$$ = \lim_{t \to 0^+} \frac{2 \left\{ -\frac{t}{2} + \frac{t^2}{3} + o(t^2) + \frac{1}{2} \left[ -\frac{t}{2} + \frac{t^2}{3} + o(t^2) \right]^2 + o(t^2) \right\} + t}{2t^2} $$
$$ = \lim_{t \to 0^+} \frac{\frac{2}{3}t^2 + \frac{1}{4}t^2 + o(t^2)}{2t^2} = \frac{11}{24} \text{，} $$

故斜渐近线为 $y = -\frac{1}{2}x + \frac{11}{24}$ .

**20.【答案】(C)**

**【解析】** 由
$$ \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\ln\left[ f(x+2) + \text{e}^{x^2} \right]}{1 - \cos x} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(x+2) + \text{e}^{x^2} - 1}{\frac{1}{2}x^2} = 2\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(x+2)}{x^2} + 2 = 4 \text{，} $$
可得 $\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(x+2)}{x^2} = 1$，因此 $f(2)=0$ .
$$ f'(2) = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(x+2)-f(2)}{x} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(x+2)}{x} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(x+2)}{x^2} \cdot x = 0 \text{，} $$
故 $x=2$ 为 $f(x)$ 的驻点，且在 $x=0$ 的某去心邻域内有 $\frac{f(x+2)}{x^2} > 0$，即 $f(x+2) > 0 = f(2)$，也即 $x=2$ 为极小值点. 故选(C).
