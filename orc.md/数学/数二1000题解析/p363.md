**20.【解析】方法一** 令 $\begin{cases} x=1+r\cos\theta, \\ y=1+r\sin\theta, \end{cases}$ 则 $D=\left\{(r,\theta) \Big| 0 \leqslant \theta \leqslant 2\pi, 0 \leqslant r \leqslant \sqrt{2}\right\}$， $\cdots\cdots 2$分

故
$$ \iint_D (x^2+x+y)\mathrm{d}\sigma = \int_0^{2\pi} \mathrm{d}\theta \int_0^{\sqrt{2}} [(1+r\cos\theta)^2 + 1 + r\cos\theta + 1 + r\sin\theta] r\mathrm{d}r \quad \cdots\cdots 5\text{分} $$
$$ = \int_0^{2\pi} \mathrm{d}\theta \int_0^{\sqrt{2}} [3r + r^2(3\cos\theta+\sin\theta) + r^3\cos^2\theta] \mathrm{d}r $$
$$ = \int_0^{2\pi} \left[ 3 + \frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}(3\cos\theta+\sin\theta) + \cos^2\theta \right] \mathrm{d}\theta \quad \cdots\cdots 9\text{分} $$
$$ = 7\pi. \quad \cdots\cdots 12\text{分} $$

**方法二** 将 $D$ 改写成 $D=\left\{(x,y) \Big| (x-1)^2+(y-1)^2 \leqslant (\sqrt{2})^2\right\}$，令 $u=x-1$，$v=y-1$，则
$$ \iint_D (x^2+x+y)\mathrm{d}\sigma = \iint_{u^2+v^2 \leqslant 2} [(u+1)^2+u+1+v+1]\mathrm{d}\sigma' \quad \cdots\cdots 4\text{分} $$
$$ = \iint_{u^2+v^2 \leqslant 2} (u^2+3u+v+3)\mathrm{d}\sigma' = \iint_{u^2+v^2 \leqslant 2} u^2\mathrm{d}\sigma' + 3 \cdot 2\pi \quad \cdots\cdots 6\text{分} $$
$$ = 6\pi + \frac{1}{2} \iint_{u^2+v^2 \leqslant 2} (u^2+v^2)\mathrm{d}\sigma' = 6\pi + \frac{1}{2} \int_0^{2\pi} \mathrm{d}\theta \int_0^{\sqrt{2}} r^2 \cdot r\mathrm{d}r \quad \cdots\cdots 9\text{分} $$
$$ = 6\pi + \pi $$
$$ = 7\pi. \quad \cdots\cdots 12\text{分} $$
