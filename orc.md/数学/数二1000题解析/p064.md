5.【答案】(B)

【解析】令 $\sqrt{x}=t$，则 $x=t^2$，$\mathrm{d}x=2t\mathrm{d}t$，且当 $x=0$ 时，$t=0$；当 $x=1$ 时，$t=1$. 所以
$$
\begin{aligned}
\int_{0}^{1} \mathrm{e}^{-\sqrt{x}} \mathrm{d} x &= 2 \int_{0}^{1} t \mathrm{e}^{-t} \mathrm{d} t = -2 t \mathrm{e}^{-t} \bigg|_{0}^{1} + 2 \int_{0}^{1} \mathrm{e}^{-t} \mathrm{d} t \\
&= -2 \mathrm{e}^{-1} - 2 \mathrm{e}^{-t} \bigg|_{0}^{1} = -4 \mathrm{e}^{-1} + 2 = 2 - \frac{4}{\mathrm{e}}.
\end{aligned}
$$

6.【答案】$-\frac{2 \sqrt{3}}{9} \pi$

【解析】
$$
\begin{aligned}
\int_{0}^{1} \frac{4 x-3}{x^{2}-x+1} \mathrm{d} x &= \int_{0}^{1} \frac{2(2 x-1)-1}{x^{2}-x+1} \mathrm{d} x = 2 \int_{0}^{1} \frac{\mathrm{d}\left(x^{2}-x+1\right)}{x^{2}-x+1} - \int_{0}^{1} \frac{1}{x^{2}-x+1} \mathrm{d} x \\
&= 2 \ln \left(x^{2}-x+1\right) \bigg|_{0}^{1} - \int_{0}^{1} \frac{1}{\left(x-\frac{1}{2}\right)^{2}+\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)^{2}} \mathrm{d} x \\
&= -\frac{2}{\sqrt{3}} \arctan \frac{x-\frac{1}{2}}{\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}} \Bigg|_{0}^{1} = -\frac{2}{\sqrt{3}}\left(\frac{\pi}{6}+\frac{\pi}{6}\right) = -\frac{2 \sqrt{3}}{9} \pi .
\end{aligned}
$$

8.【答案】$\frac{1}{2}[\sqrt{2}+\ln (\sqrt{2}+1)]$

【解析】由
$$
\begin{aligned}
\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \sec ^{3} \theta \mathrm{d} \theta &= \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \sec \theta \mathrm{d}(\tan \theta) = \sec \theta \tan \theta \Bigg|_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} - \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \sec \theta \tan ^{2} \theta \mathrm{d} \theta \\
&= \sqrt{2} - \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\left(\sec ^{3} \theta-\sec \theta\right) \mathrm{d} \theta,
\end{aligned}
$$
得
$$
\begin{aligned}
\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \sec ^{3} \theta \mathrm{d} \theta &= \frac{1}{2}\left(\sqrt{2}+\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \sec \theta \mathrm{d} \theta\right) = \frac{1}{2}\left(\sqrt{2}+\ln |\sec \theta+\tan \theta| \Bigg|_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\right) \\
&= \frac{1}{2}[\sqrt{2}+\ln (\sqrt{2}+1)].
\end{aligned}
$$
