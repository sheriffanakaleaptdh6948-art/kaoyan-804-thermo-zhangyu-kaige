第14章 二重积分

$$
=\begin{cases}
\sqrt{x^2+y^2}, & (x,y) \in D_1 \cup D_2, \\
1, & (x,y) \in D_3,
\end{cases}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\iint_{D} f(x,y) \mathrm{d}\sigma &= \iint_{D_1 \cup D_2} \sqrt{x^2+y^2} \mathrm{d}\sigma + \iint_{D_3} \mathrm{d}\sigma \\
&= \int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{3\pi}{4}} \mathrm{d}\theta \int_{1}^{\frac{1}{\sin\theta}} r^2 \mathrm{d}r + \frac{\pi}{4} \\
&= \frac{1}{3} \int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{3\pi}{4}} (\csc^3\theta - 1) \mathrm{d}\theta + \frac{\pi}{4} \\
&= \frac{1}{3} \int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{3\pi}{4}} \csc^3\theta \mathrm{d}\theta + \frac{\pi}{12}.
\end{aligned}
$$

**[图]** 坐标系 $xOy$，画有直线 $y=-x, y=x, y=1$ 以及虚线圆弧（单位圆）。区域 $D_1, D_2$ 为 $y=1$ 下方、圆弧上方的阴影部分（分别位于 $y$ 轴左侧和右侧），区域 $D_3$ 为圆弧下方、$y=\pm x$ 之间的部分。

而
$$
\begin{aligned}
\int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{3\pi}{4}} \csc^3\theta \mathrm{d}\theta &= \int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{3\pi}{4}} \csc\theta \csc^2\theta \mathrm{d}\theta \\
&= -\int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{3\pi}{4}} \csc\theta \mathrm{d}(\cot\theta) = -\left( \csc\theta \cot\theta \bigg|_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{3\pi}{4}} + \int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{3\pi}{4}} \cot^2\theta \csc\theta \mathrm{d}\theta \right) \\
&= -\left[ -\sqrt{2} - \sqrt{2} + \int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{3\pi}{4}} (\csc^2\theta - 1) \csc\theta \mathrm{d}\theta \right],
\end{aligned}
$$

所以
$$
\begin{aligned}
\int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{3\pi}{4}} \csc^3\theta \mathrm{d}\theta &= \sqrt{2} + \frac{1}{2} \int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{3\pi}{4}} \csc\theta \mathrm{d}\theta \\
&= \sqrt{2} + \frac{1}{2} (\ln|\csc\theta - \cot\theta|) \bigg|_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{3\pi}{4}} \\
&= \sqrt{2} + \frac{1}{2} [\ln(\sqrt{2}+1) - \ln(\sqrt{2}-1)] \\
&= \sqrt{2} + \ln(\sqrt{2}+1).
\end{aligned}
$$

故原式 $= \frac{\sqrt{2}}{3} + \frac{1}{3}\ln(\sqrt{2}+1) + \frac{\pi}{12}$.

**32.【解析】** 积分区域如图所示，则
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= -\int_{0}^{1} \mathrm{d}x \int_{x}^{1} (e^{-y^2} + e^y \sin y) \mathrm{d}y \\
&= -\int_{0}^{1} \mathrm{d}y \int_{0}^{y} (e^{-y^2} + e^y \sin y) \mathrm{d}x
\end{aligned}
$$
