**16.【答案】** $\left[ \frac{3}{2}, -\frac{1}{2}, 0 \right]^{\text{T}} + k[1, 0, 1]^{\text{T}}$，其中 $k$ 为任意常数

**【解析】** 对方程组的增广矩阵作初等行变换：

$$ \overline{A} = \left[ \begin{array}{ccc:c} \lambda & 1 & 1 & a \\ 0 & \lambda-1 & 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & \lambda & 1 \end{array} \right] \to \left[ \begin{array}{ccc:c} 1 & 1 & \lambda & 1 \\ 0 & \lambda-1 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 1-\lambda & 1-\lambda^2 & a-\lambda \end{array} \right] $$

$$ \to \left[ \begin{array}{ccc:c} 1 & 1 & \lambda & 1 \\ 0 & \lambda-1 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 1-\lambda^2 & a+1-\lambda \end{array} \right]. $$

由于方程组有两个不同的解，故 $r(A)=r(\overline{A})<3$，从而 $\begin{cases} \lambda \neq 1, \\ 1-\lambda^2=0, \\ a+1-\lambda=0, \end{cases}$ 解得 $\begin{cases} a=-2, \\ \lambda=-1. \end{cases}$

当 $a=-2$，$\lambda=-1$ 时，

$$ \overline{A} \to \left[ \begin{array}{ccc:c} 1 & 1 & -1 & 1 \\ 0 & -2 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 \end{array} \right] \to \left[ \begin{array}{ccc:c} 1 & 1 & -1 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 & -\frac{1}{2} \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 \end{array} \right] \to \left[ \begin{array}{ccc:c} 1 & 0 & -1 & \frac{3}{2} \\ 0 & 1 & 0 & -\frac{1}{2} \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 \end{array} \right]. $$
