第6章 二次型

将 $\lambda_1=\lambda_2=1$ 对应的特征向量 $\xi_1, \xi_2$ 正交化，得
$$ \boldsymbol{\eta}_1 = \xi_1 = [1, 0, 1]^{\mathrm{T}}, \quad \boldsymbol{\eta}_2 = \xi_2 - \frac{(\xi_2, \boldsymbol{\eta}_1)}{(\boldsymbol{\eta}_1, \boldsymbol{\eta}_1)} \boldsymbol{\eta}_1 = [1, 1, 0]^{\mathrm{T}} - \frac{1}{2}[1, 0, 1]^{\mathrm{T}} = \left[ \frac{1}{2}, 1, -\frac{1}{2} \right]^{\mathrm{T}}. $$
再将 $\boldsymbol{\eta}_1, \boldsymbol{\eta}_2, \xi_3$ 单位化，得
$$ \boldsymbol{\eta}_1^{\circ} = \begin{bmatrix} \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \\ 0 \\ \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \end{bmatrix}, \quad \boldsymbol{\eta}_2^{\circ} = \begin{bmatrix} \frac{\sqrt{6}}{6} \\ \frac{\sqrt{6}}{3} \\ -\frac{\sqrt{6}}{6} \end{bmatrix}, \quad \boldsymbol{\eta}_3^{\circ} = \begin{bmatrix} \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3} \\ -\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3} \\ -\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3} \end{bmatrix}, $$
得正交矩阵 $\boldsymbol{Q} = \begin{bmatrix} \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} & \frac{\sqrt{6}}{6} & \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3} \\ 0 & \frac{\sqrt{6}}{3} & -\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3} \\ \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} & -\frac{\sqrt{6}}{6} & -\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3} \end{bmatrix}$.

(2) 由(1)得 $\boldsymbol{Q}^{\mathrm{T}} \boldsymbol{A} \boldsymbol{Q} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & & \\ & 1 & \\ & & -2 \end{bmatrix} = \boldsymbol{\Lambda}$，又 $\boldsymbol{A} \sim \boldsymbol{B}$，则 $\boldsymbol{B} \sim \boldsymbol{\Lambda} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & & \\ & 1 & \\ & & -2 \end{bmatrix}$，$0 = 1+b+1$，解得 $b=-2$.
又有 $\boldsymbol{A}-\boldsymbol{E} \sim \boldsymbol{B}-\boldsymbol{E}$，则 $r(\boldsymbol{A}-\boldsymbol{E}) = r(\boldsymbol{B}-\boldsymbol{E})$，即 $r\left( \begin{bmatrix} 0 & & \\ & 0 & \\ & & -3 \end{bmatrix} \right) = r\left( \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 0 & 0 \\ c & -3 & 0 \\ -1 & -1 & 0 \end{bmatrix} \right)$，则 $c=-3$.
若存在正交矩阵 $\boldsymbol{P}$，则 $\boldsymbol{P}^{\mathrm{T}} \boldsymbol{A} \boldsymbol{P} = \boldsymbol{B} = (\boldsymbol{P}^{\mathrm{T}} \boldsymbol{A} \boldsymbol{P})^{\mathrm{T}} = \boldsymbol{B}^{\mathrm{T}}$，而题干中 $\boldsymbol{B}$ 不是对称矩阵，矛盾. 故不存在.

**8.【答案】(D)**
**【解析】** $\boldsymbol{A}$ 是实对称矩阵，$\boldsymbol{B}$ 是对角矩阵，若 $\boldsymbol{A}$ 与 $\boldsymbol{B}$ 相似，则必有 $\boldsymbol{A}$ 与 $\boldsymbol{B}$ 合同. 因
$$ \begin{aligned} |\lambda \boldsymbol{E} - \boldsymbol{A}| &= \begin{vmatrix} \lambda & 0 & 0 & -1 \\ 0 & \lambda & -1 & 0 \\ 0 & -1 & \lambda & 0 \\ -1 & 0 & 0 & \lambda \end{vmatrix} = \begin{vmatrix} \lambda-1 & 0 & 0 & -1 \\ 0 & \lambda-1 & -1 & 0 \\ 0 & -1+\lambda & \lambda & 0 \\ -1+\lambda & 0 & 0 & \lambda \end{vmatrix} \\ &= \begin{vmatrix} \lambda-1 & 0 & 0 & -1 \\ 0 & \lambda-1 & -1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & \lambda+1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & \lambda+1 \end{vmatrix} = (\lambda-1)^2 (\lambda+1)^2, \end{aligned} $$
则 $\boldsymbol{A}$ 有特征值 $\lambda_1=1$ (二重)，$\lambda_2=-1$ (二重)，故 $\boldsymbol{A}$ 与 $\boldsymbol{B}$ 相似且合同. 故应选(D).
