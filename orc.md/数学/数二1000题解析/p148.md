$$
|\lambda \boldsymbol{E}-\boldsymbol{A}|=\left|\begin{array}{ccc}
\lambda-1 & -1 & 2 \\
-1 & \lambda+2 & -1 \\
2 & -1 & \lambda-1
\end{array}\right|=\lambda(\lambda+3)(\lambda-3) .
$$

令 $|\lambda \boldsymbol{E}-\boldsymbol{A}|=0$，解得 $\boldsymbol{A}$ 的三个特征值为 $\lambda_{1}=3, \lambda_{2}=-3, \lambda_{3}=0$.

对于特征值 $\lambda_{1}=3$，对 $3 \boldsymbol{E}-\boldsymbol{A}$ 作初等行变换，得

$$
3 \boldsymbol{E}-\boldsymbol{A}=\left[\begin{array}{ccc}
2 & -1 & 2 \\
-1 & 5 & -1 \\
2 & -1 & 2
\end{array}\right] \rightarrow\left[\begin{array}{ccc}
1 & -5 & 1 \\
0 & 9 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 0
\end{array}\right] \rightarrow\left[\begin{array}{ccc}
1 & 0 & 1 \\
0 & 1 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 0
\end{array}\right],
$$

故 $\boldsymbol{A}$ 对应于特征值 $\lambda_{1}=3$ 的线性无关的特征向量为 $\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{1}=\left[\begin{array}{c}-1 \\ 0 \\ 1\end{array}\right]$.

对于特征值 $\lambda_{2}=-3$，对 $-3 \boldsymbol{E}-\boldsymbol{A}$ 作初等行变换，得

$$
-3 \boldsymbol{E}-\boldsymbol{A}=\left[\begin{array}{ccc}
-4 & -1 & 2 \\
-1 & -1 & -1 \\
2 & -1 & -4
\end{array}\right] \rightarrow\left[\begin{array}{ccc}
1 & 1 & 1 \\
0 & 3 & 6 \\
0 & -3 & -6
\end{array}\right] \rightarrow\left[\begin{array}{ccc}
1 & 1 & 1 \\
0 & 1 & 2 \\
0 & 0 & 0
\end{array}\right] \rightarrow\left[\begin{array}{ccc}
1 & 0 & -1 \\
0 & 1 & 2 \\
0 & 0 & 0
\end{array}\right],
$$

故 $\boldsymbol{A}$ 对应于特征值 $\lambda_{2}=-3$ 的线性无关的特征向量为 $\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{2}=\left[\begin{array}{c}1 \\ -2 \\ 1\end{array}\right]$.

对于特征值 $\lambda_{3}=0$，由(1)知，$\left[\begin{array}{l}1 \\ 1 \\ 1\end{array}\right]$ 是方程组 $(0 \boldsymbol{E}-\boldsymbol{A}) \boldsymbol{x}=\mathbf{0}$ 的一个基础解系，故 $\boldsymbol{A}$ 对应于特征值 $\lambda_{3}=0$ 的线性无关的特征向量为 $\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{3}=\left[\begin{array}{l}1 \\ 1 \\ 1\end{array}\right]$.

由于 $\boldsymbol{A}$ 是实对称矩阵，故 $\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{1}, \boldsymbol{\alpha}_{2}, \boldsymbol{\alpha}_{3}$ 是正交的，将其单位化，得

$$
\boldsymbol{\eta}_{1}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\left[\begin{array}{c}
-1 \\
0 \\
1
\end{array}\right], \boldsymbol{\eta}_{2}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{6}}\left[\begin{array}{c}
1 \\
-2 \\
1
\end{array}\right], \boldsymbol{\eta}_{3}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\left[\begin{array}{l}
1 \\
1 \\
1
\end{array}\right] .
$$

令 $\boldsymbol{Q}=\left[\begin{array}{ccc}-\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{6}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} \\ 0 & -\frac{2}{\sqrt{6}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} \\ \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{6}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\end{array}\right]$，则 $\boldsymbol{x}=\boldsymbol{Q} \boldsymbol{y}$ 是正交变换，在此变换下，二次型 $f(\boldsymbol{x})=\boldsymbol{x}^{\mathrm{T}} \boldsymbol{A} \boldsymbol{x}$ 可化为标准形 $f=3 y_{1}^{2}-3 y_{2}^{2}$.
