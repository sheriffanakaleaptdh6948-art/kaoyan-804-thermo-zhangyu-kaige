$$
[A \mid \boldsymbol{\beta}]=\left[\begin{array}{ccc:c}
1 & -2 & 3 & -4 \\

1 & 2 & 0 & -3
\end{array}\right] \rightarrow\left[\begin{array}{ccc:c}

\end{array}\right] \rightarrow\left[\begin{array}{ccc:c}
1 & -2 & 3 & -4 \\

0 & 0 & 1 & -3
\end{array}\right] \rightarrow\left[\begin{array}{ccc:c}
1 & 0 & 0 & 1 \\

0 & 0 & 1 & -3
\end{array}\right],

故 $A x=\boldsymbol{\beta}$ 有唯一解 $x=[1,-2,-3]^{\mathrm{T}}$.

记 $\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{1}=\left[\begin{array}{l}1 \\ 0 \\ 1\end{array}\right], \boldsymbol{\alpha}_{2}=\left[\begin{array}{c}-2 \\ 1 \\ 2\end{array}\right], \boldsymbol{\alpha}_{3}=\left[\begin{array}{c}3 \\ -1 \\ 0\end{array}\right]$，则
$$
[A, \boldsymbol{\beta}]=\left[\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{1}, \boldsymbol{\alpha}_{2}, \boldsymbol{\alpha}_{3}, \boldsymbol{\beta}\right], \quad B=\left[\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{2}, \boldsymbol{\alpha}_{1}, \boldsymbol{\alpha}_{3}\right], \quad \boldsymbol{\beta}=\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{1}-2 \boldsymbol{\alpha}_{2}-3 \boldsymbol{\alpha}_{3},
$$

$$
\left[\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{1}, \boldsymbol{\alpha}_{2}, \boldsymbol{\alpha}_{3}, \boldsymbol{\beta}\right]=\left[\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{2}, \boldsymbol{\alpha}_{1}, \boldsymbol{\alpha}_{3}\right]\left[\begin{array}{cccc}

\end{array}\right],

又 $\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{1}, \boldsymbol{\alpha}_{2}, \boldsymbol{\alpha}_{3}$ 线性无关，故 $B$ 可逆，可得 $C$ 唯一.
