**22.【答案】1**

**【解析】** $\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}|x| \mathrm{e}^{-x^{2}} \mathrm{~d} x=2 \int_{0}^{+\infty} x \mathrm{e}^{-x^{2}} \mathrm{~d} x=-\int_{0}^{+\infty} \mathrm{e}^{-x^{2}} \mathrm{~d}\left(-x^{2}\right)=-\mathrm{e}^{-x^{2}} \mid_{0}^{+\infty}=1 \text{ .}$

**23.【答案】(D)**

**【解析】** 由题可知，
$$f(x)=\left\{\begin{array}{ll} x, & |x|<1, \\ 0, & |x|=1, \\ -x, & |x|>1, \end{array}\right.$$
于是有
$$\int_{0}^{2} f(x) \mathrm{d} x=\int_{0}^{1} x \mathrm{~d} x+\int_{1}^{2}(-x) \mathrm{d} x=-1 \text{ .}$$

**24.【答案】** $\frac{2}{3}$

**【解析】**
$$ \text { 原式 }=\int_{-1}^{0^{-}}\left(\frac{1}{1+2^{\frac{1}{x}}}\right)^{\prime} \mathrm{d} x+\int_{0^{+}}^{1}\left(\frac{1}{1+2^{\frac{1}{x}}}\right)^{\prime} \mathrm{d} x $$
$$ =\left.\frac{1}{1+2^{\frac{1}{x}}}\right|_{-1} ^{0^{-}}+\left.\frac{1}{1+2^{\frac{1}{x}}}\right|_{0^{+}} ^{1}=1-\frac{2}{3}+\frac{1}{3}-0=\frac{2}{3} \text{ .} $$

**25.【答案】** $\pi\left(1-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)$

**【解析】方法一** 由于 $\arcsin x+\arccos x=\frac{\pi}{2}$，故
$$ \text { 原式 }=\pi \int_{0}^{\frac{1}{2}} \frac{x}{\sqrt{1-x^{2}}} \mathrm{~d} x=\pi\left(-\sqrt{1-x^{2}}\right)\left|_{0} ^{\frac{1}{2}}=\pi\left(1-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)\right. \text{ .} $$

**方法二**
$$ \text { 原式 }=\int_{-\frac{1}{2}}^{\frac{1}{2}} \frac{|x| \arccos x}{\sqrt{1-x^{2}}} \mathrm{~d} x=\int_{-\frac{1}{2}}^{0} \frac{-x \arccos x}{\sqrt{1-x^{2}}} \mathrm{~d} x+\int_{0}^{\frac{1}{2}} \frac{x \arccos x}{\sqrt{1-x^{2}}} \mathrm{~d} x $$
$$ =\left.\sqrt{1-x^{2}} \arccos x\right|_{-\frac{1}{2}} ^{0}+\int_{-\frac{1}{2}}^{0} \mathrm{~d} x-\left.\sqrt{1-x^{2}} \arccos x\right|_{0} ^{\frac{1}{2}}-\int_{0}^{\frac{1}{2}} \mathrm{~d} x $$
$$ =\frac{\pi}{2}-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \cdot \frac{2}{3} \pi-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \cdot \frac{\pi}{3}+\frac{\pi}{2} $$
$$ =\pi-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \pi=\pi\left(1-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right) \text{ .} $$
