当 $0 < t < 1$ 时，$f(t) = t \cdot \frac{1}{2}u^2 \bigg|_0^{t-1} + t \cdot \frac{1}{2}u^2 \bigg|_0^t = t^3 - t^2 + \frac{t}{2}$；

当 $t \leqslant 0$ 时，$f(t) = t \cdot \frac{1}{2}u^2 \bigg|_t^{t-1} = -t^2 + \frac{t}{2}$。

所以有
$$
\begin{aligned}
\int_{-1}^2 f(t)\mathrm{d}t &= \int_{-1}^0 \left(\frac{t}{2} - t^2\right)\mathrm{d}t + \int_0^1 \left(t^3 - t^2 + \frac{t}{2}\right)\mathrm{d}t + \int_1^2 \left(t^2 - \frac{t}{2}\right)\mathrm{d}t \\
&= \left(\frac{1}{4}t^2 - \frac{1}{3}t^3\right)\bigg|_{-1}^0 + \left(\frac{1}{4}t^4 - \frac{1}{3}t^3 + \frac{1}{4}t^2\right)\bigg|_0^1 + \left(\frac{1}{3}t^3 - \frac{1}{4}t^2\right)\bigg|_1^2 \\
&= -\frac{7}{12} + \frac{1}{6} + \frac{19}{12} = \frac{7}{6}.
\end{aligned}
$$

**9.【答案】(C)**

**【解析】** 由 $g(x)$ 是过点 $\left(-\frac{1}{2}, 0\right)$ 和 $(0, 1)$ 的直线，可得 $g(x) = 2x + 1$，故
$$
\begin{aligned}
\int_0^2 f[g(x)]\mathrm{d}x &= \int_0^2 f(2x+1)\mathrm{d}x \xlongequal{2x+1=t} \int_1^5 f(t)\mathrm{d}\left(\frac{t-1}{2}\right) \\
&= \frac{1}{2}\int_1^5 f(t)\mathrm{d}t = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 2\int_0^2 f(t)\mathrm{d}t = 1.
\end{aligned}
$$

**10.【答案】** $\frac{\pi}{8} - \frac{1}{4}\ln 2$

**【解析】**
$$
f(x) = \int_0^x f'(t)\mathrm{d}t + f(0) = \int_0^x \arctan(t-1)^2 \mathrm{d}t,
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\int_0^1 f(x)\mathrm{d}x &= \int_0^1 \mathrm{d}x \int_0^x \arctan(t-1)^2 \mathrm{d}t \xlongequal{\text{交换积分次序}} \int_0^1 \mathrm{d}t \int_t^1 \arctan(t-1)^2 \mathrm{d}x \\
&= \int_0^1 (1-t)\arctan(t-1)^2 \mathrm{d}t \xlongequal{(1-t)^2=u} \frac{1}{2}\int_0^1 \arctan u \mathrm{d}u = \frac{\pi}{8} - \frac{1}{4}\ln 2.
\end{aligned}
$$

**11.【答案】** $\frac{\pi}{6}$

**【解析】** 由题意，$f^2(x) = -x^2 + 2x + 3$，且 $f(x) > 0$，所以
$$
f(x) = \sqrt{-x^2 + 2x + 3} = \sqrt{4 - (x-1)^2},
$$
$$
\int_0^1 \frac{1}{f(x)}\mathrm{d}x = \int_0^1 \frac{1}{\sqrt{4-(x-1)^2}}\mathrm{d}x \xlongequal{x-1=t} \int_{-1}^0 \frac{1}{\sqrt{2^2 - t^2}}\mathrm{d}t = \arcsin\frac{t}{2}\bigg|_{-1}^0 = \frac{\pi}{6}.
$$
