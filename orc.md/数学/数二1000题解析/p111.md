**6.【答案】(B)**
**【解析】** 将行列式按第1行展开，得
$$ D_n = \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 1 & 0 & \cdots & 0 \\ 1 & 2 & 1 & \cdots & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 2 & \cdots & 0 \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & \cdots & 2 \end{vmatrix} = 2D_{n-1} - \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 1 & 0 & \cdots & 0 \\ 0 & 2 & 1 & \cdots & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 2 & \cdots & 0 \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & \cdots & 2 \end{vmatrix}_{(n-1)\times(n-1)} = 2D_{n-1} - D_{n-2}. $$
由此得到递推关系式 $D_n - D_{n-1} = D_{n-1} - D_{n-2}$，所以 $D_1, D_2, \cdots, D_n$ 成等差数列. 而
$$ D_1=2, D_2=3=D_1+1, $$
所以此等差数列的首项为2，公差 $d=1$，即 $D_n=n+1$. 故选(B).

**7.【答案】10**
**【解析】** 由 $r(AA^{\text{T}})=3$，知 $|A|=0$，$|AB|=|A||B|=0$，即
$$ |AB| \xrightarrow{\text{按第4行展开}} (-2) \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 0 & -a \\ -a & -5 & b \\ b & b & -2 \end{vmatrix} + (-4) \begin{vmatrix} 2 & a & -a \\ -a & 2 & b \\ b & 0 & -2 \end{vmatrix} $$
$$ = (-2) \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 0 & -a \\ -a & -5 & b \\ b & b & -2 \end{vmatrix} + (-4) \times 5 = 0, $$
得 $\begin{vmatrix} 2 & 0 & -a \\ -a & -5 & b \\ b & b & -2 \end{vmatrix} = -10$，于是 $\begin{vmatrix} -a & -5 & b \\ 2 & 0 & -a \\ b & b & -2 \end{vmatrix} = 10$.
