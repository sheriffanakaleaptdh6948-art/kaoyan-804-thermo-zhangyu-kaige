(2) 记 $A = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 3 \\ 1 & 0 \end{bmatrix}$，令 $|\lambda E - A| = \begin{vmatrix} \lambda - 2 & -3 \\ -1 & \lambda \end{vmatrix} = (\lambda + 1)(\lambda - 3) = 0$，得 $\lambda_1 = -1, \ \lambda_2 = 3$. 当 $\lambda_1 = -1$ 时，由 $(-E - A)\boldsymbol{x} = \boldsymbol{0}$，解得基础解系为 $\boldsymbol{\xi}_1 = \begin{bmatrix} -1 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}$；当 $\lambda_2 = 3$ 时，由 $(3E - A)\boldsymbol{x} = \boldsymbol{0}$，解得基础解系为 $\boldsymbol{\xi}_2 = \begin{bmatrix} 3 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}$.

令 $P = [\boldsymbol{\xi}_1, \boldsymbol{\xi}_2] = \begin{bmatrix} -1 & 3 \\ 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$，则 $P^{-1} = -\frac{1}{4} \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -3 \\ -1 & -1 \end{bmatrix}$，$P^{-1}AP = \begin{bmatrix} -1 & 0 \\ 0 & 3 \end{bmatrix}$.

故
$$
\begin{aligned}
A^{n-1} &= P \begin{bmatrix} -1 & 0 \\ 0 & 3 \end{bmatrix}^{n-1} P^{-1} = \begin{bmatrix} -1 & 3 \\ 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} (-1)^{n-1} & 0 \\ 0 & 3^{n-1} \end{bmatrix} \left(-\frac{1}{4}\right) \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -3 \\ -1 & -1 \end{bmatrix} \\

a_n + b_n = a_n + a_{n+1} = \frac{3}{4}(-1)^n + \frac{1}{4}3^n + \frac{3}{4}(-1)^{n+1} + \frac{1}{4}3^{n+1} = 3^n.
