# 第 13 章 多元函数微分学

**1.【答案】** $\frac{\pi}{2} \mathrm{d}x$

**【解析】**
$$ \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} = \frac{y - \sin(x+y)}{1 + [xy + \cos(x+y)]^2}, \quad \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = \frac{x - \sin(x+y)}{1 + [xy + \cos(x+y)]^2}, $$
故
$$ \left. \mathrm{d}z \right|_{(0, \pi)} = \left. \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} \right|_{(0, \pi)} \mathrm{d}x + \left. \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} \right|_{(0, \pi)} \mathrm{d}y = \frac{\pi}{2} \mathrm{d}x. $$

**2.【答案】** $\frac{y}{\sin x} + \frac{x}{\sin y}$

**【解析】** 因为 $z = f(\cos y - \cos x) + xy$，所以
$$ \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} = f'(\cos y - \cos x) \cdot \sin x + y, \quad \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = f'(\cos y - \cos x) \cdot (-\sin y) + x. $$
故
$$ \frac{1}{\sin x} \cdot \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} + \frac{1}{\sin y} \cdot \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = \frac{y}{\sin x} + \frac{x}{\sin y}. $$

**3.【答案】** $2y^3 x^{y^2} (1 + \ln x) f'(x^{y^2}) + y f(x^{y^2})$

**【解析】**
$$ \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} = y f'(x^{y^2}) \cdot (y^2 x^{y^2 - 1}), \quad \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = f(x^{y^2}) + y f'(x^{y^2}) \cdot (x^{y^2} \cdot \ln x \cdot 2y), $$
所以
$$ 2x \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} + y \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = 2y^3 x^{y^2} (1 + \ln x) f'(x^{y^2}) + y f(x^{y^2}). $$

**4.【答案】** $\frac{1}{5}$

**【解析】** 将 $x=0, y=2$ 代入 $(x+1)z + 2y \ln z - \arctan(xy) = 1$，得 $z + 4 \ln z = 1$. 因为 $z + 4 \ln z$ 是 $(0, +\infty)$ 上的增函数，且当 $z=1$ 时，$z + 4 \ln z = 1$，从而解得 $z=1$.

$(x+1)z + 2y \ln z - \arctan(xy) = 1$ 两端分别对 $x$ 求偏导，得
$$ z + (x+1) \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} + 2y \cdot \frac{1}{z} \cdot \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} - \frac{1}{1+(xy)^2} \cdot y = 0, $$
将 $x=0, y=2, z=1$ 代入上式，解得 $\left. \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} \right|_{(0, 2)} = \frac{1}{5}$.
