**5.【答案】(B)**

**【解析】** $F'(0)=\lim_{x\to 0}\frac{F(x)-F(0)}{x-0}=\lim_{x\to 0}\frac{g\left(x^2\sin\frac{1}{x}\right)-g(0)}{x-0}$，记
$$I_1=\left\{x\left|0<|x|<\delta,|x|\neq\frac{1}{n\pi},n=1,2,\cdots,\delta>0\right.\right\},$$
$$I_2=\left\{x\left||x|=\frac{1}{n\pi},n=1,2,\cdots\right.\right\},$$
则
$$\lim_{\substack{x\in I_1\\x\to 0}}\frac{g\left(x^2\sin\frac{1}{x}\right)-g(0)}{x-0}=\lim_{\substack{x\in I_1\\x\to 0}}\frac{g\left(x^2\sin\frac{1}{x}\right)-g(0)}{x^2\sin\frac{1}{x}-0}\cdot\frac{x^2\sin\frac{1}{x}-0}{x-0}=g'(0)\cdot 0=0,$$
$$\lim_{\substack{x\in I_2\\x\to 0}}\frac{g\left(x^2\sin\frac{1}{x}\right)-g(0)}{x-0}=\lim_{\substack{x\in I_2\\x\to 0}}\frac{g(0)-g(0)}{x-0}=0,$$
综上，$F'(0)=0$.

**6.【答案】(B)**

**【解析】** $f'(x)=\frac{\frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}t}}{\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t}}=\frac{2te^{t^2}}{3t^2}=\frac{2\mathrm{e}^{t^2}}{3t}$，当 $x=\frac{\pi}{2}$ 时，$t=1$. 则
$$f'\left(\frac{\pi}{2}\right)=\left.\frac{2\mathrm{e}^{t^2}}{3t}\right|_{t=1}=\frac{2\mathrm{e}}{3},$$
$$\lim_{n\to\infty}n\left[f\left(\frac{\pi}{2}+\frac{2}{n}\right)-f\left(\frac{\pi}{2}\right)\right]=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{f\left(\frac{\pi}{2}+\frac{2}{n}\right)-f\left(\frac{\pi}{2}\right)}{\frac{2}{n}}\cdot 2=2f'\left(\frac{\pi}{2}\right)=\frac{4\mathrm{e}}{3}.$$
