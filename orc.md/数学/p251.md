**例 9.12** $\lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^n [\ln(3n-2i) - \ln(n+2i)] = \_\_\_\_\_\_.$

**分析** $\ln(3n-2i) - \ln(n+2i) = \ln \frac{3n-2i}{n+2i} = \ln \frac{3-2\frac{i}{n}}{1+2\frac{i}{n}}$.

**解** 应填 0.

$$ \text{原式} = \lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^n \ln \frac{3n-2i}{n+2i} = \lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^n \ln \frac{3-2\frac{i}{n}}{1+2\frac{i}{n}} $$
